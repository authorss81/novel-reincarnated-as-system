# Terminology

Used consistently. In-world words are romanised. A word appears in the manuscript only with the meaning set here.

## Cultivation

**Body-Forging** — the first nine stages. Strength, bone, breath, blood. Ordinary soldiers reach stage four. Nine stages is a hard ceiling for anyone without a sect.

**Condensing** — the second realm, nine stages. Qi becomes something a person owns. Ning Yan's realm at the end of Volume 1.

**Foundation** — the third realm. The base a later technique is built on. Built once and rarely rebuilt.

**Golden Core** — the fourth realm. The furnace is what the process resembles, which is why nobody comments on it and everybody notices.

**Nascent Soul** — the fifth. The point at which a cultivator can be killed at a distance, which changes what a duel is.

**Severance** — the sixth. The point at which a cultivator stops needing a place to stand.

**Immortal Vessel** — the seventh. The last realm that can be built inside the mortal world.

**Ascension** — leaving the bound. Inside the bound this word means one thing only, and everybody uses it for something else.

**Tribulation** — the ordeal that accompanies a breakthrough. It is weather that has opinions.

**Aperture** — the centre of a cultivator's chest where qi is held. Damage to an aperture is permanent and visible.

**Meridian lines** — channels. A blocked meridian is a dead limb.

**Stage** — the ninth-numbered steps inside a realm. Written in the text as "stage six", never as a number with a percent sign.

## The world

**The bound** — what people inside the nine counties call their world. They do not say "realm"; they say "the bound" or "inside".

**The Wall** — the boundary. Named as an object for the first time in Volume 4.

**Cracking** — when a piece of the Verge's ash reaches a town, the local earth splits and a settlement is lost or founded. Eleven days of ash follow.

**The Verge** — the Ashwood Verge, the forest east of Lowwater. Quarry, hunting ground, burial ground.

**Quota** — the annual number of things, and sometimes people, a sect must supply. Set by the Bureau, filled by the sect.

**The furnace** — the practice of taking a stalled cultivator and a failing cultivator and making one pay for the other. Also the building at Qinghe.

**The kiln** — where core-stone is fired. The Broken Kiln outside Lowwater is the ruin of one.

**Merit** — the substance that settles when something the layer has listed is killed or a listed trial is cleared. It has no name in the mortal world. It is what a body refines from the air and what nobody has ever caught hold of.

## The apparatus

**The announcement layer** — the part of the world that speaks when something happens. Heavens-words. The layer announces breakthroughs, bounties and deaths to everyone inside the bound at once.

**An announcement** — the layer's sentence. Always one sentence. Always audible inside the bound. Always in the layer's voice, which is not a voice.

**A readout** — what the layer shows a cultivator to itself: name, realm, stage, pulse, intent. Nobody inside the bound can see one. Yuan sees everything.

**Designation** — Yuan's one act of authorship. He names a person Chosen, and from that hour the layer honours that person's quests.

**Chosen One** — the title the layer prints. Exactly one at a time, anywhere inside the bound.

**Quest** — a task the layer will honour, with a stated cost and a stated price. The layer cannot be lied to about either.

**Settlement** — how much of Yuan the layer has accounted for. At full Settlement he is drawn back into the machinery and ends. Settlement only goes down when Merit is spent.

**The Bureau of Records** — mortal clerks. Rice paper, fair hand, copies upward to something they never name.

**The Listener** — a Bureau clerk whose office is to hear announcements and write them down. There are nineteen inside the bound. They are dying. The job eats them.

**The Lantern order** — lamp-menders and bone-readers who follow the layer's residue and read last night's news aloud in market squares.

**The Warden's office** — what is behind the Wall. Keeps the walls. Keeps a list.

**The Order of the Recruited** — what the real realm calls its own senior practitioners. A title, and a lifestyle.

**The Scribe** — the intelligence above the layer. Plural to itself. It is the reason the walls are described as necessary.

**Crack, the** — a person whose cultivation has been opened from outside. Mortal, alive, aware, and unable to hold qi again. Common in the real realm. Never named inside the bound.

## Registers

**Present tense** — Yuan. Every sentence he speaks, inside or outside quotation marks, is written in the present tense. No other character is ever in the present tense. This is the reader's only cue for who is talking, and it never breaks.

**Bracketed lines** — the layer. Square brackets, on their own line, at most one per chapter. They announce. They never explain and never advise. Yuan is not the layer and must never be written as if he were.

## Naming

Names are two syllables and mostly plain. Sects take a place-word: Qinghe, Beihe, Canglan, Luoyuan, Shouyi, Wanzhou, Hanhui, Dushou, Tianshu. Family names are ordinary: Ning, Du, Qiu, Feng, Bai, Shen, Wei, Mei, Lu, Xie, Pei.

Recurring phrase, used once in the manuscript and never repeated: *the shape of a happy life.*

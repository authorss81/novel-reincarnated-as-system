# Power System

Two tracks move through the novel. Hers is the visible one. His is the one that matters.

## Cultivation

Seven realms, nine stages each, sixty-three steps. Progression is practice, time, materials and injury. It is not a game and it does not skip.

| Realm | Stages | Where it lands in the book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body-Forging | 1–9 | Vol 1, ch 1. Ning Yan starts at stage 3 and is at stage 9 by ch 3 — and is back at stage 4 by ch 4, because he spent it. |
| Condensing | 1–9 | Vol 1 climax (stage 1), Vol 2 to stage 5 |
| Foundation | 1–9 | Vol 2 ch 60 – Vol 3 |
| Golden Core | 1–9 | Vol 4 – Vol 5 |
| Nascent Soul | 1–9 | Vol 6 – Vol 8 |
| Severance | 1–9 | Vol 9 – Vol 11 |
| Immortal Vessel | 1–9 | Vol 12 – Vol 14 |
| The Last Ascension | — | Vol 15 – Vol 16. Never completed as a system ascension. She ends it. |

### What advancement costs

- **Time.** Real months. A breakthrough skipped in a batch outline is a broken outline.
- **Meridian damage.** Blocked meridians are permanent and visible. She loses the use of one index finger in Volume 4 and never gets it back.
- **Aperture damage.** Visible. Permanent. A cultivator with a cracked aperture is a cultivator who stops at whatever they reached.
- **Materials.** Sect money, animal core-stone, and other people's labour.
- **Witness.** Advancing inside the bound is announced. There is no private breakthrough. This is the entire reason her Volume 1 climax is a political event and not a training scene.

### Rules that hold without exception

1. A cultivator cannot hold two different techniques at breaking point at once. Advancement is sequential or it is nothing.
2. Qi is finite, slow to gather, and faster to spend than to replace. Running dry is the most common cause of death under thirty.
3. Nothing in the bound can repair an aperture. Not money, not a sect, not a miracle.
4. Realm walls do not bend for talent. They bend for documents. In Volume 2 the first thing she ever wants is not power and it is a permit.

## The layer's rules — what Yuan can and cannot do

These are the real system. They are stated once in Volume 1 and demonstrated for the rest of the book.

1. **He can read everything and touch nothing.** Every cultivator inside the bound is a readout: name, realm, stage, pulse, intent. He has no hands and no qi. He cannot lift anything.
2. **He can designate one Chosen One.** One at a time, anywhere inside the bound. Designating costs him his last coherent memory of his own name.
3. **The layer honours a Chosen One's quests.** Rewards land, qi answers her a half-step faster, and technique she could not have learned becomes learnable. This is the promise.
4. **The layer cannot be lied to about a quest.** Not by him, not by anyone. Every quest carries a stated cost and a stated price, in plain language, before she accepts.
5. **He cannot grant what he cannot pay for.** If he offers a reward the bound cannot honour, the quest fails and she takes the failure herself.
6. **She can refuse him.** A refused designation begins to rot, and he cannot stop it. This is the only leverage she has and she knows it from Chapter 3.

## Merit

Merit is the substance that settles when something the layer has listed is killed, or a listed trial is cleared. No mortal has ever held it. Cultivators breathe it without knowing.

- Normally it evaporates into the air.
- Yuan intercepts a share of it, and it settles into him as coherence.
- **He spends it.** This is the whole economy of the novel.

## The two costs

**His: Settlement.** Every use of the Chosen channel is logged by the layer. Settlement rises. At full Settlement he is drawn back into the machinery and ends, and the designation dies with him. Settlement only falls when Merit is spent. So his survival is bought with the exact resource he is trying to make her produce.

**Hers: the Foundation tax.** Merit that Yuan spends is not taken from the sky. It is taken from the strongest thing she has, which is her own cultivated foundation, and it does not come back. She gets stronger and less solid at the same time. She loses progress, not potential: the technique is gone and cannot be relearned. Nothing in the text is ever able to tell her exactly how much.

This is the cost that must be demonstrated by event, not exposition, by the end of Chapter 3.

## Yuan's own stages, and what each one takes

| Stage | Volumes | What he can do | What it costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Noise | 1 | read her, and only near her | he loses hours of his own memory at a time |
| Voice | 1–2 | speak to her at will; see through her eyes | Settlement climbs every time he does |
| Ledger | 3–5 | read other people through her; issue announcements | he starts making decisions for people who did not ask |
| Hand | 6–8 | move the world through her | he becomes the architecture he hates |
| Door | 9–11 | open walls, physical and informational | every wall he opens leaves someone permanently on the wrong side |
| Court | 12–14 | hold a shape, be addressed as a person | the Scribe can now find him, and does |
| Announcement | 15–16 | be heard by the thing that made him | the only way to be heard that far is to dissolve into the layer's substrate; there is no way back, and he knows it when he chooses it |

## The rules the reader learns once and does not hear again

- Announcements are audible to everyone inside the bound and reach no further than the bound.
- Announcements do not lie. They report. What is edited is what they are made to say.
- The layer has one Chosen One at a time. That is a structural limit, not a policy.
- A quest's cost is paid by the Chosen One. Always. Never by him.
- Merit cannot be given. It can only be intercepted.
- Nothing she loses comes back. This is the rule the entire ending is built on.

## Demonstration checkpoints

- **By end of ch 3:** the mechanic works once and the cost is visible on her body and in her memory.
- **End of Vol 1:** she ascends publicly and the layer speaks her name, which has never happened for a servant-born.
- **Vol 2:** she learns that a breakthrough needs a document as well as a body.
- **Vol 6:** the cost model is explained by an antagonist who has personally paid it.
- **Vol 11:** he learns he was part of the layer, and the reader learns the Foundation tax was the layer's own design.
- **Vol 16:** the tax is the thing she finally refuses to pay.

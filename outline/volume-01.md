# Volume 1 — A Voice in the Dark (chapters 1–58)

## Goal

> In this volume, **Ning Yan must survive her first ascension**, or **Yuan loses his only Chosen One and dies again**.

## Central pressure

The winter quota. Every year the Bureau posts a number to Qinghe Sect, and every winter the outer disciples go into the Ashwood Verge to fill it. The number counts people as well as beasts, and the difference is settled by the Elder Hall without publishing the arithmetic. Ning Yan is at the bottom of the list, which means that if she fails, she does not fail — she is substituted.

Into this arrives a dead man who cannot touch anything.

## Starting state

- **Ning Yan:** seventeen, servant-born, third-rank outer disciple, Body-Forging stage 3. Mother dead at fourteen of wet lungs, father taken for a stalled ascension when she was eleven, brother Ning Wu taken for the kiln at thirteen. One quarter-token of the winter's quota outstanding. She has been sent into the Verge with eleven other outer disciples and a Team Elder who has already decided four of them will not come back.
- **Yuan:** dead six years, unaware, lodged in the announcement layer, incoherent, with no designation and no way to speak.
- **Relationship:** none. She has never heard of him and has no reason to believe anything would ever speak to her.
- **Power:** Body-Forging 3 out of 9. The sect has not given her the map to Condensing. Servants are not given it.
- **Public standing:** a number. Her name is in the furnace column because her father was.

## Major locations

Nine Steps Terrace and the outer yard; the staging ground below the Lower Gate; the Ashwood Verge; Lowwater and the Lantern Register tower; the Broken Kiln outside Lowwater; the furnace room and the records room beneath it; the Assembly Hall.

## Major factions

- **Qinghe Sect's Elder Hall** — nine inner elders, quarrelling over quota, run this season by Elder Qiu Zhulang.
- **The furnace crew** — servant-rank inner disciples and furnace-men. Where Ning Yan ends up.
- **The outer disciples** — twelve on the run, and a hundred at the sect who will notice what she does.
- **The Lantern order** — lamp-menders and bone-readers who follow the layer's residue into the Verge and are not to be harmed, because the sect cannot read without them.
- **The Bureau of Records** — present in the volume only as a name on a form and as a clerk with a cough.

## Escalation sequence

1. The run. The voice arrives. The first kill, the first cost, the Ashwood Nine-Step gone from her legs.
2. The ledger is read in the yard and her name is in the furnace column. She becomes a document dispute instead of a person.
3. The Kiln. Her brother's record says he was *assessed*, not processed. The Clay Tablet comes out of a sealed room and the layer logs it.
4. Qiu offers her everything, sincerely, on condition that she stop asking. She asks anyway.
5. The furnace. Yuan spends her to save her life. She loses more than she had.
6. Three counties' copies of the same announcement are not the same sentence. The layer can be lied to.
7. She petitions to advance in the open, which no servant-born has done.
8. She ascends, and the layer names her.

## Midpoint reversal

Chapter 31. Qiu does not punish her for taking the Clay Tablet. He promotes her — to furnace crew, servant-rank inner disciple, which is a real and enormous advancement and a locked room. He was never trying to destroy her. He was trying to give her a reason to stop, and he does it kindly.

This turns the volume from "a girl is being hunted by a man" into "a girl is being absorbed by a system", which is the larger threat and the one that survives past this volume.

## Volume climax

Chapters 53–56. Four days from Body-Forging stage 4 to a public breakthrough, with a cracked aperture, at the Winter Assembly, in front of the whole hierarchy.

She reaches Condensing stage 1. The layer announces it to every person inside the bound at once, and for the first time in recorded history the announcement contains the words *servant-born*, and Qiu's whole arrangement is exposed in one sentence.

**The irreversible decision belongs to Yuan.** He has the authority to suppress the announcement — he is inside the machine, and a suppressed breakthrough reads as nothing having happened, which would leave her unremarked and harvestable inside the month. He lets it go out. He makes her visible, in front of everybody, in order to keep her alive, and he does not ask.

She is alive. She is also a target for the rest of the book. That is the trade and he made it for her.

## Concrete resolution

- Qiu Zhulang is stripped of the furnace at the Assembly and kept as a pensioned elder. He survives, diminished, and entirely sincere, and is a recurring figure for the rest of the series.
- The Clay Tablet is **not** surrendered. Ning Yan gives the Elder Hall the road to the Broken Kiln and keeps the plate itself, declared in a sworn entry to be nowhere in her possession, which is the first thing she has ever done that is not strictly true. The Elder's Hall never sees the plate again.
- Ning Yan's family account is written off in exchange for her silence about the layer. It is not the account she wanted settled.
- Ning Wu's record remains *assessed, reassigned, unreconciled.* Not answered.
- Du Hong was made an inner disciple on her recommendation at the winter session, and she is still furious with him about what the coat cost, and in the volume's last week she enters the whole of it on the record in her own hand — the advancement, the report order she never filed, and the fact that she has known since before the trial and said nothing until now. He is an inner disciple because she asked for it, and his name is in a file with hers in it for the rest of the book.
- Feng Lushi is left alive, coughing, with her story copied upward in his own fair hand.

## New question for the next volume

Yuan is far louder now. He can be heard by things that are not her. In the last chapter he goes quiet for four hours without warning, and when he comes back he says one sentence that changes the shape of the book: the layer did not announce her because she earned it. Something else is reading the announcement.

## Final chapter image

The first snow on the Nine Steps Terrace. The announcement is still going around in people's mouths and getting the words slightly wrong. Ning Yan stands on the sixth step with her sponsor's token in her hand, which she does not want, and asks the voice in her head what it is counting.

He does not answer. He is counting.

## Planned power and relationship progression

- **Her:** Body-Forging 3 → stage 9 (ch 3) → **stage 4** (ch 3, the tax) → stage 6 (ch 11) → stage 7 (ch 14) → stage 8 (ch 22) → stage 9 (ch 53) → **Condensing stage 1** (ch 56). Cracked aperture from ch 36 onward, permanent and visible. One lost technique: the Ashwood Nine-Step, recovered by muscle memory only, never by the page again.
- **His:** Noise → Voice. He becomes coherent, continuous and readable during the climax, and the first time the layer can log him by name.
- **Them:** suspicion with a job to do, ending in a working partnership with one unclosed argument, which is the thing he spends and the thing she notices.

## Pressure rotation across the volume

Survival and action (1–12), investigation and discovery (13–24), political pressure (25–36), consequence and recovery (37–42), character work and a deadline (43–46), action and public consequence (47–58). No two consecutive batches run the same pressure.

## Required continuity facts to preserve after the volume

1. Ning Yan is Body-Forging stage 9 and Condensing stage 1 with a cracked aperture.
2. The Ashwood Nine-Step is lost as a technique and held only as habit.
3. Ning Wu's record reads *assessed, reassigned, unreconciled*.
4. Qiu Zhulang is alive, pensioned, and not a villain in waiting — he is a man who was right about most things.
5. Feng Lushi is alive and has copied her name upward.
6. Du Hong is an inner disciple because she asked for it.
7. Yuan's Settlement has risen twice and fallen once.
8. The Clay Tablet is with Ning Yan and the Elder Hall has only been shown where the Kiln is.

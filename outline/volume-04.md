# Volume 4 — The Wall (chapters 179–238)

## Goal

> In this volume, Ning Yan must break the boundary, or neither of them ever learns what they are.

Fixed by `outline/series.md` §9. Not re-planned here.

## Central pressure

**Geography and distance.** Volume 3 was a man with a calendar and a woman who could reach every office
in her own county on foot. **Volume 4 is the first volume in the book where a person she has met is
only reachable by walking toward her**, and it is the first one in which the distance is measured in
days instead of minutes.

The opening pressure is not a shape in the sky. It is a **sheet of paper on a shelf**:

> **A Warden's office outside the bound now holds her name, her stages and her hours — in order —
> certified by her own county. The dismantling it has ordered can only be executed, and the Foundation
> relicensing she has petitioned can only be countersigned, past a boundary no county paper crosses.**

**The question is not what the Wall is. The question is what a paper in order does when the only
office that can answer it is on the other side of one.**

Both halves of that sentence are traps and both halves are true. Stay inside the bound and she is out
of the execution range of a dismantling order — and she is at Condensing nine under a ceiling of nine
with **nothing above it on the front of the document anywhere in the world.** Go past the boundary and
the countersignature is possible, and the dismantling is executable. She cannot have the counter and
not the execution. The whole volume is that scissor.

**The antagonist is the Wall and the sects that live on it** (`outline/series.md` §6 item 4), which is
**not an individual.** It is a line of work — paid by the day, crewed by people with families on it,
tended by contractors who make a living off it and are right about most things. **No new named villain.
No new final enemy. The Scribe is fixed in `outline/ending.md` and belongs to Volume 15.** The people
who live on the line win once, off page, at cost to themselves, and come back changed rather than
escalating, because `outline/series.md` §10 requires it and because it is the only ending these people
can have in a book like this one.

## The thing Volume 4 must not be

Volume 2 was confinement and paperwork. **Volume 3 was lawful hunting by an officer who never lies and
buys almost nothing, and its paper was paper already stamped twice and now being read back.** Volume 4's
pressure is **distance, labour, jurisdiction and the sound a thing makes on the other side of a line**,
and its paper is **a wage-book kept by people who are not a county and are not obliged to keep it.**
Nothing in this volume is a document being read back at her. Nothing in it is an officer with a
calendar.

## The three structural facts this volume inherits, and must not re-derive

1. **The relicensing's cost is what opens Volume 4, and it is legal mechanics before it is anything
   else.** A ceiling at nine bought a ceiling, not a body, and restored nothing. The only route to
   Foundation is a second relicensing, and **a second relicensing needs a schedule of hours worked at
   the ceiling asked for** — and to work *at* Foundation one she must already be at Foundation one,
   which is the trap, and which is why the schedule she needs has to be one **kept past this county's
   boundary by people this county does not employ, examine, or hold a second hand for.** **The whole
   volume is an argument about whose book an hour goes into.** Write what she does about it.
2. **The Hunter's referral is answered upward, and the district does not hold the instrument either.**
   The request on the shelf reads *received, numbered, referred upward, answer pending*, with **an
   empty space the width of a line where an answer was meant to go.** **Volume 4 opens on that empty
   space. Do not fill it in the first batch.** In Batch 0003 the space takes its first entry — a date
   and a word, **not an answer.** What reaches the space in Batch 0004 comes from the far side, in an
   office's hand and not a person's — **and it is not an answer to the request.** The request stays
   unanswered to the last page: a Wall writ is still not issued to a field agent, because a Wall writ
   is issued by the line's own people to somebody who lives on it and **she is not one of them and the
   line does not know her.** What goes in the space is **the countersignature on her petition**, which
   is the one thing the far side was in a position to give. The space is answered; the request is not.
   That is the volume's last joke and it is on the page, not in a card.
3. **The county is not the enemy and never was.** Sun He, the young clerk, Shen Xiu, the three sponsors,
   Feng — **institutions doing the only thing they are built to do, and that is worse.** The same is
   true of the people on the line. **Volume 4 must not turn the county, the registrar, the sponsors, or
   the line's own crews into villains.** Nobody in this volume is lying to her except one man who lies
   once, about himself, and is caught.

## The Wall — what it is, in this volume's own logic, and when it may be said

**`bible/world.md`: the Wall is not known as an object until Volume 4.** In Volume 3 it existed only as
*a writ this county does not hold*, once, in Bai Zhu's mouth at ch 174. **Nothing about it is described
before Volume 4's own logic has earned it, and the earning happens in Batch 0002, on the ground, when
she is looking at a wage-book.**

**The people who live on it do not call it a wall.** They call it **the line**, and the work **tending**,
and the job **the course**. What leaks out of a badly tended stretch is **drift**. What the drift is made
of, and where it comes from, and who put it there, is **not answered in this volume** — it is Vol 6–10,
and the honest partial answer at ch 236 is only this:

> **The line is not stone and it was never in the same place on both sides of itself. It is a line of
> work, kept up by hand, by people paid by the day. Where the work is done and done well and on time,
> the line holds. Where it is not, it leaks — and the residue of everything the bound is told runs
> along it and pools there and thickens.**

That is **ninety per cent true**, it is what the people on the line believe, it is why they are paid,
and it is why they are right. It is not the whole truth and this volume does not give the rest of it.
**Announcements are audible to everyone inside the bound and reach no further than the bound** is world
rule; when an announcement is heard *past* the line in this volume, that rule is not being broken — the
**drift is carrying it**, which is the surface answer the series owes the reader by Volume 5
(`outline/series.md` §3: *the layer is the world's own bookkeeping, and the mortal bound simply never
noticed the difference between being told and being charged*).

**The Warden’s marker post near the Broken Kiln (ch 18) is a local ward line and is NOT the Wall.** Do
not conflate them and do not send her back to it.

## The geography — the first real travel in the book

- **The course runs roughly east along the foot of the range and the working reach of the crews in this
  season is about nine days' hard road from Lowwater**, along the Verge road past the fourth bridge and
  then up out of the Verge onto the high ground. **Nine days out, one way.**
- **The season's road shut on day 117 and opens at about day 254**, the first week of the third month of
  the cold. The opening of the road is Volume 4's first deadline and it is on the page at ch 179.
- **She arrives at the course at about day 263. The countersignature that opens Foundation lands at about
  day 266. Foundation 1 is reached at about day 276.** Every later date in this outline is measured
  from that one and from the day table, never from a phrase.
- **Inside the bound, going to the line is legal and unremarkable**: a road, a contractor's cart, a
  wage paid by the day. **It is the paperwork, not the road, that stops at the county's boundary.**
- **Past the boundary is not wilderness.** It is settled, farmed, lit, and full of people who have never
  heard a stage announcement in their lives because nothing is announced out here. Volume 6 owns what
  the real realm is; **Volume 4 owns only that it is somebody's ordinary country and that the people
  there are not waiting for her.**

## Major factions and faces

**There is no villain in this volume and the outline does not create one.** These are the institutions
and people the volume runs on. Every one of them is doing the only thing it is built to do.

- **The line's crews and contractors — the antagonist (`outline/series.md` §6 item 4).** Men paid by
  the day to tend a line of work, most of them living on it, none of them paid to think about why it is
  there. **A foreman of about fifty with eleven men and a daughter on the line**, and a tender of about
  thirty who has been on the course nine years and is the first person out there to do something for
  her for nothing. **They are right about the drift and right about her and right about what happens
  to a line that is not kept, and nothing in this volume proves them wrong.**
- **The Warden's office past the boundary** — the countersignature, the certification, and an office
  whose name is written on things and not said. **It carries the office and not a person, exactly as the
  office below the county does.**
- **The wage-book** — not a faction but the volume's engine. **A column for hours worked out there,
  kept by a contractor who is under no obligation to show it to her and does it anyway.**
- **The Bureau's county office at Lowwater** — Sun He, the young clerk, the shelf by the door, the
  monthly attendance, the bar on the petition. **Not the enemy. Do not make it one.**
- **The crew at Qinghe** — nine men who have read everything and are waiting. Gao holds the frame. Qin
  stands eight feet off in a queue. **They are the cost of every decision she makes and they are in this
  volume twice, not in every chapter.**
- **The girl of twenty-four** — eight hours a week between two funds, a crooked leg, and two months of
  uncopied lists. **In Volume 4 she is the reason a Wall roster ever reaches a Lowwater wash house,
  and that is worth more to the volume than it sounds and less than a plot.**
- **Hou Sanliang's counting room** — patient, unpaid, and the place a date was entered. **Open, and not
  visited.**
- **The girl who got through — Wei Ran, twenty, mortal-born, ascends improperly.** A working woman with
  a trade and an opinion who is the first person Ning Yan meets who went through without permission.
  **She has no business in Batch 0001 and every reason to be in Batch 0003 or 0004.**

## Power progression: Foundation 1 → 9

**Foundation is a different shape of body from Condensing, and the difference is the volume's business.**
Condensing made her *full* — ch 171 has her top to the soles of her feet with no gap anywhere in her for
the first time in her life. **Foundation builds something underneath that.** It is a base to put later
techniques on, which is why the series needs it and why nothing she has spent on technique can be
rebuilt on top of it. It is slower, it is colder, it takes materials she has to buy, and **it is the
first realm whose cost is named in advance in her own body: one index finger, permanently
(`outline/series.md` §4, `bible/power-system.md`).**

**Real stages. Real months. Real costs. No skipped breakthrough. No tribulation — no trial of any kind
is drawn in this shape of book.** The volume runs **day 220 to about day 470 — about eight and a half
months — which spans the rest of the winter, the thaw, and one full working season on the line.** The
stage intervals **lengthen as the volume goes**, because the base gets heavier to carry, and every one
of them takes something out of her that does not come back.

**Every stage inside the bound is announced unless she orders it suppressed** — that is canon and Volume
3 ran on it. **Nine bracketed layer lines in sixty chapters, one each, and no more.** **Foundation 9, at

### Yuan's track, and the economy that pays for it

`outline/series.md` §4 gives him **Ledger across Volumes 3–5**. Volume 3 made it an authority and refused
its use. **Volume 4 is where it is spent, more than once, and each time at a price named before the
word.**

- **Ledger is taken in Batch 0001, once, for one thing: a name. The price is said before the word:**
  **eight days out of the ten she has.** What comes back is **the hand that wrote a date into Hou Sanliang's
  counting room in Lowwater** — a working name, a Wall hand, a person with a family on the line.
  **The claim is not resolved and the date is not torn off; what she buys is a direction.** See *The
  year* below.
- **What is left is about two days, and it is rationed.** He can now speak **roughly four sentences a
  day** and she chooses every one of them, and she keeps the count on the back of something in her own
  hand, and **nobody in this volume knows she is doing it.** That is the batch's whole pressure and it
  is the pressure of the next two batches as well: **a woman making nine days of road and a season's
  work with a man who can say about four sentences in a day.** He is worse at it than she is, because
  he wants to spend them and she wants to save them.
- **Ledger is refused at least once in Volume 4, out loud, at a moment it would be easy** — use, do not
  explain, and never as a lesson.
- **Ledger is held once more in Batch 0004**, briefly, for the crossing, at a price stated before the
  word. **Ledger as a standing held stage is Volume 5's to keep or to spend.**

**The channel, and the only way it comes back.** About ten days at ch 178, eight of them spent in
Batch 0001, **about two days of rationed speech at four sentences a day**, against a thaw at about day
254. **There is one mechanism in this world that lowers Settlement, and it is not a document.** Merit
settles when something the layer has listed is killed, **or a listed trial is cleared**; he intercepts a
share; he spends it out of **her foundation**, and it does not come back.

**So the thing that saves his life in Volume 4 is not a murder. It is a job.** The blind run at the far
end of the working course is the worst stretch on it, it is on nobody's wage-book, it is paid worst, and
it has been listed by the layer as a trial because the layer lists the jobs that settle a debt. **She
clears it in Batch 0004. Merit settles. He intercepts it. He spends it. The channel comes back — and
the tax takes the right index finger out of her hand at the moment the base under her is one stage short
of finished.** Everything she loses comes back to nobody. That is the whole economics of the book and it
lands on a body in the volume about the boundary.

**Merit otherwise: none in Volume 4. No one is killed for it. Volume 3's kill is spent.**

## The escalation sequence

1. She spends eight of the last ten days on a name, at a price said before the word, and rations what
   is left to about four sentences a day — **and the thaw is eleven days off.**
2. The road opens. The line is nine days east. Somebody is hiring, by the day, and the wage-book keeps
   hours this county does not.
3. She is relicensed at Foundation the first stage on a schedule read out of a wage-book by a clerk who
   is not obliged to show it to her, and the countersignature comes from the far side.
4. She works the line. The drift is thickest where the work is worst, and **eight Foundation
   announcements go out inside the bound and not one of them is heard past the line** — and on the far
   side a keeper who has been copying drift readings onto one board for nine years writes down, for the
   first time in his working life, a line that is not a reading. **Nobody out there has a word for it.**
5. She is hired onto the stretch nobody is hired on, and clears it, and the layer logs a crossing that
   goes into a book the far side keeps.
6. The line is closed against her, lawfully, using the Hunter's own referral. He wins, once, and it costs
   him.
7. She breaks the boundary — with her voice first and her body second — and pays for it in a finger and
   in a base that is one stage short of finished.

## The midpoint (ch 205)

**She goes out under the name that is on every document she owns, and finds a book that keeps it.** Nine
days of road, a contractor's cart, a crew of eleven men paid by the day, a foreman who wants nothing from
her but her hours and has a daughter on the line — and a **wage-book with a column for hours worked out
there**, which is the column this county has told her in writing it does not hold.

At the counter on the first working morning she is asked for the one thing she has never been able to
produce: **a name that a county has stamped.** She gives him the name that is on the schedule and on the
licence and on the referral, because that is the name her hours go in under, and **from that hour her
work on the line is entered in a book that an office past the county's boundary can read, in order, with
dates on it** — which is precisely the thing Sun He wrote on her petition in a slower hand.

**The irreversible decision of the volume is hers and it happens in this batch:** she misses the monthly
attendance, deliberately, and it is entered against her separately from her hours and **cannot be paid
for in hours.** It is permanent, it is on her member's sheet, and it is the first thing she has ever
lost by choosing to move rather than by being stopped.

## The Hunter in Volume 4

He is still the man from Volume 3 and none of his rules change: **he never lies, threatens, touches
her, enters her rooms, or pays for force, and he has never asked her a question and says so in the same
breath so that she does not have to spend a morning on it.**

- He comes east. **He buys nothing.** He attends, certifies, writes everything down.
- **He wins, once, in Batch 0003, and it costs him.** The line's own people close the course against her
  using his referral, lawfully and correctly, because the referral says the thing in her head is
  unauthorised and is carried by a licensee, and because the line is the one place where the *execution*
  of his order is possible at all. **He does not want it executed.** He wants the answer pending, because
  a pending answer is a question somebody above him has to answer and he has never once believed the
  answer would be the right one. He says so. It is not a threat; it is a professional disagreeing with
  his own office in front of the woman it is being done to, which is worse.
- **He is on the far side at the end, and the second document in this volume that carries the office and
  not a person is his.**

## Concrete resolution (ch 233–238)

- **Foundation 9 reached (ch 237), announced at her own order, and the announcement is the first one
  ever to be heard past the line.** Eight Foundation stages were announced inside the bound and one was
  suppressed and the ninth went out into a country that has never heard one.
- **The boundary broken, twice over, and both times on the page.** First by a **logged crossing** made
  by the layer itself — **the layer logs a thing leaving a place, it has always done that, and the people
  on the far side have been copying drift readings onto one board for two hundred years and have never

## Final chapter image (ch 238)

**A country where nothing is announced, and the first thing she has to do in it is write her name in a
book.**

She stands on the far side of a line of work with a wage-book open on a trestle in front of her, and the
column on the near side of it is a column of hours, and it is hers, and it goes in the way a column
goes in. **It takes her about eleven minutes, because of the flat of a second finger, and it takes her
longer than that because the hand on the other side of it has four fingers' worth of grip left on it.**

Nobody on the line asks her a question. **Nobody on the line asks anybody a question**, because a line
is a place where people are paid by the day and questions are not on the wage-book, and she has spent
three volumes in a world where every office she has ever stood in front of wanted one thing from her
and this is the first place anybody has wanted her hours and nothing else.

**So she says the other thing. She says the price, out loud, in the ordinary voice, because that is what
is said on a line**, and the man at the trestle writes the price down in the price column because that
is what the price column is for, and does not look up.

And behind her, a board on a post, dated — **nine lines in it, eight of them with something on them and
one of them with nothing on it at all**, in a hand nobody on the line can read and every person on the
line can recognise as a hand from the other side. Nine lines and eight of them written. **She has seen a
column like that before and so has nobody else in this country.** And the column under the board belongs
to a woman who worked the blind run in a season, and it is not a name any of them had before she came
and it is not a name any of them are going to be able to forget.

Nine days' road behind her, and a floor, and nine men, and a tower with a light in it.

**Everything that has ever happened to her inside that boundary was announced. She is standing in a
place where nothing is said when it happens, and it is the first silence she has ever stood in that is
not the inside of her own head, and there are eleven men on this course who have never heard one at
all.**

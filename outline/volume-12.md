## Central pressure

**She has to become visible to go up, and visibility is the harvest.**

Volume 11 made her the person who knows. Volume 12 makes her the person who has to be *seen*, and an announcement is the
only way a person in this world has ever been seen by anything above it. Nine stages past the last working point she has had
nine announcements and every one of them went into the ground, because there was nobody inside the bound to be told.

**There is nobody above the line except the people who are holding her.** To be announced she has to be recognised, and
recognition is not a thing a person is given in this world — it is a thing a person is *put on a board for*. And being on a
board is what the levy takes from.

**So the road up and the harvest are the same road.** That is the whole change of air, and it is the first time in twelve
volumes that the thing standing between her and what she wants is the thing she wants. `bible/themes.md` gives Volume 12 its
temperature as **hunger, cost, almost-ascension**, and the outline takes all three: the volume's hunger is the want and not
the belly, because she has not been hungry since Volume 4; the cost is paid in a figure she computes herself; and the
ascent is *almost*, and the volume closes on a stair it does not finish.

## What the volume must not be

Volume 11 ran asking, and what answering opens. Volume 10 ran asking a list, and what asking closes. Volume 9 ran being kept.
Volume 8 ran seduction and being wanted. Volume 7 ran rooms bigger than she is. Volume 6 ran reading and being read.

**Volume 12 is none of those, and none of its batches is a recruiting, a seduction, a tending, a ration, a hiring, a crossing
of a wall, an audit, an examination, a hunt, a signature, a keeping that is a wage-book, a debt, a family paper, an ash, a
confinement, a second demand in a room, or a second asking about a list.**

**Its pressure is being seen, and what being seen costs.** Volume 12 is the first volume in twelve in which the thing she
wants and the thing that is owed to her are the same object, and the reader must feel that as a change of air and not as the
same book with a building on the end of it.

**The one thing Volume 12 must not be is a retelling of Volume 8.** The Order returns, with a budget attached, and the return
is a refusal and an arithmetic and not an offer. **She said no to a sheet read aloud in a room at the fourth hour of the third
of the month beginning 1,304 and a man of sixty-one wrote one line and the date in the ordinary hand, and no hand in that
building takes it out.** Nothing in Volume 12 re-offers her the same trade on the same terms, and nothing in Volume 12 takes
the refusal back.

### What he does that is the volume's real damage, and it is his oldest habit again

**He keeps the record — and this time she lets him.**

Feng wrote seven sentences and the seventh is that *a thing seen truly and written truly is the only instrument in the world

## The structural decisions this outline makes, and why

**Recorded here because a close will audit against them and because a planning phase is allowed to decide a structural
question by recording the decision and its reason, and is not allowed to settle one by fiat or to leave a card needing a fact
the outline does not have.**

### D1 — What "ascend" costs here, given that the ceiling is gone

**Decision.** The ceiling is gone and there is nothing to go through, so **the ascent is not vertical through a boundary. It is
being received.** Nine stages past the last working point she has had nine announcements and every one went into the ground,
because an announcement only goes where there is something to receive it and there has been nobody above the line to receive
one. **The road up is the stair above the copying floor at Lowwater's tower**, because that is the only *up* that has ever
existed on the page: every announcement in this county has gone up that stair on a clerk's arm for twenty-two years, and a
clerk with a cough wrote them all down and never once saw who took the bundle.

**What it takes, in three parts, in the volume's order and one part per stage:** a shape, because a thing has to look like
something before anything can recognise it · a receiver, because an announcement goes where somebody is · **and a name
somebody above has to look at**, because recognition is recognition *of a person*.

**What it takes from the two people who counted together, which is the fixed goal's actual hinge.** It takes her **one more
finger off the right hand**, at the eighth first, in daylight, on a board, because the line has to be written and she is the
only one who can write it and the arm was open. It takes him **the last thing he had that she had not agreed to** — which is
that he was never anybody's column. It takes the two of them **the arrangement they have had for eight years, in which he
speaks and she decides and nothing is written**, and replaces it with a document, agreed to in daylight, at ch 689.

**Why this and not another reading.** Because it comes out of Volume 11's engine and not out of a fresh piece of furniture:
the levy is made of boards and days, the boards go up a stair, the stair is in a tower she has gone up more than once, and
the room at the top of it has been standing empty with nobody in it, while a lamp in a cage on the fourth landing has been
burning since the man who lit it died. **Nothing has to be invented for this to work. Every part of the road is already on the
page.**

stated price is inherited whole and deferred, and the outline says so rather than inventing a substitute bill.**

### D3 — Where she goes and who is there

**Decision. Volume 12 ends at a threshold.** The last chapter opens the low door at the top of the stair, and behind it is a
room with a long table in it and a lamp in a cage and **a second stair**, and the chapter closes on the second stair and
**does not go up it.** Nothing beyond that door is described, counted, named, staffed, priced or explained. No face, no
institution, no title, no scale, no crowd, no rule.

**Why.** `outline/ending.md` §8 and `outline/series.md` §9 give Volume 13 its own pressure — *she must survive being small in
a world of enormous people* — and **being small is a thing the reader has to see her be, not be told about.** If Volume 12
shows her the room, Volume 13 has nothing to take from her. If Volume 12 ends on a step, Volume 13 opens on her walking into
a scale she has no instrument for, which is the whole of Volume 13's promise. **The prohibition on a sight of what is above
the ceiling is honoured by the strongest available reading: the volume climbs to the top of what exists on the page and stops
there.** `bible/themes.md` calls this volume *almost-ascension* and this decision is that phrase made structural.

### D4 — What happens to the designation now that she knows what it is for

**Decision. The designation is a name on a board, and the harvest is the board going up a stair.**

**The whole chain, once, here, and nowhere re-derived on the page:** the designation is the one act of authorship and it
prints a name · a name is on a board · boards go up on the first of a month · **the fraction comes off a board before the
board goes up** · the levy was made to find people like her · therefore **being put where a name can be read is the same
thing as being levied**, and therefore ascending — which is being recognised — is the harvest.

**And the answer Volume 11 handed on, given its answer here: a designation made to be harvested can be carried up instead,
but it cannot be carried by a person.** It has to be carried by a piece of paper. **She has two costs that make that the only
way: she cannot be counted where she stands, and she cannot keep what she finds by holding it.** So the designation does not
go up on her. **It goes up on a line in her own hand, on a board, with a date and a place on it, saying a true thing about
her, written once, because nobody writes one twice.**

**The true thing is a figure.** Nobody on any ground has ever been told their own total. **She has been off every board in the
world for eleven volumes and she is the only person alive who can work hers out, because she counts days, and that is the
volume's discovery and it is hers and nobody else's.**

### D4a — The eight firsts, and the figure at the eighth of them

**The volume's clock, stated once here and counted on the page and not carried down from a plan.** **Boards go up on the first
of a month. There are eight firsts inside Volume 12, and they are at days 2,084 · 2,114 · 2,144 · 2,174 · 2,204 · 2,234 · 2,264
· 2,294, which are the firsts of the eight months beginning on those days.** She is on no board at the first of them and on a
board at the seventh of them and writes on the board at the eighth.

**She goes on the board at ch 674, on the twenty-fourth of the month beginning 2,054, which is day 2,077.** (2,077 − 134)
= 1,943; 1,943 mod 30 = 23; +1 = **24**, and day 2,077 falls in the month beginning 2,054, because 2,054 + 23 = 2,077. **The eighth first is day 2,294. The span is
2,294 − 2,077 + 1 = 218 days, counted with the first day as the first, the way every count in this book is counted. One in
twenty of 218 is 10.9, which is ten days and twenty-one hours and thirty-six minutes.**

**That is the figure she writes on the board at ch 717, and it is the whole of the volume's payoff and its bill in one line of
nineteen words, and it is true, and her hand cannot write a false line.** The chapter that states it derives it on the page from
the two dates and the fraction, and it does not carry the number down from this table — **this table exists so a close can check
the chapter and not so a drafter can skip the arithmetic.**

**And the human sentence that goes with it, written once and not improved on:** *She is nineteen years old and the world has
taken ten days and twenty-one hours and thirty-six minutes out of her, and there is no instrument anybody has ever heard of that
puts it back.*

### D5 — The forty-one strokes

**Decision: Volume 12 does not place them, does not answer them, and does not re-open them.** They stay exactly where the
close left them: forty-one short strokes down the right-hand margin of the fourth sheet, in Feng's hand, with no heading and
no line under them, seen at the ninth hour of the thirteenth of the month beginning 2,024, worded by nobody. **She said out
loud at ch 660 that she is not going to find out what they are, and a volume may not un-say that.**

**What Volume 12 is allowed to do with them, and it is exactly one thing: carry them up the stair.** The fourth sheet goes up
in the inside of her coat against her ribs, with the book, because it is against her ribs and she is going up and she is not
leaving it. **No chapter looks at the margin again. No chapter counts them again. No mouth refers to them.**

**Why this is a decision and not a dodge.** The prompt is right that they are not planned anywhere in the series and that the
volume therefore must not invent a placement for them. **The forty-one strokes are the volume's largest unpaid debt and the
honest thing a planning phase can do is record that the volume carries them up a staircase without looking at them.**

### D6 — Where the Order's budget is and what it is

**Decision. The budget is a total, on a sheet, with a period on it, in a hand, and it is the only new fact the Order brings
back.** Not a villain's ledger, not a hidden chamber, not a person. A figure with a sum and a period and a set of lines under
it, of which one is hers from four volumes ago.

**Why.** `outline/volume-08.md` closed with *one building, one counter, one book with four hundred and seven names on it, one
rate, one lamp on every table, one rule, and no budget, which is Volume 12's.* **A budget is a figure with a total and a
period, and she does arithmetic on figures for a living.** The reveal is that the rate she was offered at nineteen is a line
under a total, and the difference between the line and the total is not on any sheet in the building, and she says the number
out loud and nobody in the room has ever had it before.

**And nothing else.** **Where the money comes from is not stated once in Volume 12.** The Order's answer to *why a bounded
world needs readers* is not given, the moral answer is unpaid, and no chapter in this volume explains the levy, the ceiling or
the walls to anybody. **Volume 12 knows what the levy is for. It does not know what the levy is paying for, and that
difference is the entire moral argument the ending owns and this volume may not touch.**

# Volume 16, Batch 0001 — Chapters 901–915 — *the answer*

**Authority: `outline/volume-16.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline wins.
`outline/ending.md` is the authority for everything this batch spends and is not re-decided here.**

## Batch goal

Get the answer she asked for at ch 900, and let him have it. Fifteen chapters in a room at the top of a tower
with a man who says yes, and the woman who let him.

## Starting condition

Day 2,961, the eighth of the month beginning 2,954, the morning after ch 900. **At the peak of Immortal Vessel, shut
at ch 894** — not a tenth stage, a finished vessel. Nineteen years old. Three fingers and a thumb on the right hand.
**Right arm open since day 2,294 and 667 days at this morning and not going to close.** Seven of eleven holds.
**1,402 days lighter at `D − 1,559` and not one of them coming back.** Small open place low behind her fourth rib,
the size of her thumbnail. Breath four counts, seven on a rise, nine standing still. Not hungry. Nib and ink against
her fourth rib; a book of eight leaves with four blank leaves still blank.

The making-place: a table with no lamp over it and no wear in it, a place at the far side with nothing worn at all,
the register with five mercies cut into it and read twice with no conditions in any of them. **A lamp standing on
the stone beside her with the oil nearly out of it and no figure of the oil anywhere on the page and none to be
invented.** Eight flights and a stair below her, a boy carrying oil he makes and cannot buy, a room that runs
without her, a bar against a wall dated nowhere, a bundle under a table corded twice and not knotted.

**And a man at the edge of a decision who did not answer a question she asked standing in the ordinary voice
yesterday, and whom she did not fill the silence for.**

## The pressure, with a date on it

**One answer, and it is owed and has been owed for nineteen years.** She asked it at ch 900 and kept the silence
for him and went to bed and woke up and he was still there. **The batch's whole clock is that he answers on the
first page and everything after that is what an answer costs.**

The dated thing underneath it is the **first of 2,984 at ch 910**, thirty days out: a bundle goes up nine hundred
and four steps whether or not anybody is standing under it, and she is eight flights and a stair above it, and she
has not been in that room in one hundred and fifty-nine days.

## Batch midpoint (ch 908) — she does not stop him

He has shown her the whole of it and told her he is showing her on purpose, because he can no longer keep her out
of his half and because he wants her to be able to stop him. She has the standing to stop him. She has refused twice
in the dark with her hand on a sheet of paper.

**She does not stop him, and the chapter is not about her failing to.** It is about a woman who has spent nine years
being the one who decides and has finally found the one decision in the book that is not hers to make, and who
makes it anyway, out loud, in the ordinary voice, standing, in front of the thing that made him — and it is the
worst thing she has ever done and she knows it while she is doing it.

**No card may soften this, argue her into it, or have her discover she was right.** She was not right or wrong.
She did it and she goes on doing it for four minutes.

## Batch climax (ch 912) — the four minutes

**One chapter is four minutes long and the reader is inside every second of them.** He says yes, in front of her,
in the channel where the thing that made him reads. **A yes said in that channel cannot be un-said** — the fourth
term, the mirror of the page she sent down nine hundred and four steps at ch 838, and the first of the three yeses
she actually hears.

In those four minutes he is a person with everything he said he wanted: addressed, remembered, given a name that is
not Sheng, given work that matters. **She watches him be happy and says nothing at all.** The figures she has said
every morning since day 2,864 are not said in that chapter because there is no time for them, and that absence is
the only thing in fifteen chapters that she has not chosen.

## Irreversible step (ch 912) — he accepts

**One chapter, one yes, in the channel, in front of her.** He cannot unsay it, she cannot unhear it, and `outline/
ending.md` §2 puts the discovery of the tax four days later and the refusal eighteen days after that, and none of
it is a way back.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman who asked and was refused an answer to a woman who has one and cannot give it back. **She
has never in nineteen years let anything happen to him that she did not agree to, and this batch is the first time
she has watched one happen and done nothing.** Yuan goes from a man at the edge of a decision to a man who has
crossed it, in front of her, in four minutes, and **he is not relieved and he is not sorry and the reader is not
given either.**

**Lu An is on the page once, at ch 907 or ch 908, on the stair, with oil on his hands, and he is not told anything
and is not asked anything and she does not go down to him.**

## Required power changes

**None. Immortal Vessel 9, shut, throughout.** No tenth stage, no Last Ascension, no peak re-taken, no cuts, no
number, no tribulation. **Two bracketed layer lines are permitted in this batch, at ch 904 and ch 910**, and both
are announcements in the plain report grammar: one the report of the offer standing open, one the bundle on the
first of the month. **No bracket may be about her, and no bracket may explain anything.**

## Required world reveals

1. **That he has wanted this since he was nineteen, and that he says it in one line with no figure on it.**
2. **The three terms of the offer that are new to the page, read out loud by her, standing, before they are a
   line anywhere:** she ascends properly and is made an Ascendant with a Ward of her own · he is archived,
   addressed, remembered, given a name that is not Sheng, and given work that matters · the walls and the layer
   and the mercy of a bounded world all stay.
3. **That he is showing her on purpose, and why, and that it is because he cannot help it and not because he is
   being kind.**
4. **That she does not stop him.** Said out loud, in the ordinary voice, standing, and the saying is the chapter.
5. **That the four minutes exist and are four minutes and the reader spends them.**

## Open questions after the batch

Why he is answering now and not in the four hundred years before. Whether the answer changes in the four minutes
or was already there. What *work that matters* means, since he has not defined it and she has not asked. And the
question this batch does not ask and does not permit a card to ask: what keeps running.

## What Batch 0002 must not inherit as clean prose

the man in them, not on her.** That the offer is a trap or a trick or a lie — it is in good faith and every part of
it is real and nothing softens that. That *work that matters* has been defined. **It has not, and defining it is
Batch 0002's whole work and it is his discovery, not hers.** That the tax was mentioned. It has not been, and no
card in ch 901–915 may put the word tax, the recruitment, or the eleven in anybody's mouth. That the Scribe is
hostile, or is lying, or has a motive, or has been named as a speaker. That the bracket budget is spent — Batch
0001 spends two of the volume's eight, at ch 904 and ch 910, Batch 0002 spends three at ch 918, 923 and 930, and
**no card in ch 901–915 may spend a third.** That the boy has been told what is on him. He has not. That she has
forgiven anybody, thanked anybody, or said the year. `forgiv` and `thank` are both 0 across ch 901–915 and `thank`
is 0 across the whole volume.

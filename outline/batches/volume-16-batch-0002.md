# Volume 16, Batch 0002 — Chapters 916–930 — *the tax*

**Authority: `outline/volume-16.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline wins.
`outline/ending.md` §3 is the authority for the discovery and the refusal and is not re-decided here.**

## Batch goal

Make the best offer this book has ever made fail, and have the man who is being offered it be the one who finds
out why. Fifteen chapters in the same room with four minutes over and nothing changed.

## Starting condition

Day 3,002, the nineteenth of the month beginning 2,984, twelve days after he said yes. **At the peak of Immortal
Vessel, shut.** Nineteen. Three fingers and a thumb. **Right arm open 708 days** and not going to close. Seven of
eleven holds. **1,443
days lighter at `D − 1,559`.** Open place behind the fourth rib, the size of her thumbnail. Breath four counts.

The register read twice, five mercies, no conditions. **The offer is answered and nothing has happened.** He is in
her head, heard, audible, unwithdrawn, and turning something over the way she turns figures. A lamp on the stone
with the oil nearly out of it and no figure of the oil on the page.

**And she let it happen.**

## The pressure, with a date on it

**Nothing has happened for twelve days, and every day it does not happen is a day the answer stands.** A yes in
the channel cannot be un-said, so the silence after it is not a silence; it is a thing being built. **The first of
3,014 at ch 921** comes in the middle of it and the layer will have something to report, once, plainly.

## Batch midpoint (ch 923) — the tax

**His discovery, in his mouth, and it is the discovery the whole volume was built to put in the air.**

There are no conditions. She read the whole thing twice by her fingers in the dark at ch 897 and found none,
because there are none. **The tax is what the open is. It is what *work that matters* is.** The bargain needs
someone to keep the Foundation tax running, because the Foundation tax is how Chosen Ones are recruited, and every
Chosen One the layer ever made was a person being slowly made less solid by a voice in their head for exactly as
long as it took to be useful, and then filed. **Eleven before her.**

She already knows this. Volume 11 named it and it has never been re-explained in sixteen volumes. **What has never
been said is what it means for him to be handed the running of it** — that the arrangement on the table needs the
machine that was used on her, kept going, forever, by the one person in the world she trusts.

**He does not soften it and she does not soften it and neither of them argues.** It is the same figure in two
mouths: one thousand four hundred and two days of it at `D − 1,559`, in her body, and it does not come back.

## Batch climax (ch 930) — he refuses out loud, and gets to be an entry

He refuses, in front of the intelligence, on the tax, and he is correct, and **he does not get to be a person about
it: he gets to be an entry.** That is `outline/ending.md` §3 and it is the cruelty of the ending — the refusal he
has spent nine volumes earning is granted in the only form the architecture has left. He knew that when he chose
it.

**The last bracketed line in the whole manuscript is the layer's answer at ch 930**, one sentence, plain report
grammar. It does not argue, does not retaliate, does not acknowledge, and does not stop him.

## Irreversible step (ch 930) — the refusal and the dissolving

**To be heard by the thing that made him he must dissolve into the layer's substrate. There is no way back.** He
knows the exact price because he has known it since Volume 11. He does it anyway. The channel is hers alone from
this hour and she does not understand yet what that means.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman who let something happen to a woman who has watched the man she carried for nine years
give up the only thing he ever wanted, in front of her, correctly. **She does not thank him, does not forgive him,
does not tell him it was brave, and does not fill the silence after.** Yuan goes from a man who said yes to a man
who said no for the right reason and is not thanked for it and does not expect to be. **The reader's sympathy is
split across ch 923–930 and the book does not resolve the split.**

## Required power changes

**None. Immortal Vessel 9, shut, throughout.** No stage, no number, no cuts, no announcement about her. **Three
bracketed layer lines are permitted in this batch, at ch 918, ch 923 and ch 930**, which is what `outline/volume-16.md`
D3 allocates here; ch 918 is the one its own day table below spends, and a batch that carries two and says three is
the defect this line exists to prevent. Ch 930's is the last in the manuscript. No frame scene anywhere in the
batch.

## Required world reveals

1. **What the open is.** Not a condition. The mechanism.
2. **That the tax is the recruitment, and that it was his design** — not new to the reader, new to him in this
   form, for the first time, with the running of it in the offer.
3. **That refusing it costs the only thing he owns**, which is being a thing that can be addressed at all.
4. **That the Scribe does not argue.** One bracketed line and nothing else, in the whole book, and it is at ch 930.

## Open questions after the batch

What he says next, given that he can no longer be a person who says anything. Whether a person who has dissolved
into a machine can still apologise. And the thing this batch does not answer and Volume 16 does not: what the
layer does next, which is keep the walls, correctly, for another forty-five days.

## What Batch 0003 must not inherit as clean prose

That the four minutes were a mistake or that she has forgiven him. That he is thanked. That the tax is new to the
reader — it is Volume 11's own and it is not re-explained, only applied. That the Scribe argued, or is a villain,
or is lying. That anyone died. That the layer stopped. **It goes on keeping the walls, correctly, for another
forty-five days, and Batch 0003 opens with a man in a substrate who has never apologised to anybody in a way that
counted.** That `forgiv` and `thank` are anything but 0. That the bracket budget has room: Batch 0001 spent two,
Batch 0002 spent three, **Batch 0003 spends one at ch 940 and two remain for the rest of the volume, at ch 955 in
Batch 0004 and ch 971 in Batch 0005, and ch 971 is the last bracketed line in the manuscript.**

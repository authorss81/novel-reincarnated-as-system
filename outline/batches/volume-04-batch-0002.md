## Batch goal

The season's road opens on **day 254** and the line is **nine days east**, and she goes, on purpose,
with about thirty coppers and a voice she has rationed down to almost nothing. Nine days of road puts
her at about **day 263**, in a country that is settled, farmed, lit and not waiting for her, on a course
where **a contractor, a crew of eleven men paid by the day, and a wage-book with a column for hours
worked out there** are the whole of the world's machinery. She is hired on the first working morning and
asked for the one thing she has never been able to produce — **a name that a county has stamped** — and
she gives him the name that is on her schedule, her licence and her referral, and **from that hour her
work goes into a book an office past this county's boundary can read, in order, with dates on it**, which
is precisely the thing Sun He wrote on her petition in a slower hand.

The second relicensing's three legs are taken in the **Volume 3 order and not inverted** — a contractor's
clearance at the counter on **day 264**, **three marks on a sheet in a room on the line** on **day 265**,
and a **countersignature in an office's hand and not a person's** on **day 266**, applied to a schedule
made by reading the wage-book aloud, by a clerk who is under no obligation to let her watch him do it.
Ten days of hours go in after the countersignature and **Foundation 1 is relicensed and announced at ch 207,
day 276** — **the first thing the announcement layer has said since ch 171**, and on a course where eleven
men and a cook-fire hear nothing at all.

And on **day 269, the sixteenth**, the county's member sheet comes round with the work, the way a roster
comes round on a line, and finds her name on it with the attendance column ruled and **MEMBER ABSENT**
in it, and the hours column beside it in good order, and the two of them separate. **She is offered the
one word that could go in the reason column, from anybody on the course, and does not write it, because the
reason column is the same column her hours go in.** That is the batch's irreversible decision and the
volume's: **the first thing she has ever lost by choosing to move rather than by being stopped**, entered
against her separately from her hours, **not payable in hours**, permanent, on her member's sheet.

**This batch buys a book and spends an attendance.**

**What this batch does NOT buy:** an answer to the Hunter's request. The space on the shelf stays empty —
it is read once, on the last morning she is inside this county, and then she is out of reach of it for
the rest of the run. The claim is not answered and the date is not torn off and **Hou Sanliang is never
met**. Finding Luo Ping answers nothing and **the finding is not a mystery and must not be built as
one**. The office above this county is not named. Wei Ran does not appear. Nothing on the page restores
the channel — **there is no kill and no listed trial in these sixteen chapters** — and nobody out here
has ever heard a stage announcement in their lives because nothing is announced out here, so the one at
ch 207 is heard by **no one at all, including the eleven men standing six hundred yards from her.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 205)

**She is offered a word and does not write it.**

Not a choice about reaching Lowwater — **she cannot be at that counter on the 269th and no drafter may
write her turning round.** She is at the course. On the morning of the sixteenth a carrier comes through
with the **county's monthly member sheet**, which travels with the work the way a roster travels on a
line, and it has her name on it and the attendance column ruled, and in that column the county's own two
words, **MEMBER ABSENT**, and beside it a second column that is not connected to the first, in which the
hours she has worked since the 264th stand in order with dates on them in a contractor's hand. **The
attendance is entered separately from her hours and no quantity of hours reaches it.** The sheet goes back
west with the next carrier. **The tender tells her — once, in the ordinary voice, having not been asked —
that any person standing on the course may write a word in the reason column of a member's absence, and
that it goes back with the sheet, and that the word does not take the attendance out.**

**And the reason column is the column her hours go in.** So a word that a county would read and that
would say she had a reason is a line that appears in the **only book in the world that can certify an
hour she worked out there**, in a book read by an office past this county's boundary. **She does not
write it.**

What she does instead is stand at a trestle and work out, in daylight, with a sheet in front of her, that
she went east on the morning of the 254th without once counting the sixteenth — not because she was stopped
and not because she had counted and weighed it, but because she did not count — and that there is no
version of the last nine days in which anybody took the counting out of her hands.

**No Ledger in this chapter and no bracket and no stage.** The whole of what she loses is written in four
words in another office's hand and it is not payable.

# Volume 1, Batch 0004 — Chapters 37–46

## Batch goal

Ning Yan survives what Yuan did to her, finds out that the layer can be lied to, and asks for the one thing nobody in her position has ever asked for.

## Starting condition

Ch 36. Body-Forging 4 with a cracked aperture. Nineteen days to the Winter Assembly. A reassignment page with a Bureau signature. Her body is weaker than it has ever been and the log is in her coat.

## Batch midpoint (ch 42)

Three counties' copies of the same announcement are not the same sentence. The layer can be edited from above.

## Batch climax (ch 46)

She petitions the Elder Hall to advance in the open — a right no servant-born has ever exercised — and the Hall is forced to rule on a thing it has never ruled on.

## Required character changes

She does not shut Yuan out. That is the point of the batch and it must be dramatised, not stated: she finds the cost and she does not leave, and that is worse for both of them.

## Required power changes

Body-Forging 4 → 9, in eleven days, at a cracked aperture, which is close to the edge of what she is allowed to survive.

## Required world reveals

The layer can be written back by a reader. The Bureau has readers. Announcements are edited before they are broadcast.

## Open questions after the batch

If the layer can be edited, then every announcement she has ever trusted is suspect — including the ones about her.

## Chapter 37 — Stage Four

Chapter: 37
POV and location: Ning Yan. A laundry room, then the furnace crew's quarters, three days.
Immediate goal: Get off the floor and stay there.
Resistance: Everything. She cannot feel her right side properly and the crew have started being careful around her.
Major turn: She does not shut Yuan out. She could, and she knows exactly how, and she decides not to, and the decision is made in about four lines of dialogue in an unattempted conversation.
Information revealed: The designation holds without her speaking to him and he has been afraid every hour since Chapter 3 that she would find that out.
Action or decision: She tells him she is keeping him because he is the only person who has ever lied to her in a way she could check.
Emotional change: The account opens and does not close. He is not forgiven. He is employed.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: This is the chapter where the partnership survives Chapter 36. It should be quiet.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: emotional consequence — the worst possible outcome, chosen deliberately.
Next-chapter pull: She asks him what he took, precisely, and he cannot answer precisely.

## Chapter 38 — The Scar

Chapter: 38
POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace floor, night shift, week two.
Immediate goal: Find out exactly what is missing.
Resistance: Yuan can only tell her what he saw take it, and he was not looking at himself at the time.
Major turn: He describes the withdrawal as he felt it — a thickness, a stack, a shelf — and she finishes his sentence for him and says: you did not take a thing, you took the order the thing was in. She understands his vocabulary better than he does and he is not comfortable with it.
Information revealed: The tax takes sequence, not substance. That is why it can be so severe and leave the person whole.
Action or decision: She asks him to stop describing things. She reads him better than he reads himself and decides to be more careful with that.
Emotional change: Intimacy arrives in the worst possible form and neither of them names it.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: **"You took the order, not the thing."** Yuan does not understand this about himself until Volume 11.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — she has found the shape of his flaw from inside his own mouth.
Next-chapter pull: She has to get to stage 9 in eleven days and she has never advanced with a crack in her aperture before.

## Chapter 39 — Eleven Days

Chapter: 39
POV and location: Ning Yan. The ash-flats below the terrace, then the training yard.
Immediate goal: Stage 9 in eleven days, on a cracked aperture, without a seal and without a sponsor's permission.
Resistance: Every physical limit, plus the sect's own opinion that it cannot be done, which is worse.
Major turn: The crack is a channel. Qi that should leak out of a healthy aperture goes sideways into the Nine-Step pattern instead, and the body she has been carrying since Chapter 4 starts to pay.
Information revealed: Damage can be a technique if you are willing to spend it. This is her first real discovery and it is the last thing she will ever learn from the mortal bound.
Action or decision: She tells nobody. It is hers and it came out of the worst night of her life and she is not going to be thanked for it.
Emotional change: Pride, which she has not felt since she was eleven, and which arrives attached to pain.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 4 → 7.
Continuity facts to preserve: The aperture crack is permanent and is eventually used in Volume 7 against the Hunter.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: earned hook — stage 7 at day six, with four days left, and the technique she needs is the one Yuan cannot describe.
Next-chapter pull: She goes back to Lowwater, because the only person who can hand her a stage-nine breakthrough pattern is the one who has been writing them down for twenty-two years.

## Chapter 40 — Feng Lushi Writes

Chapter: 40
POV and location: Ning Yan. Lowwater, the Lantern Register tower, week three.
Immediate goal: Get a Condensing stage-nine breakthrough pattern out of Feng Lushi before he dies of it.
Resistance: He will not give it to a sect outer disciple, and the Bureau has already asked him once to stop writing down what she says.
Major turn: He gives it to her. He has already copied her story upward four times without being asked, and he tells her he did it because he has never once had a story where somebody was believed, and he wanted one.
Information revealed: The fair hand has already been doing work nobody commissioned. He is the reason the reader learns what a record is worth in this world.
Action or decision: Ning Yan teaches him nothing and gives him nothing and asks him to keep going, and he says he intends to.
Emotional change: The second person to believe her, and this one is dying of it.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 7 → 8.
Continuity facts to preserve: He has copied her story upward four times. In Volume 11 this is what gets her through the Wall. In Volume 3 it is what makes him a traitor.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: unexpected ally, and the cost of it lands quietly.
Next-chapter pull: Lowwater's Watch has heard a servant-born went into a sealed place.

## Chapter 41 — Lowwater

Chapter: 41
POV and location: Ning Yan. Lowwater streets, the same week.
Immediate goal: Leave Lowwater without being arrested for the kiln road.
Resistance: Three Watch, one of whom used to carry water for her mother, and a captain who has been given a form to complete.
Major turn: She wins it with the log — not by threatening, by reading one page of it out in a market square — and the town hears it. That is the first time a crowd is on her side.
Information revealed: Lowwater's Watch does not protect the sect. It protects the quota paperwork, which is a different thing and easier to argue with.
Action or decision: She gives up the Watch captain instead of pushing him, because he has a family on the register, and she notes the family.
Emotional change: She has started using leverage and it tastes like Yuan.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 8 → 8.
Continuity facts to preserve: Lowwater's Watch captain appears again in Volume 2 and again in Volume 5.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — the small people are not the problem and the paperwork is.
Next-chapter pull: Three counties' copies of the same announcement.

## Chapter 42 — Three Counties

Chapter: 42
POV and location: Ning Yan. Lowwater, the Lantern order's copying floor, night.
Immediate goal: Find out why the Hanhui, Shouyi and Wanzhou copies of yesterday's announcement do not match Lowwater's.
Resistance: Lantern-order copies are made by memory by unlettered women who were not in the room.
Major turn: They are not making mistakes. They are reproducing faithfully, and the four sentences are genuinely different. One county's version says *servant*. Another says *discipline*. Another omits the person's name entirely.
Information revealed: **The layer can be edited before it is broadcast.** Somebody reads it and writes it back.
Action or decision: Ning Yan does not announce this. She takes the four copies and puts them in the log, and the log becomes a thing that could kill a person.
Emotional change: The floor drops out. Everything she has believed about the sky is an office with a policy.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: This is the volume's midpoint turn. It is the clue that opens Volume 2's document plot and it pays off in Volume 5 with the signature on the reassignment page.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: revelation — the layer is written by somebody.
Next-chapter pull: She asks the voice what he knows about who.

## Chapter 43 — What the Voice Knows

Chapter: 43
POV and location: Ning Yan. A cart on the Lowwater road, moving.
Immediate goal: Get an answer out of him about who edits the layer.
Resistance: He does not know. He is inside it. Being inside something is not the same as being told what it does.
Major turn: He is more frightened by this than she is. He has assumed for six years that he is a fault in the sky and now there is a room with people in it and he does not know which of them he is.
Information revealed: He cannot read the layer's own decisions. He is read by it. That is not a small thing and it is the last piece of information Yuan has that he would rather not have.
Action or decision: She does not comfort him. She tells him she needs him sharp, because there is going to be work, and it is the first time in the book she has needed him.
Emotional change: The account becomes a working relationship again, with an edge on it.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 8 → 9.
Continuity facts to preserve: He is read, not reading. He does not know who edits the layer until Volume 11.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — he is not the mechanism. He is a record in one.
Next-chapter pull: Eleven days are gone and the Assembly is in two.

## Chapter 44 — The Terms of the Hearing

Chapter: 44
POV and location: Ning Yan. The terrace, the Assembly Hall, the day before.
Immediate goal: Be ruled on, officially, in a room with witnesses, because a room with witnesses is the only kind of record she trusts.
Resistance: Nobody at Qinghe has ever had to rule on what she is about to ask. They improvise, and the improvisation is the danger.
Major turn: The Hall's clerk rules that there is no precedent, and then rules anyway, because a ruling is safer than no ruling — and rules that a servant-born at stage nine may petition for a public advancement trial. It is absurd, procedural, and completely correct.
Information revealed: The sect's own rules contain the door. Nobody has ever pulled it, because nobody has ever needed to.
Action or decision: Ning Yan petitions for the **right to ascend in the open**, in front of witnesses, on the Assembly floor.
Emotional change: She has spent her life being looked at. She is about to ask to be looked at on purpose.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The petition is the hinge of the whole volume. The clerk's name is recorded and the clerk is later quietly ruined by Qiu's faction for it.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — she has chosen the most dangerous available option.
Next-chapter pull: Qiu's faction has nine days to stop it.

## Chapter 45 — Nine Days

Chapter: 45
POV and location: Ning Yan. The terrace and the furnace floor, the last nine days.
Immediate goal: Reach stage 9 clean and uninjured, which is not possible.
Resistance: Heaviness and four running repairs. Stage 9 arrives at day nine and the skin of her aperture has not closed.
Major turn: Qiu's faction offers her the same deal again, worse terms, through a servant-rank clerk, in a corridor, with a document already filled in.
Emotional or practical consequence: It is worse than the first offer. She does not destroy it. She puts it in the log, with a date, which is the most threatening thing she could possibly do.
Action or decision: She keeps going. She trains through the last night and does not sleep.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 9, sealed. She is the first servant-born outer disciple in three hundred years to be sealed for Condensing.
Continuity facts to preserve: The second offer is logged with its date and it reappears in Volume 4 as evidence.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: cost — she has been offered her life twice by two different people and it keeps arriving in the same shape.
Next-chapter pull: The Assembly. Nine hundred people and a weather event.

## Chapter 46 — The Petition

Chapter: 46
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Assembly Hall, the morning of the hearing.
Immediate goal: Get the petition read into the record by someone who cannot refuse.
Resistance: The Elder's Hall would rather have nobody at all, which would leave her exactly where she was.
Major turn: Nine hundred people are in the hall for the winter accounts, and the clerk has to read the petition anyway, because refusing to read a document in a room full of witnesses is itself a record.
Information revealed: The sect's whole hierarchy hears her name read out loud for the second time in the volume. The first time was in a ledger.
Action or decision: She asks for the trial to be set publicly, in the hall, with the announcement going out to the bound.
Emotional change: She has stopped asking for anything for herself and started asking for a procedure, which is the only kind of power that survives the person holding it.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The clerk who reads it is recorded. The Assembly is in front of the whole sect.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — the trial is set for the Assembly floor with the announcement going out to the bound.
Next-chapter pull: Four days of preparation, and then the trial.

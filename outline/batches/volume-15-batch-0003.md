# Volume 15, Batch 0003 — Chapters 871–885 — *the ceiling*

**Authority: `outline/volume-15.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline
wins.**

## Batch goal

Work out, out loud, standing up, out of her own arithmetic and not out of a mouth, what the ninth is a
ceiling for, who has been paying for it, and what that does to fifteen years of being angry at the wrong
person.

## Starting condition

Day 2,871, the eighth of the month beginning 2,864. Above the ninth landing. **Yuan is Announcement** since
ch 866 and she can hear his half and neither of them has said anything about it in four days. One lamp. **The
moral answer has not been given, hinted at, or gestured at anywhere in fifteen volumes and it is given
here.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 878) — the Anchor

**The walls protect the accumulated will of every soul that ever died believing it could ascend. Unbounded
ascent drains it. The Scribe is not lying about this, and that is the point.** Stated by her, in the
ordinary voice, standing up, out of four figures she has been carrying since Chapter 3 — and it takes eleven
chapters and it is not a lecture and nobody in the world tells her anything.

## Batch climax (ch 885) — it is right, and she has to live inside it

She has spent seven chapters being angry and gets the whole of it wrong, and finds out that being wrong has
not made her right. **The finding: his share of what has been taken out of her foundation is a small part of
it, and the rest went into the ceiling.** She says the two figures against each other out loud, standing, in
the ordinary voice, and then says the other half, which is that he chose it, and knew it was designed, and
did not tell her in fifteen years.

**She has been angry at the wrong thief and it does not make him right. Neither half of that is softened.**

## Irreversible step (ch 885) — she says the count out loud

**She says the number out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, at the ninth hour, in the channel the Scribe
can hear** — the number of people who have died in the whole history of the thing she lives inside believing
they could ascend. **A number is in the record that cannot be taken out.** She does it exactly right: she
says it first, in her own voice, standing, before it is a line anywhere, which is the fourth term, kept. The
damage is entirely in the audience and not at all in the method, and that is the chapter.

**No card may state where the number comes from as a source with a mouth. She has derived it. She says it as
a figure she has derived, once, and does not do the subtraction on the page a second time.**

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman carrying an unspent grievance to a woman who has found out the grievance is
real and is aimed at the wrong place and who has said the true number out loud where the thing that keeps the
ceiling can read it. **She does not forgive him and this is not forgiveness's turn.** Yuan is on the other
half of a conversation she can hear, and he has not lied in this batch, and the not-lying is worse.

## Required power changes

**None in stage terms. Immortal Vessel 9 throughout. No card in this batch may take a stage or hint that the
peak is coming.** **One bracketed line is permitted in this batch, an announcement, plain report grammar, and
it must be the plainest sentence in the chapter.**

## Required world reveals

1. **What the tax is**, worked out from the figure at the head of her own column — ten days, twenty-one hours
   and thirty-six minutes, in her own hand since the first of the month beginning 2,294 — taken against
   `D − 1,559` and against the four months of her own cultivation that went in one night in Chapter 3.
2. **That it was designed**, which Volume 11 gave her and she has not once taken out and looked at.
3. **The Anchor**, and that the walls are the reason, and that the Scribe does not lie about it.
4. **That his share is the small part**, at the climax, and that being wrong has not made him right.
5. **The count**, once, at the irreversible step.

## Open questions after the batch

What is at the top of a stair that does not stop at nine. What it has been offering him. Whether a number in
the record can be argued with. And the question this batch does not ask, because she does not ask things:
what finishing the ninth would take.

## What Batch 0004 must not inherit as clean prose

That the Scribe is forgiven or condemned. **Neither. It is right and the book does not adjudicate.** That she
has forgiven Yuan. That his share of the tax has been set to nothing. That the count was told to a person —
**it was said into the channel, out loud, standing, and there was nobody in the room.** That the bargain has
been stated, or its terms, or its price. That the peak has been taken or mentioned. That the boy's name is
known. That the light has a figure for it. That the moral answer is finished — **it is finished at ch 885 and
Volume 16 may not re-argue it, only pay for it.**

## Correction recorded after the review of this card's drafting — 15 Oct 2026

**Nothing in the day table, the midpoint, the climax or the irreversible step moves.** Three notes for anyone
reading this card:

1. **`Chapter 3` means *the third chapter of her keeping* on the page, and it is never written as a chapter.**
   The drafting phase carried the phrase itself onto the page. So did `fifteen volumes`, `six volumes` and
   `nineteen volumes` used as spans of time. **All zero across `chapters/` now.** The in-world durations are
   nine years with him, nineteen years for her own life, four hundred years for the keeping, six years for a
   belief, ten years for the column under the head and for the bar. **A volume is a book.**
2. **The batch was rebuilt at review.** No finding above was lost and no card was remade. Three things the
   card did not plan are now canon and Batch 0004 inherits them: **the fall at ch 875** and the widening of the
   open place behind her fourth rib from a coin to a thumbnail, permanent and not to be re-measured more than
   once · **the checker at ch 876**, which verifies any figure she has already written and can discover
   nothing, and therefore cannot be put the four months or a ceiling · and **a thirteenth landing** at ch 875.
3. **The card's own shape is what the review found wrong.** Eleven chapters of out-loud arithmetic in one
   unbroken voice, a named feeling in a slot at the top of every chapter, and one closing sentence fifteen
   times. Batch 0004 does not do any of that.

# Volume 2, Batch 0009 — Chapters 104–118

## Batch goal

Grief and the grant. She buries the fourth stage to keep a market from pricing her on the day she
buried Du Hong, and loses that stage off the paper forever. She works the pending filing alone, with
no surety, with a claim that has gone quiet. A request from outside the bound strikes *kiln-born*
out of every copy inside it and the door closes. She climbs to the ceiling at her own order, tells
nine counties where she is before the ink is dry on her own signature, and stands the pending filing
up. The county examines paper, not her, and the paper is excellent. **She walks out licenced, capped
at Condensing five, grieving, and legal.**

## Chapter range

Chapters 104–118. Fifteen chapters. **This closes Volume 2.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 110)

The sealed slip. Ordinary Bureau stationery comes up from the county office at Lowwater carrying a
request from **outside the bound** that the term in the second position of the status column be struck
wherever it is copied. The request names the term **by its position and not by its characters**, which
is why the keeper of the tower can act on it without reading it. Feng copies the **shape** of the
characters, strikes his copy, and keeps the shape. The office attached has a name and Yuan cannot
read it. The slip does **not** wear the unrecognised Bureau hand from ch 36. Nothing answers what
eleven became.

## Batch climax (ch 112–116)

She goes up the mountain for the furnace schedule the countersignature needs, walks into a crew of
nine that knows she killed a man, has a sponsor sign the schedule in silence, stands the filing up in
the roofless Hall in spring rain, takes **Condensing five at her own order and lets it go** — the only
announcement in the batch — and goes down to Lowwater to stand it up in a county office. Registrar
**Sun He** countersigns the Licence to Practise Past Registration under Clause Four, **to Condensing
stage 5**, with the furnace schedule attached as collateral labour and **Feng Lushi witnessing**.

## Required character changes

She spends the paper for one stage on purpose and does not get it back. She loses the right to be
absent. She finds out what the word on her own county sheet was and cannot keep it. She comes home to
a crew that will not work her shift. She is granted the only thing she wanted for four months and
finds it is a piece of paper that does not examine her.

## Required power changes

**Two stages, one silent and one announced.**

- **Condensing 3 → 4 (ch 104).** Grief training on the ash-flats in thaw mud, ten days after the
  burial. **Suppressed at her order.** No bracketed line in that chapter at all — a suppressed stage
  reads as nothing having happened. Cost: the stage never reaches a county book, and the chain of the
  filing takes a hole in it at the fourth. He asks first and asks properly; the third term is honoured
  in the correct order. She stops eating for nine days and he cannot make her eat.
- **Condensing 4 → 5 (ch 114).** Three weeks under the filing, working the furnace floor she is capped
  from ever owning. **Announced at her order.** The one bracketed line in the batch, four-clause order,
  fresh wording, and the sentence carries the ceiling. **First time she is glad of the noise.** Cost:
  the announcement tells nine counties where she is before the ink is dry on her own signature.

**No tribulation. No trial. No second Merit source. No Ledger retake — ch 80 was the once.**

## The six conditions (fixed here, carried on the licence at ch 115)

1. **Attendance.** At every county hour of the filing, in person, in her own hand. No surety may
   stand for her again without relicensing. The county will not carry a name for her twice.
2. **Hours.** On the furnace schedule of Qinghe, certified by the Hall's clerk, returned to the county
   at the quarter.
3. **The ceiling.** Practice at or below Condensing five. Nothing above is licensed.
4. **The Foundation.** No petition without relicensing.
5. **The defect.** Entered, permanent, and the licence restores nothing to the aperture.
6. **The standing.** The claim of eight hundred jin is entered **on the licence** and travels with it,
   and is not affected by the licence.

Condition 6 is the horror of the volume: the claim does not go away when she is made legal. It gets
attached to the document she carries.

## The gap (ch 115)

The county sheet reads stages one, two, three — and then five. Sun He rules on it in the open and
writes it down: an absence is not a breach. The count is one, two, three, five, and the ceiling is
five either way. Stage four is gone from paper and does not come back.

## Open questions after the batch — all answered

- **Does suppression cost the paper?** Yes. One stage. Chosen.
- **Does the pending filing become a licence?** Yes. Capped, grieving, legal.
- **What remains?** The claim standing unclaimed into Volume 3, where the Hunter uses it as a map.
  *Kiln-born* in two heads. Settlement roughly three-quarters, about six weeks.

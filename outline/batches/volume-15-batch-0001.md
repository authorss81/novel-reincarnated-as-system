# Volume 15, Batch 0001 — Chapters 841–855 — *the light, going up*

**Authority: `outline/volume-15.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline
wins.**

## Batch goal

Get her out of the room. Find out where the sentences are made, and find out on the way that her room is one
of hundreds and the second place is not a curiosity.

## Starting condition

Day 2,781, the eighth of the month beginning 2,774, the morning after ch 840. **Immortal Vessel 9**, nineteen,
three fingers and a thumb, right arm open four hundred and eighty-seven days, seven of eleven holds, 1,222
days lighter at `D − 1,559`, 480 days in that room, four hundred and seventy-nine of them in the room she
will not come back to in this volume. The cage lamp out two hundred and forty-seven days. The strip of pale
wood back under the table. The bar unlifted four hundred and thirty-seven days. A refusal sent down nine
hundred and four steps forty days ago and nothing has come back up it. A boy asleep on the fourth landing.
A voice she is not speaking to.

## The pressure, with a date on it

**One lamp, and the first of a month at 2,804 that she is not going to be standing under.** The boy's three
lamps in rotation are the only oil in a building that has had none for nine years, on a mountain that has
none, and there is no store anywhere on it. She is going to take one up a stair that has never been lit and
has never been climbed.

**And the deadline is not a figure.** **No burn rate, no cups-per-day, no hour the lamp runs out and no
exhaustion date exists on the page and none may be invented** — the defect this run has been repairing since
ch 764, repaired three times. His count is his and hers is hers. Neither is converted into the other. What is
on the page and may be relied on: a lamp has to be carried up eight hundred and eighty steps by hand, and
when it is out it is out.

## Batch midpoint (ch 848) — the cells

The third landing. More rooms, and every one of them is her room. **At the far side of each, between the
twentieth pace and the end, a place of the length of a forearm and the breadth of a hand that nobody has
ever sat in.** The finding is one sentence: her room is not a room, it is one of hundreds, and the only
reason hers was different is that somebody was in it.

## Batch climax (ch 853) — the stair does not stop at nine

Nine landings above her room, and the stair keeps going. The whole of this book lives inside a world that
counts to nine and stops. Physical risk, on the page: one lamp, one arm open at the elbow, no rail above the
fourth flight of anything she has counted, and a landing she cannot see the end of.

## Irreversible step (ch 855) — she leaves the room

**She says the six terms out loud on a stair for the first time in nineteen years, standing, in the ordinary
voice, and goes up, and does not come back down in this volume.** She takes nothing but the lamp and her own
arithmetic. She leaves the three boards face up a foot in from the near end, the terms board a foot further
along, the strip of pale wood under the table, the bar against the wall on the left, the bundle under the far
end corded twice and not knotted, and the cage lamp out in its cage.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman keeping a record in one room to a woman going up out of it, having asked nobody
for anything, and having learned that the thing she thought was hers is a cell. Yuan stays inside her head,
in Court, readable and not addressed, and **speaks very little and is not spoken to**. The boy is asked for
his lamp and is not asked his name.

## Required power changes

**None in stage terms. Immortal Vessel 9 throughout. No bracketed line in ch 841–855 may be an
announcement about her** — the layer does not announce anything that happens in a stair in this volume until
ch 866. **One bracketed line is permitted in this batch and it is the bundle coming up the second stair on
the first of the month** — a plain report, one sentence, no explanation, nothing the reader has not already
seen happen.

## Required world reveals

1. That the announcements she has been reading since she was seven are made at a place, and that the place
   is in a building she is standing in.
2. **That she counts landings and not steps, and says why out loud at ch 843.**
3. That there is more building above her room.
4. That there are cells, and what is in them, and the kept place in each.
5. **That the room carries on without her.** The second stair brings forty boards up at 2,804 and a hand goes
   along the table and there is nobody there to hear it.

## Open questions after the batch

Where are the sentences made. What is above the ninth landing. Whether a building that has a kept place in
every room and nobody in any of them has ever had a person in one who refused, and what that refusal did.
And the thing this batch does not ask: whether what is above is a person.

## What Batch 0002 must not inherit as clean prose

That the light is nearly out. **It is not, and no card may say a figure for it.** That the stair has a step
count. It has none and never gets one. That the boy is bound or named. He is neither until ch 890. That
she has forgiven him, thanked him, or not spoken to him for the rest of the book. That the second place is
reopened. That the cells are explained. That the ninth stage is finished or that the peak is mentioned. That
the moral answer has been hinted at. That the Scribe has a name, a face, a body or a room.

# Volume 1, Batch 0003 — Chapters 25–36

## Batch goal

Ning Yan gets the evidence in front of the Elder Hall, is absorbed into the sect rather than expelled from it, and goes into the furnace — and Yuan spends her to get her out.

## Starting condition

Ch 24. She is Body-Forging 8, wearing the Clay Tablet against her skin, carrying a written log, and about to walk up the mountain knowing exactly what it costs.

## Batch midpoint (ch 31)

Qiu Zhulang does not destroy her. He promotes her — to furnace crew, servant-rank inner disciple — in front of the Elder Hall, and the promotion is real.

## Batch climax (ch 36)

She goes into the furnace to take the records book out, and Yuan spends her foundation to get her out. She comes out at stage 4 with an aperture that cracked all the way through.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan refuses an offer that is genuinely better than anything she has ever had. Qiu Zhulang is established as a man whose argument is the shape of the book.

## Required power changes

Body-Forging 8 → 9 → **4**. The first time the Foundation tax nearly kills her. The scar on her back gains a second line.

## Required world reveals

The Elder Hall moves off the record when it wants to. Advancement needs a seal. Yuan can act through her hand once and it costs more than anything else he has done.

## Open questions after the batch

Can she be what she was before Chapter 36? No. What does she do with a body that is weaker than it was and an aperture that will not heal?

## Chapter 25 — The Log

Chapter: 25
POV and location: Ning Yan. A disused laundry room below the servant quarters, at night.
Immediate goal: Start a written record, in a hand nobody in the sect can read.
Resistance: No paper, no time, and a furnaceman's daughter who has never been taught to write well.
Major turn: She uses the back pages of a laundry ledger and copies the Bureau hand deliberately badly, so that a clerk skimming it sees a washerwoman's attempts at a few words.
Information revealed: Clerks do not read what they expect to be rubbish. She has learned this from a man who lived four miles away.
Action or decision: She writes the first page and dates it. That is the whole chapter and it is the most important object in the series.
Emotional change: From hunted to author.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The log is written in a deliberately poor hand in stolen ledger paper. It is the seed of Volume 4 and of Feng Lushi's fair-hand discovery in the Wall.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — she has decided to be a record rather than an entry.
Next-chapter pull: She needs her body to be fit for the Assembly and it is stage 8.

## Chapter 26 — Rebuilding

Chapter: 26
POV and location: Ning Yan. The training yard and the ash-flats, eleven days.
Immediate goal: Back to stage 9 before the winter drill.
Resistance: The missing four months, a cracked aperture rim, and the fact that stage 9 is where ordinary people stop and she has no idea whether she is ordinary.
Major turn: She works out that Yuan can see the technique she needs before she can find it, and that asking him for it is the first time she has used him as a teacher instead of a door.
Information revealed: Yuan can read the shape of a technique off a living person and cannot describe it in words, only locate it.
Action or decision: She makes him stop doing it, and then asks for it anyway, and the asking is the whole relationship.
Emotional change: Grudging, specific trust, which is the only kind she has ever offered anybody.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 8 → 9.
Continuity facts to preserve: He cannot describe a technique. He can only find it. This limitation is used in Volume 7 and Volume 13.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: earned hook — she reaches stage 9 and the sect's winter drill is in three days.
Next-chapter pull: At stage 9 she is eligible to be sealed, and being sealed is what she wants and what she is not allowed.

## Chapter 27 — Shen Xiu's Price

Chapter: 27
POV and location: Ning Yan. The lower terrace, the night before the drill.
Immediate goal: Find out what the Team Elder wants, before the Elder Hall decides it for him.
Resistance: Shen Xiu is tired, honest about being tired, and in a position where anything he does is a decision about her.
Major turn: He tells her he is going to be given an inner place if he turns her in, and that he would like to be allowed to not be told why. He has already decided to do it.
Information revealed: The sect promotes people who report servant-born with a seal on them. It is a normal Tuesday.
Action or decision: She does not beg. She offers him a different arrangement — a copy of the log, in his hand, that he can hold — and he refuses it and says she is making him stupid.
Emotional change: She is disappointed in a decent man and has no time to be.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: Shen Xiu reports her in Chapter 34, in the middle of the Assembly scheduling, and he is never forgiven for it.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: cost — there is no version of this where a kind man is spared.
Next-chapter pull: Elder Qiu finds her before the drill.

## Chapter 28 — The Elder of the Furnace

Chapter: 28
POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace room, below the terrace, morning.
Immediate goal: Keep the Clay Tablet hidden and get through the conversation.
Resistance: Qiu is kind. He asks about her father. He does not threaten her.
Major turn: He tells her, in conversation, that her father was not the only one in that furnace and that there were four others and that he remembers all five. He is sixty-one and he is not lying.
Information revealed: The Elder of the Furnace keeps the names in his head, not on paper. He has never once been asked to produce them.
Action or decision: Ning Yan asks him for the five names. He gives them to her. All five. Without hesitation, and not as a favour.
Emotional change: The scene the book needs most in the middle, because it makes the villain a man.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The five names. One of them is not a servant. This is planted here and not explained until Volume 5.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: revelation — the person she came to destroy will hand her the list if she asks.
Next-chapter pull: She asks him about Ning Wu next.

## Chapter 29 — Ning Wu

Chapter: 29
POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace room, still.
Immediate goal: Ask about her brother.
Resistance: He answers truthfully. That was always going to be the problem.
Major turn: *He was reassigned.* Qiu does not know where. He says the reassignment was signed above his authority, in a hand he does not know, and that he was not told and did not ask, because he has not asked about anything above his authority in forty years.
Information revealed: The furnace is a department, not a system. It has a manager.
Action or decision: Ning Yan decides that she is going to find out what is above the furnace's authority, and it is the first decision in the book she makes entirely on purpose.
Emotional change: She came to get a monster and got a function.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: *He was reassigned, signed above his authority, in a hand she does not know.* Feng Lushi will recognise that hand in Volume 5.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — the hunt acquires a direction and it points upward.
Next-chapter pull: She says the next question out loud: what happens to the ones you don't reassign.

## Chapter 30 — The Offer

Chapter: 30
POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace room, still, later the same day.
Immediate goal: Understand the offer he is going to make.
Resistance: He makes it anyway, and it is a real offer: furnace crew, servant-rank inner disciple, a bed indoors, a stipend, her family account written off on the day she signs, and the condition that she stop asking about the reassignment.
Information revealed: Her family account is the sect's single most useful lever over servant-born, and Qiu has been the one applying it, for six years, on his own initiative.
Action or decision: She asks what happens to the ones he does not reassign. He tells her the truth again. He is a man who keeps answering honestly and it is the most frightening thing about him.
Emotional change: She wants it. She wants the bed more than she has wanted anything.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The offer is repeated in Volume 4 by a different man with a different lever, and she refuses it both times.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: emotional consequence — she is going to refuse something she needs.
Next-chapter pull: The refusal is going to cost her more than the acceptance would have.

## Chapter 31 — The Turn

Chapter: 31
POV and location: Ning Yan. The outer yard, three days later.
Immediate goal: Sign nothing and lose nothing.
Resistance: She is summoned to the Elder Hall for the ordinary business of a stage-nine outer disciple who has no sponsor.
Major turn: Qiu petitions the Hall to admit her to the furnace crew as a servant-rank inner disciple. It is approved in eleven minutes. It is a genuine promotion. It puts her inside the building.
Information revealed: The volume's midpoint reversal. He was never going to destroy her. He was going to give her a reason to stop.
Action or decision: She accepts, because refusing would be pure, and she is not in a position to be pure — and then she keeps the log.
Emotional change: Trapped inside a kindness. Much worse than being hunted.
Power or resource change: Rank up. Nothing in her body.
Continuity facts to preserve: Furnace crew, servant-rank inner disciple. Bed indoors. Stipend. Family account deferred, not settled.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — she wins the argument and loses the fight.
Next-chapter pull: She is inside the building. The records room is under it.

## Chapter 32 — Inside

Chapter: 32
POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace crew's quarters and the furnace floor, ten days.
Immediate goal: Learn the building's routines well enough to be useful and not noticed.
Resistance: She is extremely good at this. She was a laundry-hand's daughter for eight years and reading a room is the family trade.
Major turn: The furnace floor gives her the procedure in full, live, in front of her, and it is exactly what the wall in the kiln said, word for word, and worse in practice because here it is warm and the men doing it are on first-name terms with the elders.
Information revealed: Furnace-men are not monsters. They are employed, undertrained, and paid badly, and the thing is worse for being ordinary.
Action or decision: She makes two friends in the crew, both men who will matter in Volume 2.
Emotional change: Hard, clean hatred, which is better for her than the complicated kind.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: Two furnace crew members, unnamed here, both die in Volume 2.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — the horror is administrative, which is the book's whole argument.
Next-chapter pull: The records room is under the floor and the furnace crew go down there twice a month.

## Chapter 33 — Du Hong

Chapter: 33

Resistance: He is now an inner disciple by her recommendation and he is finding out what that costs, and he will not go back to being nobody for her.

Opening gate: n/a.

Next-chapter pull: Shen Xiu does the reporting instead, three days early.

## Chapter 34 — The Report

Chapter: 34
POV and location: Ning Yan. The records room, below the furnace.
Immediate goal: Get the records book out of the room, because that is the only copy of the reassignment page that exists in nine counties.
Resistance: Shen Xiu reports her three days early, for his own reasons, and she is in a sealed sub-basement when the door is barred.
Major turn: She cannot be got out by anybody. Yuan says the words and means them: the only way out of this room is through you, and it will cost everything you have, and I cannot tell you how much.
Information revealed: Yuan can act through her hand and body. It costs more than the Foundation tax and it will not be partial.
Action or decision: She says yes, and she makes him say the whole thing first — that the price is not the room, it is her body — and he tells her, and she says do it.
Emotional change: Consent given with the facts in front of her, which is the only kind of consent this book contains.
Power or resource change: Undetermined until ch 36. It is going to be enormous.
Continuity facts to preserve: This is the first time she consents to the tax having been told the price. She never does this again until Volume 16.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — the irreversible one. She cannot undo it and neither can he.
Next-chapter pull: The furnace floor, from the inside.

## Chapter 35 — The Furnace Floor

Chapter: 35
POV and location: Ning Yan. Inside the barred records room, being moved.
Immediate goal: Get out alive with the book.
Resistance: Qiu's people, not Qiu. He is not down here and does not know this is happening, and that fact matters for the rest of his life.
Major turn: He comes down anyway, on his own authority, and puts himself between the Elder Hall's men and the records room, and she has to decide whether to let him.

Emotional change: Complicated beyond the reader's comfort, which is the point.
Power or resource change: None yet.
Continuity facts to preserve: Qiu is not a coward and he is not a martyr. He is a department head who has decided to lose this argument.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — the man she came to destroy saves her from his own institution.
Next-chapter pull: The spend is about to happen and she does not know it yet.

## Chapter 36 — What It Cost

Chapter: 36
POV and location: Ning Yan. Out of the furnace, two days later, in a laundry room.
Immediate goal: Read the page.
Resistance: She cannot hold the brush. She cannot get her feet under her. The book is in her coat and she cannot get the coat open.
Major turn: Body-Forging stage 4. Not 8 — 4. Her aperture has cracked the whole way through and it will not heal. The four months from the Verge are nothing next to what this took. The missing knowledge is different and worse: it is not four months of practice, it is everything she learned between stage 4 and stage 8, and the Nine-Step goes with it.
Information revealed: The Foundation tax is not a percentage. It is a withdrawal, and it takes what is on top.
Action or decision: She reads the page anyway. It has a signature on it in a hand nobody at Qinghe recognises.
Emotional change: She does not shout. She asks him, quite calmly, what she used to be, and he tells her, and she says: that was mine.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 9 → **4**. Aperture cracked permanently. Second scar, from the left shoulder to the hip. Yuan's Settlement spikes to over half.
Continuity facts to preserve: **She is permanently weaker for the rest of the book and every volume says so.** The signature on the reassignment page is Bureau hand and Feng Lushi will recognise it.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: cost — the irreversible cost, paid in full, in public skin.
Next-chapter pull: The Assembly is in nineteen days and she is at stage 4 with a cracked aperture and cannot ascend without a seal, and there is no seal for her.

## Batch goal

The crossing, and what it costs.

Batch 0001 was ration and silence. Batch 0002 was a road, a counter and a wage-book, and it bought a book
and spent an attendance. Batch 0003 was the daily work of tending a line, and the people who live on it won,
lawfully, correctly, and at their cost. **This one is the crossing.** It is not another walk, not another audit,
not another document read back at her, and not Batch 0003's tending again.

Four Foundation stages go through her body in sixteen chapters, at the pinned chapters, on the pinned
days, each one paid for in a different thing and each cost named once in new words: **what the ear is told
apart from what the board is owed · three days no book can find · an hour worked that leaves the same width
of nothing on the far side · a sound heard where nothing is ever announced, paid for out of a hand.**

And she is hired onto the stretch nobody is hired on, and clears it, and the layer logs a crossing that
goes into a book the far side keeps. Merit settles on a finished job rather than on a dead man. He
intercepts it and spends it out of her foundation and it does not come back. The channel comes back out of
a job. The right index finger goes at the blind run, permanently.

And an announcement goes past the line. The course stays closed against her. She breaks the boundary with
her voice first and her body second. The last page puts her on the far side with a wage-book open on a
trestle in front of her, writing her name in a book, in a country where nothing is announced.

**This batch puts her across and leaves her there.**

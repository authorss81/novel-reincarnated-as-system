# Volume 8 Batch 0001 — chapter cards, ch 421–434

**`outline/volume-08.md` is the authority for Volume 8 and is not to be re-planned by the drafter.** These cards are
the batch. Fourteen chapters, day 1,091 to day 1,152, and nothing outside that range.

**Batch goal: AN ARRIVAL.** The first person who comes for the reader walks into a shed at a mill; the two lines and
the date are quoted back to him out loud by a stranger who has only got those three things; the whole of the offer is
stated inside eight chapters and every term of it is reasonable; she spends the only question she has on somebody
else's sheet; she says a date out loud to somebody who will use it; and **Nascent Soul 8 at ch 434 costs her the
ability to call a halt, eleven days after she agreed to something.**

**Batch irreversible step: ch 432.** She agrees to stand at a place eleven miles east on a date, somebody else enters
it, and she cannot un-agree.

**Batch physical-risk card: ch 430.** Eleven miles on foot on a body with one arm at half and twenty-one coppers, to a
gate at the end of the long road where a man with a chain turns people back — **and she is turned back once.**

**Batch bracket budget: ONE, at ch 434, in the far-side house form. Zero bracketed body lines anywhere else in these
fourteen chapters.** This is the first batch in eight volumes to carry a single bracket.

## Ch 421 — The Eighteenth, And Nothing Else

- **POV and location:** Ning Yan, close third. Flat ground four miles east of a mill whose wheel has not turned since
  before she came. The rail at the sixth hour, then the shed with boards on the floor.
- **Immediate goal:** get through a morning. There is no season, no board, no wages, and she has read the forty-nine
  lines on the rail and can take none of them.
- **Resistance:** the ground keeps asking her things. The boy from the stair asked about carts. A man of thirty asked
  about a dressed stone going into a cart. The woman who keeps the stove came up nine steps she has no business on.
  Four people on that floor ask her a question a month and write the answer down, and none of the four had ever asked
  her anything in seven months before that.
- **Major turn:** she goes back over the strip that went east on the end-of-month cart three months ago. She worked
  out the mechanism herself at ch 366: it comes back down on the ninth with the empty trays, and a strip that goes east
  does not come back on that cart, because that cart does not go east. **She cannot read the word at the head of it and
  has refused, forever, on purpose, to have it read.** She works out that whatever went east has now been sitting on a
  shelf for three months and has not been sent back, and that a thing which is not sent back is not a thing anybody is
  waiting on.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** she says the six words out loud once in the shed, into empty air, and they
  sound the same the second time. **She wants to know what the fine hand says about her and has decided, in a room with
  a lamp in it, that she would rather have the standing arrangement than the line.** Carry the refusal. Do not
  restate it, do not soften it, do not let anyone query it.
- **Required continuity facts:** NS 7, taken day 1,076. Four fingers and a thumb on the right hand; right arm open half
  the time; eight of eleven holds; short breath four, five inside a loaded reach. Twenty-one coppers in the fold.
  The relicensed licence says Foundation the first stage on the front of it and she has never opened it. The Clay Plate
  is worn against her ribs and **is not described**. The six costs stand and none is re-derived. The year of
  non-forgiveness is not mentioned.
- **Ending hook:** on the thirtieth of a month, at the seventh hour, a cart she does not know comes down the mill road
  and does not stop at the counting house, and the man on it is not getting out at a gate.

## Ch 422 — The Woman Who Came On Foot

- **POV and location:** the shed at the mill, second hour of the first of a month; then the mill road in daylight.
- **Immediate goal:** work out where that cart came from and what it is doing on a road it has no business on.
- **Resistance:** she cannot ask. The cart is going the other way and the man on it does not stop for a woman standing
  in the road with a bar over her shoulder, and the only office inside nine days of road whose name she knows is two
  miles behind her and does not send for anybody.
- **Major turn:** **the strip came back.** It is dated the eighteenth — the day of the fine hand — and there is nothing
  on it but the date, in a hand that comes about nine times a year, and it was not on the cart that goes down with the
  empty trays. **It came on a cart that does not go east.** So either the cart does now, or something came back
  instead of a strip going out, and she cannot tell which from a date.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** she does not go to the post. She does not go up the hill. She stands in a
  road with a bar over her shoulder and works it out four times and gets the same answer four times, and the answer is
  that somebody knows the date she was asked about and has written it down and sent it to her, and that no office on
  nine days of road has ever sent anybody anything in eleven hundred years of practice.
- **Required continuity facts:** the fine hand, the man who can read it, and her refusal are **spent at ch 417 and not
  given back**. Nothing in this chapter may reopen it. `Volume 6` is **not** a phrase that may appear on the page — see
  the repair block in the drafting prompt.
- **Ending hook:** a woman gets off the cart at the mill, eleven miles from where she was picked up, at the seventh hour,
  and walks to a shed, and asks the keeper of nothing for a woman of eighteen.

## Ch 423 — Nobody Has Ever Answered One

- **POV and location:** the shed, seventh hour, daylight, in the open doorway with the boards showing. **The batch's
  central scene.**
- **Immediate goal:** get rid of a stranger at her door without giving her anything.
- **Resistance:** the woman is **Xie Changyin, sixty-one**, and she does not ask for anything. She has walked eleven
  miles. She has no folder, no men, no seal, no cart, and no office behind her of any kind. She says, in the ordinary
  voice, that she came because a line went up on the nineteenth of a month and nobody on her side had a hand that
  answered it.
- **Major turn:** **she quotes him.** She has the two lines and the date and the ordinary voice — *a line has not been
  carried to a room, and a holder is required against it* — and the date, the nineteenth of a month five months back.
  That is exactly and only what `outline/volume-07.md` said would happen: *He will be asked about it by somebody he has
  never met, at some point, by somebody who has only got the two lines and the date and the ordinary voice.* **She has
  no third thing. She has no name. She does not know there is a man.** She is asking about a hand, not about him.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** Ning Yan's first act is not fear and it is not anger. **It is to work out,
  standing in a doorway, that the only way to answer that question correctly is to tell the truth, and that the truth is
  a thing she has never said out loud to anybody, and that saying it would put a voice in a stranger's book.** She says
  nothing. She does not lie either.
- **Required continuity facts:** **he is answerable as of ch 401 and nobody has asked him yet.** This is the ask. **His
  unentered state, his unnamed state and the date of the morning he died are all still gone and none may be given
  back.** Every sentence he speaks in this chapter is in the present tense.
- **Ending hook:** the woman says she did not come eleven miles to ask a question, and puts a folded sheet on the
  boards and does not open it.

## Ch 424 — The Four Hundred Times

- **POV and location:** the mill road and a cart stand at the turn, second and third hours. **The structural answer,
  first mouth of six.**
- **Immediate goal:** find out who sent the woman and where from, using the two men on that ground who have been
  furthest east.
- **Resistance:** **a carter, about thirty-four, who took a strip east three months ago and came back with nothing.** He
  does not want trouble and he is not being evasive; there is simply nothing to tell, because nothing happened to him
  on either leg of it.
- **Major turn — the structural answer, four or five short sentences, in his mouth, broken across beats, never a
  speech, never in the same words twice:** *Something at the east end of the road keeps strips and does not send
  anything back.* **A journey with nothing on it is not a journey.** He was paid for the going and not for the coming
  back, and there was no difference between the two journeys as far as anybody's book was concerned. He has been
  turned back at the end of the long road four hundred times and turning back is the ordinary end of the journey, and
  people pay for it.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** **she does not tell him anything and he does not ask her anything, and the
  eight coppers she is owed come up here and are not paid**, because the road east is a trade and she has no money and
  cannot be paid and cannot give.
- **Required continuity facts:** **The tenth part is not given and nothing proves the crews wrong.** The surface answer
  paid at ch 267 is not said, hinted at, gestured at or paraphrased in any mouth in this batch — `drift`, *charged is
  charged*, *told is told* and *sound comes east* are all **0**. **The Order is not allowed to explain why a bounded
  world needs readers.**
- **Ending hook:** the carter says the woman walked from the far side of the turn and did not come back on the cart,
  and that nobody who walks in from there has ever walked out again, and that this is not a thing anybody thinks about.

## Ch 425 — A Rate And A Room And A Roll

- **POV and location:** the shed, seventh hour, the folded sheet still on the boards, four days running.
- **Immediate goal:** find out what a woman from east of the turn wants, in terms that can be refused.
- **Resistance:** **she will not say it in the abstract and she will not be hurried.** She says it in the order it was
  built, which is the order it was offered to her at nineteen, and she is patient about it and she is not performing
  anything.
- **Major turn — the offer, stated in full and every term reasonable:** *There is a building eleven miles east with a
  counter in it and a lamp on every table and about a hundred people reading.* *There is a rate per page and a rate by
  the difficulty of the hand.* *There is a licence that is not Foundation the first stage.* *There is a room, and there
  is standing, and there is a name in a book that is not a name at the head of a strip.* **And she gives the argument,
  once, in her own mouth, and it is not wrong:** *You have been a reader for two years and you have done it out of a
  shed for nothing, and it has already taken a finger and half an arm out of you. Be one properly and I will put the
  list of what it costs in front of you in a column, and you can argue with a number instead of with a feeling.*
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** she asks for nothing and buys nothing, in this chapter or after. **She does
  not mention the fine hand, does not mention the strip against her ribs, and does not know that Ning Yan refused to
  have it read.** She offers to be asked one question and one only, at whatever time Ning Yan chooses.
- **Required continuity facts:** **the Order is named here, once, as an institution, and the naming is an event.** After
  this chapter the word is a thing on the page and not a reveal. **No department, no floor, no rank list, no committee,
  no house of the Order, no title held by anybody in it beyond the fact of being recruited.** **No budget — where the
  money comes from is not stated once in Volume 8.**
- **Ending hook:** Ning Yan asks what happens to a person who says no, and Xie Changyin tells her, in the ordinary
  voice, that nothing happens at all, and that this is the first honest thing anybody has said to her about a trade in
  two years.

## Ch 426 — The Question She Had Not Thought To Ask

- **POV and location:** the shed and the made road, the fourth hour, over five days.
- **Immediate goal:** work out whether this woman is dangerous, using the only instrument she has, which is asking
  people about other people.
- **Resistance:** **she cannot ask the Hunter yet and she will not ask him twice for the same thing, and Volume 8
  gives her two pages with him in the whole volume.** Everything else on nine days of road either does not know or is
  owed something by her.
- **Major turn:** she works out the order of the trade instead of asking, because she is good at that and it has never
  let her down. A reader is called in because a hand cannot be read. She has been called in for two years by four
  people who did not know what they were calling. **The four people who knew were four hundred and six names in a
  book she has never seen, and one of the books has her gift in it and the other four hundred and five do not, and
  somebody worked that out before she did.**
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** **she feels something she has not felt in two years and she does not have a
  word for it and it is not fear and it is not hope.** She is eighteen years old at a gate and the first time anybody
  has ever said they wanted what she could do, and she has four hours of it before it turns into an argument about
  what it costs.
- **Required continuity facts:** the Hunter's rules do not change — he never lies, threatens, touches her, enters her
  rooms, pays for force, or asks her a question. **He is on the page twice in Volume 8 and only twice, and she asks
  him both times.** Nothing about his paper, his order or her brother is raised.
- **Ending hook:** she says out loud, in a shed, to nobody, that she would like to know one thing about one person and
  not one thing about herself, and Xie Changyin says that is allowed, and that it will be done in a room with a lamp in
  it.

## Ch 427 — The Sheet At The End Of The Arm

- **POV and location:** **the lifting floor**, hundred feet long, nine men, a bench, a chain, eleven dressed stones.
  Four miles up the made road. **The batch midpoint.**
- **Immediate goal:** spend the one question she has on somebody who is not her.
- **Resistance:** she is the smallest thing in the room and the only person in it who can read a sheet, and the sheet is
  Geng's, and it now has **a second column on it with a heading a man of forty wrote over it nineteen years into the
  trade because she read the sheet out loud and stopped him in the middle of it.** It has been empty for nineteen years.
  It is not empty now.
- **Major turn:** **she does not ask about her own line, about the strip, or about the fine hand.** She asks for the
  lifting floor's own work to be read by somebody qualified, and a reader from the east end of the road — a young man
  with a good eye and no standing — goes over nine days of Geng's figures and **finds nothing wrong in any of them, and
  says so, out loud, in a room with nine men in it, in the ordinary voice.** **It is the first time in nineteen years
  that the lifting floor's work has been vouched for by anybody who was not standing on it.** Geng does not thank
  anybody. He writes the reader's name at the foot of the sheet in his own hand, because that is what a floor does with
  a name.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** **she spent the only question she had on a stranger and got a stranger's good
  name into a room of nine men who have all worked out where she stands, and she does not know what to do with her face
  and she leaves the room.** The dressed stone is still four feet off its frame and it is not going back and this is
  carried, not fixed, and nobody offers to fix it.
- **Required continuity facts:** **the two-column sheet at ch 420 is the volume's own instrument and it is used again
  here as furniture, not as a lesson, and the second column is not explained.** A floor that runs a second column is a
  floor that will be asked to run it again. **No office is named.** The reader of the east end of the road is not a
  member of anything and does not join anything and says so.
- **Ending hook:** Geng says, after she has got to the door, that the floor was a block short before she came and is
  two blocks short now, and that nobody has yet worked out how to write that down.

## Ch 429 — The Eight Coppers She Is Owed

- **POV and location:** a bank on the made road at the seventh hour, then the shed.
- **Immediate goal:** find out what eleven miles east costs and whether there is any way to pay it.
- **Resistance:** **Wei Ran, twenty, mortal-born, one team, a trade, a rate and an opinion.** She quotes a rate per
  person and a rate per load and does not look at her while she does it, because she has been doing this for a year and

- **Major turn:** the rate is more than she has. Ning Yan has twenty-one coppers and cannot be paid, and has eight
  coppers she is owed that her own hand will not take, **and Wei Ran takes her east for the eight and not for the

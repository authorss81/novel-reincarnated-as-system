## Batch goal

The quarry runs out and takes the last paid work in the county with it. She works the cold end she is
still capped from owning, alone, for the fifth week of five, and at the ninth hour **announces Condensing
nine at her own order in front of a crew that has stopped moving to the other end of the room** — handing
nine counties her exact position a fortnight before the referral goes up the stair. The winter return is
**read in order** at the county office at the second hour: one, two, three, five, six, seven, **nine**,
with the two absences ruled and written down the way Sun He writes things down. A patient man who has
never lied to her **certifies in a Warden's hand that the System is real, unauthorised and carried by a
licensee in good standing, and refers the dismantling upward for want of a Wall writ.** She petitions for
Foundation on the relicensed paper and is barred without a second relicensing, and the volume closes on a
licence nailed straight above a bunk by a woman with one arm.

## Chapter 164 — The Last Card

Chapter: 164. POV: Ning Yan. Location/time: the cut three miles east on the Verge road, the first and
second hours. Day 187.

Immediate goal: Get four more days of stone out of the cut, because there is no other paid work in this
county and about two hundred and thirty coppers in a tin will not take a winter and four miles of hill.
Resistance: **Bo calls five men where there were eleven**, the frost is in the face and the light is
going off the grade earlier every day, and he will not run the hoist after the light because the haul
road ices. He asks her one question in the whole run — not about the quarry.
Information revealed: the cut has four days of stone in it and then it closes until the thaw, and nobody
in the county knows when that is. **Kan has written forty-one cards and puts an hour in at the left and
the load at the right on one line, and he is the reason the hours exist on paper.**
Action or decision: She takes the four days. And she tells the quarry that the last week's cards go down
as one line with the county's irregular stamp on the front of them, the fourth time this year, because she
is not going to have a week of her hours disappear into a gap at the worst possible month.
Emotional change: the fear of the winter leaving her was abstract on day 184 and is a cut with four days
of stone in it this morning.
Power or resource change: nothing. Condensing 8, suppressed, unmentioned.
Ending type: loss begins.

Next-chapter pull: The last load has to come off before the light.

## Chapter 178 — Straight

Chapter: 178. Day 219. **THE FINAL CHAPTER IMAGE. SEVEN ELEMENTS, ALL OF THEM, THEN STOP.**
Immediate goal: Put the licence up straight, and find out how much is left.
Resistance: she has **two arms' worth of practice at nailing and one arm**, and the one thing about a nail
is that you cannot correct it afterwards. Ten sleepers in twelve bunks. The tower lamp burning down in
Lowwater, **carried this quarter by another hand**. A referral gone up the stair past every office she has
ever stood in front of, to an office **whose name he still cannot read**.
Action or decision: she nails it. Then she asks him how much is left, and he tells her the truth:
**about ten days.** She does the arithmetic against a calendar that has nothing in it but a barred
petition, and **she does not tell him to stop.** She does not exercise the standing either — the Ledger is
hers to order and his to hold only on her word, and she does not order it.
Emotional change: the year of not forgiving him continues and is not resolved, and is not going to be.
Power or resource change: none. **The channel figure is said here and nowhere else: about ten days.**
Ending type: the final image. **No epilogue, no summary, no softening line.**

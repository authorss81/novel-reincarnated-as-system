# Volume 11, Batch 0004 — Chapters 646–660

## Batch goal

Take the ninth stage past the ceiling, bury the clerk who believed her, learn the whole of it, and not leave.

## Starting condition

Day 1,997, the fourth of the month beginning 1,994. The tax is named as designed. She stayed. Asking does not
hold. Knowing does.

## Batch midpoint (ch 653)

Feng Lushi takes the Lung. There is no giving it back. The instrument is spent the moment it is in his hands.

## Batch climax (ch 654–658)

The writing (ch 654, in his fair hand) · the death (ch 655, ordinary: a cough, a hand, a light gone out) · the
reading alone (ch 656, cannot be un-read) · the learning whole (ch 657, fragment, harvest, designed tax, kept
name) · the staying (ch 658, out loud, once, cannot be un-stayed).

## Required character changes

Feng Lushi goes from the man who believed her to the man who wrote for her, dead. Ning Yan goes from knowing
the design to having read it true. Yuan goes from known-about to read. Ning Wu goes from walked-past to walked
back to. Nothing is forgiven.

## Required power changes

Severance 9 at ch 648 on day 1,997 (80 days after Severance 8). Cost named once: she cannot go back to not
knowing. The batch's one bracket line at ch 648 and nowhere else. No further stage in this realm.

## Required world reveals

The fifth origin mouth (ch 652–654, Feng's fair writing — the only mouth written on the page). The full origin
at ch 657, read not told. The road up standing open at ch 659–660.

## Open questions after the batch (Volume 12's engine)

What does the ascent take from two people who counted together? Can a designation made to be harvested be
carried up instead? What is the sky, now that nothing announces under it?

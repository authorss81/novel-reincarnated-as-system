# Volume 16, Batch 0005 — Chapters 961–975 — *the sentence*

**Authority: `outline/volume-16.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline wins.
`outline/ending.md` §4 is the authority for the sentence, for the fall that follows it, and for what the fall does

## Batch goal

Find nine words and say them standing, and start something that cannot be stopped. Fifteen chapters of one woman
with nothing in her head but a silence that has a shape.

## Starting condition

Day 3,125, the twenty-second of the month beginning 3,104, five days after the four hundredth. **At the peak of
Immortal Vessel, shut.** Nineteen. Three fingers and a thumb. **Right arm open 831 days** and not going to close.
Seven of eleven holds. **1,566 days lighter at `D − 1,559`.** Open place behind the fourth rib, thumbnail-sized.
Breath four counts, seven on a rise, nine standing still. **A lamp she cannot refill and a book of pages in her hand
with four blank leaves still blank.**

**He is in no chapter in this batch.** No bracket about him, no line of his, no figure of what is left of him, no
sentence anybody quotes. The silence is the absence. It was named once, at ch 945, and **this batch may not name it
again** — and the pressure of fifteen chapters with him gone in it is the batch's job, and it is done by what she
does and not by what she notices about what is not there.

**Four hundred people are in her arithmetic and she does not know what any of them cost her.** The layer is keeping
the walls, correctly, and has **one bracketed line left in it in the whole book**, at ch 971, and it is going to be a
bundle arriving on time.

## The pressure, with a date on it

**The last bundle in the world comes up nine hundred and four steps at ch 965, the first of 3,134**, four hundred
years after the first one, carried by a man who has been paid out of a forearm for all of it, **and nobody tells him
it is the last.** He is on the page once in this volume and this is that page.

After 3,134 there are no more bundles, and after the sentence there is no layer to bring one, and **the first of
3,164 at ch 976 is the first of a month with nothing on it in nine hundred years.** That is the tenth chapter of the
next batch and it is the first thing anybody in the world knows.

## Batch midpoint (ch 968) — she finds what the sentence has to be

**She finds it. Nobody gives it to her, and the Scribe never speaks a term.** She has been reading the incoming end
of the announcement layer since she was seven years old and the outgoing end has never once been in front of her.
**She knows the grammar. Nobody tells her what a refusal sounds like, because nothing in the world has refused the
layer and there is no example on the page to copy.**

**The content is fixed and is not this card's to draft:** it is that she was never chosen, she was **told**, and
there is a difference. **The outline's batch table calls it nine words, and that is the only number attached to it in
the plan.** The wording is found in the chapters and not in the card, and **if the sentence she reaches lands on some
other count, the count is hers and the outline's nine is corrected on the page at the Volume 16 close, not before.**

**No card may have her find it in a document, a register, a book, a wall, a mouth or a memory of somebody's words.**
She finds it out of her own arithmetic, standing up, in bad weather, with four hundred people in her head and no
instrument but arithmetic that is running out.

## Batch climax (ch 975) — she says it

**Standing, in the ordinary voice, first in her own voice, before it is a line anywhere.** The fourth term, the last
time it is used. **The layer writes it and then it cannot unsay itself, and the fall begins in the same hour.**

**She is not brave and it does not feel like winning, and no card may give her a triumph, a crowd, a cheer, a light,
a warmth, or the sense that the argument was won.** The argument was had at ch 899, it was had properly, and it was
right, and it did not settle anything. **She does not refute it here and she does not accept it here. She does
something else and it is colder.**

## Irreversible step (ch 975) — the sentence, and the fall that starts

**One sentence, nine words, spoken standing in the ordinary voice by the only person the layer still recognises, and
a layer that cannot survive being told it.** There is no way back from the hour it is said. **Everything after this
chapter is consequence and none of it is a choice and she makes no further choices for the rest of the book.**

**It is only possible because he dissolved.** It requires a Chosen One and a person, and the person is gone, and the
channel is hers alone for the first and only time. **That is the last thing he does and it is why the apology was
worth taking a long time over, and no card may say that to her.**

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman carrying four hundred people and a sentence she has not found to **a woman who has said
the sentence and is the only person in the world who can still be asked what happens next.** She has never had power
before and it is the coldest thing she has ever held. **She does not enjoy it, does not celebrate, does not get a
crowd, and is not thanked, and `thank` is 0 across the whole volume.**

Yuan is not in this batch. **The batch that ends with the channel hers alone must not put him back in it**, and the
discovery that made it possible is hers to have and his absence is not to be scored.

## Required power changes

**None. Immortal Vessel 9, shut, throughout.** No stage, no number, no cuts, no announcement about her, no frame
scene, no eleventh frame anywhere. **Exactly one bracketed layer line is permitted in this batch, at ch 971, and it
is the last bracketed line in the whole manuscript**: an ordinary report of a bundle arriving on time, one sentence,
plain report grammar, the plainest thing in the book. **No card may improve it, extend it, or give it a feeling.**

## Required world reveals

1. **What the sentence has to be, and that she found it and not that she was given it.**
2. **That the channel is hers alone, and why that is the reason a refusal can be heard at all.**
3. **That the last bundle in the world came up nine hundred and four steps, carried by a man who was paid out of a
   forearm for four hundred years, and that nobody told him.**
4. **That the layer writes the sentence and cannot unsay it.** It does not argue, does not retaliate, does not
   acknowledge. **Its last act in this book is to obey.**

## Open questions after the batch

Whether the fall kills anybody. It does not, and no card in any batch may hint that it does before the ninth day is
over. What comes down, and where the ceiling actually is. Whether anybody in the world understands what has happened.
And the thing nobody has asked in nineteen years and she has still not asked: what the announcements are made of, and
who is left to refuse them.

## What Batch 0006 must not inherit as clean prose

That the sentence was a victory, or that she was brave, or that anybody cheered. **Nobody cheered.** That the layer
argued, fought, retaliated, acknowledged or explained. **Its last act is to obey, and it obeyed in the last hour of
its existence.** That the fall is announced by anything. **No bracket after ch 971, no announcement from any source,
and no card in ch 976–988 may have anybody say what has happened before the chapters show it.** That anybody dies in
the nine days. **Nobody does — not in Batch 0006, not in an offstage report, not in a rumour.** That the sentence was
found in a book, a wall, a document or somebody's words. That anybody thanked anybody, or that she was thanked. That
the bearer was told it was the last bundle. **He was not, and nobody ever tells him.** That the ceiling comes off in
one hour. It takes nine days and the last of them is ch 984. That `thank` has appeared. **It is 0 across the whole
volume.** That the bracket budget has anything left: **six of the volume's eight are spent, this batch spends the
seventh at ch 971, one remains in the entire book, and no batch after this one may spend it.**

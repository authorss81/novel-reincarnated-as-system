# Volume 1, Batch 0001 — Chapters 1–12

## Batch goal

Ning Yan survives the winter Verge run, comes back with her brother's token, and learns — from her own body, not from a lecture — that something in her head is spending her.

## Starting condition

Ning Yan, Body-Forging stage 3, at the staging ground below Qinghe's Lower Gate with eleven other outer disciples. Winter quota due at dawn. Her brother is unaccounted for. She has never heard of Yuan.

## Batch midpoint (ch 7)

She hears an announcement about a harvest that was not on any quota, in a county she has never visited, naming a servant she has never heard of. The layer is bigger than the sect and the sect is not the top of it.

## Batch climax (ch 12)

The run's ledger is read aloud in the outer yard and Ning Yan's name is in the furnace column.

## Required character changes

Yuan goes from incoherent noise to a continuous voice. Ning Yan goes from alone to partnered, and from not knowing what he is to knowing exactly what it costs.

## Required power changes

Ning Yan: Body-Forging 3 → 9 (ch 3) → **4** (ch 3, the tax, on the same page as the peak). The drop is the cost, shown as an event. Yuan: Noise → Voice.

## Required world reveals

The layer announces to everyone inside the bound at once. Merit settles when a listed thing dies. A quest has a stated cost and cannot be lied about.

## Open questions after the batch

Why is her name in the furnace column? What did her brother take with him into the Kiln? What is counting itself in the voice's head?

## Chapter 1 — The Bell at Ninth Watch

Chapter: 1
POV and location: Ning Yan. Staging ground below the Lower Gate, dusk, three hours before the quota bell.
Immediate goal: Finish her quarter-token before the bell rings. If she does, the run counts her as whole and she is on the list as a person; if she does not, the Elder Hall may substitute her for one of the four who are expected not to come back.
Resistance: Twelve outer disciples, one Team Elder who has already decided four names, and a token four hands short.
Major turn: A voice arrives inside her head that is not a spirit and not a god. It knows her name, her realm, her stage, and the number of her breaths per minute, and it cannot tell her anything else about itself.
Information revealed: Readouts exist and nobody inside the bound can see one. The layer can speak into one person out of everyone in the world.
Action or decision: She does not run and does not pray. She tests the voice with a question only a thing that had been inside her head could answer, gets an answer, and then asks it what it wants instead of who it is.
Emotional change: Terror converts into something much harder to shake — a working arrangement made in nine minutes by a starving seventeen-year-old.
Power or resource change: Zero. She is stage 3 and she stays stage 3.
Continuity facts to preserve: Yuan died six years ago in a different sect's furnace. Her brother Ning Wu was taken at thirteen. Quota bell is at dawn. Her father Ning Bo is in the furnace column of the ledger.
Opening gate: Objective by word 200. Anomaly by word 450. Consequence by word 750 — she speaks the name Ning Wu aloud while testing the voice, and Team Elder Shen Xiu hears his own name-list react to a sound in the air.
Ending type: decision — she walks out to the run rather than to the infirmary.
Next-chapter pull: She goes into the Verge with a voice and no plan.

## Chapter 2 — Ashjaw

Chapter: 2
POV and location: Ning Yan. First night in the Verge, the old watchtower line.
Immediate goal: Make the quota number before the ash-fall starts, which is eleven days after a Cracking and nobody's idea how long until then.
Resistance: A listed beast called an Ashjaw at a bounty nobody can afford to be wrong about, and eleven disciples who would rather follow her than fight beside her.
Major turn: Yuan offers the first quest — clear the watchtower, take the tower's record plate, file the kill. He states the cost in plain language before she accepts: it will take a half-stage of her progress and he cannot take it back.
Information revealed: The layer honours a Chosen One's quests. Rewards land. Quests cannot be lied about.
Action or decision: She accepts, and she kills the Ashjaw herself, with the Nine-Step, and the thing that settles into the voice is warm.
Emotional change: The first time in her life something has ever paid her.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 3 → 3. Body-hardening from the fight. Merit settles into Yuan; his Settlement drops by a sliver.
Continuity facts to preserve: The Ashwood Nine-Step is the whole of her skill and she learned it off the back page of a laundry manual.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — she is stronger than she was a moment ago by a margin that should not exist.
Next-chapter pull: Something has to have been taken to pay for that.

## Chapter 3 — What It Took

Chapter: 3
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Verge, second night, a fight with two outer disciples who want her kill-plate.
Immediate goal: Keep the plate and keep her hands.
Resistance: Two senior disciples who can file a competing claim, and a death in the dark that neither of them will admit to.
Major turn: Yuan spends the Merit he has just accumulated to stop a killing stroke that would have opened her throat. She survives. The price comes out of her foundation instead of the sky.
Information revealed: The cost is real and it is hers. Four months of Body-Forging practice do not come back and cannot be relearned.
Action or decision: She wakes up at stage 4 with a scar from her sternum to her hip and cannot find the Nine-Step anywhere in her head. She asks him what it took. He does not answer.
Emotional change: From partnership to accounting. This is the chapter the whole book is built on and it must play as an event, not an explanation.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 9 → **4**. Yuan's Settlement rises hard. The mechanic and its cost are now demonstrated, before any exposition.
Continuity facts to preserve: The scar is real and stays for the rest of the series. The Ashwood Nine-Step is gone as a technique.
Opening gate: n/a. This chapter closes the opening gate's second requirement.
Ending type: cost — she says she will find out herself.
Next-chapter pull: She has to walk, and she cannot walk the way she used to.

## Chapter 4 — Nine Steps

Chapter: 4
POV and location: Ning Yan. Third day, the Verge floor, moving slowly.
Immediate goal: Cover ground with a body that no longer knows how she walks.
Resistance: Eleven stages of memory missing and a leg that will not pick up.
Major turn: The footwork comes back as habit before it comes back as technique — her body still knows it, her head does not. That is worse, because it means the layer can take things without taking the habit.
Information revealed: Practice is stored twice, in the head and in the body, and only one of them is written in.
Action or decision: She chooses to keep moving instead of resting, because the ones resting are the ones Shen Xiu counted.
Emotional change: She stops blaming him and starts watching him, which is a colder place to be.
Power or resource change: None in stage terms. She re-learns to move, badly, with help from habit.
Continuity facts to preserve: Habit survives the tax; technique does not.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: cost — she realises the four months are not coming back and neither is the certainty that anything ever will.
Next-chapter pull: She wants the voice's number, and it will not give it to her.

## Chapter 5 — The Second Watch

Chapter: 5
POV and location: Ning Yan. The watchtower, first half of the night.
Immediate goal: Hold the tower until dawn with a body at stage 4 and eleven disciples who can tell how weak she is.
Resistance: Two senior disciples who filed a competing claim on the Ashjaw plate, and a wind that turns the tower into a funnel.
Major turn: She wins the argument without winning the fight, by giving away the thing she actually wants — the claim — to a boy named Du Hong who has wanted an inner place since he was eleven.
Information revealed: She reads shoulders. A servant's skill, and it wins her fights.
Action or decision: She files the Ashjaw plate under Du Hong's name and keeps the record plate from the tower.
Emotional change: She has made a friend for the first time in her life and did not notice it happening.
Power or resource change: Du Hong's standing rises; hers does not.
Continuity facts to preserve: Du Hong is eighteen, ambitious, bad at lying, and becomes her first ally inside the sect.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — the boy she used is now something she has to answer to.
Next-chapter pull: She needs to know how many of the twelve are coming back.

## Chapter 6 — What the Layer Says

Chapter: 6
POV and location: Ning Yan. Watchtower, night, the whole bound listening.
Immediate goal: Get four hours of sleep she does not trust herself to take.
Resistance: Every person in the bound hearing the same sentence at the same moment, including people three counties away who have no reason to care about her.
Major turn: An announcement goes out — a breakthrough, somewhere else, announced to everybody. Ning Yan understands for the first time that the layer is not a sky. It is a room with people in it.
Information revealed: Announcements reach everyone inside the bound and no further. They are audible, not written.
Action or decision: She counts the seconds the sentence took to arrive and works out how fast sound is, which is an absurd thing to do at four in the morning and is exactly who she is.
Emotional change: The world gets larger and colder in the same breath.
Power or resource change: None. Knowledge only.
Continuity facts to preserve: Announcements are the bracketed lines in this book, at most one per chapter, and they never explain or advise.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — the layer speaks to everyone equally, which means it is not on her side.
Next-chapter pull: The next announcement is not a breakthrough.

## Chapter 7 — The Thirty-Third

Chapter: 7
POV and location: Ning Yan. Watchtower, dawn of the fourth day.
Immediate goal: Stay alive through whatever the rest of the run does to the people around her.
Resistance: An announcement about a servant in a county she has never visited, taken in a harvest that was not on any quota she has ever seen.
Major turn: Thirty-three this season. The quota said twenty-eight. Somebody is filling the number off the book and the layer announced it anyway.
Information revealed: The layer reports; it does not editorialize. Whatever is in the arithmetic gets said out loud.
Action or decision: She decides, out loud, that she is going to find out what the thirty-third was for.
Emotional change: The first cold anger in the book. It is not hot and it does not go away.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: Winter quota is twenty-eight at Qinghe this year. Twenty-eight was the number everyone in the yard believed.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: revelation — the number in the yard and the number in the air are different numbers.
Next-chapter pull: She needs a record, and the only records are in a kiln nobody is supposed to enter.

## Chapter 8 — The Tree Line

Chapter: 8
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Verge's inner tree line, fourth day.
Immediate goal: Get the record plate back to the boundary before the ash-fall.
Resistance: An Ashjaw sheath with nine of them in it, at stage 4, with a boy who has never fought one.
Major turn: Yuan offers the second quest and states the cost: this time it will be a stage, not a half, and there is a real chance of the aperture opening.
Information revealed: The layer can be bargained with, badly, and the price is always paid by her and never by him.
Action or decision: She takes it. She refuses to let Du Hong be inside the kill radius and it costs her the only advantage she had.
Emotional change: She is frightened of dying for somebody she met four days ago and cannot make herself stop.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 4 → 5 over the fight. Aperture survives, cracked at the rim. Yuan's Settlement falls by a sliver.
Continuity facts to preserve: Yuan cannot lie about a quest's cost and she now knows he is bound by that, which is the only leash either of them has.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: earned hook — she clears the quest and the layer announces a kill she did not file.
Next-chapter pull: The announcement puts a bounty on the sheath and the boy in it.

## Chapter 9 — Deadweight

Chapter: 9
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Verge floor, fifth day, carrying someone.
Immediate goal: Get the wounded inner disciple back to the boundary before the ash starts falling on them both.
Resistance: A body heavier than her, a distance that is four hours' walk at full strength, and two hours at stage 5.
Major turn: Shen Xiu, the Team Elder, turns up to do half of it and says nothing at all about it, which changes what he is.
Information revealed: The Elder has decided who dies, and has not been told anything new in the four days she has been gone.
Action or decision: She chooses to give up her own coat, which is the only warm thing she owns, and the next morning the boy's fever breaks.
Emotional change: The first unselfish act she has ever committed, and it is not noble, it is just arithmetic about a boy who would otherwise die.
Power or resource change: None in stage terms. Cold, exhaustion, and a reputation.
Continuity facts to preserve: Shen Xiu is not a villain. He is a tired man with four names and a quota.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: emotional consequence — she has spent something she did not have, for no strategic reason, and does not know what to do with that.
Next-chapter pull: The route back goes past Lowwater, and there is a lamp-tower in Lowwater.

## Chapter 10 — A Fair Hand

Chapter: 10
POV and location: Ning Yan. Lowwater, the Lantern Register tower, sixth evening.
Immediate goal: Get the record plate copied before she loses it in the dirt.
Resistance: A consumptive clerk who has written down every announcement for twenty-two years and is going to die of the job.
Major turn: Feng Lushi believes her. He does not argue with the voice. He asks what the voice said and writes it down, in his fair hand, on rice paper, and that is the first time anything anyone has written about her.
Information revealed: There are nineteen Listeners inside the bound and the job eats all of them.
Action or decision: She gives him the tower's record plate to copy and keeps the original, which is the first thing she ever plans to keep.
Emotional change: She has been believed. She does not know what to do with her face.
Power or resource change: None in stage terms. Evidence acquired.
Continuity facts to preserve: Feng Lushi is alive in this chapter and does not die until Volume 11. He betrays her in Volume 3 and she has to deal with the man who believed her.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: unexpected ally — she walks out with a name she never had to trade for.
Next-chapter pull: The copy has a date on it, and the date is after her brother's kiln record was closed.

## Chapter 11 — The Return

Chapter: 11
POV and location: Ning Yan. The road up to the Lower Gate, seventh day.
Immediate goal: Get eight bodies up the mountain and have them counted as whole.
Resistance: Two who will not make it, one who will not be carried, and Shen Xiu, who has to write four names in a column.
Major turn: He writes three, and leaves the fourth line open, and she catches him doing it.
Information revealed: He is deciding live, in front of her, and she is one of the ones he has not decided.
Action or decision: She carries the fourth man herself, into the yard, on her own feet, in front of the whole outer yard, so that the line cannot be written.
Emotional change: She uses the one thing she has — being visible — for the first time on purpose.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 5 → 6 through four days of load-carrying. Reputation, in the only currency she has.
Continuity facts to preserve: Shen Xiu's open line is never explained to her and he never admits it again.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — she has announced herself without meaning to and cannot take it back.
Next-chapter pull: The ledger is read in the yard tomorrow morning.

## Chapter 12 — The Reading of the Ledger

Chapter: 12
POV and location: Ning Yan. The outer yard, eighth morning.
Immediate goal: Stand in the yard, hear her own quarter-token settled, and be counted as a person who came back.
Resistance: The Ledger Reader, the Elder Hall, and a column she has spent her whole life trying not to be in.
Major turn: Her name is in the furnace column. Not as a person's entry — as a *credit*. Her name has been sitting in Qinghe's furnace books for six years as an unsettled account, and the reading of the ledger this morning is the moment it gets noticed publicly by the wrong people.
Information revealed: Death by harvest leaves an account. It follows the family for three generations. Her father's death has been running through the sect's books under her name because she is the nearest unspent thing.
Action or decision: She does not speak. She takes the token, and she goes to find out what a furnace column is.
Emotional change: She stops wanting a document that says her brother is dead and starts wanting one that says what actually happened to him.
Power or resource change: None in stage terms. Her account is now a public object.
Continuity facts to preserve: The furnace column. The three generations rule. Her father's death is why the furnace has an interest in her.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — she survived the run and the thing waiting for her at home is a ledger line.
Next-chapter pull: She goes to the kiln to find out what the furnace column is for.

# Volume 15, Batch 0002 — Chapters 856–870 — *the address*

**Authority: `outline/volume-15.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline
wins.**

## Batch goal

Put something in the room with them that is not her and not him, and let her hear both sides of the first
conversation either of them has ever had with a third thing.

## Starting condition

Day 2,826, the twenty-third of the month beginning 2,804. She is above the ninth landing of a building she
has been inside for four hundred and eighty-five days and has never been up. One lamp, the oil nearly gone
and no figure for it. Nine landings and a stair that does not stop. **Yuan is Court, third rung, readable by
the Scribe since ch 810, found, and not addressed.** He said yes once at ch 812 where she could not hear it
and she does not know.

## Batch midpoint (ch 863) — the address

**`outline/series.md` §7 milestone 8, spent here and only here: the Scribe addresses him directly and she
hears it happen.**

The mechanism, and no card may substitute another: a fragment of a layer is carried by that layer and cannot
step outside it, and the layer's only instrument in the world is the Chosen One, and there has been exactly
one at a time for fifteen years and it has been her. **So the address arrives in her, in the announcements'
own grammar, one sentence at a time, in the plain report form — and she hears him receive it.**

**She does not faint, does not fall, and does not need to be told what it is. She names it in the ordinary
voice, standing, before she argues with it, because that is what she does.**

## Batch climax (ch 870) — she wants it for him

The thing it has said to him is good. Not perfect, not merciful — **good, in the sense she has used the word
for six volumes, which means it costs the person who is not in the room.** And she wants it for him. **She
has never wanted anything for him. She has wanted him not to die and that is a different sentence.** That is
the climax and it is not a defeat and it is not a fall and it is the standing loss she makes up for having
lost standing twice in Volume 14.

## Irreversible step (ch 866) — he takes Announcement

**On purpose, knowing the price, without asking her, and it cannot be un-taken.** Cost, named once, in her
hearing, in the ordinary voice, at ch 866: **he can be anywhere but inside her, and she can hear his half.**
Every sentence he has said to her in fifteen volumes is now a thing the Scribe can hear. **From ch 866 there
is no room in her head that is his alone and there never will be one again.**

She finds out and cannot stop it and does not withdraw from him and does not forgive it, and **she says one
thing about it, once, and it is not the thing she would have said.** `bible/themes.md`'s flaw running at full
volume: *who is the decision for*.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman who has refused twice for both of them to a woman who has heard somebody else
make a decision for the two of them. Yuan goes from readable to **announced**, by choice, at a price he
priced correctly. The boy is not in this batch's changes and is not bound and is not named.

## Required power changes

**His only. Court to Announcement at ch 866.** No numbered stage of hers is taken and no card may take one.
**One bracketed line is permitted in this batch and it is an announcement, in the plain report grammar, one
sentence, never advice** — and **it is the first bracketed line in the volume that is about either of them,
and it must be the plainest thing in the chapter.**

## Required world reveals

1. That the Scribe speaks in the grammar of an announcement, and that she has been hearing that grammar since
   she was seven years old without knowing it had a source.
2. That it does not lie, does not explain, does not ask, and answers figures rather than questions.
3. That it cannot reach him except through her, and that this is not a courtesy.
4. **The bearer's name at ch 857** — the first name she has asked for in nineteen years, and he says it, and
   she does the arithmetic in her mouth instead of on a board.
5. That the room at the bottom of the tower is still being worked, at ch 869, on a first of a month, with
   nobody in it.
6. **That a first of a month came up that stair at 2,834 while she was under the building and she did not go
   down for it** — named the day before at ch 859, counted inside a figure at ch 860. **She knows the boards
   went up. She does not know what was on them and does not go and find out.**

## Open questions after the batch

What the thing that spoke to him said in full, and whether it is still saying it. **Why he decided it for the
two of them.** What she is supposed to do with a thing she can hear the other side of. And the question this
batch does not ask, because she is the one who works things out: what the ceiling is for.

## What Batch 0003 must not inherit as clean prose

That the Scribe is a villain or a liar or a charlatan. **It is correct and it is good and she wants it.**
That he has been forgiven or thanked. That she is angry at him about the tax — **she has not met the tax
yet** and Batch 0003 opens that. That the moral answer has been given. That the bargain has been stated.
That he is a good man for deciding. That she can hear his half without cost. That the boy's name is known.
That the finding day of the second place has been settled, or the gap in the wear resolved, or the fair hand
identified with the third hand.

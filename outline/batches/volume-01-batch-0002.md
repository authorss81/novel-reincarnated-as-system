# Volume 1, Batch 0002 — Chapters 13–24

## Batch goal

Ning Yan finds out what the furnace column is for and what actually happened to Ning Wu, and she gets something out of a place that is supposed to be sealed.

## Starting condition

Ch 12. She has just heard her own name in the furnace column of Qinghe's ledger. She is Body-Forging 6 with a cracked rim on an aperture she does not know she has. Yuan is at Voice stage and his Settlement has risen twice and fallen once.

## Batch midpoint (ch 18)

Feng Lushi's own record, kept outside the sect's books for twenty-two years, lists Ning Wu in a column nobody inside the bound has a word for.

## Batch climax (ch 24)

She takes the Clay Tablet out of a sealed room. The layer logs an object leaving a place it should not have been able to come out of, and Yuan loses a piece of his own memory to do it.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan moves from anger to investigation, which is the wrong direction for her and takes her two chapters to notice. Wen the Lamp-mender introduces the possibility that the layer is not the sky.

## Required power changes

Body-Forging 6 → 8. Yuan holds a quest for the first time that he cannot pay for, and cannot stop the seal being read anyway.

## Required world reveals

The Broken Kiln is sealed by the layer, not by masonry. Records distinguish *assessed* from *processed*. The layer can refuse a place to the voice that is inside it.

## Open questions after the batch

Who reassigned Ning Wu, to what, and does the reassignment have a word that means what she thinks it means?

## Chapter 13 — Public Ground

Chapter: 13
POV and location: Ning Yan. The outer yard and the terrace steps, the day after the reading.
Immediate goal: Work out whether the furnace column is a debt, a threat, or a record, before anyone comes to collect.
Resistance: Du Hong telling her to leave it alone, at length, with reasons that are all good.
Major turn: The furnace column is an *account*, and accounts are settled with people. Her name is not on it because she is a threat. It is on it because the sect has been quietly billing her family for six years.
Information revealed: An unsettled harvest account follows the family for three generations and can be settled in kind or in service.
Action or decision: She asks Du Hong one question — whether he thinks the sect would ever settle it honestly — and takes his answer seriously, which is new.
Emotional change: Anger becomes something colder and much more useful.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: Du Hong is trying to protect her from a specific number. He does not know the number.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — the account is a debt and she has been paying it in air.
Next-chapter pull: She wants to see the books, and the books are in a records room under the furnace.

## Chapter 14 — The Book of Accounts

Chapter: 14
POV and location: Ning Yan. The training yard, training with Du Hong and losing badly.
Immediate goal: Get back to stage 9 before the sect's winter drill in nine days.
Resistance: Her body is stage 6 and remembers stage 9, and the gap between those two things is exactly the gap Yuan bought her life with.
Major turn: She catches herself performing a technique she cannot remember learning and has to decide whether to tell him about it.
Information revealed: The body keeps what the head lost, and the head can be made to keep things too.
Action or decision: She asks him, plainly, whether he took something else while he was in there. He answers truthfully and incompletely, and she lets him, and both of them know she let him.
Emotional change: The first deliberate omission between them. It is worse than the theft.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 6 → 7.
Continuity facts to preserve: This is the first of the omissions. She starts a list, later, in a log. The log matters in Volume 2.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: emotional consequence — they both let a lie stand, on purpose, and neither of them can go back.
Next-chapter pull: She needs a sponsor to be allowed to train past stage 9 at all.

## Chapter 15 — The Map She Was Not Given

Chapter: 15
POV and location: Ning Yan. The outer disciples' scripture hall.
Immediate goal: Find the map to Condensing, which every inner disciple receives and no servant-born has ever been shown.
Resistance: The map is not locked. It is simply not in the room, because the room hands books to people whose names are on a list.
Major turn: Mei Jiu, the inner disciple who has been beating her in the yard for a year, tells her the Condensing stage-ninth breakthrough is not a technique problem. It is a document problem. It requires a sect seal, and a servant-born cannot be sealed.
Information revealed: Inside the bound, advancement past stage nine of a realm is a permit as much as a practice.
Action or decision: Ning Yan does not ask Mei Jiu for help. She asks her whether Mei Jiu got hers honestly, and Mei Jiu tells the truth about what it cost her, and it is not what Ning Yan expected.
Emotional change: Her rival turns out to be a person with her own account in someone else's column.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: Mei Jiu is not a bad person. She wants the same thing Ning Yan wants and got it earlier and it has gone badly for her.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — the obstacle is not strength, it is paperwork, and paperwork has an address.
Next-chapter pull: The address is Lowwater, and there is one set of records in the nine counties that is not the sect's.

## Chapter 16 — Wen the Lamp-mender

Chapter: 16
POV and location: Ning Yan. Lowwater road, the second week, travelling in daylight for once.
Immediate goal: Reach the Lantern Register tower before the evening reading and ask Feng Lushi a question she cannot ask anyone else.
Resistance: The Lantern order will not stop for a sect outer disciple, and Wen will not answer anything about the layer.
Major turn: Wen answers one question, and it is: no, it is not a sky. Somebody speaks it. There is a difference between weather and an office, and the layer is an office.
Information revealed: Somebody reads the layer and writes it back. The Bureau of Records is the part of that anybody inside the bound can see.
Action or decision: Ning Yan does not push further. She lets it go, and the letting go is the first time in the book she chooses not to know something.
Emotional change: She has been operating inside a comfortable idea and has to move the furniture.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The layer is an office. Nobody inside the bound knows this yet and the reader should not be far ahead of Ning Yan here.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — the thing she has been praying to is administered.
Next-chapter pull: Feng has her brother's name in his own hand.

## Chapter 17 — Fair Hand

Chapter: 17
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Lantern Register tower, an hour before the evening reading.
Immediate goal: Ask Feng Lushi one question about Ning Wu and get an answer she can hold.
Resistance: Feng has written down everything for twenty-two years and has never once been asked to look at it. It is four hundred volumes and he is dying and the index is in his head.
Major turn: Ning Wu's name is in Feng's register, and he is not in the furnace column. He is in a fourth column that has no heading in the copies Ning Yan has seen.
Information revealed: Feng's register has a column the sect's books do not have.
Action or decision: She asks him to copy it out, and he does, in one sitting, with a hand that is getting worse by the hour.
Emotional change: Hope, which she has not felt since she was thirteen, and which arrives in the worst possible handwriting.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The fourth column. Feng's handwriting degrades as the book goes on and the degradation is a plot fact.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — her brother is not where she has spent six years assuming he was.
Next-chapter pull: The kiln road, and the sealed door.

## Chapter 18 — The Kiln Road

Chapter: 18
POV and location: Ning Yan. The road from Lowwater to the Broken Kiln, the last four miles.
Immediate goal: Reach the kiln before dark, unescorted and unrecorded.
Resistance: Sect watch on the kiln road, a Warden's marker she cannot see, and a Lamp-mender's cart that will not go past a sealed place.
Major turn: Wen will not go to the kiln. Not unwilling — unable. Her cart stops at the marker and she says, "Not here," and does not say why.
Information revealed: The kiln is sealed by the layer's marker and the Lantern order physically cannot cross it.
Action or decision: Ning Yan walks the last four miles and arrives after dark with an hour of light left.
Emotional change: Small, useless, real terror.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The Warden's marker is a marker on a post. It is described three times across the series and it is the same post in Volume 4.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: danger — she is on the wrong side of a thing she has been told does not exist.
Next-chapter pull: The seal does not stop her body. It stops Yuan.

## Chapter 19 — Sealed Ground

Chapter: 19
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Broken Kiln, the yard, after dark.
Immediate goal: Get inside the assessment room.
Resistance: The building is ruin. The door is ruin. And the moment she crosses the threshold, Yuan goes completely silent for the first time in the book.
Major turn: The layer will not let him read inside the sealed ground. He is not blocked by a wall. He is not present at all, for the length of a breath, and comes back with a count that is wrong.
Information revealed: The seal is not masonry and not a door. It is an instruction, and he is inside the thing that issued it.
Action or decision: She goes in anyway, alone, with no voice at all, which is the most frightening thing that happens in this volume.
Emotional change: She finds out what it is like to be without him, for about forty minutes, and it is a taste.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The forty minutes inside the seal are the only time in the volume she is truly alone. Remember the taste.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: reversal — the one thing she had been relying on is gone exactly where she needs it.
Next-chapter pull: The room itself, and what is in it.

## Chapter 20 — The Assessment Room

Chapter: 20
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Broken Kiln, the assessment room.
Immediate goal: Find Ning Wu's record.
Resistance: Records on fired clay, arranged by year, and no names on the outside of anything.
Major turn: *Assessed, not processed.* The kiln does not do what she thought it did. It evaluates. Somebody, somewhere, at some point, looked at a thirteen-year-old servant and wrote down what he was worth, and then the record stopped.
Information revealed: The fourth column is *reassigned*. There is a category of person who leaves the kiln assessed and does not go into the furnace.
Action or decision: She does not steal anything yet. She copies the date into her own hand in the dark on her own knee, which takes an hour and nearly kills her hands.
Emotional change: Not relief. Worse than relief. Her brother was worth something to somebody.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: *Assessed, reassigned, unreconciled* is the exact wording in every record of Ning Wu from here to Volume 10.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: revelation — the answer is not an answer and it is still the first real news she has had in six years.
Next-chapter pull: The procedure on the wall tells her what "reassigned" means in practice.

## Chapter 21 — What They Take

Chapter: 21
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Broken Kiln, the procedure wall.
Immediate goal: Understand what is written on the wall.
Resistance: It is written in Bureau hand, and it is written for somebody who already agrees with it, which makes it very hard to read as anything other than housekeeping.
Major turn: She reads what the kiln is for. It is not a place where people are killed. It is a place where cultivators are *manufactured* — a stalled disciple is opened, the useful part is moved into a failing one, and both are filed under the same number.
Information revealed: The furnace is a supply chain, and everyone in it is a servant.
Action or decision: She decides she is going to take the tablet, and she says it out loud to the empty room, which is the first time she has talked to a building.
Emotional change: Disgust is a cold, useful thing to have.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The Clay Tablet is a single fired plate the size of a hand, with a surface like skin. Its texture is described once here and remembered in the final chapter.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — an irreversible one, made in a sealed room, to a building, with nobody listening.
Next-chapter pull: She has forty minutes before the seal's marker reports her weight.

## Chapter 22 — The Weight at the Marker

Chapter: 22
POV and location: Ning Yan. Out of the kiln and back down the four miles.
Immediate goal: Get clear of the marker before the night watch.
Resistance: The seal logs her. The moment she leaves with an object that was inside it, the layer makes a note, and the note goes somewhere she cannot see.
Major turn: Yuan comes back in her head and the first thing he says is that he cannot undo this and cannot tell her what it costs.
Information revealed: The layer logs removals. The Clay Tablet is the first thing in sixteen years that has come out of a sealed place.
Action or decision: She keeps walking. She does not ask him to pay for it and he does not offer.
Emotional change: The partnership has a ledger now, and she is holding one end of it.
Power or resource change: Yuan's Settlement rises again. Her foundation tax is untouched for this one, which she notices and he does not comment on.
Continuity facts to preserve: She does not ask him to pay. He never volunteers. This pattern repeats until Volume 5 and it is the worst thing about him.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: cost — they are both keeping books now.
Next-chapter pull: She has the tablet and no idea who she can show it to.

## Chapter 23 — Wen Again

Chapter: 23
POV and location: Ning Yan. Lowwater, the lamp-mender's yard, two days later.
Immediate goal: Have the tablet read by somebody who has handled one before.
Resistance: Wen will not touch it. Not fear. Disgust, professional, and specific.
Major turn: Wen tells her what a tablet is made of, and why, and the answer is worse than the kiln and the answer is: the tablet is made of the people it describes.
Information revealed: Core-stone is not stone. It is fired qi, and the kiln's output is partly made out of the assessed.
Action or decision: Ning Yan does not put the tablet down. She puts it in her coat and wears it against her skin and deals with what that feels like later.
Emotional change: A thing she had thought was evidence turns out to be a piece of a person.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: She wears the tablet against her skin for the rest of Volume 1 and it is never comfortable.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: cost — evidence she will now have to hold rather than show.
Next-chapter pull: She has to decide who sees it, and there is one person she has not tested.

## Chapter 24 — The Tablet

Chapter: 24
POV and location: Ning Yan. Qinghe Sect, the outer yard, a weekday morning.
Immediate goal: Decide what to do with the only object in the world that proves the furnace column is not bookkeeping.
Resistance: Showing it means admitting she went to a sealed place, which is a capital matter. Not showing it means carrying a piece of a person forever.
Major turn: She tries the voice. Yuan can hold the tablet through her — he can touch the world through her hand for exactly as long as the designation holds — and he tells her it is the first time in nineteen years he has been able to pick anything up.
Information revealed: He can act through her. It is slower and it costs her more and he has been saving it.
Action or decision: She does not let him do it twice. She reads the tablet herself and writes it out in her log, in her own hand, in characters a Bureau clerk cannot read quickly.
Emotional change: She has started the log. The log is the first thing in the whole series she does entirely for herself.
Power or resource change: Body-Forging 7 → 8 lands here, out of walking and carrying rather than a fight. Yuan's Settlement spikes on the touch and he loses something — the first private detail he ever tells her about, which is his mother's name, and then never again.
Continuity facts to preserve: Yuan loses his mother's name when he touches a thing. He has not had one for six years and he says so, and then never says it again.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: discovery — the log exists, and it is the weapon for the whole volume.
Next-chapter pull: She goes up the mountain with the tablet and finds Elder Qiu waiting for her.

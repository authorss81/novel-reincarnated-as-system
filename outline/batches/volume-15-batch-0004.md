# Volume 15, Batch 0004 — Chapters 886–900 — *the bargain*

**Authority: `outline/volume-15.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline
wins.**

## Batch goal

Reach it. Put the whole bargain on the table, read it out loud, have the argument, and end with the offer
standing and one question asked that nobody has ever asked him before.

## Starting condition

Day 2,916, the twenty-third of the month beginning 2,894. Above the ninth landing, on a stair that does not
stop, with a lamp and no figure for what is left in it. **Immortal Vessel 9**, the ninth taken at day 2,732
and open ever since. **Yuan is Announcement**, she can hear his half, and neither of them has mentioned it
in thirty-one days. One number in the record that cannot be taken out. One boy under her, unnamed, unbound,
who has been in the record since ch 807 without knowing there was one.

## Batch midpoint (ch 891) — the ninth is not finished

The peak is a finished vessel and not a tenth stage. **No new pair of cuts, no new number, no announcement:
there is a thing that is shut.** She says it in the ordinary voice as arithmetic and it takes her nine
sentences. **And she works out, out loud, standing, that a vessel is a container and that every stage she has
taken for six volumes was built out of her own foundation, and that a container with nothing in it is not a
container.** What finishing it costs is a person, and the only person in the building who has ever been in
the same room as her and not been sent anywhere is a boy she has never asked the name of.

## Batch climax (ch 899) — the argument is had, and she does not win it

She asks the question the whole volume was built to put in the air, in the ordinary voice, standing up:
**who pays.** She is told, correctly, that the ceiling pays, that the people under it pay, that they are told
nothing, and that this is the best arrangement available and has been for nine hundred years and would be
for a thousand more. **She does not refute it and does not accept it, and the volume's goal is met because
the argument has now been had.**

## Irreversible step (ch 894) — the peak, and one person bound

**Ch 890: she asks him his name on a landing, going past him on the stair, and he says it.** She cannot bind
a person she has not got a name for, because a record with nothing in it is not a record and because her
hand cannot write a false line.

**Ch 894, the sixteenth of the month beginning 2,924, day 2,939, two hundred and seven days after the ninth
at day 2,732, and she says why that and not sixty-six out loud, standing, as arithmetic: the ninth was taken
against a refusal and this is taken against a person, and a person is not got in a hurry, and she has known
that since she was nine.**

She says the binding out loud first, in the ordinary voice, standing, before it is a line anywhere — the
fourth term, kept — **and the layer writes it, because it writes when it decides something about a person,
which is the rule she worked out at ch 838 and spent a volume on.** **The Sisterhood's first entry. The price
is on the record. Nobody bound him but her and nobody sent him anywhere and nobody entered him.**

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman who has refused twice for both of them to a woman who has done the thing herself,
out loud, in the ordinary voice, standing up, and has asked him what he wants. **She does not become
monstrous and she is not forgiven and she does not ask to be.** Yuan goes from a man who has been offered
something to a man who has been asked a question for the first time in nineteen years, and does not answer
it.

## Required power changes

**The peak of Immortal Vessel at ch 894.** Not a tenth stage, no numbered step, no announcement, no tribulation.
**Cost named once at ch 894: she cannot finish the ninth without a person in her arithmetic.** One person
bound. **No bracket may appear anywhere in ch 886–900** — the budget in `outline/volume-15.md` D3 is spent by
the end of Batch 0003.

## Required world reveals

1. **The making-place at ch 892**: a room with a table in it, no cage lamp, no wear, and a place at the far
   side with nothing worn at all, because nobody has ever needed keeping here. **This is the other end of the
   trade and her room has been the receiving end for four hundred years.**
2. **The bargain, in full, at ch 893 and ch 896–897**, written on the making-place's own register and read
   out loud by her, standing, twice — and every part of it is a mercy and **there are no conditions in it at
   all**, which is ch 832's method run once and not twice.
3. **That it is made in good faith**, and that the book does not adjudicate whether that is sincere.
4. **That the offer needs a thing it will not name in Volume 15**, and that she does not notice the thing,
   and that `outline/ending.md` §3's discovery is Volume 16's and is not spent here.

## Open questions after the batch

Whether he will answer her. Whether a number in the record can be argued with. Whether a binding can be
unmade, and by whom, and whether the person it was made on is ever going to be told by anybody. And the
question Volume 16 opens on: whether a man who has been an entry in other people's records for six hundred
years can turn one down without becoming an entry in his own.

## What Volume 16 inherits, and what may not be re-opened

**Inherited as fact:** the offer is on the table and unanswered and every part of it is real · he is
announced and heard and can be anywhere but inside her · he said yes twice where she could not hear him and
she said no twice where everything could hear her, and both stand and they do not cancel · one number is in
the record that cannot be taken out · one person is bound and the price of each binding is on the record ·
she is at the peak of the seventh realm and knows exactly what he is · she asked him what he wants and he
has not answered.

**May not be re-opened:** the moral answer, which is finished at ch 885 and is paid for and not re-argued ·
whether the Scribe was sincere, which the book refuses to adjudicate · the second place at that table, which
stays kept and empty and refused twice · the second term *in daylight*, spent at ch 830 · the six terms, and
there is no seventh · the four erased months, named once at ch 873 and never again · the bar, dated nowhere
and not lifted · the finding day of the second place, the gap in the wear, and whether the fair hand and the
third hand are the same hand, all three still open.

**And the one thing this batch must not spend:** `outline/ending.md` §3's discovery — **that the bargain
requires someone to keep the Foundation tax running, and that this is the term he cannot live inside.** He
may hear the tax in this volume. **He may not notice that the bargain needs it, and no card may put the two
in the same sentence.** That is Volume 16's turn and it happens in four minutes.

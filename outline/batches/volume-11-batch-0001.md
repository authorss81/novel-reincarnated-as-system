# Volume 11, Batch 0001 — Chapters 601–615

## Batch goal

Stand her at the turn with his talking in her ears, feed her by trade, and ask the question out loud that she
has carried since the month beginning 1,574.

## Starting condition

Day 1,831, the eighteenth of the month beginning 1,814, at the turn on the store road. Severance 6. Nine
coppers she cannot count. Six blank leaves. A brother walked west past her. A voice still talking.

## Batch midpoint (ch 606)

She decides the question is askable. Not the answer. The asking.

## Batch climax (ch 609)

She asks him what he was before he started counting, out loud, alone, at dusk. He does not answer yet — he
names the terms instead: one at a time, in daylight.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from never having asked him to having asked him. Yuan goes from waiting to be asked to answering
on terms. Ning Wu goes from found to walked past. Nothing is forgiven.

## Required power changes

None in stage terms. Severance 6 throughout. Merit settles once, on listed work cleared past the line, not on a
death. No figure of what is left of him.

## Required world reveals

The first origin mouth (ch 608, the furnace-man's mate). Nine coppers that will not count, shown working. The
terms of answering: one at a time, in daylight.

## Open questions after the batch

What was he before he started counting? What does one-at-a-time cost against eleven days? Who counts bricks at
a furnace nobody pays him to count?

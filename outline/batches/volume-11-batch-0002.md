# Volume 11, Batch 0002 — Chapters 616–630

## Batch goal

Take the seventh stage past the ceiling, write his hated name where no hand can take it out, and be given the
harvest truth that does not hold her.

## Starting condition

Day 1,842, the twenty-ninth of the month beginning 1,814. The asking stands. The terms stand. The furnace
sect's ground lies east nobody has walked her to.

## Batch midpoint (ch 624)

She writes YUAN SHENG in her own book where no hand can take it out. It cannot be put back un-written. This is
the batch's irreversible step.

## Batch climax (ch 630)

The harvest named truly by the widow's shut book: a furnace-man's son, nineteen, taken for a senior disciple's
stalled Condensing, burned out of a layer. The telling is true and does not hold her.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from asking to knowing-the-name. Yuan goes from terms-named to told-about. The widow goes from
shut book to opened page. Nothing is forgiven.

## Required power changes

Severance 7 at ch 616 on day 1,842 (70 days after Severance 6). Cost named once: she cannot hear what she is
told about him. The batch's one bracket line, far-side house form, on its own line, at ch 616 and nowhere else.

## Required world reveals

The second origin mouth (ch 622, the widow). The hated name Yuan Sheng, in her own book. The harvest mechanism,
truly written nineteen years ago.

## Open questions after the batch

If telling does not hold her, what does? Where is the furnace mouth she was shown? What does the clerk's fair
hand hold that the widow's shut book does not?

# Volume 2, Batch 0007 — Chapters 73–88

## Batch goal

Four months of tithe hours, counted slowly by an enemy, against a licence with three legs and one she owns. She gets to Lowwater. She earns clearance. And the county refuses her anyway, on the bounty standing, in the same hour she signs away the ceiling.

## Chapter range

Chapters 73–88. Sixteen chapters.

## Starting condition

Day 23 of the winter, sixth day of the thaw. Condensing stage 1. Household 211 worked / 729 standing; her own signed line nine hours. Crew nine men, quarters sleep ten. Refused slip under tithe-book cover. Road open for market traffic. Bounty standing, two proofs worked.

## Starting relationships

Employed not forgiven, three terms. Du Hong alive at the tower. Feng holding four copies behind the fifth item, column at five. Shen Xiu holding the winter book. Three sponsors holding seal, schedule, plate clause overdue. Girl of twenty-four at wash house with lath splint.

## Batch midpoint (ch 78)

Condensing stage 2, announced bound-wide at Yuan's release, not her order. Five and a half weeks of one-handed tithe, left-hand form rebuilt from Nine-Step habit, crack-channel practice in the cold. Cost: two weeks fever, a month of short breath that never fully leaves.

## Batch climax (ch 80)

She orders him to read him. He holds Ledger less than a day, reads Hou Sanliang's man through her eyes, pays the spike to about six and a half weeks of channel, and refuses to hold it again the same day and permanently. Hers is the order and the escalation.

## Required character changes

She works the tithe as labour rather than as waiting; she takes the announcement without thanks and without stopping him; she orders Ledger and carries the ordering; she signs the ceiling on herself while refused and does not ask to be forgiven and forgives nobody.

## Required power changes

One stage only: Condensing 1 → 2 at ch 78, announced. Nothing else. Yuan at Voice throughout except Ledger held less than a day at ch 80.

## Required world reveals

Tithe arithmetic closed: clearance costs every hour worked and the days not worked. Proof-buying in Lowwater is a man in a good coat employed by a broker with a counting room. County office on the square and copying floor in the tower are different rooms. Pending filing carries a ceiling at Condensing 5. Foundation on the sponsors' writ dies on the pending page.

## Open questions after the batch

Licence refused, pending filing signed and capped. Claim standing and unanswered. Foundation writ dead and three men silent. Du Hong's name in a county book. She is on the ground at Lowwater with a tower, a square, and a man hunting proofs.

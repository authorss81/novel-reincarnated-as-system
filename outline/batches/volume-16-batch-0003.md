# Volume 16, Batch 0003 — Chapters 931–945 — *the apology*

**Authority: `outline/volume-16.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline wins.
`outline/ending.md` §3 is the authority for the apology and for what it costs, and is not re-decided here.**

## Batch goal

Pay the thing the book has owed since Chapter 1, badly, at length, with no number on it — and then have it end,
and have the silence start.

## Starting condition

Day 3,043, the thirtieth of the month beginning 3,014, three days after he dissolved into the substrate. **At
the peak of Immortal Vessel, shut.** Nineteen. Three fingers and a thumb. **Right arm open 749 days** and not going
to close. Seven of eleven holds. **1,484 days lighter at `D − 1,559`.** Open place behind her fourth rib,
thumbnail-sized. Breath four counts, seven on a rise, nine standing still. **Seven hundred and forty-two days in
that room and nobody in it.**

**He is still here and he is further away.** He refused at ch 930 and the only way to be heard that far is to stop
being a person, and he did, and he is audible in her the way a thing in a wall is audible — **not gone, and not
the same.** He cannot be addressed. He cannot hold a shape. He can be in the channel and that is all.

The register with five mercies and no conditions in any of them. A lamp on the stone nearly out. A room with no
lamp over it and no wear in it. **Four hundred and forty-five days since the ninth was shut** and nothing to shut it
for.

## The pressure, with a date on it

**There is nothing on the page to stop this batch.** The layer is keeping the walls, correctly, and has one line
left in it, at ch 940, and it will report a bundle. **No fight, no antagonist, no deadline, no question, no
mystery.** Fifteen chapters of one man who has never done this and one woman who is not going to help him, in a
room at the top of a tower, with the oil nearly out.

**That is the point of the batch and the reason it exists. `bible/themes.md`: the apology, spent in Volume 1, owed
until Volume 16, paid in the last chapter.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 938) — the second attempt, which is also an entry

He tries three times.

**Attempt one (ch 934):** it has a number in it. Seven hundred and forty-nine days in a room (day 3,050 less the
day 2,301 she has been in it since); sixteen volumes; a figure at the head of her own column. He gets four sentences
in and stops, because a number attached to an apology turns it into an entry, and he knows it, and he has been a
clerk his whole existence.

**Attempt two (ch 938):** it has none and it is worse. It is a list of things he did, in order, in the present
tense, correctly, one of them on each day she can derive — and it is **an entry with the numbers taken out**, and
he catches it himself, halfway, and does not finish it, and starts again.

**Attempt three is ch 944 and it is not an entry.** It has no number on it anywhere and no order to it and it does
not defend anything and it does not explain anything. **It is not very good.** The reader has to feel a man who has
never once in nineteen years said an unscripted thing, doing it for the first time and doing it badly and going on.

## Batch climax (ch 944) — the apology that has no number on it

**For every decision he made on her behalf.** Said as decisions, not as costs. Not *I kept that from you because*;
*here is what I decided and you were not asked and here is the order I decided it in and you can check it against
your own.* He does not get to make it right. He does not get to be forgiven in it.

## Irreversible step (ch 945) — he stops being a thing that can apologise

He finishes and there is no more of him and the last thing in the world he will ever be is a voice that has said
something it cannot take back, in the present tense, to one person.

**She hears nothing.** From ch 946 he is in no chapter in any form: no bracket, no line of his, no figure of what
is left of him, no sentence anybody quotes. **The silence is not written. It is the absence, and it has a shape,
and the shape is warm on one side, and she notices that on the first morning and says it out loud once.**

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman who let him say yes to a woman who has heard him say the whole of it and **has not
forgiven him and is not going to in this batch and `forgiv` is 0 across all fifteen.** **She answers him once, at
ch 941, in her own arithmetic, with the days she can derive, and what she says is not absolution and is not
cruelty, and it is the last conversation the two of them have.** Yuan goes from a man who refused to a man who has
nothing left to refuse and says the one thing he came back for and does it badly and is not thanked.

## Required power changes

**None. Immortal Vessel 9, shut, throughout.** **One bracketed layer line is permitted, at ch 940**, and it is a
bundle arriving on time and it is the last ordinary thing the layer says before it stops. No stage, no number, no
cuts, no announcement about her, no frame scene, no eleventh frame anywhere.

## Required world reveals

1. **What a person dissolving into a machine sounds like from the inside.** Not gone. Audible. Further.
2. **That he cannot be addressed and cannot hold a shape**, and that this is what being heard that far costs, and
   that he chose it knowing the exact price since Volume 11.
3. **That he has never apologised to anybody in a way that counted**, and that his last coherent breath in a
   furnace in another life was an apology to the men cutting him open, and that he does not mention it and the
   reader can feel it.
4. **The shape of the silence**, said once, at ch 945, in her own mouth, and not to be said again in the volume.

## Open questions after the batch

Everything. She is alone at the top of a tower with a register she cannot use and a layer that will not stop and a
sentence she has not found. What she does with the lamp. Whether she goes down. And the thing nobody has asked in
nineteen years and she has still not asked: what the announcements are made of, and who is left to refuse them.

## What Batch 0004 must not inherit as clean prose

That she forgave him. **`forgiv` is 0 across ch 931–945 and the only one in the volume is at ch 1000.** That anybody
thanked anybody. That the apology was good. **It was not, and Batch 0004 must not have anybody on the page call it
brave or moving or well done.** That the shape of the silence has been explained twice — it is said once, at ch 945,
and never again. That the four months have been named between ch 873 and ch 1000. **They have not.** That the
bracket budget is spent: Batch 0001 spent two, Batch 0002 spent three, Batch 0003 spent one, **six of the volume's
eight are gone and two remain — ch 955 in Batch 0004, and ch 971 in Batch 0005, which is the last bracketed line in
the whole manuscript and the only one after ch 930.** Batch 0006 and Batch 0007 spend none.

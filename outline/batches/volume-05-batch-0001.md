## Batch goal

She works the far reach at five coppers a day and is kept by a wage-book that already holds her; the
claim stands read aloud in a room that can act and is not answered here; the country is ordinary, farmed
and lit, and asks nothing; Feng's stair still carries. **This batch establishes keeping — and leaves her
kept.**

**What the batch is, in one sentence of pressure:** the far side is not a place she is passing through.
It is a set of books that hold her hours in one hand, in order, with dates, and a season is a thing that
can be entered into those books and cannot be taken back out.

**This batch buys a term of employment and the price of acting, and pays for the term in the only
currency the far side accepts, which is days.**

**What this batch does NOT buy:** an answer to the claim. **The claim stands.** Nobody tears off the date
in Hou Sanliang's counting room, nobody puts Hou in a room, and nobody sends a draft west — **the draft is
Batch 0002's.** No card re-lists the seven relicensing conditions; the seventh was read aloud once, at
ch 237, and that was the whole of it. An answer to the Hunter's request. The request stays pending, the
space holds a countersignature dated from the far side, and **that is not an answer.** No stage, no
Golden Core, no bracketed layer line anywhere in fourteen chapters — **Golden Core 1 is ch 258, day 512,
in Batch 0002.** No tribulation. No suppression and no absence arithmetic. No Merit settled, nothing
killed, nothing cleared, the channel not restored, and **no figure of what is left of him stated on any
page.** The Ledger **is not ordered, is not held, is not priced and is not refused in this batch** — hers
to order, and she does not order it. **Feng Lushi does not appear.** The fair copy of the Foundation
column going past the line into a book the far side keeps is **ch 262, Batch 0002**, and no card here may
spend it or hint at a document arriving. Nothing is opened: the relicensed licence is not opened, the
register is not corrected, the Clay Plate is not described, *kiln-born* is not spoken. No second
consecutive batch on the same pressure: **this one sets the rotation, and it is keeping and being kept
and nothing else** — not ration, not hiring, not tending, not crossing, not examination, audit, hunt or
signature, and not confinement. Wei Ran on one card at most, as rate and road. The Hunter on one card at
most, buying nothing and certifying nothing. **Inside the bound by consequence only, once, at ch 244, and
no scene west of the line anywhere in this batch.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 246)

**She understands, in plain terms and in short sentences, that the board that now matters is the one
four hundred yards behind her at a trestle — and that every hour she works past the line makes her the
only person in nine days of road whose hours can be set against a figure no county can answer for.**

**It is a decision, not a speech.** She works it out standing at the plank over two days with the pay-day
of the twenty-seventh already gone and the next one eleven days off, in the ordinary way she works
everything out, and it arrives as four plain sentences with no machinery in them at all:

# Volume 2, Batch 0008 — Chapters 89–103

## Batch goal

Pursuit and the bounty. She holds the pending filing on the ground at Lowwater with a tower, a square and a man hunting proofs. She climbs one stage at her own order and pays for the noise. She works out alone what being Chosen costs. She kills a man to stop him killing another, and the killing pays for Yuan's life. Du Hong dies of her papers.

## Chapter range

Chapters 89–103. Fifteen chapters.

## Starting condition

Day 81, county office square. Pending filing signed, capped Condensing 5, defect in her own hand. Seal, clearance, surety assembled, licence refused on bounty standing. Condensing 2, short breath two counts, arm about a third. Tower loft mat, carrying work under schedule. Buyer at three desks, counting room behind him. Settlement past nine-tenths, about six and a half weeks.

## Starting relationships

Employed not forgiven, three terms. Du Hong living surety in county book. Feng column five, four copies walled, none upward. Three sponsors silent on dead writ. Girl of twenty-four at wash house alive. Hou Sanliang never in a room with her.

## Batch midpoint (ch 92)

Condensing 2 → 3, released at her order, announced bound-wide. Four weeks of Lowwater carrying work, tower loft, built out of load. Arm settles at a third and stays. Cost: the announcement tells the market where she sleeps. One bracketed layer line, four-clause order with stage three.

## Batch climax (ch 100–103)

Attempt at the tower foot, killing, death, Merit. Hou's men stop Du Hong on the Lowwater road at the foot of the Lantern Register tower. He gives his own name, burns one sheet, refuses the place. Beaten for the place, left in thaw water. She kills the second man with the Nine-Step, habit not technique, one working arm, very nearly killed. No aperture-channel. He dies before night out with Feng holding the lamp. Proof fails on the body; bounty stands unclaimed. Merit from the listed kill only, told-before-spent, yes, left-hand sequence gone, Settlement to roughly two-thirds (~11 weeks). Burial lists four causes, all hers.

## Required character changes

She orders the noise and carries being known; she hears the lie and does not take it for shelter; she works out the price alone and does not forgive; she kills to stop a killing and pays the burial in her own hand.

## Required power changes

One stage only: Condensing 2 → 3 at ch 92, released at her order and announced. Nothing else. No suppression here. No tribulation. No Ledger retake.

## Required world reveals

Pending-filing life as use; pursuit from records to bodies; the lie as exaggeration with the eleven as evidence; *kiln-born* on paper as first new fact of the eleven; working-out that designation prices rather than shelters; Merit from one listed kill; burial that closes the cheap door.

## Open questions after the batch

Pending standing, capped at 5. Claim standing, unclaimed, proof failed. Writ dead, three men silent. Du Hong dead, debt hers. Settlement ~11 weeks. She does not forgive him. *kiln-born* held in two heads, no paper; tower column five; Feng alive, unaudited, no Lung.

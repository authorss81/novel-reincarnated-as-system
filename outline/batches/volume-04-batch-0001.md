## Batch goal

She spends eight of the last ten days of the voice in her head on one question — *whose hand wrote a
date into Hou Sanliang's counting room in Lowwater* — and what comes back is a working name and a
household of somebody's own out past the boundary, which is not an answer and is not the claim and does
not tear the date off. She rations what is left to about four sentences a day, chooses every one of
them, and keeps the count on the back of a dead quarry weight ticket in her own hand. **Nobody in these
fourteen chapters knows she is doing it.** The year of non-forgiveness that has run since ch 100 does
not close; it changes shape, because the sentence she has kept back once a year to say the thing out
loud in a room goes on the price instead, and what replaces the refusal is not forgiveness but use.
The empty answer space on the public shelf is read and read and read and stays empty for all fourteen
chapters. The quarry has been dry since day 189, the tin is about a hundred and forty coppers, and at
four loads a week at nine coppers it is empty on about day 246 — eight days short of the season's road.

**This batch buys a direction and spends a man to get it.**

**What this batch does NOT buy:** an answer to the Hunter's request. The space stays empty. The claim
stays standing and the date stays in it and Hou Sanliang is not met. The Foundation is not reached —
**Foundation 1 is ch 207, in Batch 0002.** No stage is taken and no bracketed layer line exists in these
fourteen chapters. No kill, no Merit, nothing on the page that puts the channel back. No work for
anybody and no money made. She does not forgive him and nothing here is a reconciliation.

## Batch midpoint (ch 186)

**People and paper, not power.** Nine men are owed hours and cannot claim them.

The word on the front of the Qinghe furnace schedule is *irregular* for the fourth time this year, and
the winter has no extra work in it — the cut ran out on day 189 — so the crew's hours are asked and
worked and enter a sheet that is not a claim. Gao has done the sum out loud in a hall once already so
that nine men would hear it from a mouth; this card is the second time and it costs him something to
say it. **The turn:** she works out, standing at the frame, that the one asset she owns — eleven
minutes in her own hand and four miles of road — is worth exactly nothing to nine men, and that she has
been carrying a thing she cannot spend. She is eighteen, licensed, hunted in writing, and the only
person in that room who cannot pay anybody.

No Ledger in this chapter, no stage, no announcement, no bracket. **This is the lowest card in the batch
and it is where the reader is supposed to feel the price of ch 187 arriving three chapters before she
pays it.**

## Batch climax (ch 187)

**She says the word, and eight of the last ten days of him go on one name.**

It is done once, in the standing form the book fixed: **the price named before the word, in the ordinary
voice, to nobody, on a mat in a cold room with nine men asleep** — the ch 149 form, and no ceremony, and
no one else in the room. He says the price. She says it slower. **Eight days out of about ten.** Then she
says the word.

It runs **less than a day** and it stops on its own. What comes back is **the hand that wrote a date
into Hou Sanliang's counting room in Lowwater: a working name, and a household of his own somewhere past
this county's boundary.** What it does not buy is on the page too and is not argued: not her, not the
claim, not the design, not the reason for anything. **It is a direction and not an answer.**

**This is the batch's irreversible decision.** Eight days of channel do not come back. She can never
unlearn a name. And after today she is a woman who buys people on purpose with the only currency she
has.

## Required world reveals

What the reader learns in ch 179–192 that they did not know at ch 178. **The Wall is not one of them and
is not hinted at as one.**

1. **The empty space is a place where an entry gets made.** It is not an unanswered question; it is a
   ruled space on a form, and the office that fills it is the one the referral went to, and **nothing in
   this county can make that entry.** The shape of the boundary is visible from inside the boundary and
   there is no way round that from here.
2. **A county publishes a day for the road and the carriers do not believe it.** The notice on the shelf
   and the carriers' word differ by about a week, and the week is the whole of her margin.
3. **A Ledger price is paid out of a quantity of channel and not out of time.** The reading itself is
   short — less than a day, at ch 149's precedent — and the price is what is gone afterwards. The
   account is kept in her own hand on a scrap of a dead quarry weight ticket, and the ration is hers.
4. **A voice can be rationed, and the person in it is worse at it than the woman doing it**, because he
   wants to spend them and she wants to save them.
5. **Nine men and a woman in twelve bunks keep their own book and are owed hours, and *irregular* on
   the front of a schedule makes those hours unclaimable.** A furnace crew in the deep cold with no extra
   work in it is a wage that arrives late or does not arrive.
6. **Her eleven minutes and her four miles are worth nothing to anybody.** She is the only person in that
   room who cannot pay.
7. **A hand that writes dates for a living is a working man with a household of his own past the
   boundary**, and a date against a claim is a schedule, and a person who keeps a schedule does not stop
   keeping it because you know his name.
8. **The tin of about a hundred and forty coppers at four loads a week at nine coppers runs out on about
   day 246**, eight days short of a road that opens on about day 254. That is the batch's real deadline
   and it is arithmetic, not weather.

## Chapter 190 — A Bed of Ash

## Batch goal

The line walked, and the people who live on it.

Batch 0001 was ration and silence. Batch 0002 was a road, a counter and a wage-book, and it bought a book
and spent an attendance. **This batch is the work.** It is what a line of work looks like when it is being
tended every day by people paid by the day, and it is the argument — *the line holds because people tend
it* — shown rather than stated, and **the people are right and nothing in this batch or this volume proves
them wrong.**

Four Foundation stages go through her body in fourteen chapters, **at the pinned chapters, on the pinned
days**, each one paid for in a different thing and each cost named once in new words: **what the six
coppers stop covering · the count of breath that does not come back · the order in her left hand · a name
struck out of a wage-board.**

And **the line's own people close their course against her, lawfully and correctly, using the Hunter's
referral** — because the referral says the thing in her head is unauthorised and is carried by a licensee
in good standing, and because the line is the one place in the world where the execution of his order is
possible at all. **He does not want it executed. He says so. It costs them, not him.**

And she comes out the other side of it knowing something about **the sound a thing makes on the other side
of a line that no person within nine days of road can name** — because she is the only person alive who has
been announced, and she is standing in a foot of the residue of her own announcements in the ground of a
country that has never heard one.

**This batch walks the line and is closed on it.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 215)

**The Hunter tells her the one thing he came out here to say, and it is that he does not want it executed.**

He has walked east to a line of work because his referral is executable here and nowhere else. A field
agent of a Warden's office cannot unmake a System inside a bound — that is what a Wall writ is for, and a
Wall writ is issued by the line's own people to somebody who lives on it, and **she is not one of them and
the line does not know her.** What the line can do instead is close its own ground against a person it does
not carry, and it can do that lawfully, and it has done it twice, and both times it was right.

**He wants the answer pending.** A pending answer is a question somebody above him has to answer, and he
has never once believed the answer would be the right one. **He says so out loud, in the ordinary voice, in
front of the woman it is being done to, and she has two questions she does not ask.** It is not a threat. **It is a professional disagreeing with his own
office in front of the woman, and it is worse than a threat and it is the reason the reader believes the
rest of it.**

He buys nothing, threatens nothing, touches her, enters nothing, and **does not ask her a question and says
so in the same breath.** He is not named on any page and **no document in this batch carries his name: the
referral that goes on the course is the same paper that went up the stair and it carries the office.**

**No bracket, no stage, no Ledger, no question of hers answered, and nothing she does about it is a speech.**

## Batch climax (ch 218)

**The line's own people close their course against her, lawfully, and she is in the camp when it happens.**

The decision is made in a room she is not in, at a table, by the people whose course it is, and it reaches
her the way everything reaches a line: **somebody says it out loud in the ordinary voice and everybody
carries on.** No meeting is staged, no villain delivers it, and no one explains themselves.

The rule is nine years old and it is not a secret. **It is why the Hunter is in the camp at all** — she
works that out standing at a trestle, one day after he told her, and it is the whole of what she learns
from him and it is not a favour.

**They are right.** The thing in her head is unauthorised. It is carried by a licensee in good standing.
Both are true, both are on the same paper, and a line that kept its working ground open to an unauthorised
thing would not be a line. **Nothing in this batch or this volume proves them wrong.**

**And it costs them.** The stretch she worked goes back to what it was before she came, in three days,
because it is nobody's job now; the contract is smaller; the board has a hole in it; and **the girl of
nineteen is idle, and she is somebody's daughter.**

The climax is not that she is hurt. **It is that they are right and she cannot answer them and they will
carry the cost of being right themselves.**

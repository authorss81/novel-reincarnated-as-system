# Volume 3, Batch 0002 — Chapters 133–148

## Batch goal

She works **two schedules at once** under a ceiling of nine, reaches Condensing 6 and announces it at her
own order, and the sentence tells nine counties where a relicensed licensee now stands. A lawful request
for the tower's paper upward is answered truthfully by a keeper who cannot lie, and the answer opens a
tower that has been closed for twenty-two years. A man who keeps that tower is offered mercy with a hook
and takes it, because his lungs will not stand another winter of copying cold. And on the escort road a
bounty man working the entered act puts his hand on a boy, and she kills him with one working arm and
very nearly dies, and the only thing that pays for it is the body.

## Chapter range

Chapters 133–148. Sixteen chapters. **Sized to its own arc** — audit and mercy — not to a fixed count.
The arc closes on ch 148 with stage seven announced and two weeks of fever paid for it.

## In-world clock, canon for this batch — READ THIS BEFORE WRITING ANY DAY

six weeks between them and the other thirteen chapters carry five.** A drafter who spreads sixteen

## Chapter 133 — The Two Clocks

Chapter: 133. POV: Ning Yan. Location/time: the crew quarters at Qinghe and the furnace floor; the
hiring lane behind the well at Lowwater; the county office on the square. Day 42.

Immediate goal: Get one full week of the double schedule to hold without a gap in the filing.
Resistance: The county hour is at the third hour and the lane is nineteen minutes from the square and
four miles from the quarters, and every county hour costs four loads. The furnace shift is at the ninth
hour. The arm is out a third of the time and he tells her before it goes. Da Shan's board has been wiped
and chalked again and nobody at it has heard of a licence.

Information revealed: The relicensing has changed what her hours are and not what they are worth. She
now has two sets of hours, one of which a man at a board wipes off at the end of every day, and one of
which a registrar writes into a book with a nib. **The annex column is working** — the paid-work hours
are being annexed onto the Qinghe furnace schedule by the Hall's copyist and certified, and they are
real hours in a real column, and nobody has ever paid for them before.

Action or decision: She builds the week out of the two clocks and works it, and takes the county hour
loss as a fixed price rather than trying to arrange it away.

Emotional change: Relief at a ceiling of nine, curdled by the discovery that a ceiling is a permission
and not a wage, and the only new thing she owns is a column that counts what she already does.

Power or resource change: **None. Condensing 5.** Four loads a county hour. **No bracketed line.**

Continuity fact to preserve: Ten sleepers, nine men, twelve bunks. Da Shan and the board carry over and
stay minor. **Do not re-list the seven conditions.** **Do not touch a price.** Hou does not appear.
Feng unaudited. **No weekday.**

Ending type: cost — the double schedule holds, and it holds by paying half a day's pay every time.

Next-chapter pull: The annex is a column in a book, and a book is read.

## Chapter 134 — The Annex Is Work

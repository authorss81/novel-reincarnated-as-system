# Volume 11, Batch 0003 — Chapters 631–645

## Batch goal

Take the eighth stage past the ceiling, hold the seventh frame scene, and tell him the tax was designed — and
stay anyway.

## Starting condition

Day 1,917, the fourteenth of the month beginning 1,904. His hated name is in her book. The harvest is named.
Telling misses. Asking still holds, barely.

## Batch midpoint (ch 638)

She walks to the furnace mouth she was shown at ch 632 and keeps walking past it. The handing was ch 632; the
road is this chapter. The two are different beats.

## Batch climax (ch 644–645)

The seventh frame scene (ch 644): whether a fragment can be known without being used up — it can. Then (ch
645) she tells him the Foundation tax was designed as recruitment, out loud, once, and stays. Irreversible.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from knowing-the-name to knowing-the-design. Yuan goes from told-about to told-to-his-face. The
clerk and the kiln boy go from names to mouths. Nothing is forgiven.

## Required power changes

Severance 8 at ch 632 on day 1,917 (75 days
after Severance 7). Cost named once: she cannot keep him by asking. The batch's one bracket line at ch 632 and
nowhere else. Merit settles once, on listed work, not on a death.

## Required world reveals

The third and fourth origin mouths (ch 636 the clerk, ch 640 the kiln boy grown old). The seventh frame scene.
The tax named as designed — said once, in her mouth, at ch 645.

## Open questions after the batch

What does staying cost now that asking does not hold? What will Feng Lushi do with twenty-two years of fair
writing? What is the road up, and who pays for it?

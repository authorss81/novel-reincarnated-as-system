# Volume 16, Batch 0007 — Chapters 989–1000 — *the courtyard*

**Authority: `outline/volume-16.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline wins.
`outline/ending.md` §5 and §7 are the authority for the account, the forgiveness, the final image and the five
obligations of the last chapter, and none of it is re-decided here. **Written 15 Oct 2026 at the Volume 16 planning
review, out of the outline's own batch table and day table, to close a gap in the dispatch chain. This card fixes no
chapter, no day, no midpoint and no climax.****

## Batch goal

Nineteen years of ordinary life in twelve chapters, and a woman who has to go back to teaching on the last page of
the book. This is the only batch in sixteen volumes that goes past the event.

## Starting condition

Day 3,190, the twenty-seventh of the month beginning 3,164, two days after the ceiling came off. **At the peak of
Immortal Vessel, shut**, and the strongest mortal in the world by an order of magnitude, **and she cannot ascend,
because there is nowhere to ascend to any more.**

Three fingers and a thumb. **Right arm open 896 days** and not going to close, and not going to close in the next
nineteen years either. Seven of eleven holds. Open place behind the fourth rib, thumbnail-sized, stated once and
never re-measured. Breath four counts, seven on a rise, nine standing still. **A lamp she cannot refill and a book of
pages in her own hand, with Feng Lushi's last fair handwriting as the first leaf, and that is now the founding
document of the world and nobody has said so out loud.**

**He is in no chapter in this batch.** No bracket, no line of his, no figure of what is left of him, no sentence
anybody quotes. **There has been no sound in her head for years and there is not going to be one, and the shape of it
was named once, at ch 945, and it is not named again here.**

## The pressure, with a date on it

**The first of 3,164 at ch 990 is the last time a chapter in this book is dated by the layer's calendar.** It is the
first month in nine hundred years with nothing on it at all, and after it, days are counted the ordinary way and no
figure of the layer's calendar is available to anybody, her included.

**At ch 989 she says out loud, once, that the figures have run out.** It is the first loss she has ever had that she
cannot compute, and the reader is meant to feel it before it is explained. **No card may have her compute it a
second time**, and after ch 989 her figures are **days, weather, bodies and hands** — that is the change the reader
feels before anybody explains it.

**Nineteen years in twelve chapters: six thousand eight hundred and forty days, and no chapter may skip a year
without saying so out loud, in her own arithmetic, in the ordinary voice.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 994) — the four hundred are a school

**The Sisterhood is not a sect, an order, a church, a conspiracy, a chamber or a school. It is four hundred people in
a woman's arithmetic. And then, about five years in, it is a school, and it became one without anybody deciding that
it would.** It happened because a person stayed, and then another person stayed, and there was nowhere for them to
go.

**No card may fix the number of people who come to the courtyard, or which of the four hundred are in it.** The four
hundred is `outline/ending.md` §7 and it is fixed; the overlap is not, and a card may not state it.

## Batch climax (ch 999) — the account, nineteen years, and no sound

**The account has been carried since Chapter 3 and it closes here because he is gone, not because the debt was
settled.** `outline/ending.md` §5: the forgiveness is not a relief, it is housekeeping, and **that is the last thing
the reader is asked to sit with.** `forgiv` is **1 in the whole volume** and it is at ch 1000 and not here; `thank`
is **0 in the whole volume** and **the last time anybody in this book has a chance to thank anybody, nobody does.**
`the year` is at most **2** and both are at ch 999–1000.

**She has waited for a sound for years and there is not going to be one. It is not silence exactly. It has a shape,
and the shape is a man standing in a doorway with his hands empty, waiting to be let in.** That is named once and it
was named at ch 945; what ch 999 does is sit with a woman who has stopped expecting it and has not stopped noticing
the shape of it.

## Irreversible step (ch 1000) — she forgives him, and it is housekeeping, and she goes back to teaching

**One word, in one chapter, at the end. No card may prepare for it or soften toward it, and no chapter before ch 1000
may contain a word of it.**

**The account closes because he is gone.** That is the final emotional answer of the book and it is not a resolution
and it is not a reward and nobody is told the debt was settled, **because it was not.**

**The dramatic event of ch 1000 is not a recall.** It is a young man in a courtyard failing to break stage nine, and
there being nobody in the world who will say so out loud, and her watching it happen, and going back to teaching.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman who ended a layer to **a woman of thirty-eight who teaches in a courtyard above Lowwater
and waits for a sound that is not coming, and who forgives a man who is not there for the only reason available to
her, which is that there is nobody left to not forgive.** She is not at peace and no card may make her sound at
peace. **She is going back to teaching**, which is the hardest thing in the book and is stated as an ordinary action.

## Required power changes

**None, and there will not be another in this book.** No stage, no number, no cuts, no frame scene, no bracket. A
cultivator held at stage nine fails in the last chapter and **nobody announces it and nobody writes it down and
nobody is coming to say so**, and that is `outline/ending.md` §4 and it is the last fact the world has, not the last
fact the reader gets.

## Required world reveals

1. **That she cannot ascend any more, and that nobody else is going to either, and that the ceiling was removable by
   one woman saying nine words.**
2. **That four hundred people became a school by staying.**
3. **That the account closes because he is gone.** Nothing else closes it.
4. **The five obligations `outline/ending.md` §8 gives to ch 1000**, and four of them are a sentence each, in her
   head, in passing, and **five beats given room is five beats too many — the chapter's weight is the failing student
   and the silence.**

## Open questions after the batch

None. **This is the last batch of the book.** What remains open is what the book refuses to open, and it refuses it on
purpose: whether the Scribe was sincere, what happens to the Anchor once it is released, whether anything answers
from beyond, whether Ning Wu forgives her, and what the announcements were made of.

## What no batch after this inherits, because there is none

**The book ends at ch 1000 and it ends in a courtyard, on an ordinary action, without a line pointed at itself.** A
volume is a book and nothing else, and this volume does not say so and does not say that it is the last one.

**Five obligations, four of them a sentence each, in her head, in passing, and the chapter's weight is the failing
student and the silence:** the announcement that named her, quoted in her memory in her own words and **not in square
brackets, because the bracket is the layer's voice and there is no layer left to speak it** · Feng Lushi's last fair
handwriting as the first leaf of the founding document · Qiu's line, *the shape of a happy life*, spoken once more by
Ning Wu, meaning it differently, **and the book does not explain what he means by it** · the Clay Tablet's texture ·
the four months erased in Chapter 3, named once as a loss that is never recovered and is not forgiven.

**And these are the things the last chapter must not do:** it must not be a summary · it must not have anybody
explain the book · it must not answer the Anchor, the Scribe's sincerity, or whether anything answers from beyond ·
it must not put a sound in her head · it must not have her at peace · it must not thank anybody · it must not say
what the announcements were made of.

# Volume 2, Batch 0006 — Chapters 59–72

## Batch goal

Trapped on the mountain by ice with a price on her head, Ning Yan files for the only paper
that lets her practise at all, and by the end of the batch she has the name of the document,
two of its three legs, and two funerals she caused.

## Chapter range

Chapters 59–72. Fourteen chapters.

## Starting condition

Ch 58. Ninth day of an eleven-day ash fall, first grey snow. The pass at the fourth bridge
iced over in the night; the road opens for market on the third day after, which is ch 61, and
the fall ends two days after ch 58, so ch 60 is the last day. Condensing stage 1, aperture
cracked through, right arm out of function. Her four county copies are going down the
mountain in Du Hong's coat. Eight hundred jin stands on the Chosen One, proof defined as a
body, a place and a name. She refused four better rooms in ch 55 and is in the second bunk.

## Starting relationships

Employed not forgiven, on three terms: the work is her brother; nothing decided about her
without telling her first. He broke the third term in ch 54. She stopped him once, in ch 57,
with *not tonight*, and it worked. Du Hong knows she knows and his file carries her hand
twice. The three sponsors have never asked her a question. Mei Jiu gave an unwritten
compression for nothing in ch 47 and has not been thanked by either of them.

## Batch midpoint (ch 65–66)

The sponsors' inspectors come for her papers. She refuses the examination on the
maintaining-member consent regulation, aloud and before witnesses, and the inspectors take
the roster instead. The two friends who signed for her shift are doubled onto the settling
floor.

## Batch climax (ch 71–72)

The two friends die under a falsified roster in a room she knows is unsafe. She says both
causes aloud at the burial and does not let Yuan soften either, then goes back to work the
tithe.

## Required character changes

She spends two other people's lives on a decision that was hers to make, says so out loud
where it cannot be softened, and keeps working. Nobody forgives her and she does not ask. She
tells Yuan what the work is and he does not try to make it smaller.

## Required power changes

**None.** Condensing stage 1 for all fourteen chapters. The batch is about paper, ice, and
the arm that does not come back. No breakthrough, no tribulation, no announcement.

## Required world reveals

The licence exists and has a name, an issuing office, a clause number and three legs. The
sponsors' plate clause is an article their own paper demands and that she has sworn in her
own hand she does not have. Quota clearance is arithmetic and the arithmetic says spring. The
eight hundred jin has a buyer who is local, can afford it without selling land, and keeps his
paper in the same book the sponsors keep theirs in.

## Open questions after the batch

Two must be live going into ch 73. What does Shen Xiu's clearance actually cost in hours, and
who is buying proof in Lowwater now that the road is open.

## Chapter 59 — The Closed Pass

Chapter: 59
POV and location: Ning Yan. The fourth-bridge barrier and the Nine Steps Terrace, second hour of the morning, the day after ch 58.
Immediate goal: Get down the mountain to Lowwater before the pass shuts behind her for the winter.
Resistance: The pass iced over in the night and the Watch has the road barred. She cannot leave legally and nobody will say that out loud. Captain Lu Tao has a line in his own book with her name on it and the word *held*.
Weather and season: Tenth day of the ash fall. Grey snow packed into the stone treads, ash still coming down thin over the top of it. Deep winter, the coldest night so far.
Major turn: She turns back at the barrier with the papers still in her coat and understands that she is wintering on the mountain with a bounty standing — no flight, no Lowwater, no tower.
Information revealed: No paper moves down and no help moves up until the road opens. The barrier is not a punishment; it is a schedule.
Action or decision: She walks back up all nine steps and takes the second bunk again on purpose, in front of the crew, where she can be counted.
Emotional change: The visibility of Volume 1 becomes confinement. Fear narrows into something she can carry.
Power or resource change: None. Condensing 1.
Continuity facts to preserve: Right arm a third of the time and untested more than twice a day; the iron token still in the coat, unwanted; one day of the fall left after this one.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — she winters where she is.
Next-chapter pull: The crew quarters sleep twelve and two of the bunks have questions.

## Chapter 60 — The Second Bunk

Chapter: 60
POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace-crew quarters, fourth hour past midday.
Immediate goal: Sleep one night in her own bunk without being moved, watched, or honoured.
Resistance: Eleven crew and eleven kinds of curiosity, the memory of four rooms already refused, and the two unnamed friends from ch 32 who want the story of the floor and will not stop asking for it.
Weather and season: Eleventh and last day of the ash fall. Ash in the bedding, in the wash water, and in the cough of the whole room. By evening it stops.
Major turn: She tells them the true part — the arm, the crack as a mouth, the eight hundred jin — and withholds the tablet and the log. They decide to stand her shifts when she is at the Hall, and she lets them.
Information revealed: The quarters' roster can be signed by another hand, and a signed shift is a legal fact regardless of who works it.
Action or decision: She accepts their cover, knowing exactly what a roster is.
Emotional change: A warmth she does not trust, and takes anyway. The only easy hour of the batch.
Power or resource change: None.
Continuity facts to preserve: The two friends stay unnamed for the rest of the book. The roster rule is stated once here and demonstrated, never re-explained. Last day of the fall. The cover is for her **work**, not for her goods — the two threads are separate and must not be merged.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: emotional consequence — friendship as a loaded thing.
Next-chapter pull: A clerk's folded sheet is waiting at the Hall's table. The licence exists and it has a name.

## Chapter 61 — The Licence Clerk

Chapter: 61
POV and location: Ning Yan. The Assembly Hall landing, ninth hour of the morning. **Not** the records room, which is beneath the furnace and was used in ch 34–35.
Immediate goal: Learn what paper lets a servant-born practise past registration.
Resistance: Xie Shuo is gone from the Hall's table, ruined quietly after ch 56. The new clerk knows her only as a file and is more afraid of three sponsors than of her.
Weather and season: The fall ended overnight. First clear day, hard sky, smoke lying flat on the terrace. **This is also the third day after ch 58 and the day the road opens for market** — the news is on the terrace before she reaches the Hall.
Major turn: The clerk shows her Clause Four of the Servant Advancement Settlement and the Licence to Practise Past Registration. Sponsors' seal, quota clearance, county countersignature. Three legs, and she owns one.
Information revealed: The document's name, the issuing office — the Bureau of Records' county office at Lowwater — the clause, and the three countersignatures. Realm walls bend for documents, and this is the one they bend for.
Action or decision: She copies the clause into the log in wash code, in her own poor hand, and resolves to file.
Emotional change: A goal with edges for the first time since the trial. Hunger replaces dread.
Power or resource change: None in stage terms. New resource: the name of the document and the shape of it.
Continuity facts to preserve: Registrar Sun He's name is on the clerk's lips once and only once. Feng's witness function is mentioned as procedure, not as a favour. The Hall still has no roof and is used for paper, never for shelter. The four rooms were refused in ch 55 and are not re-offered here.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: new question — what does the quota leg cost in hours?
Next-chapter pull: The sponsors send for her, and the leg she owns has teeth.

## Chapter 62 — The Price of Paper

Chapter: 62
POV and location: Ning Yan. The sponsors' corridor off the roofless Hall, sixth hour of the evening.
Immediate goal: Get Lu Kang to re-affix the sponsors' seal to the licence application.
Resistance: Lu Kang, Deng Chao and Pei An, courteous and unhurried. The seal leg is theirs, and they know what it buys and what it will cost her later.
Weather and season: Snow hardening to ice. Braziers burning in a roofless corridor, every breath visible.
Major turn: They seal the application, and in doing so she reads the plate clause again against what she swore at ch 56. The sponsorship **requires** a fired plate the size of a hand to be surrendered to the maintaining members within the week. Her own sworn entry says she cannot produce it and does not undertake to. So the paper demands an article that the only person who could produce it has sworn in front of forty-one witnesses she does not have.
Information revealed: The trap is one she built and they built together, in the same hour. They want the furnace schedule worked on their account and her mouth shut about the voice, in that order.
Action or decision: She accepts the sealed application. Irreversible in miniature: it enters her name in the county queue, which is a public fact. **This is preparation, not the filing. She files once, at ch 70.**
Emotional change: Cold anger, banked. She is bargaining again, and this time she notices herself doing it while it is happening.
Power or resource change: Application sealed. One leg owned. Sponsors' leverage deepened.
Continuity facts to preserve: **The sworn non-possession is Ning Yan's, not the sponsors'** — ch 48 made the paper demand the plate, ch 56 made her deny it, and ch 56 is where she says to Lu Kang's face that it is false. The clause is explained once here and never re-explained. Hou Sanliang is not named in this chapter.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: cost — the help and the harness are the same sheet of paper.
Next-chapter pull: The road opened this morning and the Watch will be at the barrier.

## Chapter 63 — The Lowwater Market

Chapter: 63
POV and location: Ning Yan. The terrace rail and the road below it, at market hour. Du Hong's thread is carried in a letter, not a POV break.
Immediate goal: Get word down to Du Hong and up from Feng before the market crowd scatters.
Resistance: She cannot go down. The Watch holds her at the barrier by name, politely, for her own safety, which is the bounty made courteous. Captain Lu Tao writes down the time of her asking without looking up.
Weather and season: Two days after the road opened, the first market after the ice. Hard snow on the ground, throwable light off it, and the smell of nine counties' smoke coming up the slope.
Major turn: A letter comes up. Du Hong has delivered the four county copies to the tower with nobody's name on them, and Feng has walled them in behind the fifth item and entered none of them upward. And in the Lowwater square a man has been to three county desks asking which clerk keeps the copies of the Qinghe advancement, and has been paying for the answer.
Information revealed: The bounty has moved on from wordings to **records**. The ears were established in ch 55 and ch 58 and must **not** be re-revealed; what is new is the direction they turned.
Action or decision: She writes back one line in her own unreadable hand: *copy nothing for him.* It will not be enough.
Emotional change: The confinement turns outward. The danger is down the mountain and she cannot reach it.
Power or resource change: None. Feng's unindexed column stays at five.
Continuity facts to preserve: Four copies went down, not two, and none of them is an original — those stay in the log. Lowwater is both one of the nine counties and the town under Qinghe Sect. The Watch owe her nothing and remember everything.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: new danger — the proof-hunt named by function.
Next-chapter pull: The tithe book opens in Shen Xiu's office, and her hours go into it.

## Chapter 64 — The Furnace Tithe

Chapter: 64
POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace floor, night shift, fifth hour past midday into the first night hour.
Immediate goal: Work the first block of tithe hours one-handed without being carried by anybody in the crew.
Resistance: The furnace procedure as she learned it standing on the floor in ch 32, word for word; a dead right arm; slag heat; and a drill-master who pities her, which is worse than contempt would be.
Weather and season: Pit winter. Slag light against snow glare through the door crack, and iron smoke in the lungs by the fourth hour.
Major turn: She finds a one-handed rhythm using the cracked aperture as a channel to steady heat, and holds the shift. **Damage as technique is spent (ch 39) — use it, do not explain it again, and do not let her narrate it as a discovery.** She does not tell anyone on the floor what she is doing.
Information revealed: The tithe book is kept by Shen Xiu's office, not by the Hall. It is counted in **hours, not weeks**, and every hour she works is an hour in the enemy's ledger. The clerk who writes it counts slowly on purpose.
Action or decision: She signs the tithe book with her left hand, entering herself as labour toward her own clearance.
Emotional change: Grim satisfaction. The grinding of the volume begins and she is good at it.
Power or resource change: The first block of tithe hours entered. The crack-as-channel is used and stays hers.
Continuity facts to preserve: The wash-code log entry for the crack-as-channel already stands from ch 39; nothing new goes in the log in plain hand. Known to Du Hong, Mei Jiu, Qiu and the wash code, and to nobody else. **It is held for Volume 7 and is not used again in this volume.** The tithe pays off at ch 88.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: win with cost — the shift held, the lungs worse.
Next-chapter pull: Two men with a paper about effects are on the quarters stair.

## Chapter 65 — Bunk by Bunk

Chapter: 65
POV and location: Ning Yan. The crew quarters, eighth hour of the morning.
Immediate goal: Keep the log, the tablet and the county originals unsearched.
Resistance: Two inspectors with a sponsors' paper authorising examination of crew effects after the trial debris inventory; eleven watching crew; and the two friends' signatures already dry on the roster.
Weather and season: Hard frost. Breath in the room and frost on the inside of the shutters.
Major turn: The two friends stand up in front of eleven people and claim her shift aloud, which gives her a legal hour off the furnace floor to be at the Hall — and at the same hour the inspectors begin walking the bunks toward hers.
Information revealed: **A maintaining member's effects may not be examined without her consent, and consent may be withheld without a reason.** This is stated once here and never explained again. It is a consent right and has nothing to do with which shift she is working — the cover protects her labour, the consent protects her goods, and the two must not be confused.
Action or decision: She takes the hour and goes down to get the schedule certified, letting the cover stand. **She does not file anything today.**
Emotional change: Gratitude with teeth. She knows exactly what she is accepting, and she accepts it.
Power or resource change: None. Cover in force.
Continuity facts to preserve: She has been a maintaining member since ch 56 and this is the first time she uses the fact on her own behalf. The consent regulation is new and is stated once. Roster signatures already dry.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: earned hook — the search and the certification are the same hour.
Next-chapter pull: She stands on the regulation in front of two men who expected paper, not a person.

## Chapter 66 — The Inspectors' Paper

Chapter: 66
POV and location: Ning Yan. The crew quarters doorway and the Hall landing, tenth hour through midday.
Immediate goal: Refuse the examination without breaking the sponsorship.
Resistance: The inspectors' paper is genuine. Lu Kang's courtesy is absolute. The regulation is real and narrow, and standing on it moves the roster weight onto the two people who signed.
Weather and season: Frost lifting to a grey noon. Boots loud on frozen steps.
Major turn: She refuses the examination on the maintaining-member consent regulation, aloud, before witnesses — and the seal holds. The inspectors take the roster instead, because the roster is sect property and she is not, and the two who signed for her shift are doubled onto the settling floor for repair work through the thaw.
Information revealed: They did not come for the plate. One of the inspectors says the sponsors already have the plate described in their own hand, which tells her exactly what the clause in ch 62 is for and who wrote it.
Action or decision: **The batch's irreversible decision.** She holds the refusal knowing the roster consequence, to protect the tablet and the log. It cannot be undone. It costs two lives at ch 71. It closes the option of ever surrendering the plate quietly.
Emotional change: Adult cold. The ch 56 falsehood has grown into a decision that spends other people.
Power or resource change: Tablet and log safe. Roster weight moved off her and onto two people who did not agree to carry it.
Continuity facts to preserve: The plate is demanded by the sponsors' paper and denied by her sworn entry; neither is restated. The refusal is entered in the minute book and is retrievable in Volume 4. The inspectors' paper is uninvolved in the accident at ch 71 and must not be re-invoked after this chapter.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: decision — the worst right thing she has done.
Next-chapter pull: Hours counted, and a debt paper in the clerk's tray with a name she has not heard before.

Immediate goal: See the man's face without being seen to look at him.

Two of the three proofs, asked for out loud, in front of forty people. ## Chapter 69 — The Quota Table

Chapter: 69
POV and location: Ning Yan. The quota office anteroom, sixth hour of the morning through the long wait.
Immediate goal: Ask Shen Xiu for quota clearance toward the licence.
Resistance: He will not look at her, will not hurry, and is not cruel. The waiting room, the slow brush, the books that must balance before any name leaves them. His son's clerkship and his early report (ch 27, ch 34) sit unspoken between them.
Weather and season: Grey close weather, the thaw not yet on the mountain. Melt in the eaves and no sun anywhere in it.
Major turn: He does not refuse. He sets the figure: the tithe worked in full, the household owing nothing, the winter book square. Clearance when the books say so, and the books say spring.
Information revealed: Clearance is arithmetic and not favour. No plea moves it, no sponsor moves it, only hours move it. Institutions do not need persuading.
Action or decision: She accepts the arithmetic and goes back to the furnace. No plea and no scene — the volume's thesis enacted.
Emotional change: Something close to respect for a man she cannot forgive, which confuses her more than hatred would have.
Power or resource change: None. Clearance priced: the full tithe, and spring.
Continuity facts to preserve: Shen Xiu is tired, honest and never a villain. His ch 34 report is unforgiven and is not spoken aloud in this chapter. He does not apologise and does not explain himself.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: emotional consequence — the enemy is a ledger.
Next-chapter pull: The licence queue moves without her and a refusal arrives on paper.

## Chapter 71 — The Left Hand's Work

POV and location: Ning Yan. The furnace floor and the settling floor during thaw repairs, night shift into the second night hour.
Immediate goal: Hold her own shift while the two friends work their doubled repair shift above the slag channel.
Resistance: A short crew, a swollen channel, a foreman signing speed over shoring, and her own left-handed slowness. She can see that the beam is wrong and cannot get to it in time one-handed.
Weather and season: Thaw mud over frost. Steam, slag light, shouting through steam.
Major turn: A slag beam falls onto the doubled shift. Both friends die before the healer reaches the floor — under her roster, on her cover, in the room she refused to let examined.
Information revealed: None about the world. Everything about cost. A signed shift is a legal fact and a body both, and the two things are the same size.
Action or decision: She stays on the floor, holds the channel with the crack open, and keeps eleven other people alive through the night. Competence as atonement, and atonement does not accept it.
Emotional change: Grief without performance. The batch's temperature breaks.
Power or resource change: None. Two lives gone. The quarters sleep ten.
Continuity facts to preserve: Deaths by furnace accident under a falsified roster. No bounty involvement and no inspectors' involvement after ch 66. The beam is wrong because a foreman was short-handed — that part is ordinary and is not her doing; the roster is.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: loss — real, permanent, and hers.
Next-chapter pull: A burial, and the obligation to say what she did.

## Chapter 72 — Two Names in the Entry

Chapter: 72
POV and location: Ning Yan. The burial ground above the Lowwater road, then the Hall landing, from the burial hour to the ninth hour.
Immediate goal: Bury the two friends with their names said aloud, then go back to work.
Resistance: The sect buries furnace dead quickly and quietly, and saying causes aloud is not done. The two names go into a register, and a register is a document that will be read in four years.
Weather and season: First hard frost after the thaw. The ground workable and cold on top of it.
Major turn: She says both causes aloud at the burial — the trip to the Hall that made the cover necessary, and the refusal that moved the weight onto them — and does not let Yuan soften either. Then she enters both names in the burial register in her own hand, and carries the refused licence slip down and puts it into the tithe book as the reason she will be on the furnace floor in the morning.
Information revealed: The roster still carries a signature that is not hers, and somebody will read it. The register entry is the first document she has written that names her as a cause of anything.
Action or decision: She goes back to the furnace for the next block of tithe hours. Grief enacted as labour.
Emotional change: Grinding accepted as the form grief takes in this book.
Power or resource change: None. Condensing 1. Tithe continuing.
Continuity facts to preserve: Two friends dead by a roster accident she caused, causes stated aloud once and never softened afterwards. First filing refused at ch 70 and standing. The road is open for market traffic, so Lowwater access opens from ch 73.
Opening gate: n/a.
Ending type: emotional consequence — loss carried, not closed.
Next-chapter pull: Down the open road: Lowwater, the tower, and the ears that have started buying records.

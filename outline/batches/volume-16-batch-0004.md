# Volume 16, Batch 0004 — Chapters 946–960 — *the descent and the four hundred*

**Authority: `outline/volume-16.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline wins.
`outline/ending.md` §1 is the authority for the four hundred and is not re-decided here. **Written 15 Oct 2026 at
the Volume 16 planning review, out of the outline's own batch table and day table, to close a gap in the dispatch
chain: cards 0004–0007 did not exist and no phase had been given the job of writing them. This card fixes no
chapter, no day, no midpoint and no climax.****

## Batch goal

Get her off the mountain and put three hundred and ninety-nine people into her arithmetic, out loud, standing, in
front of each other. Fifteen chapters, and the loudest movement in the book is not an event. It is people.

## Starting condition

Day 3,084, the eleventh of the month beginning 3,074, the morning after the silence starts. **At the peak of Immortal
Vessel, shut.** Nineteen. Three fingers and a thumb. **Right arm open 790 days** and not going to close. Seven of
eleven holds. **1,525 days lighter at `D − 1,559`.** Open place behind the fourth rib, thumbnail-sized. Breath four
counts, seven on a rise, nine standing still. **A lamp she cannot refill.**

**He is in no chapter from here on and never again.** No bracket, no line of his, no figure of what is left of him,
no sentence anybody quotes. **The silence is the absence and it has a shape and the shape was named once, at ch 945,
and this batch may not name it again.** She has been at the top of that tower for a long time and has never once been
anywhere for a reason that was not the next day.

One person is in the Sisterhood, bound at ch 894 by her alone and told nothing, eight flights below her in the
doorway of the stair, carrying oil he makes and cannot buy. **She does not go up for him. He is not on this page until
the last chapter of it, and he is not told anything then either.**

## The pressure, with a date on it

**Four miles down a mountain and then out, on foot, in six chapters and sixteen days** — day 3,084 to day 3,099,
counted the way the volume counts the nine days of the fall, so sixteen inclusive. **It is the longest physical
movement in the book and the first time in seven hundred and eighty-three days that she has been anywhere for a
reason that was not the next day** — day 3,084 less the day 2,301 she has been in that room since, on the same series
the Batch 0001 prompt carries. **It is not four hundred and seventy-nine: that figure belongs to Volume 15 and is
about a different span, and a card that revives it is carrying a number from another book.**

**No card may state the height of the tower, the length of the descent, or a figure of how far Lowwater is, in any
unit in any direction.** No card may state a distance between anything above the line and anything below it. Four
miles is not hers to say; it is the outline's and it is not to be put in her mouth as a measurement.

The dated thing underneath it is **the first of 3,104 at ch 954**, a bundle comes up nine hundred and four steps
whether or not anybody is standing under it, **and it is the fifth first of this volume.** After ch 965 there is one
more, and then there are none at all.

## Batch midpoint (ch 953) — she asks the second one, out loud, in front of everybody

The first of the three hundred and ninety-nine goes fast and badly and she is bad at it, and the chapter is not a
failure. **At the midpoint she asks the second one in front of a crowd, which is a different act from asking a
stranger on a stair, and she does it standing and in the ordinary voice, and people watch her do it.** The first
person to agree is not a hero and does not understand what they have agreed to and says so. **Nobody is sent. Nobody
is entered. Nobody is commanded. There is no rank and no oath and no procession.**

**The Sisterhood is not a sect, an order, a church, a conspiracy, a chamber, or a school yet. It is four hundred
people in a woman's arithmetic.** A card that needs an institution gets a queue of people on a road in bad weather
and a woman who has to say the same sentence out loud until it stops being frightening.

## Batch climax (ch 960) — the four hundred

**The count is reached standing, in the ordinary voice, and the layer writes it, and it cannot be un-bound.** One was
bound in Volume 15 at ch 894; three hundred and ninety-nine are bound here, and this is not 399 chapters and the
volume does not become a list. **A small number are found on the page, named by her, with a face and a thing they
wanted and a reason they gave. The rest rise as a count she speaks aloud, and the count is a pressure, and the cost
is a body.**

**What she does not know, and does not say, and has never known: what the price is to her.** She has carried the
figure for sixteen volumes without one. She says out loud, once, in the plainest words she has, that she does not know
what it is costing her, **and that is the whole of her relationship with cost and no card may improve on it.**

## Irreversible step (ch 960) — the four hundred, said standing, written, and not un-bindable

**One count, said out loud by her, in the ordinary voice, standing, before it is a line anywhere — the fourth term,
for the three hundred and ninety-ninth time.** The record of the price of each is the layer's record and the layer
writes it. **Nobody has ever un-bound a person by wanting them less.** After this chapter the Sisterhood exists and
cannot be argued back down to one, and the sentence she needs at ch 975 is heavier than it was, and that is the point
of doing it now.

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman alone at the top of a tower with a lamp she cannot refill to **a woman who has said a
number out loud three hundred and ninety-nine times and has four hundred people in her arithmetic and no way to
explain to any of them what it will cost her.** **She does not get relief, she does not get a name for what she has
become, and no card may let her be glad.** From ch 946 she is the only person doing anything in the world, and the
card has to be built so the reader feels that before it is said.

Yuan is not in this batch. **A card that wants his weight has to use the silence, and the silence is not written, it
is the absence, and it may not be described as a feeling in a slot.**

## Required power changes

**None. Immortal Vessel 9, shut, throughout.** No stage, no number, no cuts, no announcement about her, no frame
scene, no eleventh frame anywhere. **One bracketed layer line is permitted, at ch 955**, and it is an ordinary report
of an ordinary thing. **The layer is still keeping the walls, correctly, and it is on this page and it has nothing to
do with what she is doing.**

## Required world reveals

1. **That binding is a person put into her arithmetic and kept there, and the price of each is on the record, and she
   does not know what the price is.**
2. **That three hundred and ninety-nine people said yes out loud, each one told what is on the record first, in the
   plainest words she has, before they answered.**
3. **That the layer records a Sisterhood without being asked to and goes on keeping the walls.**
4. **That the first of 3,104 came up nine hundred and four steps with nobody under it, for the fifth time in this
   volume and one of the last two there will be.**

## Open questions after the batch

What a sentence has to weigh when it is carried by four hundred people instead of one. Whether she can still get down
a mountain and out of the high country after three hundred and ninety-nine agreements. What the last bundle is, and
who carries it. And the thing nobody has asked in nineteen years and she has still not asked: whether nine hundred and
four steps is a distance or a count.

## What Batch 0005 must not inherit as clean prose

That the four hundred is a sect, or that anybody in it has a rank, an oath, a uniform or a name for what they are.
That anybody was sent, entered or commanded. That she knows the price. **She does not and said so out loud at ch 960.**
That the price was named, priced or quantified. That the four hundred were counted as an achievement. That the layer
objected, refused, bargained or was threatened. **It recorded it and went on keeping the walls, correctly.** That
anyone died. That the tower is on the page again. **It was left at ch 946 and no card may go back up it.** That the
silence has been described twice. **It was named once, at ch 945, and this is the whole of what the reader gets for
the fifty-five chapters that run from the descent to the last page.** That the first of 3,134 has happened. **It has
not: it is ch 965, it is the last bundle in the world, and the bearer does not know.** That the bracket budget is
spent: Batch 0001 spent two, Batch 0002 three, Batch 0003 one, this batch spends one at ch 955, **one remains in the
whole volume, at ch 971, and it is the last bracketed line in the manuscript.**

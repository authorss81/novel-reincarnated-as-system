# Volume 16, Batch 0006 — Chapters 976–988 — *the fall*

**Authority: `outline/volume-16.md`. This card does not re-plan the volume. Where they differ, the outline wins.
`outline/ending.md` §4 is the authority for the fall, for the ceiling coming off, and for the fact that nobody dies,
and none of it is re-decided here. **Written 15 Oct 2026 at the Volume 16 planning review, out of the outline's own
batch table and day table, to close a gap in the dispatch chain. This card fixes no chapter, no day, no midpoint and
no climax.****

## Batch goal

Watch the world come apart for nine days and keep everybody alive through it. Thirteen chapters, no bracket, no
announcement, no death, and a ceiling that does not come back.

## Starting condition

Day 3,164, **the first of a month and the first day of the fall**, the morning after she said it. **At the peak of
Immortal Vessel, shut.** Nineteen. Three fingers and a thumb. **Right arm open 870 days** and not going to close.
Seven of eleven holds. **1,605 days lighter at `D − 1,559`.** Open place behind the fourth rib, thumbnail-sized.
Breath four counts, seven on a rise, nine standing still.

**He is in no chapter in this batch.** No bracket, no line of his, no figure of what is left of him, no sentence
anybody quotes, **and no present-tense sentence in any mouth but his — which means no character in this batch is
allowed the present tense, and the reader's cue for who is talking is simply gone.**

**Four hundred people in her arithmetic. A lamp she cannot refill. A book of pages in her hand. The layer, obeying,
saying nothing, having said its last thing at ch 971.**

## The pressure, with a date on it

**A first of a month came up nine hundred and four steps and nothing was on it, and that is the first thing anybody
in the world knows.** Nine hundred years of a first with something on it, and the first one that is empty.

**Nine days: day 3,164 to day 3,172, ch 976 to ch 984, and then four more chapters after it.** Nobody dies. The
Nine Steps Terrace comes down because the terrace was a bound-marker and bound-markers do not survive, **and there
are people on and under it, and they get off.**

## Batch midpoint (ch 982) — the seventh day, and a county's worth of ground has changed

**Ch 982 is the seventh day, not the sixth: ch 976 is day one and one day is one chapter through to ch 984 on the
ninth.** The outline's day table carries the ordinal and this card is bound to it.

**The midpoint is not a discovery about the fall. It is that the ground has changed under a whole county and that
the people who were standing on it are standing somewhere else now, and that this happened while they were talking,
and that nobody in the county has a word for what has happened.** `outline/ending.md` §4 requires that cultivators held
at stage nine go past it in the following decade and **nobody writes down what happened to them, because there is
nothing left to write it down with** — and no card in this batch may show somebody going past nine, or announce it,
or put it in a report.

## Batch climax (ch 984) — the ninth day, and nobody dead

**The last day of the fall. The reader has been waiting nine days for a death and does not get one, and that is the
point the book has been building since ch 901 and the reason it does not feel like a letdown: nobody dies. Not the
boy, not the bearer, not one of the four hundred, not a cultivator in any of the nine days, and no card may put a
death in an offstage report either.**

**The cost of Volume 16 is real and it is not a body.** It is paid in one man dissolving at ch 930, in her own body,
in a terrace in rubble, in nineteen years, and in a silence that has a shape.

## Irreversible step (ch 988) — the ceiling is off

**Ch 988 is the last chapter of the batch and the hour after which nothing can put it back.** The ceiling is off and
every bound realm in the world is open at once. **No card may hint that anybody wants it back. Nobody in the world
wants it back, and that is not a triumph and it is not a reward, it is nine hundred years of a ceiling turning out to
be removable by one woman saying nine words.**

**After this chapter the layer's calendar stops being a calendar (D1).** Days go on and are counted the ordinary way
and no figure of the layer's calendar is available to anybody, her included. **She says so once, at ch 989, in
Batch 0007, and no card may have her compute it a second time.**

## Required character changes

Ning Yan goes from a woman who has just started something she cannot stop to **a woman who has watched it finish and
is still standing.** She is the strongest mortal in the world by an order of magnitude and she cannot ascend, because
there is nowhere to ascend to any more, **and no card may give her a stage, a number, a cut or a feeling about the
stage.** She does not get a reward, a recognition, a crowd or a name. **What she gets is nine days of work keeping
people alive, and it is not enough and it is not a failure and it is not a triumph.**

## Required power changes

**None, and the absence is the change.** **No bracketed layer line anywhere in this batch** — D3 puts the last one in
the manuscript at ch 971 and this batch opens the part of the book where there is nothing left to speak. No stage,
no number, no cuts, no announcement about her, no frame scene, no eleventh frame anywhere.

**Cultivators held at stage nine go past nine in the decade after, and nobody writes it down and nobody announces
it, and this batch may not show it happening on the page.** **No card may state a figure of how the layer worked,
merit, settlement, designation, column, readout, check or the tax: it is gone (D2), and whatever a card needs to know
about how the layer worked has to have been established before ch 976 or it does not exist.**

## Required world reveals

1. **That a ceiling can come off, and that it comes off over nine days and not all at once.**
2. **That the Nine Steps Terrace was a bound-marker and bound-markers do not survive, and that people were on and
   under it.**
3. **That nobody died.** The reader expects otherwise for nine days and is wrong, and being wrong is the promise the
   book made at ch 1.
4. **That the layer has stopped keeping anything**, and that what replaces it is not a better layer and is not a
   person and is not her.

## Open questions after the batch

Whether anything comes back. It does not, and the book does not promise that nothing ever will. What she does with
nineteen years. Whether the four hundred can be taught anything. What is left of the world that people will actually
miss. And the thing the next batch answers in the worst quiet in the novel: she has to teach, and she has never in
her life been taught by anybody.

## What Batch 0007 must not inherit as clean prose

That anybody died in the nine days. **Nobody did, and no card may imply it, including in a rumour or an offstage
report.** That anybody wants the ceiling back. That the layer was defeated, attacked, or fought. **It obeyed.** That
a cultivator was shown breaking stage nine on the page. That the calendar resumed, or that any figure of the layer's
system survived past ch 976 (D2). That Ning Wu forgave her. **He did not, `forgiv` is 1 in the whole volume and it is
hers at ch 1000 and not his.** That the fall was a victory. It was a thing she said and then had to live in. That the
four hundred were saved, or rewarded, or told what happened. That the terrace can be rebuilt — **it was a bound-marker
and it is rubble and the rubric does not permit rebuilding it.** That there is anything left of him. **There is not,
and the shape of the silence was named once, at ch 945, and is not named here.** That there is any bracketed line
left. **There is not: eight were spent, the last at ch 971, and the next bracketed line in this manuscript does not
exist.**

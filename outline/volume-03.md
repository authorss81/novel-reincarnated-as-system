# Volume 3 — The Hunter (chapters 119–178)

## Goal

> In this volume, Ning Yan must survive being hunted as an unauthorised System, or Yuan is dismantled and she is told she imagined him.

Fixed by `outline/series.md` §9. Not re-planned here.

## Central pressure

Not another winter. Volume 2 was claustrophobic, grinding, and ended with her legal for the first
time in her life. Volume 3 is the opposite shape: **open, lawful, and fast in the wrong places.**

Two things changed on the last page of Volume 2 and they change the shape of everything after it.
She is now **legal and licensed** — the county examines paper, so being hunted changes form: no man
needs to stop her on a road when a clerk can read her hours in order. And she is now **visible on
paper with a standing attached to it** — the eight hundred jin travels with the licence, so every
room she is allowed into is a room the claim is allowed into.

The antagonist is **Bai Zhu, the Hunter** (per `outline/series.md` §6 item 3): a field agent of the
Warden's office, sent down the Lowwater road to find and dismantle an unauthorised System. Polite,
patient, and he never lies to her. He does not hurry because he does not need to. The paper moves
at the quarter, the announcements move at the body's pace, and both of them move toward him.

## Starting state

- **Ning Yan:** seventeen, Condensing stage 5, announced at her own order (ch 114), capped there by
  her own hand. Licenced to Practise Past Registration under Clause Four, defect entered, six
  conditions on the back, the eight hundred jin standing claim among them as the sixth. Short breath
  two counts. Right arm open a third of the time. The fourth stage of her life on no paper in nine
  counties. Wears the Clay Tablet; log in the lining; iron token unwanted. Claim standing,
  unanswered, now on her licence. She does not forgive him.
- **Yuan:** Voice. Ledger held less than a day at ch 80 on her order and refused permanently the
  same day; not retaken since. Settlement a little under three-quarters, about six weeks. Four
  conditions hers (hours only; no Merit off her body; nothing decided about her body including its
  rate; no stage above five; he tells her on the day it is gone), three his (unchanged since the
  ninth step).
- **In-world date:** the first week of summer. **Day 0 is the last day of the thaw**, and every day in
  this volume is counted from it. Spring rain through the roofless Hall. The licence
  nailed crooked above the second bunk, going soft in the damp. Ten sleepers in twelve bunks. Feng's
  lamp burning down the mountain. A stranger at the first desk in Lowwater twice a week, buying
  nothing, giving his name to nobody, telling carriers he is not from the Bureau, asking what a
  furnace schedule is. Unnamed.
- **The return, and its two numbers (canon change 30 — do not re-derive, do not move).** Ch 116 fixes
  it: **four weeks off the last day of the thaw, the crate goes up on the day and not the one before,
  the working count is five weeks, and Sun He reads a return in the first week after it.** So the
  crate leaves the tower on **day 28**, which is ch 129 and the last day of the fourth week of summer,
  and the first return is **read in the fifth week of summer**. **Her five weeks, the number she has
  been counting by since ch 118, is the working count** — crate day plus Sun He's reading week — and is
  not a claim about the crate day. Batch 0001 runs day 2 to day 39.
- **Held objects:** the Clay Tablet (hers, lied about on the record); the log with the four county
  originals and the struck line that is not the word; the licence itself, with the filing copy worn
  soft in the same lining.

## Starting power level

- **Her:** Condensing stage 5 of 9, capped there. Cracked aperture, permanent. One arm at a third.
  No technique left that is written down anywhere except as habit.
- **His:** Voice. Can read her, only her, only near her. Cannot read past her skin except at her
  spoken order, at about four and a half weeks a holding.

## Major locations

The crew quarters at Qinghe and the second bunk; the furnace floor and the cold end; the roofless
Assembly Hall (paper only, never shelter); the fourth bridge and the Lowwater road; the market
square and the first desk; the copying floor inside Feng Lushi's tower; **the Bureau of Records'
county office on the square**; the paid-work lanes outside the crew (stone, oil, paper, water, and
one road-guard hire); the tower loft at the ninth-hour line.

**Geography rule, unchanged.** Lowwater is a county *and* the town under Qinghe Sect. The Bureau's
two Lowwater premises are different rooms: the copying floor inside Feng Lushi's tower, and the
county office on the square. Never merge them. **There is no Qinghe county office; Qinghe is a
sect.** Hanhui is a place eleven days down the road.

## Major factions

- **Bai Zhu, the Hunter** — one man, a Warden's writ, a room in Lowwater taken under a clerk's
  misspelling, and a calendar. The engine of the volume.
- **The Bureau's county office at Lowwater** — Registrar Sun He, nineteen years at that table. Stamps
  paper and copies everything upward to something it never names. It examined her paper once and
  found it excellent; it will examine her again.
- **The Lantern Register tower** — Feng Lushi, coughing, five in an unindexed column nobody reads,
  four county copies walled behind the fifth, one fair line at the back of a fair volume now going
  up the stair.
- **The three sponsors** — Lu Kang, Deng Chao, Pei An. Hold her seal, her schedule, and a dead writ.
  They sealed her once; everything past five needs them again.
- **Shen Xiu's quota office** — holds the winter book (closed square) and the only hand that can
  certify a second season of hours.
- **Hou Sanliang's counting room** — patient, unpaid, never met. Holds paper on three of the nine
  struck households. The claim's buyer, waiting for the paper to ripen.
- **The Lowwater Watch** — holds the barrier and the roster copies. Lodged and closed; do not reopen.
- **The paid-work hirers** — quarrymen, oilmen, carriers, and one escort contractor on the Lowwater
  road. They pay by the load and the day, and their sheets are the only other schedule in reach.

## Escalation sequence

1. She lives licensed for the first time: county hours, paid work outside the crew, a first return
   coming due in five weeks.
2. The stranger at the first desk buys his first thing: a certified copy of her schedule. He is
   right about the hole.
3. She works two schedules at once to earn a relicensing, and the sponsors have to seal the thing
   they lost.
4. The tower is audited from inside its own stair, and Feng is offered a Lung.
5. A listed kill on the escort road buys the channel eleven weeks, told-before-spent, at the cost of
   another sequence.
6. She is offered a true mercy in writing — handover instead of hunting — and reads the offerer to
   check it.
7. She refuses it aloud, before witnesses, and is entered as hunted in writing.
8. She climbs past the old ceiling on the new paper, suppresses once, announces twice, and the
   county reads it all in order at the winter return.

## Midpoint reversal

Chapter 150. Bai Zhu lays the handover paper on a table in front of two witnesses: the Bureau will
certify the voice as a misread natural echo, draw the designation down painlessly over one winter,
leave her licence and her stages standing, and answer the eight hundred jin out of its own purse.
Every term is real. He has never lied to her and he is not lying now — she checked, the day before,
through his own eyes, at the cost of four and a half weeks she named before she said the word.

She refuses it. She signs the refusal in her own hand, and the refusal is entered in a Warden's
book, and from that hour she is not a licensee under examination but a subject under hunting, in
writing, where no clerk can unwrite it.

**The irreversible decision of the volume is hers and it happens in the midpoint chapter:** she
chooses to be **hunted** rather than **handed over** (per `outline/series.md` §7 item 4). This
mirrors the Volume 1 midpoint (a promotion that is a locked room) and the Volume 2 midpoint (a
ceiling signed while refused) with a refusal that is a hunt: she had a true way out, verified true,
and closed it herself.

This turns the volume from *survive examination* into *survive hunting*.

## The relicensing — decided here

Both structural questions gathered in `state/current.md` (section *Volume 3 — the two open
structural questions*) are decided in this outline and nowhere else.

**Route: through the sponsors, put in front of her by the Hunter.** She does not ask for it. Bai
Zhu, needing a subject he can lawfully examine past stage five, tells Sun He that a licensee capped
at five cannot be examined past five; Sun He tells the sponsors that their sealed member's paper
stops where his examination stops. The sponsors must either surrender the seal or raise the ceiling.
They raise it — to Condensing 9, not to Foundation — because a member examined inside their own
schedule is the only thing on it that ever worked, and a member surrendered to a Warden's office is
a book opened.

**The three legs, second time down:** the sponsors' seal (Lu Kang, Deng Chao, Pei An, all three on
the same sheet, ch 131); a second quota clearance from Shen Xiu's office (the annex hours level
with the asked figure at the close of the season, ch 130); the county countersignature (Sun He,
ch 132). **The order is inverted from Volume 2 on purpose and must not be tidied back.** Volume 2
ran seal → clearance → countersignature. Here the clearance comes first because Shen Xiu's annex
figure has to be level before three men will put a seal on a ceiling above five: a member they
cannot account for in the winter book is the exact liability they have spent two volumes refusing
to create, and they know it because ch 69 put her hours in front of them. The seal then has
something to seal (ch 131), and Sun He has a document worth his stamp (ch 132). **A drafter who
"corrects" this order destroys the sponsors' motive and moves the Hunter's ch 126 purchase out of
causation.** The hours record is continuous from the same origin date as the first — the day the tithe
book opened — because ch 115 reads that requirement aloud and it holds for every application after
it. The paid-work hours outside the crew are annexed to the furnace schedule and certified by the
same Hall clerk under Lu Kang's stamp, which is why condition two can be amended without breaking
it: the annex is furnace-schedule hours by certification, not by place.

**Signature:** all three sponsors, Shen Xiu's clearance, Sun He's countersignature. No leg is
skipped and no signature is forged, borrowed, or worked around.

**Cost, all of it on the page:**

1. A new ceiling in her own hand at Condensing 9 (ch 131), defect re-entered, Foundation barred
   again without a second relicensing — which is Volume 4's engine, not this volume's. The
   relicensing buys a ceiling, not a body, and restores nothing.
2. The claim re-entered on the new licence as the last condition (Sun He's ch 115 precedent: a
   claim in a county book cannot be unlisted by a man stamping a different document, and he is
   required to enter it). The Hunter now holds fresh paper with her name, her annex address, and
   her standing in order.
3. Seven conditions on the new licence (the six amended, plus one: the annex hours are certified
   furnace hours and return with the quarter). Referenced after ch 132, never re-listed.
4. She surrenders use of the nine-household knowledge for the whole volume: nothing about the
   sponsors' quiet book is lodged, copied, or spoken anywhere in ch 119–178. The seal was bought
   with silence.
5. She works both schedules at once for a season with no surety — every county hour in person, in
   her own hand — and a missed hour is a gap that breaks the filing.

**What relicensing does not do:** it does not restore the aperture, remove the defect line, create a
surety, shorten the road, revive the sponsors' Foundation writ, or touch the fourth stage. The
count still reads one, two, three, five.

## Power progression: Condensing 5 → 9, then the petition

Real months, real recovery, no skipped breakthroughs. The volume runs **about six months** — first
week of summer to first week of winter — which is sixty chapters at the established rate, and every
interval below is stated against it.

**Every Condensing stage inside the bound is announced unless she orders it suppressed.** That is
canon and this volume runs on it. **No tribulation** — no trial is drawn in this shape of book.

- **Condensing 5 → 6 (ch 135).** Six weeks of double-schedule labour (furnace floor plus
  paid-work lanes) under the relicensed ceiling, sleeping in the quarters, arm at a third
  throughout. **Announced at her order.** The sentence carries the new ceiling the way ch 114
  carried the old one. Cost: the announcement fixes her annex address in nine counties, and Hou's
  counting room opens its book again.
- **Condensing 6 → 7 (ch 148).** Five weeks of escort-road work under licence scope, hired guard
  on the Lowwater road, built out of load and watching. **Announced at her order.** Cost: two weeks
  of fever and the short breath deepening to three counts on stairs, which never comes back.
- **Condensing 7 → 8 (ch 160).** Five weeks of winter-quarry carrying, grief worked the way ch 104
  worked it. **Suppressed at her order.** A suppressed stage reads as nothing having happened; the
  county sheet takes its second hole (one, two, three, five, six, seven, then nine). Cost: Sun He's
  precedent holds it as absence, but the Hunter's office reads absence as concealment — which is
  where he is right, and nothing stops it.
- **Condensing 8 → 9 (ch 171).** Five weeks under the winter return, working the cold end she is
  still capped from owning. **Announced at her order, witnessed by the crew.** The ceiling reached
  in her own hand. Cost: nine counties are told exactly where she stands a fortnight before the
  referral goes up the stair.

**Foundation is petitioned, not taken.** At ch 175 she files a Foundation petition on the relicensed
paper; it is barred without a second relicensing, and the second relicensing needs a schedule the
county does not keep past the boundary. Foundation 1 belongs to Volume 4. No stage is skipped and
none is drawn out of the weather.

### Yuan's track, and the economy that pays it

**He is at Voice until ch 150 and at Ledger-on-her-word after it.** `outline/series.md` §4 assigns
Ledger to Volumes 3–5. This volume makes it a standing authority, not a standing use:

- **Ch 119–149** he cannot read past her skin except at her spoken order, at about four and a half
  weeks a holding. The price is paid, never restated.
- **Ch 149** she breaks her own ch 118 term in public, on her word, with the price named before she
  says it: *read him.* He holds Ledger for **less than a day**, reads Bai Zhu through her eyes, and
  pays the spike. He refuses to hold it again the same day, permanently for the volume.
- **What the reading buys:** the Hunter's method in full — the eight remaining proofs listed in
  order, the first quarterly return already sent upward with her hours in it, and the handover
  paper verified true before it is offered. It does not buy the office name. It does not buy the
  eleven. It does not buy design.
- **From ch 150** Ledger is hers to order and his to hold only on her word — authority standing,
  use refused. Ledger as a held stage belongs to Volumes 4–5.

**Merit in this volume comes from one listed kill and no other.** At ch 140, on the escort road, a
bounty man working the entered act moves on a carrier's boy the way the ch 101 man moved on Du
Hong's file; she kills him with the Nine-Step habit, one working arm, and very nearly dies. Yuan
tells her before he spends it — what it costs, what it buys, out of her foundation. She says yes.
The tax takes another sequence: the escort-road pace she rebuilt at ch 135 is gone and cannot be
relearned. Nothing she loses comes back.

## The Hunter's schedule

He is patient and he does not lie. A patient professional is only frightening if the reader can see
his calendar. His reads:

- **Weeks 1–3 (ch 119–125).** At the first desk twice a week, market days, buys nothing. Asks what
  a furnace schedule is until a carrier's girl tells him it is a sheet. Reads public announcements
  only. **Refuses:** force, payment for bodies, the tower stair uninvited, meeting Hou Sanliang,
  entering the quarters. **Waiting for:** the first quarterly return.
- **Week 4 (ch 126).** Pays for the first thing in the volume: a certified copy of her schedule
  at the county office, coin on open wood, receipt kept. Reads the hole (one, two, three, five) and
  says so to Sun He's clerk, in daylight: the next stage must be announced or the paper fails its
  own terms. **He is right.** The reader has seen Sun He's ruling and knows the county reads absence;
  the reader also knows the Warden's office is a different reader, and nothing stops the second
  reading. **This is the batch 0001 midpoint** — it lands two thirds of the way through ch 119–132
  and it is the thing that puts the relicensing in motion. It is not a ch 131 beat: the purchase,
  being right about the hole, and the sponsors being told are one causal run, and the relicensing
  cannot begin until the man who made it available has gone back to his room.
- **Weeks 5–10 (ch 127–147).** Takes a room in Lowwater under a clerk's misspelling of a name that
  is not his. Serves Feng a lawful request for the stair's paper upward — which is what audits the
  tower, because a lawful request is answered in writing. Offers Feng the Lung after the audit finds
  the four copies: a Listener's appointment, a living wage, oil for the cough. Mercy with a hook.
- **Ch 149–150.** Lays the handover paper before two witnesses and waits three days for an answer.
  He does not hurry the answer. Hurry would be a lie about what it is.
- **Weeks 13–end (ch 151–178).** Examines her lawfully past the relicensing: attends both
  announcements from the square, buys nothing else, writes everything down. Refers the matter upward
  instead of dismantling inside the bound — a Warden's field agent does not unmake a System without
  a Wall writ, and he says so plainly. That refusal is why Volume 4 is *The Wall*.

**What he never does:** lie, threaten, touch her, enter her rooms, pay for force, or ask Feng for
the word. The one time he is asked whether he is Bureau, he says he is not, which is true.

## The audit and the Lung

Both planned here, neither spent before its chapter.

**The audit (ch 136–140).** The Hunter's lawful request for the stair's paper upward is answered by
Feng in writing, truly, because a keeper answers truly. The answer carries the intake line at the
ninth hour (unchanged, unaudited as a change — the request is for the stair, not the line), the
column at five, the four walled county copies, and the ch 117 fair line. The Bureau finds
concealment: four county copies walled behind the fifth item and never entered upward, one of them
struck on a request from an office whose name is not in any book the county keeps. **The reader sees
it coming from ch 117** — the line goes up the stair at the quarter — and nobody can stop a lawful
request being answered.

**What the audit costs him:** suspension from the stair for one quarter (a clerk carries the upward
bundle, not him); the four copies taken into the county office and read; his fair-hand line
re-entered truly upward, which puts her hours in order into the Hunter's hands. He keeps the tower,
the cough, and the column. He is not dismissed. Dismissal would end the observation, and the Bureau
does not end observations.

**The Lung (offered ch 141, taken ch 143).** A Listener's appointment: a living wage, oil and winter
coal for the cough, and a chair in the county office on reading days — in exchange for reporting
upward truly everything the stair carries. He takes it because the suspension leaves him with a
tower he cannot feed paper through and lungs that cannot stand another winter of copying cold. The
cost is the appointment itself: **from ch 143 every hour he copies for her goes upward truly,** and
the Hunter stops needing to ask. Feng survives, coughing less, writing more, owned. He dies in
Volume 11 taking a second Lung and writing down what he saw (per `bible/characters.md`); nothing of
that is spent here.

## The milestone and the Ledger break (ch 149–150)

Per `outline/series.md` §7 item 4, in Volume 3 she chooses to be **hunted** rather than **handed
over**. The shape of the decision:

- **Ch 149.** In the tower loft, alone, with the handover paper unopened on the mat: she orders the
  Ledger break. *Read him.* Price named (four and a half weeks out of about eight), told-before
  spent, yes said. He reads Bai Zhu through her eyes for less than a day — the counting-room list,
  the sent return, the handover terms verified line by line — and puts it down the same day. Her
  ch 118 term breaks in public: Feng carries the order down the stair as a tower change because she
  speaks it in front of him. That is the beat the outline promised: her breaking her own written
  term, in public, for something specific.
- **Ch 150.** She opens the paper, hears it read, asks the two questions a competent person asks of
  a mercy (who pays, and what happens to the thing in my head), gets true answers, and refuses it
  aloud. She signs the refusal. The refusal is entered. **The paper carries no agent's name** — it
  is signed by the Warden's office — and the clerk who enters the refusal writes in the name of the
  agent who laid it, which is how the stranger at the first desk is named in this chapter and not
  one chapter later on a document he never signs. **Nothing after this chapter can unwrite it:** he
  was never a rumour again. The non-forgiveness holds through both
  chapters — she does not thank Yuan for the reading and does not forgive him for the price of it.

## Volume climax (ch 169–175)

First snow on the Lowwater road. Stage nine announced at her order on the cold end, witnessed by a
crew that has stopped moving to the other end of the room. The winter return read in order at the
county office: one, two, three, five, six, seven, nine, with the two absences ruled and written
down the way Sun He writes things down. The Hunter attends, buys nothing, and certifies in a
Warden's hand what the paper now proves: the System is real, unauthorised, and carried by a
licensee in good standing — and he refers the dismantling upward to the Warden because a field
agent does not unmake a System inside the bound without a Wall writ.

**The irreversible decision was hers at ch 150** and it is paid here, not restated: hunted in
writing means the referral has a subject, and the subject is standing in the room.

## New question for Volume 4

Volume 4 is ***The Wall***, ch 179–238: *In this volume, Ning Yan must break the boundary, or
neither of them ever learns what they are.*

Volume 3 opens onto it out of the Hunter's referral and the relicensing's cost, and nothing else:
a Warden's office outside the bound now holds her name, her stages, and her hours **in order**,
certified by her own county — and the dismantling it has ordered can only be executed, and the
Foundation relicensing she has petitioned can only be countersigned, **past a boundary no county
paper crosses.** The question is not what the Wall is. The question is what a paper in order does
when the only office that can answer it is on the other side of one.

**Nothing Volume 4 owns is spent here:** no description of the Wall, no crossing, no geography
past the marker post, no Warden but his field agent's signature.

## Final chapter image (ch 178)

First week of winter. The licence — the second one, with seven conditions — nailed straight above
the second bunk, because she has two arms' worth of practice at nailing now and only one arm. Ten
sleepers. The tower lamp burning, carried this quarter by another hand. A referral gone up the
stair past every office she has ever stood in front of, to an office whose name he still cannot
read. She asks how much channel is left. He tells her the truth: about ten days. She does the
arithmetic against a calendar that has nothing left in it but a barred petition, and does not tell
him to stop.

## Pressure rotation across the volume

Examination and exposure (119–132), audit and mercy (133–148), hunting with a calendar (149–163),
cost and signature (164–178). No two consecutive batches run the same pressure, and **none of them
is Volume 2 again**: Volume 2 was confinement and paperwork, labour and grinding, pursuit and the
bounty, grief and the grant. The pursuit here is lawful hunting by an officer who never lies and
buys almost nothing — not a broker's proof-hunt. The paperwork here is paper that has already been
stamped twice and is now being read back.

## Hard rules for drafters (restated once, not twice)

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the
  present tense. No other character is ever in the present tense** — with the standing exception, set
  by ch 57, that statements about completed third-person events keep the past tense.
- At most one bracketed layer line per chapter, on its own line, in square brackets, never bold, never
  italics, never a heading. They announce; they never explain and they never advise.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or
  italic. **No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a
  paragraph; break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly 1,800–2,700 words, floor 1,300.
- **Close every speech.** `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` before finishing.
- No harem. **Nothing in Volume 3 is a romance.** Wei Ran does not appear before Volume 4.
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until Volume 16, by Ning Wu. Ning Yan never says her mother's
  name. Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before Volume 11, and does not learn she saw
  him as a readout before Volume 11 either.
- Do not re-explain known principles: the four seconds, the release rule, tax-takes-order,
  layer-is-editable, damage-as-technique, the eleven designations, the bounty, the six licence
  conditions, what a surety is for, the suppression arithmetic, the position-not-characters rule, the
  name in the wrong order, or the crew head count. **Walk the construction budget at the foot of
  `state/continuity.md` before writing and confirm nothing from it is reused as emphasis.**
- **Do not introduce a new final enemy.** The Scribe is fixed in `outline/ending.md` and is not
  improvised at the end of the run.
- **Volume 3 is hunted, not another winter.** It must be a different shape of pressure from every one
  of Volume 2's four.

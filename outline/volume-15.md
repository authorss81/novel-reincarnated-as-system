## Central pressure

**She sent a no down nine hundred and four steps to a thing that reads records, and the thing that reads has
answered — not to her, but to the voice in her head — and it has to speak to him through her, because she is
the only instrument it has. The moment it does that, she hears his half of every conversation for the rest of
the book.**

Volume 11 made her the person who knows. Volume 12 made her the person who has to be seen. Volume 13 made her
the person who is seen and entered and does not get to write it. Volume 14 made her the person who is offered
a keeper and refuses it. **Volume 15 makes her the person who can hear what is said to him and cannot be
kept out of it, and sends her up out of the only room she has had for four hundred and seventy-nine days to
find out who is speaking.**

`bible/themes.md` gives Volume 15 its temperature as **pursuit and theology** — and this outline takes both
literally and in order. **The first three batches are pursuit: a woman with three fingers and a thumb and one
lamp on a stair that does not stop at nine, going up, out of the light, after an answer. The last batch is
theology: the argument is had, and the thing she has reached is right, and that is worse.**

## What the volume must not be

Volume 14 ran temptation in one dark room. Volume 13 ran being small. Volume 12 ran hunger and cost.
Volume 11 ran dread and recognition. Volume 10 ran a list. Volume 9 ran being kept. Volume 8 ran seduction.

**Volume 15 is none of those, and none of its batches is a recruiting, a hiring, a wage-book, a debt, a family
paper, an audit, an examination, a hunt, a signature, a confinement, a second asking about a list, or a
second deciding look.** The look happened once at ch 776 and there is no second look, no appeal, and no card
spends one.

**The one thing Volume 15 must not be is Volume 8.** Volume 8 was an institution that wants her as an
instrument. **The Scribe does not want her for anything in Volume 15. It wants him. She is the wire.** That
is the volume's whole structural inversion and it is not negotiable in a card.

**Nor is it Volume 12.** Volume 12 was the road up and it cost her everything it took. **Volume 15 is not a
road and it is not an ascent in the realm sense: she does not take a stage by travelling and the stages are
not the point. She goes up a building.** No tribulation, no weather with opinions, no gate, no toll, no
monastery, no village that cheers or hides her.

**And it is not Volume 4.** Nobody in Volume 15 is hunted, and nobody runs, and there is no one sent after
her. The danger is a stair, a lamp and a building that does not stop at nine.

### Why the Scribe must use her to speak to him

**Stated once, in the outline, because three cards will want to invent a mechanism and only one is true.**
The announcement layer is one layer. Yuan is a fragment of the intelligence that runs it, lodged in the
announcement end. A fragment inside a layer is carried by that layer and cannot step outside it. **The only
instrument the layer has in the world is the Chosen One.** There is exactly one at a time and for fifteen
years it has been her, and she has never been told and has never asked.

So the address arrives in the only channel there is, and it arrives in her, and **she hears both sides**. The
horror is not that it is hostile. The horror is that it is the first voice she has ever known that talks to
him the way she talks to him, and it is polite, and it is correct, and it is offering.

## The ascent, and why it is up

**The whole of it, stated before any card spends it, because three cards will otherwise invent a road.**

She has never once been to where the layer's sentences are made. She has been reading them since she was
seven. She has asked, in the whole of the record, where a thing comes from before she uses it — and she has
never asked this one, and the reason is that the layer is not a thing anybody has a maker of, and she has been
in a room for four hundred and seventy-nine days where the layer is not present at all.

**The tower is the answer and it is not a discovery, it is a building.** A tower is a thing that is built
tall on purpose. She has stood at the top of eight flights and eight hundred and eighty steps for four
hundred and seventy-nine days, counted the stair, put her arithmetic out loud under a dead lamp, and never
once gone up, because there has never been a reason. **The reason arrives at ch 843 and it is four words
long: she has been reading the wrong end of it.**

**What is up there, in order, and the order is the volume:**

1. **The cells (ch 848, the third landing).** More rooms. A table twenty-two paces long in each, a cage lamp
   out in each, forty boards face up under the middle of each, a bundle corded twice under the far end of
   each, and **at the far side of each, between the twentieth pace and the end, a place of the length of a
   forearm and the breadth of a hand that nobody has ever sat in.** The whole of what she carried for a
   volume. **The finding is one sentence: her room is not a room. It is one of hundreds, and the reason hers
   was different is that somebody was in it.**
2. **The stair (ch 851–853).** It does not stop at nine. **Nine landings above her room and the stair keeps
   going**, and that is the volume's image, because fifteen volumes of this book live inside a world that
   counts to nine and stops, and this building does not stop.
3. **The making-place (ch 892).** At the top. Not a machine, not a throne, not a hall of mirrors. **A room
   with a table in it, and no cage lamp, and no wear, and a place at the far side with nothing worn at all —
   because nobody has ever needed keeping here. This is the other end of the trade. Her room is the receiving
   end and has been for four hundred years, and this is where the sentence is put together before it goes
   anywhere.**
4. **The wall that is not a wall (ch 878–885).** What the ceiling is for. Found, not told: she works it out
   out of her own arithmetic, over eleven days, because there is one figure she has never once been able to
   account for and it is the one at the head of her own column.
5. **The bargain (ch 893–899).** Put on the table in full and read out loud by her, in her own voice, in the
   ordinary voice, standing, because nothing in this volume is offered out loud by anybody.

## D-rules — the structural decisions this outline makes, and why

### D1 — No chapter of Volume 15 relights the cage lamp, and no store is replenished

The cage lamp is out and **no chapter relights it** — any chapter that lights it owes the reader an on-page
reason and a cost, and no card in this outline spends that. What lights Volume 15 is the boy's oil, and the
whole of Batch 0001's pressure is that **there is one of him, there is no oil in that building and there has
not been for nine years, and there is none on that mountain.** **No burn rate, no cups-per-day figure, no
hour the lamp runs out and no exhaustion date exists on the page and none may be invented** — that defect has
been repaired in this run three times and Volume 15 does not reintroduce it. His count is his and hers is
hers and neither is converted into the other.

### D2 — She does not count the stair above the room, and says why at ch 843

**Stated before any card spends it, and it is a decision and not a lapse.** She has counted everything for
nineteen years, including a stair she had walked four times, and at ch 839 she went down eight hundred and
eighty without counting and said what merit is and is not for. **On this stair she counts the landings and
not the steps**, and she says out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, at the first hour of the first day
she goes up, that a landing is a thing she can be wrong about and a stair is not, and that she is going up
it for the last time and does not want to arrive holding a number. **No card states a step count for it. No
card may.** The landings are countable and the count is nine and then the stair keeps going.

### D3 — The brackets come back, and there are three

**Volume 14 spent nothing in square brackets anywhere in sixty chapters**, because above the line the layer
is not in the room. **Volume 15 is the volume the layer is in**, and the bracket is the only honest signal
the reader has for the difference. **Budget: three in sixty, one in each of Batches 0001, 0002 and 0003, and
none at all in ch 886–900.** Each is an **announcement** — one sentence, the layer's plain report grammar,
never advice, never an explanation, never about anything the reader has not already seen happen. **No card
may put two in one chapter and no card may bracket the bargain or the binding or the count.**

### D4 — The offer is in writing and is read aloud by her, and nobody says it out loud

**Carried forward from Volume 14's D2 and unchanged.** Nobody in Volume 15 offers anybody anything out loud.
The bargain arrives on the making-place's own register at ch 893, **she reads every line of it out loud in the
ordinary voice, standing, before it is a line anywhere** (the fourth term), and it is read twice, because the
first reading of a bargain always finds conditions and the second never does — **that is ch 832's method and

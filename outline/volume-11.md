## Central pressure

**She has run out of books and there is one voice left she has never read.**

Volume 10 made her the person who asks instead of the person who is written, and it turned out that asking
closes. Volume 11 makes her the person who asks the one keeper who cannot close — because he is wrong-sided,
answerable, and hers — and it turns out that a voice that is asked what it was does not shut. It answers, and
the answer is older than her counting, and the counting was the answer all along.

**And the pressure is the only one she has never had.** In nine years she has refused documents, refused
readings, refused keepings, survived being written, and asked a list shut. **She has never once asked the voice
what it was before it started counting, because asking him was the one thing her hand never had to do while there
was any other book to ask.** There is no other book now. The list is shut. The stone is bare. The six leaves are
blank. The brother is alive and will not walk with her.

Nobody is offering her anything. Somebody is waiting to be asked, and he has been waiting eight years, and this
volume is the one where she finally does it. That is the whole change of air, and `bible/themes.md` gives
Volume 11 its temperature as **grief, then a name said out loud**.

## What the volume must not be

### What he does that is the volume's real damage, and it is his oldest habit yet

**He treats himself as an entry in order to stay findable by her, and he tells her he is doing it while he does
it.** That is the flaw stated once in the outline and once on the page: `bible/characters.md` — *he treats
people as entries… he lies to her by omission and always calls the omission a kindness.* **In Volume 9 he stopped
omitting and showed the price. In Volume 10 he named another name as an entry out loud. In Volume 11 he names
his own — Yuan Sheng, furnace-man's son, harvested at nineteen — in daylight, knowing she can now file him, and
she files him and stays.**

### How she catches all of it and permits all of it — pinned, and it is her own instrument

She does not catch him the way she caught the columns. **There is no second voice to read against his, because
no other voice has ever counted with her.** What she does instead is what she has done since the eighth of a
month ten volumes back: she counts. **She counts the days since the shutting, counts the coppers she cannot
count, counts the furnaces that went cold in a sect she has never stood in, counts the nineteen years of a boy
she never met — and when the count runs out she tells him to go on answering.** The permission is hers, out
loud, once, at the turn, at dusk.

### The frame scene — yes, Volume 11 takes one, and it is pinned

**Ch 644. Away from her hearing, in the layer's own reading-space, a man who is permanently on the wrong side of

## Hard rules for the Volume 11 drafters (restated once, not twice)

  and no other character is ever in a marked present frame** — with the standing ch 57 exception about completed
  third-person events.

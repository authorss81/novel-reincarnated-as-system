# Volume 2 — The Chosen One (chapters 59–118)

## Goal

> In this volume, **Ning Yan must earn the document that lets her advance at all, or she stays a servant with a god in her head for the rest of her life.**

Fixed by `outline/series.md` §9. Not re-planned here.

## Central pressure

Not another hall. Volume 1 was four months long, public, and ended with her the most
visible person in nine counties. Volume 2 is the opposite shape: **claustrophobic,
grinding, and containing a real loss.**

She is wintered on the mountain by ice, by a right arm that does not come back, by a
sponsorship owned by three men who want something, and by an eight hundred jin price on her
head that pays in body, place and name. She cannot practise legally, cannot leave legally,
and cannot be guarded, because a guard is a place. The rooms get smaller every batch: the
terrace, the crew quarters, the furnace floor, the settling floor, the tower loft, a
counting room.

The antagonist is **Shen Xiu and the quota machinery** (per `outline/series.md` §6).
Institutions are worse than men because they do not need persuading. Shen Xiu himself is
unchanged — tired, honest, never forgiven — and the machinery he operates does the harm
without his permission and without his malice.

## Starting state

- **Ning Yan:** seventeen, Condensing stage 1, aperture cracked through and stabilised
  as a mouth. Right arm out of function; about a third of it comes back and she has
  stopped testing it more than twice a day. Inner rank on paper only (signed ch 56),
  still in the second bunk of the furnace-crew quarters. Wears the Clay Tablet against
  her skin; log in the coat lining; maintaining members' iron token in the same coat,
  unwanted. The register of four counties reads *reconciled* for her brother, which is a
  lie she bought and carries. **Eight hundred jin stands on the Chosen One inside the
  bound**, proof defined as a body, a place and a name, not withdrawable by the
  designation.
- **Yuan:** Voice. Settlement past two-thirds — roughly four months of channel. Read once
  a day for six years by an office outside the bound whose name he cannot read. Ledger
  offered ch 54 and refused; he permanently lost the ability to read past her skin. The
  layer has a name for him, Yuan Sheng. He refused to finish describing the designation's
  purpose (ch 57) and was stopped for the first time by *not tonight* (ch 57).
- **In-world date:** winter, the ninth day of an eleven-day ash fall, first grey snow. The
  pass at the fourth bridge is iced shut and **the road opens for market on the third day
  after ch 58**. The fall ends two days after ch 58, so ch 60 is the last day and ch 61 is
  the first day of clear weather and also the day the road opens.
- **Held objects:** the Clay Tablet (hers, lied about on the record); the log with the
  four county originals; four copies of the county sheets sent to Lowwater with Du Hong
  (ch 58, no name on them); the second offer, logged word for word, untouched, held for
  Volume 4.

## Starting relationships

- **Employed, not forgiven.** Three terms stand unchanged: employed not forgiven; the
  work is her brother; nothing decided about her again without telling her first. He has
  broken the third term once (ch 54). She has stopped him once (ch 57).
- **Du Hong** knows she knows, and his file carries her hand in two places. He is the
  cheap door into her and he said so before she did.
- **The three sponsors** hold her rank, the Foundation writ and the plate clause, and have
  never once asked her a question.
- **Mei Jiu** gave an unwritten compression for nothing in ch 47 and has not been thanked.
  She returns in Volume 2.
- **The Lowwater Watch** owe her nothing and remember everything.

## Starting power level

- **Her:** Condensing stage 1 of 9. Cracked aperture, permanent. One dead arm. No
  technique left that is written down anywhere except in a laundry manual's back page, and
  then only as a habit.
- **His:** Voice. Can read her, only her, only near her. Cannot read past her skin.

## Major locations

The Nine Steps Terrace and the second bunk of the furnace-crew quarters; the furnace
floor and the settling floor beneath it; the records room beneath the furnace; the roofless
Assembly Hall (used for paper, never for shelter); the fourth bridge and the Lowwater
road; the Lowwater roundhouse and market square; the copying floor under the Lantern
Register tower; **the Bureau of Records' county office at Lowwater**; Hou Sanliang's
counting room (one scene only).

**Geography rule for this volume.** Lowwater is one of the nine counties *and* the town
under Qinghe Sect. The Bureau has two presences there and they are different rooms: the
copying floor inside Feng Lushi's tower, where county sheets are copied upward, and the
county office on the square, where licences are issued. Never merge them. **There is no
Qinghe county office; Qinghe is a sect.** **Hanhui is a place**, eleven days down the road —
never a firm, a house or a trading company.

## Major factions

- **The three sponsors** — Elders Lu Kang, Deng Chao, Pei An. Hold her inner rank, the
  Foundation-outside-the-schedule writ, the schedule of nine struck households over eleven
  years, and a clause **requiring a fired plate to be surrendered to them** — a clause she
  then had to lie about.
- **Shen Xiu's quota office** — holds the winter quota books for the Verge run and the
  only signature that can certify a household as owing nothing. Will not look at her. Will
  not hurry. Loses nothing by waiting.
- **The Bureau's county office at Lowwater** — mortal clerks under Registrar Sun He.
  Stamps paper and copies everything upward to something it never names.
- **The Lantern Register tower** — Feng Lushi, coughing, five items in an unindexed column
  nobody reads, and the county copies walled behind the fifth.
- **Hou Sanliang** — grain and core-stone broker **of Lowwater**, holding paper on three of
  the nine struck households. The one man inside the bound who can pool eight hundred jin
  without selling land. Polite, unhurried, never raises his voice. The engine of the
  bounty.
- **The Lowwater Watch** — Captain Lu Tao, Old Gao. They hold the barrier, the roster
  copies and the water cart, and they keep all three.
- **Mei Jiu** — fourth-rank inner, stage 8, and the only person on the mountain who knows
  how the county's schedule is actually kept.

## Escalation sequence

1. The pass closes. She winters where she is with a price on her head and cannot leave.
2. The licence is named. Three legs. She owns one, and the leg she owns is a leash.
3. She learns what the plate clause actually is: an article the sponsors' paper demands
   and that she has sworn in her own hand she does not have.
4. The road opens and a man is buying records instead of wordings.
5. She finds the price has a shape, and the shape is held by the same book the sponsors
   keep their struck households in.
6. The crew signs for her. The signature is a legal fact and also a body.
7. She refuses a search to protect the log, and the weight moves onto two people.
8. Shen Xiu sets a number instead of an answer, and the number is spring.
9. The paper is refused — and in the same hour she signs the condition that will cap her,
   because a refused application is cheaper than no application at all.
10. Two people die under her roster. She buries them and works the tithe.
11. He gets his reach back on purpose, once, and is frightened by it.
12. She kills a man to stop him killing another, and the killing pays for his life.
13. She works out alone that the designation is not a shelter. She does not forgive him.
14. The county countersigns. She is legal, capped, grieving, and it holds.

## Midpoint reversal

Chapter 88. She earns quota clearance — Shen Xiu signs it, looking past her, in a scene
with no cruelty in it — and walks into the Lowwater county office certain the licence is
hers. The registrar refuses it on the bounty standing. A licenced practitioner cannot stand
under an unanswered claim, and the claim can be answered only by body, place and name.

Then the registrar reads her the terms of the only thing the county will give her instead: a
**pending filing** — her hours, her stages and her defect on the county's own sheet, counted
from the day the tithe book opened, carrying a ceiling at Condensing stage 5 because that is
where a cracked aperture stops. She signs it in the same hour she is refused, because a
refused application is worth nothing and a pending one is worth everything except the
Foundation.

**The irreversible decision of the volume is hers and it happens in the midpoint chapter:**
she signs the ceiling down on herself, in her own hand, while being refused. The paper is
the leash, the lever and the room, all three, and she takes it anyway.

This mirrors the Volume 1 midpoint — a promotion that is a locked room — with an
institutional lock instead of a personal one, and it turns the volume from *earn the paper*
into *survive the price of the paper*.

## The document

The promise of the volume is a permit she has to earn. It is paper, it has an office, and
it has a clause number.

**The Licence to Practise Past Registration**, called on the mountain the *open-practice
licence*. Issued at the **Bureau of Records' county office at Lowwater**, under **Clause
Four of the Servant Advancement Settlement**: a servant-born who breaks in the open may
draw sect qi, take paid work, and be taught past her stage only under licence countersigned
at the county.

**Renumbering note:** Clause **Four**, not Nine. Chapter 41 already put a ninth clause in
this book — the assessed, four lines, nobody reads it. Two ninth clauses is a fault in a
novel whose engine is clause-reading.

Without it, any gain past Condensing stage 1 is unregistered practice. Realm walls do not
bend for talent, they bend for documents (`bible/power-system.md` rule 4). This is the
document they bend for.

**What stops the sponsorship being breached for thirty-seven chapters.** From ch 62 the
licence application is attached to the furnace schedule, and the **furnace schedule is sect
labour the county already reads.** Practice worked on the schedule is not unregistered
practice; it is the schedule producing the return the three sponsors signed for. Her tithe
hours in Shen Xiu's book are the same hours the county counts at the countersignature. The
three sponsors know exactly what she is doing and say nothing, because a servant-born who
advances inside their own schedule is the only thing on it that ever worked.

**The three legs,** demonstrated by event and never explained twice:

1. **The sponsors' seal** (have, ch 62) — three men, on paper they own.
2. **Quota clearance** (ch 88) — Shen Xiu certifies the household owes nothing and the
   winter book is square. Arithmetic, not favour.
3. **The county countersignature** (ch 115) — Registrar Sun He, at Lowwater.

**What it costs her:**

1. **Four months of furnace-crew labour tithe**, entered in Shen Xiu's book in hours by the
   enemy's clerk. Four months, not six — the volume is four months long and the price is
   paid on the page across it.
2. **A living surety** — a name in the county book standing for her attendance at every
   county hour. Du Hong stands it at the second filing. His death voids it and the debt
   transfers to her.
3. **Her defect, entered in her own hand on the county sheet at ch 88:** *aperture cracked
   through; stabilised at the crack; permanent.* The licence stops at **Condensing stage
   5** and bars any Foundation petition without relicensing.

**The cap is real, and the Foundation writ dies with it.** The sponsors' writ (ch 48:
Foundation within two years, outside the ordinary schedule, on their own signature) reaches
Foundation only through a licence. Without one, Foundation on that writ is unregistered
practice under a schedule it does not appear on, which is a breach of the sponsorship that
issued it. She works this out at ch 88. The writ was never a shortcut; it was a leash with a
better room in it.

## The three moments she goes after it

1. **Preparation and sealing (ch 62, ch 65).** The sponsors affix their seal to the
   application, which enters her name in the county queue and is therefore a public fact.
   The legal hour she takes at ch 65 is spent getting the schedule certified, not filing
   anything. **She files once.**
2. **First filing (ch 70).** At Qinghe, on the sponsors' seal alone, before the thaw.
   Refused for want of quota clearance. She learns the licence is a three-legged thing and
   she owns one leg.
3. **Second filing (ch 88) and the pending filing.** At Lowwater, with clearance earned and
   Du Hong standing surety. Refused for want of standing — and then offered the pending
   filing, which she signs. The volume's midpoint and its irreversible decision.
4. **Final grant (ch 115).** With the bounty attempt failed on proof and the surety dead,
   the pending filing is countersigned by Registrar Sun He with the furnace schedule
   attached as collateral labour. Feng Lushi witnesses it. She walks out licenced to
   Condensing stage 5, capped there, grieving, and legal.

## Power progression: Condensing 1 → 5

Real months, real recovery, no skipped breakthroughs. The volume runs **about four
months** — late winter into spring rain — which is the four months of channel Yuan said he
had left. Volume 1 ran 58 chapters in four months; this runs 60 in four, so the rate is
held at about 3.5 chapters to the week and every interval below is stated against it.

**Every Condensing stage inside the bound is announced to the whole bound.** This is canon
(`bible/power-system.md`; `state/continuity.md` world rule 3) and Volume 2 runs on it
rather than around it. **There is no tribulation.** Ch 52 asserted that every advancement
inside the bound carries one; that was a generalisation by a man who had watched one, and
her stages five through nine in ch 39 all went without. No stage in this volume is drawn
out of the weather.

That makes each stage a decision rather than a scene:

- **Condensing 1 → 2 (ch 78).** Five and a half weeks of one-handed furnace tithe,
  left-hand forms rebuilt from the Nine-Step habit, crack-channel practice in the cold.
  **He lets the announcement go.** Four months after the bound learned she existed, it is
  told again what she has become, and every man buying proof now has a number to buy
  against. Cost: two weeks of fever and a month of short breath that never fully leaves.
- **Condensing 2 → 3 (ch 92).** Four weeks of Lowwater carrying work under licence
  application, sleeping in the tower loft. Built out of load, as stage 8 was. Her arm
  settles at a third of the time and stays there. **He lets it go again, at her order.**
  Cost: the announcement is the first thing in four months to tell a market where she
  sleeps.
- **Condensing 3 → 4 (ch 104).** Begun in the weeks after Du Hong's burial. Grief training
  on the ash-flats in thaw mud. **She tells him to suppress it.** A suppressed stage reads
  as nothing having happened, and nothing having happened voids the pending filing, because
  the filing is worth nothing without a continuous record of hours and stages. She chooses
  to lose the paper for one stage and to keep it for the next four. Her third term is
  honoured in the correct order: he asks first, and asks properly. Cost: she stops eating
  for nine days and Yuan cannot make her eat.
- **Condensing 4 → 5 (ch 114).** Three weeks under the pending filing, working the furnace
  floor she is capped from ever owning. **She tells him to let it go.** The filing needs
  the stage recorded, and being legal is the entire point of the volume. For the first
  time she is glad of the noise. Cost: the announcement tells nine counties where she is
  before the ink is dry on her own signature.

### Yuan's track, and the economy that pays it

**He is at Voice for the whole volume.** `outline/series.md` §4 assigns Ledger to Volumes
3–5. He takes it back **once, on purpose, at her spoken order**, and that one exception is
deliberate and recorded here so no drafter treats the band as broken:

- **Ch 68** he states the price of taking it and she does not order it.
- **Ch 80** she orders it: *read him.* He holds Ledger for **less than a day**, reads Hou
  Sanliang's man through her eyes, and pays a spike past nine-tenths. He refuses to hold it
  again the same day, permanently, and ends the volume able to take it at her word and
  bound by her three terms not to. Ledger as a standing stage belongs to Volumes 3–5.

**The Merit source is the kill, and there is no other.** Merit settles when something the
layer has listed is killed or a listed trial is cleared. Volume 2 has **no trial** — Volume
1 spent that shape and this volume is explicitly not another hall.

The listing exists before the kill and is not invented at it: the eight hundred jin is an
**entered act against the Chosen One**, made by the layer in four counties, and a man
working an entered act is working inside it. Hou's man is on the layer's list the moment he
takes the claim up, and Ning Yan is the one who reads that fact off the announcement at
ch 68 and files it.

At the moment the Merit settles, Yuan tells her **before** he spends it — what it costs, what
it buys, and that it comes out of her foundation. She says yes. This is her third term paid
in full, four months late, and the first time in the book he asks instead of acting. The tax
takes **sequence, not substance**: a left-hand sequence she rebuilt at ch 78 is gone and
cannot be relearned. Practice rebuilds substance and cannot rebuild order. Nothing she loses
comes back.

**The Settlement clock.** One row per row, and it is checkable against the 3.5-chapters-a-week
rate established above. Channel is stated in weeks of ordinary use remaining:

| Point | Settlement | Channel left | Cause |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ch 58, end Vol 1 | past two-thirds (~0.67) | ~17 weeks (four months) | five rises, two falls, ch 54 |
| Ch 80 | past nine-tenths (~0.91) | ~6½ weeks | Ledger retake, held under a day — costs ~4½ weeks |
| Ch 100 | unchanged (~0.91) | **about five days** | twenty chapters of ordinary use since ch 80 |
| Ch 101 | back to roughly two-thirds (~0.68) | ~11 weeks | Merit from the listed kill, spent |
| Ch 118 | roughly three-quarters (~0.75) | ~6 weeks | seventeen chapters of ordinary use |

**Without the kill at ch 100 he is dry inside ch 101.** The margin he bought at ch 80 is
about five days. This is the volume's quiet engine and only a killing stops it.

## The loss

Two deaths are written here before drafting, with mechanism and her agency in both.
Feng Lushi survives and is **not** resolved.

**The two furnace-crew friends (ch 71–72, batch 0006 climax).** The unnamed pair made in
ch 32 and seen again in ch 47 cover her absence on the work roster while she is at the Hall.
Inspectors from the sponsors' faction come for the log — not the plate, which their own
paper already demands. She refuses the examination on the regulation that a maintaining
member's consent is required and may be withheld without reason, and she withholds it
aloud, before witnesses. The seal holds. The inspectors take the roster instead, because the
roster is sect property and she is not: the two who signed for her shift are doubled onto
the settling floor for repair work through the thaw.

A slag beam falls. Both die before the healer reaches the floor. Mechanism: a furnace
accident under a falsified roster — the beam is wrong because a foreman was short-handed,
which is an ordinary thing and not her doing. Agency: she chose the trip that made the cover
necessary, and she chose the refusal that moved the weight onto them. She names both causes
aloud at the burial and does not let Yuan soften either.

**Du Hong (ch 102–103, batch 0008 climax).** She lists the causes at his burial and they are
all hers:

1. She put her hand in his file (ch 56).
2. She sent him down the Lowwater road with her copies in his coat (ch 58).
3. She let him stand surety for her licence (ch 88).
4. She killed one of them when she came down the road, and it was the killing that finished
   it.

Hou Sanliang's men stop him on the **Lowwater road at the foot of the Lantern Register
tower** — not at the pass, which is four miles above and iced; one location, not two. He
carries her hand in his file and her copies in his coat. He gives them his own name instead
of hers and burns one sheet, and refuses the place. They beat him for the place and leave him
in the thaw water at the tower's foot. She arrives and kills the second man with the
Nine-Step, which is a habit and not a technique and is the only thing left to her. He dies of
it before the night is out with Feng holding the lamp.

The proof fails on the body, so the bounty stands unclaimed and the price holds into Volume 3.
Every paper that made him worth stopping has her hand on it.

**What she does not use.** She does not open the aperture as a channel to do it. That
technique is hers and unshared and it is held for Volume 7 against the Hunter
(`state/continuity.md`). The kill is done with a laundry manual's back page remembered in
her legs, one working arm, at Condensing stage 1, and it very nearly kills her.

**Feng Lushi survives Volume 2 and is not resolved in it.** He receives the four county
copies on market day (ch 63) and walls them in the tower behind the fifth item rather than
entering them upward; the unindexed column stays at **five**. He coughs, worse. He copies
the shape of the characters on the sealed slip at ch 110 without reading them and strikes his
copy while keeping the memory. He witnesses the countersignature at ch 115 and writes to the
last page of the volume.

**No Bureau audit of him in Volume 2.** The four upward copies make him a traitor in Volume
3 and the audit belongs there. **The Bureau's offered Lung is not offered and not refused in
Volume 2.** He takes one in Volume 3 (`bible/characters.md`) and the taking is that volume's
beat. Drafters must not spend either here.

## The bounty arc (ch 68–118)

The ch 55 claim — eight hundred jin, proof as body, place and name, priced at a
furnace-crew's year so that somebody inside the bound could afford it. The somebody is **Hou
Sanliang**, a Lowwater grain and core-stone broker holding paper on three of the nine struck
households.

The ch 55 inference — that the price was either set by machinery or entered by somebody who
knew who could pay — is **spent and is not argued again**. What ch 67 adds is a name and a
document, not a restatement: Hou's name is on a debt paper in the tithe clerk's tray, and the
striking dates on his three struck households match the sponsors' eleven-year schedule
exactly. The sponsors' quiet book and the broker's paper are the same book.

- **Ch 63:** the road opens. The ears were established in ch 55 and 58 and are **not**
  re-revealed. What is new is that they have moved on from wordings to **records**.
- **Ch 67:** Hou's name, and the striking dates that line up with the sponsors' schedule.
- **Ch 68:** he works the proof-hunt in daylight — the place where she sleeps and the name on
  her carriers' papers. Two of three proofs, asked for politely.
- **Ch 88:** the bounty blocks the second filing.
- **Ch 100–103:** the attempt. They take the cheap door, as Du Hong himself predicted in
  ch 55. She kills one of them. He dies anyway. The claim stands, unclaimed, into Volume 3 —
  where the Hunter will use it as a map.

## The eleven designations (ch 96, ch 110)

Volume 2 opens the door one hand's width and closes it again. It opens **on paper**, not
through Yuan's sight — he cannot see past a seam (`ch 57`) and must not be given the power
here.

- **Ch 96:** the tower's volumes carry a status column. Eleven entries across six hundred
  years, each with a status word, each in a copying hand nobody living uses. The nearest one
  before hers says *kiln-born*. Lowwater's county sheet of her own advancement carries the
  same word in a second position that no other county's sheet has; of the four hand-copies
  in the tower, one carries it. It is the first new fact about the eleven, and it is on
  paper. She speaks the word to Yuan once. He does not recognise it. He has no word for it,
  and says so.
- **Ch 110:** a sealed slip comes up from the county office at Lowwater on ordinary Bureau
  stationery, carrying a request from outside the bound that the word be struck wherever
  copied. The office attached to the request **has a name and Yuan cannot read it.** Feng
  copies the shape of the characters without reading them, strikes his copy, and keeps the
  memory. The door closes.

**Two boundaries on this.** The slip does **not** wear the unrecognised Bureau hand from
ch 36 — Feng recognising that hand is a Volume 5 payoff and must stay unspent. And nothing
here answers what became of the eleven; that is deliberately unexplained for the whole run.

## The partnership lie (relationship milestone 3)

Per `bible/characters.md` and `outline/series.md` §7: in Volume 2 he lies about what she
is; she works it out alone and does not forgive him for a year.

**The lie is not a reversal of ch 57.** He told her on the ninth step that the designation is
a *writ* and not a reward, and he does not unsay it. The lie is an **exaggeration of her own
ch 55 conclusion** — which is how he lies to her, and always has: he takes the half-truth she
has already drawn and hardens it into a floor.

- **Ch 92–94**, after the Ledger retake and before the loss: he tells her that being Chosen
  is the reason the eight hundred jin cannot take her quietly. His evidence is not the claim —
  the claim is the first in the bound's history and she knows that better than he does — but
  the eleven. Six hundred years, eleven of them, every one a body the bound had to account
  for, and **the designation is the reason there is a record of any of them at all.** A
  record is why a person cannot be taken without one. The protection and the danger are the
  same line; he gives her that sentence and tells her it is the safe side of it.
- **Ch 100**, in the tower loft, alone, with the tower volumes and the tax sequence in front
  of her: she works out that the designation does not protect her, it **prices** her. The
  eleven were spaced irregularly because they were *spent at different rates*, not found at
  different times. And this is not a new cost — it is the arithmetic of an old one. Every
  quest the layer honours her is answered out of the same foundation the tax already draws
  from, so being Chosen does not add a cost. It adds occasions for an existing one. The
  thing he called a shelter is the thing that makes her cheaper.

She tells him she worked it out. She sets no new rule — the three stand — and she does not
forgive him. **The non-forgiveness runs from ch 100 through Volume 3 and into the opening of
Volume 4.** That is the year the milestone requires; Volume 2 alone is four months and
Volume 3 is four, and eight months is not a year.

**Volume 11 keeps its answer.** What she works out in ch 100 is *her position in the pattern*
— that Chosen Ones are used up and she is next in a queue she cannot see the end of. She
does **not** conclude that the Foundation tax was designed by anybody. The design is the
Volume 11 origin answer (`outline/series.md` §3) and is not spent here.

## Volume climax (ch 112–116)

Spring rain on the roofless Hall. The bounty attempt has failed on proof; the surety is dead
and the debt is hers; the request from outside the bound has been answered inside it.

She stands the pending filing up. The countersignature is a formality and the formality is
the horror: the county does not examine her, it examines the paper, because that is what a
county office does, and the paper is excellent. Registrar Sun He countersigns the Licence to
Practise Past Registration to Condensing stage 5. Feng witnesses it.

**The irreversible decision was hers and she made it at ch 88**, signing the ceiling down on
herself — capped at stage 5, barred from Foundation without relicensing, the sponsors' writ
dead on the same page — to be allowed to climb at all. She paid for legality with the top of
her own future, in ink, in front of witnesses, and has spent four months finding out whether
it was worth it.

## Concrete resolution

- **Licence granted (ch 115)**, capped at Condensing stage 5, defect entered in her own hand
  at ch 88. She is a legal practitioner for the first time in her life.
- **Condensing stage 5 reached (ch 114)**, announced to the bound at her own order,
  witnessed by the crew.
- **Du Hong dead and buried (ch 103)**; his file carries her hand forever and the bounty men
  know the cheap door is gone.
- **Two crew friends dead and buried (ch 72)**; the quarters sleep ten instead of twelve.
- **Hou Sanliang unpaid and patient.** The eight-hundred-jin claim stands into Volume 3.
- ***kiln-born*** struck from paper, held in two heads, the office name still unreadable.
- **Feng alive, coughing, writing, the column at five, unaudited, no Lung offered.**
- **Shen Xiu unchanged, unthanked, still holding the quota books.**
- **The three sponsors** hold a Foundation writ that the licence has just killed, and know
  it, and say nothing.
- **Settlement at roughly three-quarters; about six weeks of channel.** The clock runs into
  Volume 3.
- **The register still reads *reconciled*.** The truth is unchanged. The plate is still
  against her skin.

## New question for Volume 3

A field agent of the Warden's office is coming down the Lowwater road asking after
unauthorised Systems, politely, patiently, and without ever lying. In the last chapter Feng
hears a second cough in the tower stairwell that is not his. The Hunter has arrived. **Bai
Zhu is not named in Volume 2.**

## Final chapter image (ch 118)

Spring rain falling through the roofless Hall onto the furnace floor schedule. The licence
nailed above her second bunk, the defect line going soft in the damp. Ten sleepers instead
of twelve. Her right hand open and empty a third of the time. Feng's tower lamp burning down
the mountain.

She does not ask what it is counting. She asked that on the sixth step in Volume 1, it
counted him, and the question is spent.

She asks how much channel he has left, because that is the number she can use. He tells her
the truth: about six weeks. She does the arithmetic against the calendar and finds that six
weeks is less time than the licence has conditions in it. She does not tell him to stop. She
tells him what the conditions are.

## Pressure rotation across the volume

Confinement and paperwork (59–72), labour and grinding (73–88), pursuit and the bounty
(89–103), grief and the grant (104–118). No two consecutive batches run the same pressure,

## Hard rules for drafters (restated once, not twice)

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written
  in the present tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense.
- At most one bracketed layer line per chapter, on its own line. They announce; they never
  explain and never advise. There is no breakthrough in batch 0006, so the layer's presence
  in those fourteen chapters is a matter of judgment, not of entitlement.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold
  or italic. No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two to four
  sentences per paragraph; break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly
  1,800–2,700 words.
- **Close every speech.** `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` before finishing. If a speech
  runs over a paragraph, the first paragraph ends with a closing quote and the next opens a
  fresh one. Narration never sits inside an open quote.
- The announcement wording is fixed (ch 53) and is quoted word for word in ch 1000:
  *Assembly Hall of Qinghe. Ning Yan of the Qinghe furnace crew, servant-born. What was held
  is let go. What was taken is held.*
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until Volume 16, by Ning Wu. Ning Yan never says her
  mother's name. Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before Volume 11, and does
  not learn she saw him as a readout before Volume 11 either.
- No harem. Nothing in Volume 2 is a romance. Wei Ran does not appear before Volume 3.
- Do not re-explain known principles: the four seconds, the release rule, tax-takes-order,
  layer-is-editable, damage-as-technique, the eleven designations, or the bounty. Watch the
  spent constructions listed in `state/continuity.md`.
- Volume 2 is grinding, claustrophobic, a real loss. Do not write another Volume 1.
- **Every stage is announced to the whole bound by default.** The one exception in this
  volume is ch 104, where she orders it suppressed — and a suppressed stage reads as nothing
  having happened. Do not soften the default, and do not let the exception spread.

## First batch: Volume 2, Batch 0006 — chapters 59–72

Full batch cards are maintained in `outline/batches/volume-02-batch-0006.md`.

### Batch scope

Fourteen chapters, one dramatic unit.

**Batch goal.** Trapped on the mountain by ice with a price on her head, Ning Yan files for
the only paper that lets her practise at all, and by the end of the batch she has the name of
the document, two of its three legs, and two funerals she caused.

**Starting condition.** Ch 58, ninth day of an eleven-day ash fall, the pass at the fourth
bridge iced shut, her four county copies going down the road in Du Hong's coat, Condensing
stage 1, one arm.

**Starting relationships.** Employed not forgiven, three terms, one of them broken in ch 54
and one of them tested in ch 57. Du Hong on the road with her hand in his file. Three
sponsors she has never once asked a question. Mei Jiu owed a thank she has not said.

**Batch midpoint (ch 65–66).** The sponsors' inspectors come for her papers. She refuses the
examination on the maintaining-member consent regulation, aloud and before witnesses. The
inspectors take the roster instead. The two friends who signed for her shift are doubled
onto the settling floor.

**Batch climax (ch 71–72).** The two friends die under a falsified roster in a room she knows
is unsafe. She names both causes aloud at the burial — the trip to the Hall and the refusal —
and does not let Yuan soften either.

**Required character changes.** She spends two other people's lives on a decision that was
hers to make, says so out loud where it cannot be softened, and keeps working. Nobody forgives
her and she does not ask to be.

**Required power changes.** **None.** Condensing stage 1 for all fourteen chapters. The
batch is about paper, ice, and the arm that does not come back.

**Required world reveals.** The licence exists and has a name, an office, a clause number and
three legs. The sponsors' plate clause is an article their paper demands and that she has
sworn she does not have. Quota clearance is arithmetic and the arithmetic says spring. The
eight hundred jin has a buyer who is local, can afford it without selling land, and keeps his
paper in the same book the sponsors keep theirs in.

**Open questions after the batch.** Two must be live going into ch 73: what does Shen Xiu's
clearance actually cost in hours, and who is buying proof in Lowwater now that the road is
open.

**Action and deadline requirement.** The fourth-bridge ice and the market-day road window
(ch 59, ch 61, ch 63); the inspection (ch 65–66); the furnace accident (ch 71).

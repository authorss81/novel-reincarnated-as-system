# Volume 2 — The Chosen One (chapters 59–118)

## Goal

> In this volume, **Ning Yan must earn the document that lets her advance at all, or she stays a servant with a god in her head for the rest of her life.**

This sentence is fixed by `outline/series.md` and is not re-planned here.

## Central pressure

Not another hall. Volume 1 was four months long, public, and ended with her the most
visible person in nine counties. Volume 2 is the opposite shape: **claustrophobic,
grinding, and containing a real loss.**

She is trapped on the mountain by winter, by a dead right arm, by a sponsorship owned
by three men who want something, and by an eight-hundred-jin price on her head that
pays in body, place and name. She cannot practise legally, cannot leave legally, and
cannot be guarded, because a guard is a place. Every chapter takes place within a
smaller room than the last until the spring thaw: the terrace, the crew quarters, the
furnace floor, the Lowwater road, the tower. The temperature of the volume is set by
`bible/themes.md`: grinding, claustrophobic, a real loss.

The antagonist of the volume is **Shen Xiu and the quota system** (per
`outline/series.md` §6): institutions are worse than men, because they do not need
persuading. Shen Xiu himself remains what he was — tired, honest, never forgiven —
and the machinery he operates does the harm without his permission and without his
malice.

## Starting state

- **Ning Yan:** seventeen, Condensing stage 1, aperture cracked through and stabilised
  as a mouth. Right arm out of function; about a third of it comes back and she has
  stopped testing it more than twice a day. Inner rank on paper only (signed ch 56);
  still in the second bunk of the furnace-crew quarters. Wears the Clay Tablet against
  her skin; log in the coat lining; maintaining members' iron token in the same coat,
  unwanted. The register of four counties reads *reconciled* for her brother, which is
  a lie she bought and carries. **A bounty of eight hundred jin stands on the Chosen
  One inside the bound**, proof defined as a body, a place and a name, and it cannot
  be withdrawn by the designation.
- **Yuan:** Voice. Settlement past two-thirds — roughly four months of channel left.
  Read once a day for six years by an office outside the bound whose name he cannot
  read. Ledger offered in ch 54 and **refused**; he permanently lost the ability to
  read past her skin. The layer has a name for him, Yuan Sheng. He refused to finish
  describing the designation's purpose (ch 57) and was stopped for the first time by
  *not tonight* (ch 57). She keeps him on three terms: employed not forgiven; the work
  is her brother; nothing decided about her again without telling her first.
- **In-world date:** winter, ninth day of the eleven-day ash fall, first grey snow. The
  pass at the fourth bridge is iced shut; the Lowwater road opens for market on the
  third day after ch 58. The fall ends two days after ch 58.
- **Held objects:** the Clay Tablet (hers, lied about on the record); the log with the
  four county originals; copies of the four county sheets sent to Lowwater with Du Hong
  (ch 58, no name on them); the second offer, logged word for word, untouched, held for
  Volume 4.

## Major locations

The Nine Steps Terrace and the furnace-crew quarters (second bunk); the furnace floor;
the roofless Assembly Hall (used for paper, never for shelter); the fourth bridge and
the Lowwater road; the Lowwater roundhouse and market square; the Lantern Register
tower; the Bureau of Records' Qinghe county office at Lowwater; the Hanhui broker's
counting room (one scene only).

## Major factions

- **The three sponsors** — Elders Lu Kang, Deng Chao, Pei An. Hold her inner rank, her
  Foundation-outside-schedule promise, the schedule of nine struck households over
  eleven years, and an undocumented clause about a plate that does not exist. They want
  the Kiln road worked and her mouth shut, in that order.
- **Shen Xiu's quota office** — holds the winter quota books and the only signature
  that can clear a household for licensed practice. Will not look at her; will not
  hurry; loses nothing by waiting.
- **The Bureau of Records, Qinghe county office** — mortal clerks at Lowwater under
  Registrar Sun He. Issues licences, stamps paper, and copies everything upward to
  something it never names.
- **The Lantern Register tower** — Feng Lushi, coughing, with five items in a column
  nobody reads and the four county copies hidden in his wall, unentered.
- **Hou Sanliang of Hanhui** — grain and core-stone broker holding paper on three of
  the nine struck households. The one man inside the bound who can pool eight hundred
  jin without selling land. Polite, unhurried, never raises his voice. The engine of
  the bounty arc.
- **The Lowwater Watch** — Captain Lu Tao, Old Gao. Owe her nothing and remember
  everything.

## The document

The promise of the volume is a permit she has to earn. It is not a mood. It is paper.

**The Licence to Practise Past Registration** — called on the mountain the *open-practice
licence*. Issued by the **Bureau of Records, Qinghe county office at Lowwater**, under
**Clause Nine of the Servant Advancement Settlement**: *a servant-born who breaks in
the open may draw sect qi, take paid work, and be taught past her stage only under
licence countersigned at the county*.

Without it, any gain past Condensing stage 1 is unregistered practice: unbillable,
unteachable, and — because the sponsorship binds her to the furnace schedule — a breach
that reinstates everything the signature struck. Realm walls bend for documents
(`bible/power-system.md`); this is the document they bend for.

**What it costs her:**

1. Six months of furnace-crew labour tithe, worked one-handed through winter — the
   grinding of the volume, paid in the body.
2. A living surety: a name in the county book standing for her attendance. Du Hong
   stands it at the second filing. His death voids it, and the debt transfers to her.
3. Her defect, entered in her own hand on the licence schedule: *aperture cracked
   through, stable, unrepairable*. The licence caps her at Condensing stage 5 and bars
   any Foundation petition without relicensing. She signs the ceiling down on herself
   to be allowed to climb to it. This cap is the pressure of Volume 3.

**The three moments she goes after it:**

1. **First filing (ch 64).** At Qinghe, on the sponsors' seal alone, before the thaw.
   Refused for want of quota clearance — Shen Xiu's office will not sign that a
   household with a standing bounty claim owes nothing, and Shen Xiu will not be hurried.
   She learns the licence is a three-legged thing and she owns one leg.
2. **Second filing (ch 87).** At Lowwater, with quota clearance earned and Du Hong
   standing surety. Refused for want of standing — a licenced practitioner cannot be
   under an unanswered bounty claim, and the claim cannot be answered except by body,
   place and name. The licence and the bounty are the same knot. This is the volume
   midpoint turn: the paper she wants is blocked by the price on her head, and the
   price is blocked by nothing at all.
3. **Final grant (ch 115).** After the bounty attempt fails on proof (wrong body) and
   the surety is dead, she files a third time standing her own surety, with the defect
   entered in her own hand and the Kiln road schedule attached as collateral labour.
   Registrar Sun He countersigns; Feng Lushi witnesses it in the tower book as a sixth
   item that is a copy of a licence and not an announcement, which is why the column
   stays what it was. She walks out licenced to Condensing stage 5, capped there,
   grieving, and legal.

## Power progression: Condensing 1 → 5

Real months, real recovery, no skipped breakthroughs. The volume spans late winter to
spring rain — about four and a half months, matching the four months of channel Yuan
said he had left.

- **Condensing 1 → 2 (ch 78).** Six weeks of one-handed furnace tithe, left-hand forms
  rebuilt from the Nine-Step habit, crack-channel practice in the cold. No tribulation;
  a slow, ugly, earned stage. Her right hand does not improve. Cost: two weeks of fever
  and a month of short breath that never fully leaves.
- **Condensing 2 → 3 (ch 92).** Five weeks of Lowwater carrying work under licence
  application, sleeping in the tower loft. Built out of load, as stage 8 was. Her arm
  returns to its third-of-the-time state and stays there. Cost: she spends her first
  Merit-adjacent reserve to keep Yuan's Settlement off full — and feels the tax take
  the order of a left-hand sequence she had just rebuilt. Practice rebuilds substance;
  it cannot rebuild order. Nothing she loses comes back.
- **Condensing 3 → 4 (ch 104).** Four weeks after Du Hong's burial. Grief training on
  the ash-flats in thaw mud. The stage arrives without announcement fanfare because
  stages inside a realm are not announced — only realm crossings are — and the quiet of
  it frightens her more than the trial did. Cost: she stops eating for nine days and
  Yuan cannot make her eat; the partnership's worst fortnight.
- **Condensing 4 → 5 (ch 114).** Four weeks under the granted-pending licence, working
  the furnace floor she is capped from ever owning. The breakthrough is witnessed by
  the crew and entered by Feng, and the licence's defect line holds: the layer
  announces nothing (no realm crossed), and for the first time she is glad of the
  silence. Ends the volume licenced, capped, and alive.

Yuan's track: at Voice throughout, Settlement grinding upward toward full as the months
run. At ch 80 he **retakes Ledger deliberately and on purpose** — at her spoken order,
*read him*, to read Hou Sanliang's man through her eyes — holding it for less than a
day and paying a Settlement spike past nine-tenths. He then refuses to hold it again,
and ends the volume able to take Ledger *at her word* but bound by her three terms not
to. The engine of the volume is that refused-then-retaken reach: he gets it back on
purpose, uses it once, and is frightened of what it felt like. Ledger as a standing
stage belongs to Volumes 3–5.

## The loss

Two deaths are written here before drafting, with mechanism and her agency in both.
Feng Lushi survives.

**The two furnace-crew friends (ch 71–72, batch 0006 climax).** The unnamed pair made
in ch 32 and seen again in ch 47 cover her absence on the work roster while she files
the first licence papers at the Hall. Inspectors from the sponsors' faction come for
her papers — the log is the shape they are feeling for — and she refuses the search of
the crew quarters, standing on the regulation that a maintaining member's effects are
sealed. The inspectors take the roster instead: the two who signed for her shift work
a double in the furnace during Cracking-settling repairs, short-handed, in a room she
knows is unsafe because she has worked it. A slag beam falls. Both die before the
healer reaches the floor. Mechanism: a furnace accident under a falsified roster.
Agency: she chose the refusal that moved the weight onto them, and she chose the
filing trip that made the cover necessary. She names both causes aloud at the burial
and does not let Yuan soften either. Irreversible, claustrophobic, and hers.

**Du Hong (ch 102–103, batch 0008 climax).** Three causes, all hers, and she lists all
three at his burial: she put her hand in his file (ch 56), she sent him down the
Lowwater road with her copies in his coat (ch 58), and she let him stand surety for
her licence (ch 87). Hou Sanliang's men stop him at the fourth bridge and demand the
three proofs — the body (hers), the place (where she sleeps), the name (whose hand is
on the paper). He carries her hand in his file and her copies in his coat. He gives
them his own name instead of hers, burns one sheet, and refuses the place. They beat
him for the place and leave him in the thaw water; he dies of it the next day in the
tower loft with Feng holding the lamp. The proof fails — wrong body — so the bounty
stands unclaimed and the price holds into Volume 3. Mechanism: a bounty examination
that became a killing. Agency: every paper that made him worth stopping has her hand
on it. She does not forgive herself, and Yuan does not ask her to.

**Feng Lushi survives.** He receives the four county copies and does not enter them
upward — he walls them in the tower behind the fifth item, keeping the unindexed
column at five. A Bureau Listener audit in ch 108 finds five items and passes him. His
cough worsens; a Lung is offered by the Bureau in ch 112 and he refuses it, coughing,
saying the tower needs a man who can still hear. That refusal is the setup for Volume
3, where he takes one. He witnesses the licence (ch 115) and lives to the volume's
last page, writing.

## The bounty arc (ch 68–103)

The ch 55 claim — eight hundred jin, proof as body, place and name, priced at a
furnace-crew year so that somebody inside the bound could afford it — is the engine of
the volume's second half. The somebody is **Hou Sanliang of Hanhui**, broker of grain
and core-stone, holding paper on three struck households: he can pool the price
without selling land, which is exactly what the pricing knew.

- Ch 68: his man comes to the Lowwater market asking after lamp-menders' wordings —
  ears, not eyes — and Yuan is ordered to read him (the Ledger retake begins here).
- Ch 87: the bounty blocks the second filing — a licenced practitioner cannot stand
  under an unanswered claim, and the claim cannot be answered but by proof.
- Ch 95–103: the attempt. Hou's men take Du Hong instead of her (the cheap door, as he
  predicted in ch 55), examine him for the three proofs, fail on the body, and kill
  him for the refusal. The claim stands, unclaimed, into Volume 3 — where the Hunter
  will use it as a map.

## The eleven designations (ch 96–110)

Volume 2 opens the door one hand's width and closes it again.

- Ch 96: holding Ledger on purpose, Yuan deliberately reads to the edge of the nearest
  seam and brings back one fragment: a single status word from a prior designation's
  announcement, in the layer's flat grammar — the word is *kiln-born*. He speaks it to
  her once. It is the first new fact about the eleven in six hundred years.
- Ch 110: a sealed slip arrives at the Lowwater Bureau office from outside the bound —
  no seal she knows, a hand nobody recognises (the ch 36 plant, still unrecognised) —
  requesting that the fragment be struck wherever copied. The office attached to the
  request has a name. Yuan cannot read it. Feng copies the shape of the characters
  without reading them, strikes his copy, and keeps the memory. The door closes. What
  remains: one word, *kiln-born*, held by two people and no paper, and the knowledge
  that a request from outside the bound is answered inside it without argument.

## The partnership lie (relationship milestone 3)

Per `bible/characters.md`: in Volume 2 he lies about what she is; she works it out
alone and does not forgive him for a year. The lie: that the designation is a reward
that protects her — that being Chosen is why the bounty cannot take her quietly. In
ch 100, alone in the tower loft with Feng's eleven volumes of advancement orders, she
works out from the *kiln-born* fragment and the tax sequence that Chosen Ones are
*manufactured to be spent* — the designation is a writ for making a person less solid
on schedule, and the Foundation tax was designed, not accidental. He knew the shape of
it since ch 57 and refused to finish. She tells him she worked it out, sets no new
rule (the three stand), and does not forgive him. The non-forgiveness runs through
Volume 3.

## Midpoint reversal (ch 88)

She earns quota clearance — Shen Xiu signs it, looking past her, in a scene with no
cruelty in it — and walks into the Lowwater office certain the licence is hers. The
registrar refuses it on the bounty standing. The paper she has chased for thirty
chapters is blocked by the price on her head, and the price is blocked by nothing.
The volume turns from "earn the paper" to "survive the price of the paper." This
mirrors the Volume 1 midpoint (a promotion that is a locked room) with an institutional
rather than personal lock.

## Volume climax (ch 112–116)

Spring rain on the roofless Hall. The bounty attempt has failed on proof; the surety
is dead and the debt is hers; the outside-bound request has been answered. She files a
third time standing her own surety, enters her defect in her own hand, attaches the
Kiln road schedule as collateral labour, and Registrar Sun He countersigns the
Licence to Practise Past Registration to Condensing stage 5. Feng witnesses it. The
irreversible decision of the volume belongs to **her**: she signs the ceiling down on
herself — capped at stage 5, barred from Foundation without relicensing — to be allowed
to climb at all. She pays for legality with the top of her own future, in ink, in
front of witnesses.

## Concrete resolution

- Licence granted (ch 115), capped at Condensing stage 5, defect entered. She is a
  legal practitioner for the first time in her life.
- Condensing stage 5 reached (ch 114), unannounced, witnessed by the crew.
- Du Hong dead and buried (ch 103); his file carries her hand forever, and the bounty
  men know the cheap door is gone.
- Two crew friends dead and buried (ch 72); the quarters sleep ten instead of twelve.
- Hou Sanliang unpaid and patient; the eight-hundred-jin claim stands into Volume 3.
- The *kiln-born* fragment struck from paper, held in two heads.
- Feng alive, unaudited-clean, Lung refused, writing.
- Shen Xiu unchanged, unthanked, still holding the quota books. Returns in Volume 5.
- The three sponsors hold her Foundation promise and the undocumented plate clause.
  They return in Volume 3 with the Hunter behind them.
- Settlement at roughly three-quarters: Merit spent against the Ledger spike. About
  two months of channel left. The clock runs into Volume 3.
- The register still reads *reconciled*. The truth is still *assessed, reassigned,
  unreconciled*. The plate is still against her skin.

## New question for Volume 3

A field agent of the Warden's office is coming down the Lowwater road asking after
unauthorised Systems, politely, patiently, and without ever lying. In the last chapter
Feng hears a new Listener's cough in the tower stairwell that is not his. The Hunter
has arrived. (Bai Zhu is not named in Volume 2.)

## Final chapter image (ch 118)

Spring rain falling through the roofless Hall onto the furnace floor schedule. The
licence nailed above her second bunk, the defect line in her own hand going soft in
the damp. Ten sleepers instead of twelve. Her right hand open and empty a third of
the time. Feng's tower lamp lit down the mountain. She asks Yuan what the row is
counting now, and he tells her the truth: it is counting her. She does not tell him
to stop.

## Hard rules for drafters (unchanged from Volume 1, restated once)

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. Every sentence Yuan speaks is
  written in the present tense. No other character is ever in the present tense.
- At most one bracketed layer line per chapter, on its own line. They announce; they
  never explain and never advise.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse
  bold or italic. No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two
  to four sentences per paragraph; break over about 90 words; never over about 120.
  Roughly 1,800–2,700 words.
- Close every speech: `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` before finishing. Narration
  never sits inside an open quote.
- The announcement wording is fixed (ch 53) and quoted word for word in ch 1000:
  *Assembly Hall of Qinghe. Ning Yan of the Qinghe furnace crew, servant-born. What was
  held is let go. What was taken is held.*
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until Volume 16, by Ning Wu. Ning Yan never
  says her mother's name. Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before
  Volume 11, and does not learn she saw him as a readout before Volume 11 either.
- No harem. Nothing in Volume 2 is a romance. Wei Ran does not appear before Volume 3.
- Do not re-explain known principles (four seconds, the release rule, tax-takes-order,
  layer-is-editable, eleven designations) unless the repetition changes meaning. Watch
  the spent constructions: nobody asked, nobody was thanked, there is no form, there
  is no column, the record remained, nobody knew.
- Volume 2 is grinding, claustrophobic, a real loss. Do not write another Volume 1.

## First batch: Volume 2, Batch 0006 — chapters 59–72

### Batch scope

Fourteen chapters, one dramatic unit. **Batch goal:** trapped on the mountain by ice
with a price on her head, Ning Yan files for the only paper that lets her practise at
all, and learns the paper needs a quota signature she does not own, a standing she
does not have, and — by the batch's end — two lives she did not mean to spend.

**Batch midpoint (ch 65–66):** the sponsors' inspectors come for her papers; she
refuses the search of the quarters and the weight moves onto the roster — the two
friends sign for her shift.

**Batch escalation:** ice → inspection → bounty ears in the market → the furnace
accident.

**Batch climax (ch 71–72):** the two friends die in the furnace under her roster; she
buries them, files the first licence papers anyway with their names still wet on the
brush, and is refused for want of Shen Xiu's clearance. Grief and paperwork in the
same hour. The irreversible decision of the batch belongs to her: the refusal of the
search (ch 66), which she will not undo and which cost two lives.

Power in this batch: none gained. Condensing stage 1 throughout. The batch is about
paper, ice, and the arm that does not come back. (Action/deadline requirement: the
fourth-bridge ice and the market-day road window in ch 63; the inspection search in
ch 66; the furnace accident in ch 71.)

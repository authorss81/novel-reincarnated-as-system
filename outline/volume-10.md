## Central pressure

**She has asked the only man who cannot be stopped writing her what else he has written, and the price of the
asking is that the list starts closing.**

Volume 9 made her nobody's kept thing and it turned out there was one book that did not need her agreement. Volume
10 makes her the person who asks about that book, and it turns out that a list that is asked about is a list that
closes — not from cruelty and not from punishment, because the Order's answer to everything is silence, but because
a keeping that has been looked at straight on cannot stay open. **Nobody is offering her anything. Somebody is
finishing writing, and she asked him to.** That is the volume's whole change of air, and `bible/themes.md` gives
Volume 10 its temperature as **hope, then a door that closes**.

**And the pressure is the only one she has never had.** In eight years she has refused documents, refused readings,
refused keepings, and survived being written. **She has never once asked a keeper for what he keeps, because asking
is the one thing her hand does not do.** The list cannot be refused into staying open, because it was never hers to
refuse. It can only be asked about, and asking is what closes it.

## What the volume must not be

Volume 9 ran being kept, and what a person is kept on · somebody writing her down without asking · surviving it in
her own hand. Volume 8 ran seduction and being wanted · what she is worth in a room · refusing the Order. Volume 7
ran rooms bigger than she is · what she is worth · the reach · what it costs to be right. Volume 6 ran reading and
being read · work without hire · sending and being sent for · standing and refusing to stand. **Volume 10 is none
of those, and none of its batches is a recruiting, a seduction, a tending, a ration, a hiring, a crossing, an
audit, an examination, a hunt, a signature, a keeping, a debt, a family paper, an ash, a confinement, or a second
demand in a room.**

**Its pressure is asking, and what asking closes.** **Volume 10 is the first volume in ten in which she asks a
keeper for what he keeps instead of refusing what he offers, and it must be written so that the reader feels that
as a change of air and not as the same book with a shorter list in it.**

### What he does that is the volume's real damage, and it is his oldest habit yet

**He treats one name as an entry in order to keep another name findable, and he tells her he is doing it while he
does it.** That is the flaw stated once in the outline and once on the page: `bible/characters.md` — *he treats
people as entries… he lies to her by omission and always calls the omission a kindness.* **In Volume 9 he stopped
omitting and showed the price. In Volume 10 he names the entry out loud, in daylight, in front of a third person —
and this time the entry is somebody else's name, and she lets him carry it.**

### How she catches half of it and permits all of it — pinned, and it is her own instrument

She does not catch him the way she caught the columns. **There is no second list to read against his, because no
page of his book is read out loud in this volume or any other.** What she does instead is what she has done since
the eighth of a month nine volumes back: she counts. **She counts the days to the closing, counts the names she
has been told, counts the names she has not, counts the bread, counts the miles — and when the count runs out she
tells him to go on answering.** The permission is hers, out loud, once, at the wall, in daylight.

### The frame scene — yes, Volume 10 takes one, and it is pinned

**Ch 584. Away from her hearing, in the layer's own reading-space, a man who is permanently on the wrong side of a
wall he opened reads what holding a list open past its closing would cost and finds out what a list is when it is

says in the frame is said away from her hearing and is not a speech to her.

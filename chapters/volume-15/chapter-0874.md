# The Shape of Taking

One hundred and forty-eight days since the ninth stage. Five hundred and seventy-nine days in that room. The cage out three hundred and forty-six days. Right arm open five hundred and eighty-six days and it is not going to close. One thousand three hundred and twenty-one of the days gone at this morning and not one coming back.

There was a lamp burning on the stone of the third landing above a room that was not a room, and there was a woman sitting beside it who had said four months out loud after fifteen volumes, and there was a taking with a first night and a dateless head, and she was going up to find out what it is for.

## What It Is For

She said it out loud, sitting, in the ordinary voice, at about the sixth hour of the seventeenth of the month, before she did anything else about it.

She said, “Figures first and the reason after. One hundred and forty-eight days since the ninth. Five hundred and seventy-nine in that room. Three hundred and forty-six since a lamp in a cage went out. Five hundred and eighty-six with this arm open and it is not going to close. One thousand three hundred and twenty-one of the days gone and not one coming back.”

She said, “And here is the reason. Three days ago the head did not cover the body. Two days ago the price had no day. Yesterday four months went said after fifteen volumes silent. This morning the three stand together, a first night, a dateless price, a running count, and no instrument in the world will tell me what the taking is for, so I am going to work it out of my own arithmetic.”

She said, “It is hunger, and it is not in the belly, and it is the first such hunger on this stair.”

She said, “Not fear, which I had yesterday and know its shape. Hunger to know what a taking is for, because a taking with no purpose is theft, and a taking with a purpose is a keeping, and I have called it theft for fifteen volumes without once asking.”

She said, “And what I want is the shape of it. Not the whole. The shape. I can hold a shape.”

She left the lamp burning on the third landing and went up past it in the dark with nothing in either hand, with her left hand on the stone, saying the landings where they were used, fourth through eighth, because the hunger needed the discipline the way a niche needs a wall.

## Less Solid, Stronger

She came onto the ninth landing and stopped, because the body had something to say before the arithmetic, and she said it out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice.

She said, “Here is what my body knows. I have been getting stronger every span since the ninth, and I have been getting less solid at the same rate, and both are true, and I have said neither out loud until now.”

She stood with that said, and her arm shook, and she stood still until it stopped, and his half went on inside her where she could hear it.

She said, “And here is the whole of it, said plain. Stronger means the ninth holds more. Less solid means there is less of me to hold it. The two go together like a lamp burning brighter with less oil, and I have felt both for one hundred and forty-eight days, and I called the first the stage and the second the stair, and neither name was honest.”

She went on up past the tenth with no doorway in it, past the eleventh, with her left hand on the stone, and the cold came up into her arm, and she stopped until the shaking stopped, and then she went on.

She stood on the twelfth landing in the dark with nothing in either hand, and the sentences went on, one at a time, plain, reporting, through her to him, and she said the arithmetic standing.

## The Arithmetic of Thinness

She said, out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, “Here is the shape, from my own arithmetic. Four months went in one night. Ten days and hours and minutes stand at the head of every span. One thousand three hundred and twenty-one are gone now. And I am less solid at the rate I am stronger.”

She stood there for about a count of a hundred with that in her, and then she said the rest.

She said, “A taking that leaves a person stronger and thinner is not eating the strength. It is eating the solidity and leaving the strength. The strength is the vessel filling. The thinness is the vessel wall going thin to hold it.”

She said, “And here is the whole of what that means, said plain. Something takes foundation out of me daily, dateless, and puts capacity in its place. The capacity is real. I can feel it. The foundation is gone. I can feel that too. One thousand three hundred and twenty-one is not days lost. It is wall thinned.”

He said, “You hold on.”

She said, “I do. And holding on finishes nothing, and I am saying so out loud. It is one shape found, and the taking goes on.”

## What Thinning Is For

She sat down on the stone of the twelfth landing with her back against the wall, because the shape needed sitting the way a finding needs a floor, and she said the rest sitting, in the ordinary voice.

She said, “Here is what thinning is for, from the arithmetic and not from any mouth. A wall thinned to hold more is a ceiling made thin so more can pass. The ninth is a vessel, and a vessel thinned at the top lets something through. What goes through a thinned ninth is ascent itself, mine, going up and out.”

She said, “And anger, sudden, hot, and I am not going to cool it. Fifteen volumes I thought the taking fed him, and the arithmetic says the taking feeds a passage. My foundation thinned is a hole made larger, and ascent going through a larger hole is easier, and I was never told, and he never told me.”

She said, “And here is the part that steadies, said plain. A ceiling thinned for one person is a mercy to that person. A ceiling thinned for every person with a ninth is a keeping for all of them. I do not know which this is, and I am saying that I do not know, and the not knowing is the next work.”

She got up and stood with nothing in either hand, and she said it standing, because the shape was found standing and must be kept standing.

She said, “Stronger and thinner at one rate. That is found, and it holds.”

## The Passage Priced

She stayed on the twelfth landing after the shape was kept, standing with nothing in either hand, because the passage wanted pricing, and she priced it standing.

She said, out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, “Here is the passage, said plain. A wall thinned lets ascent through easier. Easier ascent for one is mercy. Easier ascent for all, thinned daily without telling, is a road kept open by walking it with foundation. One thousand three hundred and twenty-one from me is so much road walked.”

She said, “And here is the whole of what road walking means, said plain. Every morning I thinned, the road stayed open. The ninth I carry is not only mine. It is a span of road. My thinness is the road smoothed. My strength is the feet grown for it. Both true. Neither told.”

She said, “And resolve, hot, new, and I am naming it because it is the first resolve after anger, and it is large, and I am not going to make it smaller than it is. A road I walk unwitting I will now walk witting, and witting walking is different feet, and tomorrow the feet go walking and working at once.”

He said, “You walk it.”

She said, “I do. And walking it finishes nothing, and I am saying so out loud. It is one road found, and the taking goes on.”

## The Road Walked Witting

She stayed standing after the road was found, with nothing in either hand, because the witting wanted saying, and she said it standing.

She said, out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, “Here is the witting, said plain. Walked unwitting fourteen mornings of finding, walked witting from tomorrow. Witting feet count differently. They count the thinning with each landing, the taking with each breath. Different counting, same stair.”

## Down With the Shape

She came down in the dark with her left hand on the stone, past the eleventh, past the tenth with no doorway, past the ninth, saying each one where it was used, and she took longer over the ninth than the rest, because the shape touched the ninth and the ninth was under her feet.

She sat beside the lamp, and the light went six paces out from where it stood and stopped, and she stayed inside the reach of it until her breath went four counts sitting still.

She said, out loud, sitting, in the ordinary voice, “Lamp on the third landing. Burning. Mine. A taking that thins the wall to let the strength through. That is the record and there is nothing else in it.”

There was a lamp burning on the stone of the third landing, and a shape of thinning found out of three figures with no mouth in it, and a woman sitting beside the light with anger and steadiness both in her.

And she had the shape of it, and the shape pointed at a ceiling, and that was the day, and the ceiling would be there in the morning to be asked what it protects.

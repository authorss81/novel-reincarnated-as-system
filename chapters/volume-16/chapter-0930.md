# Withdrawn

It was the second hour on the twenty-seventh of the month beginning three thousand and fourteen and she was standing at the near end of her own table with the lamp on the stone and her hands flat on the wood.

He said it.

“No,” he said. “I will not keep the Foundation tax running. I will not be the hand that finds the next one, and I will not be the hand that files the one that is finished, and I will not stand in a keeping for six hundred years on the strength of the fact that I was the piece of it that was willing.”

It came out of her mouth.

That was the part. She had asked him for it on the twenty-fifth and she had said yes to it standing up, and it went out of a woman at the near end of a table eight flights and a stair below a room with no wear in it, in the ordinary voice, in the channel, before it was a line anywhere.

## Something Looked

Something came up through her and went over him once, the way a hand goes over a thing to find what it is.

It did not stop it.

It had no shape and it did not argue and it did not take anything back. It found the refusal and it stayed on it, and she felt the whole of the weight of the layer come down onto one sentence in a woman’s mouth, and it held.

“Ning Yan,” he said.

She did not answer.

“Ning Yan, I am going. I want you to know that I am going and I am not able to stop halfway through it, because it is not a going the way a man leaves a room. There is no door in me. What there is, is a man standing in a line, and a line stops being a man.”

She felt him begin.

She felt it the way she had felt a sentence of hers go out, except that this one was coming the other way, up through her, out of her, and it did not have the end on it that sentences have.

## The Small Thing

“Move the lamp a little to the left.”

He said it in the middle of it.

She moved the lamp a little to the left.

That was the last thing he asked for in six hundred years. She did it without stopping and she did not say anything about it, and not one person in the world was ever told that she had done it, and she was not going to tell him that she was not going to be thanked for it, because he would not have been able to hear it.

Then he went.

He stopped in the middle of a sentence. There was no last word and there was no silence, because a silence is a thing you can have a length of, and this had no length. One moment there was a man in her head who had been there since she was ten years old, and then there was not one, and the room was a room.

She stood at the near end of her own table with the lamp burning a little to the left of where it had been and her hands flat on the worn wood.

She said his name into the room.

Nothing came back, and it was not the other kind of nothing. She had been listening for nine years for the difference between a man thinking and a man gone, and there was nothing in her head to tell her anything at all.

## The Line

[The answer of the seventh is withdrawn. The Foundation account stands open and unanswered. It has not begun.]

She stood there and let it go past her one piece at a time, plain, in the grammar the keeping used for everything it said.

It was not about him. It did not explain anything. It did not say that a man had refused anything, and it did not say that anything had been refused at all. It said that an answer given on the seventh had been withdrawn, and that an account was open, and that it had not begun, and that was all of it, and it was in the same words it used about a bundle of boards going up nine hundred and four steps.

Eight flights and a stair above her a room had no wear in it, and the offer was standing open on a table where she had left it, open until it is answered or withdrawn, and the walls were up, and the layer was up, and the layer was going to go on keeping them.

## What She Did Not Know Yet

She did not know, standing there five hours on at the seventh hour of the twenty-seventh of the month beginning three thousand and fourteen with a lamp a little to the left of where it had been, that a refusal is only heard in a keeping when a person says it, and that the layer had exactly one of those, and that she was the one, and that it had never had one before.

She did not know that the channel was hers alone.

She stood at the near end of her own table and waited for something to happen the way she had waited every morning of her life for a figure to turn up, and nothing came, and there was nothing in her head to come, and she had never once in nineteen years had nothing in her head.

# Nobody Named On The List

It is not a field and it is not a room with a floor and a table in it.

It is a space with nothing in it that he can stand on and a thing in it he can look at, and both of those are the first things he checks, because he has been inside the layer twice in his life and both times he started by working out what kind of place he was in before he let anything arrive.

There is no light in it that comes from anywhere and no shadow. That is not new and he has never got used to it.

What is in it this time is a list.

## The List

It runs away from him in one direction and there is no end to it that he can find in the time he has, and every line on it is in the ground's own wording.

Thirty-one days of it, and it is thirty-one days of work, and it is the work of a lane two miles east of a gate she has never been through.

There is a length of sill at the crossing, forty yards, by the length, on the sixth.

There is a load off a frame at the low end of the bank on the fifteenth, by the weight, the two that go up and not down.

There is a length of sill at the crossing, forty yards, by the length, on the twenty-first.

There is water in a ditch, cut side, the branch end, and a chain stiff on a gate and oil for it, and a man off a frame on the sixth day of a month with a leg wrapped to the ankle, and six carts off a country road before the middle of the day, by the load.

That is all of it. That is all of what he put where somebody's hand already was, in twelve days, in a lane, and it is thirty-one lines long and not one of them has a name on it anywhere.

## The Question He Goes Looking For

He has one question and he has had it since the sixth, and he comes here to answer it on the twenty-sixth because he does not want to answer it on the sixth.

“Does the layer honour a listing for a person who is not the Chosen One.”

He goes and looks for the answer the way he looked for the morning and did not find it.

The list does not carry a name. It does not carry a name because nothing on it is shaped to hold a name, and the ground's own wording has never in eleven hundred years of practice on this ground contained the word for the man who does the work.

He looks for a settlement. He looks for the place a settlement would be entered if it were entered, the way a date is entered in the left column of a ruled sheet, in a hand nobody in a nine-foot room has ever put there.

It is not there.

## The Answer

The layer will not honour a listing for a person who is not her.

That is the answer and it has been the answer since the sixth, and he worked it out on the sixth inside a quarter of an hour, and he has carried it around for twenty days like a man carrying a stone he cannot put down.

Not because it is a rule. There is no rule anywhere that says it and he has been in here twice before and there was no rule in it then either. It is because the layer only has one counterparty and the counterparty is her foundation, and every settlement the layer has ever made has gone to one place, and a man at a gate who took a bar off a wall is not that place and never was and the machinery has no second door.

He looks for the one thing that would make it otherwise and there is not one.

## Why It Does Not Matter

He has known this since the sixth and it is the reason he is in here today and not the reason he is satisfied.

The layer is not what pays. The layer has never paid anybody, in any of the times he has been inside it, and it is not going to start, and the settling it does is the same word doing the same job and it goes into the ground.

What pays is a woman of fifty in a nine-foot room at the end of the lane.

She has a book with a rate at the head of it in a hand that is not hers. A man comes down the lane on the thirteenth and says he has laid a sill and she writes the thirteenth against it in the right-hand column and pays him out of what the floor takes in, and she did not consult anything. She did not know there was a question. She would have done it out of her own hands if there had been nobody left to pay from.

He has been looking for the paying for twenty days and the paying is in a shed with a shutter open, and it has been in a shed with a shutter open the whole time, and it does not know he exists.

That is what it means to spend out of her foundation and have a stranger clear it. The stone moves. The man falls in a ditch and gets out. And forty days later a woman writes a figure in a book because he came down a lane and said he had done a thing.

The mechanism is hers. He has been spending her ground and she has been running the settlement, and neither of them has ever been in the same room, and she is the one who closes it.

## What He Wrote In The Left Column

He goes and looks at the dates on the list because that is the thing he cannot stop looking at.

The sixth and the twenty-first. The day it happened, written in a column, in the ground's own wording, which is the only wording there is.

He did not put those dates there. He put the work there.

A listing carries what a piece of work is, and a piece of work on this ground has a day it happened on it and a day anybody found out on it, and the layer does not distinguish between those two because the layer has never had a reason to.

It writes both. It writes both in a book on a board floor, in a ruled cell, in a hand she cannot read, four days before the woman who owns the floor finds out anything at all.

Four days is how long it takes for a man to come down a lane.

He did that.

He has spent as long as she has been on nine days of road finding out what a number costs when it is not hers to pay, and he finds out on the twenty-sixth that the number was never the expensive part. The number was the easy part. The number is what he says out loud at night in the present tense and she cannot stop him saying it.

What is expensive is that she found it. Not him. She found it, on her knees, four inches off a sheet, with an instrument that has never once in her life been worth anything to anybody who was not her.

## The Third One He Is Not Spending

He has had it since the twenty-second.

On the sixth he spent five days and two hours and it went out of the boards under a shed at the end of a lane, and a man went into a ditch and came out of it.

On the fifteenth he spent four days and it went out of the same ground, and two loads came off the low end of a frame that had not been counted twice in nineteen years.

On the twenty-first he spent again, and it cost the same and it went the same way and it settled in the same nine-foot room in the same ordinary hand.

That is the whole of what a spend looks like from inside this and it does not look like anything.

On the twenty-second he reached for the fourth and he has had it in his hand for five days now and he cannot give her a reason.

She read a column. She read a ruled cell on a board floor four inches off the paper and there is a day in it four days before anybody in that building knew anything, and it is hers, and it is there because of him, and he worked out how that worked in five months ago and he has not been able to un-work it since.

He has gone looking for a reason in here twice and there is nothing in this space that answers anything, because there is no question in it that can be asked of it, and he is the one who puts the questions and he is the one who answers them, and that is a closed room he built himself out of his own habit.

## What He Cannot Find

He goes looking for the last thing, the way he went looking for the morning at the seventeenth of a month five months back, and he does not find it, and he is not going to look again.

There is no listing in here with him on it.

That is the finding and it is the whole of what these twenty days have been worth and it took him twenty days to arrive at it.

He can put a piece of work where any hand on nine days of road will find it. He can spend her foundation and make a stranger's body carry the difference. He cannot point the thing at himself, because a listing is work, and the only work available is somebody else's, and there is no line in the ground's own wording for what he would be doing if there were.

He has been treating people as entries since before she found him. He has never once been an entry. He looked for it on the seventeenth of a month five months back for the morning and did not find it then either, and it is not here now, and it is not going to be here on any future night he stands in here and looks.

## What He Does About It

He goes and looks at the list one more time, the thirty-one lines of it, and there is still no name on any of them, and there is still nothing in it that is going to pay for a man's leg.

He puts the twenty-second where the sixth is and where the fifteenth is and where the twenty-first is. He does not spend it.

And he stands in a space with nothing under his feet and takes the twenty-seventh of the month and goes back out, and she is two miles away on boards in a shed that smells of standing water, and she has heard one number out of three, and the one she heard was on the fifth of a month in a shed at the end of a lane and she did not know then what it was buying.

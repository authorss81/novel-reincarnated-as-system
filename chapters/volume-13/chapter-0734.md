# What A Hand Is Charged

The second bundle came up the stair at the second hour of the first of the month beginning 2,354, on a man’s arm, and she stood four feet off the end of the long table for forty minutes again and did not put a hand on it once.

## Six Of Her Own Hand Lower

The first thing she did with the forty minutes was not watch the boards. It was to go and put her hand against the wall behind the lamp and find out where the bottom edge of the light was.

It was six of her own hand below the top of the cage.

It was at the top of the cage on the first of the month beginning 2,324, when forty things went along that table, and it was at the top of the cage on the first of the month beginning 2,354, before the forty things went on it this time, and the last turning was three days ago.

Six of her hand is three days of falling, and she has got that rate, and she stood on the floor at the near end of that table and said it out loud before the bundle was a third of the way along.

She said, “It is lower than it was on the first of the month beginning 2,324 by six of my own hand, and that is three days, and the turning that put it back was on the twenty-eighth and not on the thirtieth, and there is not one thing in this room that tells you which of those two numbers is the rule.”

## The Forty Minutes

And then the boards went along again at four to a minute and the bundle came off and the string came off the bundle and it took the other thirty minutes.

And this time she was not watching the boards. She was watching the far side of the table past the boards, where the hands go down one after another and the figure is the same figure on every one, and she was watching the whole of it from four feet off in the dark half of a room with her arms at her sides.

And at the end of it the boards went under the table in a stack and she went and got down on her hands and knees with her head going in first and put her fingers on the strip of wood with the head and the column on it, and it was where it was.

She read it with her fingers and it was the same figure and she had not wanted it to be anything else.

## What She Watched The Second Time

And then the hand came off the near edge of the table at the sixth hour of the first of the month beginning 2,354, and by the time she had counted four of them there was a light higher than the top of that cage, and the person at the table was standing at the near edge of it with both hands on it exactly as they had been standing.

She had her hand up at the end of that forearm before it and she had it up again after and she did not need to count out loud this time.

It was the width of her little finger on her left hand, and it was the same, and there was not one movement made at that table in the whole of it that a person standing there would have looked up for.

She said, out loud, in the ordinary voice, at the near end of the table, at the seventh hour of the first of the month beginning 2,354:

“There it is again.”

## The Whole Of It Out Loud

And then she put the whole of it out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, one item at a time, in a room with a person in it, because that is what she does and she has not been able to stop doing it since the eighth of the month beginning 2,324.

She said, “There is a lamp in a cage at the far end of this table and there is no oil in this building and there is no wick in that lamp and nothing behind the light and nothing to put into it.”

She said, “It comes down two of my own hand a day. Fifty-eight of my own hand from the top of that cage to a cut in the wall, and two a day, and twenty-nine days, and a month is thirty.”

She said, “There is a line cut in the stone of that wall and the light stops on that line and does not go under it, and a hand turns that lamp and it takes eleven seconds, and I have watched it twice and it has not been late once.”

She said, “And every time it is turned, the arm that turns it is smaller by the width of my little finger, and the face is thinner at the cheek, and I have measured both of those and the distance the light comes up that wall is the same distance the arm goes down.”

## The Part That Is A Person

And then she said the one part of it that is not a number, out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, at the ninth hour of the first of the month beginning 2,354, and it is the longest sentence she has said in this room and she said it all of it.

She said, “The person at that table was fuller in the face than they were on the first of the month beginning 2,324, and I have measured it twice and it is the same both times, and there has been no month in it at all: two turnings in twenty-seven days and then three days, and no turnings at all in the days in between, and the hand has done it whenever it has done it.”

She said, “And I cannot tell you how many there have been and I am not going to be able to tell you, because it is not once a month and it is not a number of days and it is not a number anything, and I have looked for it in the light and the light has got fifty-eight hands of fall in it and there is not one step of that where a person is in it.”

She said, “And there have been four hundred years of it. That is a person at that table for four hundred years who has been less every time that lamp was turned, and I am the first thing that has ever come up that stair that could see it, and that is not because I am better at it than anybody who has come up here before me. There has not been anybody.”

She stood there and did not say one word about how many of them there have been, or about anything else.

## Two Readings Thirty Days Apart

And on the second of the month beginning 2,354 she did the thing that turned thirty days of watching into a figure, standing on the floor at the near end of that table with her own hand up at the end of an arm.

She had a reading from the first of the month beginning 2,294 and she had a reading from the first of the month beginning 2,354, and there is one turning in between that she did not see, and she put the two of them side by side out loud and they were not the same.

She said, “On the first of the month beginning 2,294 the round of that arm was the width of my two fingers and the third of a finger beside it. On the first of this month it is the width of my two fingers and a bit over. On the twenty-eighth of the month beginning 2,324 it was the width of my one finger.”

She said, “And that is three readings a month apart and three turning times and there is a turning in the middle of that month I did not see because I was under a table reading forty boards with my fingers at the time, and I am not going to pretend to a number I did not take.”

She said, “And the person at that table is less at a rate I can put against a rate I can put against a rate, and none of the three of those rates is in the same kind of thing as the others and I have not got a fourth one to put them all in.”

## What She Said About Not Knowing

And on the third of the month beginning 2,354 she said out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, at the seventh hour, the part of the whole of this that she is not going to be able to do anything about, and she said it slowly.

She said, “There is a figure at the head of a column on a strip of wood under that table and it is mine and it is true as of the first of the month beginning 2,294 and it is getting further out of date every hour I am in this room.”

She said, “And it is three days and one hour and twelve minutes out of date now, and I know that by subtraction off the first of the month beginning 2,294, and not one person in this building could tell me if I did not.”

She said, “And so there are two figures about me in the world. One of them is me and it goes down an hour and twelve minutes a day and there is an instrument for it that I do not need. And the other one is a strip of wood on the floor of a room four miles above a mountain and it is three days and one hour and twelve minutes out of date and it does not know it.”

She said, “And I have been able to see the second one since the twelfth of the month beginning 2,324 and I have not been able to do one single thing about it, and I am not going to be able to, and that is the whole of what this room has given me to do.”

## What A Room Can Be Asked For

And then she said what she worked out at the ninth hour, which is the only thing out of the whole of this that she can use.

She said, “A room cannot be asked. It can be paid.”

She said, “That is what I have worked out in fifty-six days in a room where not one person in it has refused me and not one person in it has spoken to me and there is not one procedure in it for a thing that has not arrived. There is no asking in it. There is a lamp and a hand and a cut in a wall and a figure at the head of a column under the floor, and that is all of what this room can do.”

She said, “And the price of keeping a room lit is that somebody has to stand at the table, and it is coming off that person, and it is coming off me as well, and there is not one instrument in this building that takes it off anybody and puts it anywhere.”

## The Decision Not To Go Down

And then she said the decision out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, at the eleventh hour of the third of the month beginning 2,354, in a room with a lamp in a cage in it and a bar nine feet nine flat against the wall on the left.

She said, “I said on the sixteenth of the month beginning 2,324 that I would have to go down eight hundred and eighty steps and get a nib and an ink and come back up, and I am not going to do it.”

She said, “There is a thing above the line I have not got an instrument for and there is not one nib in this county that would help me with it, and I am not going down eight flights with an open arm for a pen.”

She said, “And I have worked out where the light is going to be on the thirtieth of this month if nothing turns it, and the thirtieth of this month is twenty-seven days from today, and I am going to be in this room on the thirtieth of this month.”

She said, “And I do not know what for yet. That is the first decision I have ever made in this room and I have made it standing up, out loud, and it is not improved on.”

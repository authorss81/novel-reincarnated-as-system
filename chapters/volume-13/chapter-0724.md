# The Second Place At The Table

She could not reach the table, and it took her four days and four tries to get a hand on the edge of it, and on the fourth try she nearly went down on the floor of that room from four feet up, and not one person in it would have heard her land.

## Four Tries On Four Days

On the twentieth of the month beginning 2,294, at the fifth hour, she stood at the near end of the table with her right arm up and her left hand flat on the wood and jumped.

The edge of the table was a hand’s width above the top of her reach and she got her knuckles to it and her knuckles are not a hand.

On the twenty-first she put her left shoulder into the frame of it under the top and pushed, and the table moved. It moved a quarter of an inch on the floor and every one of her four counts of breath went out of her at once, and she stood with her hands flat on it and her heart going like a bird’s and did not move it again.

On the twenty-second she got three fingers and a thumb over the edge from underneath and hung on, and the wood came out of her hand before she had her weight on it, and she was on the floor on both knees in the dark half of that room with an open arm and her breath at nine and nothing broken.

## What It Was Like On The Second Try

And on the twenty-first of the month beginning 2,294, at the second hour, with the table four feet above her and the light at the far end of the room and herself in the dark half of it, she put her left shoulder into the frame of that table under the top and pushed, and the table moved.

It moved a quarter of an inch on the stone.

She let it go and stepped back and her heart was going and she stood with her hands flat on the wood for a count of sixty.

And then she said out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, in a room with a person in it:

“I have been in this building for a month and there is a lamp in a cage burning at the far end of this room and I have never once been in a place where the furniture moved when I touched it.”

She said, “And there is a person nine feet back along this table who has both hands on the edge of it, and a table that moves a quarter of an inch is a table that has been pushed.”

She said, “And nothing happened. The hands stayed where they were and the head did not come up and the table came back to where it was on its own in a count of forty, because it is on stone and it has been on stone for a long time, and I have not told it that I am in the room.”

She stood there in the dark for a count of a hundred afterwards with her hands on it, and she did not put her shoulder into it again.


## A Hand On It At Last

On the twenty-third she went to it at the fifth hour and put her left hand flat on the top of the table with the fingers spread and got her weight on the arm that is closed four days in five, which was not this day, and she had it.

She held it for a count of nine before she moved anything else.

And then she put her right hand on the table too, an arm that has been open since the first of this month, and got up on it in three movements, and the table did not move, and she was on the top of a table four feet off the floor of a room at the top of eight flights and eight hundred and eighty steps in a tower four miles up a mountain from a square.

Her breath went to nine standing up on it and stayed at nine for a count of sixty, and she knelt there and looked along the length of the table toward the only light in that building.

## What Four Days Of Failing Costs

Four days of failing at a table is not four days of nothing. She worked out on the twenty-third what those four days were, standing at the near end of it with a hand on the edge, and she said it out loud because she does not leave a thing standing.

She said, “On the twentieth I found out that I cannot reach a table that is the height of my own chest, and I did not know that before, and there are a hundred things in this county I have been assuming I could do because I have never tried them sitting down.”

She said, “On the twenty-first I put my weight on it and it moved, and I have been carrying things for nine years and I have never once been the reason a thing moved that another person was standing in, and I felt that.”

She said, “On the twenty-second I hung on that edge with three fingers and a thumb on an arm that is open and it came out of my hand, and I was on my knees on a stone floor in the dark half of a room four miles above a mountain with my breath at nine, and there was not one person in this building who would have heard me fall over.”

She said, “And today I got a hand on it. That is what four days came to. It is not a figure anybody would put in a column and it is the first thing that has happened to me in this room.”

## What It Is Like From The Top Of It

And then she said the thing about being up there, out loud, kneeling on the top of a table four feet above the floor of a room she cannot walk the whole length of, in the ordinary voice, at the sixth hour of the twenty-third of the month beginning 2,294.

She said, “I am on the top of a table I am not allowed to be on and I can see along it and I cannot see into any of the dark at the near end of it.”

She said, “I can see the grain and the marks, forty of them, in one span, which means forty things have gone along that table and come off it and gone somewhere, and I have not seen one of them leave this room.”

She said, “And I can see the far side of it now and the far side is bare, and I have already said out loud that there is something on the far side of that table, and I am not going to say one word more about it, and I would like to be able to take that back and I cannot.”


## Along It

A long table with nothing on it from the near end to the lamp’s shelf, and the grain of the wood running the whole way, and the boards that go on it on the first of a month go across the grain and leave a mark on it, and there are forty of those marks in one span of that wood and she could count them from where she was kneeling.

The near side of the table is where the work is. Both hands go on that edge and the head comes down over it and that is the whole of what a person does at this table.

The far side is bare.

It is bare the whole way, and she looked along it for a count of ninety expecting it to be bare, and it is not, and she stopped counting.

There is a stretch of the far side, about the length of her own forearm from the wrist to the elbow, where the wood is a different colour from the wood either side of it. It is paler, and it is smoother, and it has got a shape into it that is not a mark anybody made with a tool, and there is nothing in front of that stretch of the far side of that table and there is nothing on it.

It is not a chair.

It is not a seat, and it is not a bench, and it is not a place where a thing is put down, and she looked at it for a count of two hundred from four feet above the table with her knees on the wood and she could not put a name to the shape that is worn into it.

## What She Did Not Do

She did not put her hand on it.

She got up off her knees and she stepped down off the far side of the table onto the floor, which is four feet, and she did it with her left hand on the wood and her back to the light and she did not land on her arm.

And then she stood up in front of it at the near end of a table with nothing on it and she did not say one word about it out loud.

That is the whole of what happened at that place and she is not going to improve on it later. She did not touch it and she did not name it and she did not say out loud, standing, in the ordinary voice, in a room with a person in it, that there was anything there at all. She has said out loud every other thing she has found since the eighth of this month and she was not going to start on this.

## Two Places

She went back to the near end of the table and sat down on the floor with her back against the wall on the right where the door is, and she put the finding out loud the way she puts findings out loud, with the part she cannot use left out of it.

She said, “There are two places at that table.”

She said, “One of them is being used. I have watched it being used every day since the eighth of this month and the hands go on that edge and the head comes down over it and the light is on it, and that is what the room is for.”

She said, “And there is another one on the far side with nothing at it, and I am not going to say anything else about it, and I am not going to be the first person in four hundred years to reach across a table four feet above the floor for a thing that has not asked to be reached for.”

She said it at the sixth hour of the twenty-third of the month beginning 2,294, and then she was quiet for the rest of that day, and at the far end of the table the hands went on the near edge and the head stayed down.

## What She Wanted

And the thing she wanted, which she said out loud once and did not say again, was not to see the far side of that table.

It was to get a hand on the near edge of it so that she would be able to put one thing down on it, and she has not got one thing to put down, and she has not been able to be given anything in nineteen years, and she worked that out on the seventh of this month at the foot of a stair eight hundred and eighty steps under this one.

So she has a hand on the table now and there is nothing to put on it, and that is the first thing this building has let her do and it is worth nothing at all.

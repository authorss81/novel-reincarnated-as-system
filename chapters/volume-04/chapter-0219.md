# Standing Off

It was back in three days.

She had watched this one, and the last time it had gone in three days too, over a ring two seasons out of date and worked by eleven men, and she had said the arithmetic out loud at a trestle and been right about it.

This was faster. She did not work out why it was faster and she did not have to, because she knew the four places.

It comes up over the top of a boot in four places on that stretch, and she knew where the four places were the way a person knows the four treads on a stair, and on the eleventh of the month she stood outside the wire and watched a man she had never spoken to put his boot into the first of them and lose it.

The boot came out. He took the weight out of it and got down and pulled it and got the boot back and carried on, and it took him about as long as it takes to say something, and he did it without looking at his feet because he knows the place.

He did not know the place. He knew the ring.

## Three Days Is Right, And So Is Everything Else They Said

It is three days because the work above it is not done, and the work above it is not done because the season before it was not done, and the season before that, and no one of them is the man standing there now.

That is a schedule. Schedules do not argue back. They are the worst kind of fact there is, because they are made out of days and days do not come back once they have not been used.

She stood outside the wire on the eleventh, at about the sixth hour, and a man came up out of the ring about four feet from her and stood there and got his breath for a while and then went back down into it, and he took about four times as long to turn a bar over as she did, and she did not say a word about it and he did not look at her.

He was not slower than she was. He was slower than she is.

He had been on that ring about eleven years and had the ring and not the hand, and the hand is the thing that went in June on a low side of the forty, and there was no way on earth to give him the hand and she was not allowed to stand in that ring to try.

He will be measurably worse at that job for the rest of the season than she was at it, and he does not know it, and never will know it, and there is nobody in nine days of road he could ask.

## The Sixth Hour Of The Eleventh

There were about six men idle that day and by the sixteenth there were nine.

A course is paid by the day out of a stretch count. A stretch that is not on the run is not a stretch. So a run of nine days with a hole in it is a run of nine days with less in it, and the less comes out of eleven men at the same rate, and there is no mechanism anywhere in the world by which a rule that is correct produces a good afternoon.

They stood about in the camp. That is what idle looks like on a course: men standing about in the open with nothing in their hands, near the trestle, near the wire, near the fire, for about three hours a day, waiting to see whether the count changes.

It did not change.

One of them asked the foreman, on about the fourteenth, whether the stretch would come back on, in the ordinary way a man asks a foreman a thing at a trestle.

Cui Bao said it would not and did not give a reason.

That is a thing a foreman can do and it is not a lie and it is not unkind. There is nowhere in that camp to put the reason. The reason is a certificate in a book in a county four hundred miles west and a rule nine years old, and a man standing in the open does not need any of it.

## The Girl

She was idle from the eighth of the month.

She worked the twenty-eight on the ninth because the twenty-eight wanted it and the twenty-eight is on the run. After that the run had four stretches on it that need a hand and there are about nine men who can be put on them, and she is one hand, and she is nineteen, and she is faster than four of them.

She was not put on any of them.

Cui Bao did not put his daughter on the run because the run is a run and the run is not a place for a foreman's family, and that is not coldness and it is not even much of a rule, it is a thing a man of about fifty does on a line where a foreman's daughter working beside him every day is the fastest way in the world to have the whole camp decide what he is.

She stood about too. She sat on a cart wheel about nine days' worth of afternoons and she did not ask and she was not sulking and she was nineteen and idle in a working season in a country where nobody is paid for anything they are not doing.

## The Fourth Hour Of The Fourteenth

She went out past the wire with a bar.

Not far. About two hundred yards, along the track that runs outside the ground, to the place where the ground is worst on the whole nine days and where there is nobody, because the ground is closed and a closed ground is a ground that is not anybody's job.

That is not trespass in any sense this world has a word for. There is no county here. The rule is the rule and it is nine years old and everybody on that course knows it and not one of them would have stopped her and not one of them would have said a word.

She stepped over a wire that is about a foot and a half high with a bar in her hand at about the fourth hour of the fourteenth.

She stood in the ring and put the bar in.

The boot went.

It went the way it goes. It came up over the top of it and the leg kept going and her weight went down into a foot of grey that is wet all the way down under the grey, and she had about a second and a half in which the whole of the thing she was going to do next was decided by what she did with a body at the fourth Foundation that had lost the order out of its left hand three weeks before.

She put her weight over the pull.

That is the whole of working the ring. That is all it has ever been. She got her weight over and the bar came up and about two feet of the worst ground on nine days came up with it, and her boot came out of the hole with the whole of her on top of the bar.

She did not fall.

Nothing broke. There was nobody there to see it and nobody was hurt, and it was not a rescue and it was not a close thing, and her arm was not even open.

She stood outside the wire in the worst ground on nine days for about an hour and a half.

## What That Was

There was nothing on earth she could do with being strong.

That is the whole of the combination and it is the worst combination available to a person. She was the strongest body on nine days of ground, and the ground is not a fight, and there is not one thing in the world that being the strongest body on nine days of ground is for, except turning a bar over in a foot of stuff that a man with eleven years of it in his hands and no order left in one of them can also turn.

She stood there with a bar and a foot of drift on it and she was of no use at all.

## The Thing She Was Ashamed Of

She was jealous of the nine men standing about in the camp at the west end.

On the fourteenth, in the ring, alone, with the boot coming up over the top of it, she worked out that she was jealous of nine men who are going to be measurably worse at a job in about a fortnight than she was at it last month, and that they are going to be worse at it because she is on a piece of paper.

And being ashamed of it did not stop it, and being right about being ashamed of it did not stop it either.

That is the honest shape of a person. There is no part of this in four years that has gone better than that: knowing exactly what you are doing, being certain that it is small, and doing it anyway.

She got back over the wire at about the sixth hour with the bar over her shoulder and the drift drying on the bank behind her in a heap that looks exactly like the ground it came from.

Nobody saw it. There was nobody within a hundred yards and about nine of the men who might have seen it were standing about in the camp at the west end not looking in that direction.

That is the point of it and it is the only thing about it that was any good.

## The Sixteenth

The month's sheet went west that evening in the satchel with the lamp and a tally stick and a strip of oiled paper with something off a stone on it.

It had nine days of her hours in it in a contractor's hand, all in order, all good, and a column with a foot and a half of paper in it and nothing in that, and it was going nine days west to a county that would file it.

She stood outside the wire in the dark and did not go and look at the board, because the board has a hole in it and she had already seen the hole.

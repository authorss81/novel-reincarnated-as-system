# The Courtesy

The sheet came round on the sixteenth and it was on the trestle in the morning with the day's other paper on it, and she was already at the trestle, because she was at the trestle at the fourth hour every morning and had been for five weeks.

She had been waiting for it since the eighth, which is a thing she would not have admitted to a person.

She got it out of a fold in her own head and looked at it in the ordinary way, standing there doing nothing in particular, at about the distance of six feet.

## The Second One

Two words in the county's own hand, in the attendance column, on the line for the sixteenth.

**MEMBER ABSENT.**

The same two words, in the same hand, on the same line, put there the same way by the same clerk in a room nine days and four hundred miles west in which there had been nobody at all, and she had now seen it twice and it was not going to change and there was not one page in the county that would ever take it out.

Beside it, in a column that is not joined to it, in a contractor's hand in ink that was two days old, eleven days of her hours in order with dates on them, every one of them good.

There is no quantity of hours that reaches it. She had worked that out standing at a trestle on the last of that month before, at length, and it takes her about as long as it takes to turn a bar over now.

It is not payable in hours. It is not payable in money. It is not a debt and it is not a fine and it is not a thing anybody did to her, and it is on her member's sheet in a column that belongs to a woman and not to a worker, and it is permanent, and it travels with the work.

She looked at it for about as long as it takes to breathe out.

## The Space Beside It

There is a space at the end of the line the width of about a foot and a half where a word could go.

Any person standing on the course may write a word there. He said so himself, at this trestle, on the sixteenth of the last month, unasked, in the ordinary voice, and he did not offer it as a favour and he did not offer it as a term and he did not make it into anything.

It says why. That is all a word does in that column. It does not take the attendance out and it does not put one hour on either side of it.

She had a true reason in her mouth that day and she had one today, and it is about nine words long and it is the same nine words: *on contract nine days east, working, paid by the day, no county within reach.*

And it goes in the column that the hours go in.

Every word she writes in that space is a line in the only book in the world that can say she worked a day at a bar in a country where no county paper has ever crossed. It is read in order, with dates, by an office past the boundary, on the strength of which she is licensed. Every word, for the rest of the season and after the season, in order, with the dates on.

So she did not write it.

That was the same decision and it took about as long as turning a bar over, and it is not what this morning was about.

## What Was Not Said The Second Time

At about the fifth hour he came past the trestle, and he read her line, because he comes and looks at everything that comes round, and he read the attendance column and he read the fourth column and then he stood there for a bit with his hands at his sides.

And then he went away.

That is all.

He said nothing. He did not say the sentence again. He did not say *I will tell you what that is* and he did not say *anybody on this course can write there* and he did not put his thumb on the paper well clear of the ink.

He read her line and he stood there and he went and wrote a man's hours against a stretch.

That is the whole of what happened at that trestle on the sixteenth of the month, and there was no second offer, and there was no refusal either, because a thing that is not said a second time has not been refused.

## What That Was

She stood there and worked out, in about the length of a bar being turned over, what she had been standing in for five weeks.

He was about thirty. He had been on that course nine years. He keeps a board. He brought her a blanket in the cold and a second blanket after the first Foundation without a word, and he told her the line out of his board in under a minute twice, both times for nothing, both times because she was standing in front of the board and he keeps it.

He does things for people and does not make conditions out of them.

The rule about the reason column is not a rule about the reason column. There is no rule about the reason column at all; it is a space on a sheet, and it was the space on the sheet that made the thing worth offering, and it would have been worth offering to anybody, and he would have said it to a carter standing in the same place.

What he did was say it once.

And saying it once was all of it, because offering it a second time would have turned a kindness into a term of something, and he is not that kind of man, and she knew that inside about a second of noticing that it had not come.

A person who is owed a kind word is not owed it twice.

And she was not even a woman to him. She was a hand on a board.

## The Thing She Had Not Known She Was Doing

That was the part that cost her.

Not the attendance. The attendance had been decided at the first of the month of the month before, in daylight, in front of people, and it had been hers.

This was something else entirely, and it was small, and she had not known it was there.

She had known since about the fourth hour of some day in the last month that a man on this course would say something, and she had gone on working, and the going-on-working had been made of the knowing.

There is a difference between a thing that has happened and a thing that a person has decided to expect, and the difference is that a thing that has happened has an end and an expectation does not, and an expectation is a structure you have built out of nothing that a person can take away in nine seconds by not arriving.

It took nine seconds to come down and it was still standing.

He had not known he was doing it. He had said one true thing in the ordinary voice to a woman standing in front of a board, and she had gone away and built a floor out of it.

That is the whole of what she had, and she had gone away and built it herself, out of about nine seconds of a man being decent.

## The Cold Was Still In Her

It was in her on the sixteenth, two inches under the breastbone, and it had been in her since the ninth of the month before last.

It had been four weeks then and it had not gone for any of the four weeks, and it was not better at the fire and it was not better under three blankets, and it was not the weather because eleven men were taking their coats off at the second hour.

She had not been hungry once in thirty days. That had gone with the first Foundation and it had not come back and she had stopped expecting it to and she had not had time to put anything under it either.

It had nothing to do with the money, because she had five days of it behind her and ten in front and had not been hungry through any of it, and a body that has never been full and cold at the same time is a body with one of the two switched off and not a body that is short of anything.

That was a thing she noticed about nine times a day for a working season and she never once got to the bottom of it, because the bottom of it was about ten minutes long and she did not have ten minutes in her that were not somebody else's.

She put her hand flat on a bunk board and thought about it for the length of a count.

Then the carrier's boy came up the track and somebody called the sixth hour and she went and got her bar.

The sheet went west that afternoon in a satchel, and the satchel went on the wall, and the sheet had two words in one column and eleven days of good hours in the other and about a foot and a half of empty ruled paper in between.

There is a foot and a half of paper going nine days west with a date on it, and a county clerk is going to look at it, and rule it, and write nothing in it, and put it in a file.

The county will read the whole of it as a fact about a woman who does not attend.

She chose the blank. She chose it in daylight on the third of a month for money that was already spent, and she chose it for the same reason she chose it the first time, and it is not a reason that is going to wear out.

# The Pressing

He was not there on the twentieth of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and sixty-four and she said that out loud at the seventh hour, standing, at the near end of that table, in the ordinary voice, in the dark, before she did anything else.

She said, “There is a boy of sixteen four miles down this mountain and he is not on that stair tonight, and this is one of the three, and I told him which three on the eighth of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and thirty-four and he has not told me which.”

She said, “That is the arrangement and I am not going to stand in a dark room being frightened of a boy who is four miles away doing the thing I told him to do.”

## Eleven And Fifteen

And then she did the sums, because that is what the ninth hour is for and because there was nothing else in the room to do them to.

She said, “Eleven days to the first of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and ninety-four, and fifteen days to the fifth of that month.”

She said, “Fifteen days, and that is the day, and I have had it in front of me since the twelfth of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and thirty-four and I have not been able to make it into a reason yet.”

And then she made it into a reason, and it took her about a count of ninety, and it came out in four sentences and she said them out loud in the dark.

She said, “A stage is taken in light. That is the whole of it. Every one of the seven I have taken was taken with a lamp burning at the far end of that table and a hand that came down over a board, and there is not one instrument in this building that can write a figure anywhere and I do not have a hand.”

She said, “And the only light in this room comes down to a cut in the stone on the first of a month. And there has not been anything in that cage since the first of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and thirty-four, so the one day out of thirty that I can be certain about is the one day out of thirty that this room is going to be dark.”

She said, “And it is going to be dark on the first of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and ninety-four, and it is going to be four days after that one that there is a lamp in this room, because that is the day the man brings forty boards up that stair and puts them down on that table in front of one oil lamp.”

She said, “So I am taking the seventh stage on the fifth of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and ninety-four, in oil, and the fifth is the one day I have, and I have had it since the twelfth and it is the first decision I have made in this building out of a lamp instead of out of a rule.”

## The One Objection

And the voice in her head said the thing about it before she had got as far as the figures, and he said it in the present tense, which is the only tense he has, and he said it once.

He said, “You are taking it against a lamp a boy carried up for you.”

She said, “I am.”

He said, “Then it is not taken against anything in this room.”

She stood there for a count of ninety and let that go all the way in, because it was the whole of the objection and it had been standing behind her since the twelfth of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and thirty-four and she had not heard it in that shape before.

She said, “Say it the way you want it said.”

He said, “A stage taken against a thing that can be carried back down a stair is a stage taken against something somebody can take away, and you have spent nineteen years on the rule that nothing in this room can be taken away from you, and that is the first one you have broken on purpose.”

And she stood there in the dark at the near end of that table with her right arm open at her side and worked on it, and it took her about a hundred and forty counts, and she did not get an answer.

She said, “I know.”

She said, “I have got both halves of that and they are both true and I have not got a way of standing on both of them, and I am going to leave it there until I have said it out loud in this room on my own for about eleven days, because that is the rule I keep.”

He said, “You keep it.”

She said, “I have kept it for nineteen years and it has not once been wrong and it is going to be wrong here.”

He said, “Then take it anyway.”

She said, “I am not taking it tonight. Tonight I am doing the figures, and the figures are the only part of this I am allowed to be sure about.”

## Seventy-Four

And then she did the subtraction, and she did it out loud, and she had never once done a subtraction forward in nineteen years.

She said, “Two thousand five hundred and ninety-eight less two thousand five hundred and twenty-four.”

She stopped, because she had said it that way before and got it wrong, and she started it again, and the second time it came out whole.

She said, “Seventy-four days. That is from the sixth stage to the seventh and it is the biggest one I have had since the fourth and it is four hundred and forty-eight days since the first, and I have never taken one over a hundred and twenty-one.”

She said, “And I have not got a rule. I have got three. The third was at one hundred and twenty-one and the fourth was at one hundred and thirteen and the fifth was at fifty-eight and the sixth was at sixty-three, and every one of those was a different reason for a different room, and I am not going to make one up tonight for the seventh.”

He said, “You do not know why it is seventy-four.”

She said, “No.”

He said, “You picked the day before you knew.”

She said, “I picked the day because it is four days past the one day out of thirty I can be certain about, and seventy-four is what the day turned out to be, and a subtraction is not a reason and I have been confusing the two of them for about four days and it is the reason I could not sleep on the nineteenth.”

## If There Is No Lamp

And then she did the sum she had been not doing since the fourth of that month, which was the one that mattered, and it came out in a very short time because she had done it four nights already.

She said, “If there is no lamp in this room on the fifth of the month beginning two thousand five hundred and ninety-four then I do not take the seventh stage.”

He said, “You do not have to.”

She said, “That is not why I am saying it and you know it is not. I am saying it because there is nothing in this building that can write a figure in the dark and there is not one instrument in this county that can do it, and I have been taking a stage in light since the seventh of the month beginning two thousand three hundred and eighty-four and I have never once done it in anything else.”

She said, “So on the fifth of that month, if he is not on the stair, then I stand four feet off the near end of this table at the ninth hour and I say seven stages into the seventh realm out loud, in the ordinary voice, standing up, in the dark, to a room with a person at the far end of it who has not looked up in two hundred and eighty-two days.”

She said, “And there is not one instrument in the world that would tell me whether it happened.”

He said, “That would be the same as saying it.”

She said, “It would not be the same as saying it, and that is the whole of what I have learned in nineteen years and I learned it in a ground with two hundred stones in it when I was nine years old, and I am not going to have a boy of sixteen tell me at the ninth hour on the fifth of that month that the two of them are the same.”

She said, “And then the seventh stage waits. And the interval is seventy-four days whatever I do, and the next one after that is a number I will not be able to pick, because I pick days for reasons and the reason for that one is the dark and there is going to be no dark.”

## Fifteen Nights

And at the ninth hour she said the last of it, standing, out loud, in the dark, with a room with nothing in it.

She said, “It is eleven days to the first and fifteen days to the fifth, and fifteen nights of this room between tonight and the day I have named, and on one of them the light does not depend on anybody at all and on the other fourteen it depends entirely on a boy of sixteen who has told me it will be three of every six and not which three.”

She said, “He said at the fourth of this month that he would be here on three of every six nights and that which three was his, and he gave that to me and not to me and I have not asked, and that means there is a lamp in this room on the fifth of that month or there is not, and I have not got one word in my head that tells me which.”

She said, “And that is the first stage of my life I have set that has an oil lamp in it instead of a lamp in a cage, and if there is no lamp on that day then I will not take it, and that is not frightening, and I have said that out loud and I would like somebody in this building to have heard it.”

He said, “I heard it.”

She said, “I know you did. That is the second thing that has happened in this room this month that I did not do on purpose.”

And she went down the low door and took the first four hundred and forty with her left hand on the rail, because it is worse going down, and the last four hundred and forty took her more than the first one did, and she did not go on to the second stair.

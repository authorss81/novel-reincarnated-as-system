# Priced From Above

The strip was on the gate post at the fourth hour on the twenty-fourth, and it was the same cloth, cut to the same shape, and it had the same three columns ruled on it.

She read it before she went down the bank because it was on the post at eye height and there was nothing to be done about reading a thing that is put up at eye height.

The rate for a reach of drain, off the ground of this gate, was five coppers.

On the fourteenth of this month it had been seven, said out loud by a man at his own gate in his own afternoon.

## Pei

He came out of the house about nine minutes later with his coat half on and read it standing in the same place he had stood on the fourteenth.

He read it twice, which she had not expected him to do.

“That is off this gate,” he said.

“Yes.”

“That is not you on it. That is the gate.”

“Yes.”

He folded it once and put it inside his coat and went to the shed and got the bar out of it, and then he came back and gave her the bar, which is what he had done on the fourteenth and was what he was going to do every day for as long as there was a drain on his ground.

“Seven is what the other gate pays,” he said. “So that is not his fault.”

“No.”

“It is the same work either side of a hedge.”

“No.”

He was right. That was the whole difficulty and it had been true since about the fourth hour of the fourteenth and had got truer by about nine minutes on the twenty-fourth.

A man with a field and a drain and a household does not decide for himself what an office above a copy floor thinks a woman is worth. He is told, and he pays what he is told, and he goes on being right.

## What The Word Is

She went down into the cut at about the fifth hour and worked a reach and a little over, and she thought about it the whole way down and all the way up.

There is a word at the top of that column.

It is the word that was put there at the second hour of the seventeenth of a month by her own mouth, in the open, in front of eleven men, and it went up a stair on the first cart after the thirtieth, and a man with a brush read it off a sheet in a room she had stood in nine hours ago.

It is a good word. There was not one thing wrong with it.

A good word in that sort of book is a certificate.

A certificate says the person does not need the whole of a day’s rate.

That is what it is for. That is what it has always been for. She had gone into the middle of a reach at the last working point in the light of a morning when she was seventeen because the only thing on that ground that could check a woman was a stage that eleven men could see her take, and she had got it, and it was still checking her, and this is what checking costs.

She wanted to be checked. She has been checked.

It is cheaper than her and she is the one being sold at it.

## Six

At the seventh hour he paid her out of the fold of cloth in the inside of the door frame, into her own hand, with the count done by him out loud.

Six.

She put it in the front of her coat where she kept it and stood at the gate with her hand closed over it and did the sum, because on this day she did the sum.

A reach and a little over at seven had been eight.

A reach and a little over at five was six.

Two coppers a reach, and a reach was what she could open, and there was not one single thing in the country between here and the far end of that flat ground that would let her open more of one in a day than she had opened on the sixteenth with four fingers and an arm at half and a bank two feet of clay over her on the day before last.

## The Third Reach

There were three reaches of it on that low ground and she had opened one and a little over.

The third one was the one that had been under the bank.

She looked down the bank at the low side from the top of it, at about the eighth hour, in the last of the light, and the cut was where she had been two days ago and the water was coming through the silt in it at about the rate water comes through silt, which is to say it was not coming through at all.

That was going to want a man in it with a spade and two men on a rope, and Pei knew that, and Pei had not asked her to go into it, and she had told him about the low side at his gate on the twenty-second and had not told him anything else.

She was not going to be the person in that cut. She had worked that out under two feet of clay and it had taken her about four minutes and she had not enjoyed any part of the four minutes.

## The Anger

It came up in her on the way back and it was not a hot thing.

It was the flat thing that comes when a person finds out that the whole of what has been done to her in this country was done to her by somebody who was right.

Five months. The claim answered in a room with nine men in it by a clerk who was right. A date taken off a page by a hand that had been bought and was right to take it. A furnace-man’s account closed in the only book on that ground where it could be closed, by a man who was right to close it. A brother’s name put in a line with no mark on it by a man who was right not to mark it. A word at the top of a column, entered by her, in daylight, on purpose.

Every single part of it had been done correctly.

And this morning a strip of cloth had come down off a cart and a woman had gone down into a drain for a day for two coppers less than she had gone down into it for the day before, and the word that had done that was the one she had wanted most in the world when she was seventeen.

That is the first thing she has found out in this country that she cannot spend on anybody, and she is not going to get it back by being angry at a farmer at a gate who has a crop in a field.

## What She Did Not Do

She did not say anything about it.

He had not asked her what she thought of a strip. She had not asked him to read it out to her. She had read it herself at eye height off a post and gone to work.

She did not thank him for paying her.

She was not going to thank him for paying her. It was not in her to make a thing of a man doing his own arithmetic at his own gate, and there was no arrangement between the two of them that could be finished with the word, and she had been on this ground long enough to know that putting the word in would have been the same as putting a mark on a line.

The arrangement stood. The strip stood. The word stood.

She went back up the bank at about the eighth hour and stood at the top of it in the dark with the bar across her shoulders and six coppers in a closed hand inside her coat, and she had opened a reach and a little over for two thirds of the money she had opened the same reach and a little over for on the sixteenth, and she was going to be back at that gate before the light.

A certificate is worth two thirds of a wage, and she had put it in herself, in the open, in daylight, in front of eleven men, for free.

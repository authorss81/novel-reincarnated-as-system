# The Long Table And The Grille

She went up at the third hour of the sixteenth of the month beginning 2,054, because she had told a boy of sixteen the night before that she was going up on the sixteenth and she had said it out loud and she has never yet gone back on a thing she said out loud.

She went up four flights to the copying floor and then kept going.

That is the whole of what is unusual about it and it took her about nine seconds. There is a stair at the west end of the copying floor behind the shelves where four hundred volumes are, and she has walked past that stair four hundred times in five years, and on the sixteenth of the month beginning 2,054 she went through the gap in the shelves and put her left hand on the rail and went up.

## Four Flights Above The Floor

First above the floor is the same nine feet by eleven as the third landing and it is not where the breath goes wrong. Second is the same. Third is not the third landing she knows and it does not take her breath, and she noted that and moved on.

Fourth is where it went. Not a landing. A step down off the fourth and then the top of the stair, and her breath went to nine and stayed there for a count of forty, and she stood on the top step in the dark with her left hand on the rail and her right arm open, four days in five, and let it be nine.

The dark was not dark. There was light coming up through a grille from the other side of a door, grey and flat, the kind of light that has been coming off the same four feet of sky for nine years and has never been moved.

There was a smell of cold iron and old smoke and paper that had been shut up longer than the boy of sixteen has been alive.

## The Low Door With The Grille

The door is low. She had to duck to get her eye to the bars, which are flat and close set in the top half, and there is a bar across the outside of it at about the height of her hand, and the bar is not locked, and it has not been lifted, because the dust on it is not broken anywhere.

She put her eye to it.

There is a long table down the middle of the room.

The table is plain deal and it is about the length of the bench on the floor below and there is nothing on it. There is a lamp at the far end on a shelf of the same kind as the shelf under the lamp on the fourth landing, and the lamp is out, and there is a stub of a candle beside it that has gone into the dish.

There are no chairs.

She looked for a while, with her eye against cold iron, at a room with a long table in it and a lamp that is not burning and no chairs, and then she went back down to the third landing and sat down on the step with her back to the wall, and did not go back up.

## Nine Years

The boy was at the top of the stair on the fourth landing when she came down and he had heard her go and had not come up.

She said, “How long has it been shut.”

He said, “I do not know.”

She said, “Look at the outside of the bar.”

He said, “I have looked at the outside of the bar.”

She said, “Then look at it properly with your face near it and tell me what you see.”

He put his face near it and she watched him do it and watched his face change, and it took no longer than a breath at seven.

He said, “Dust on the top of it.”

She said, “That is nine years of dust and not nine months. Nine months of dust in this building would be grey. That is the same colour as the sill and it has not been moved by anything and the bar has not been lifted and the door has not been opened.”

He said, “Nine years.”

She said, “Nine years.”

He said, “He never took me up.”

She said, “No.”

He said, “He kept the lamp going on the floor for twenty-two years and he never once took me up those four flights.”

## The Room With A Bar Across It

She went up again at the fifth hour and she went up it alone and she stood in front of the bar with her hands at her sides for a long time and did not put anything through it, and here is the whole of what she worked out, and she worked it out standing in the cold on a top step at the top of eight flights with her arm open and her breath on nine.

A room with a long table in it and a lamp on a shelf and no chairs is not a room for reading in. A room with a long table in it and a lamp and no chairs is a room that is stood in, at one end of the table, by a person who is waiting for something to be said to them.

A person stands at one end of a table to have a thing said to them.

She said it out loud on the top step, at the fifth hour, with the rail under her hand:

“An announcement goes where there is something to receive it.”

She said, “And a room like that has got nothing in it to receive one.”

She said, “And there is not one person in it.”

She said, “And those are one fact, and not two, and I have been carrying them as two for two days.”

She put her forehead against the back of her own wrist, which is the only way she has of standing still at height when her legs will not be trusted with it.

She said, “There has been nothing to receive an announcement in this room for nine years. And there has not been anybody to receive one for nine years. And there has not been anybody to receive one for nine years because there has been nothing to arrive for nine years, and there has been nothing to arrive for nine years because there has been nobody in it to arrive.”

She said, “A man of sixty-seven kept a lamp burning on the floor underneath this one for twenty-two years and made a boy of sixteen feed a lamp on the fourth landing for eighteen days, and he kept both of them going and he never once opened that door, and I am going to have to decide whether he was protecting something or whether he was the reason there was nothing there.”

She said, “And I am not going to be able to decide that from a stair.”

## The Bar Is On The Outside

She got up off the step and went back up the four flights and put her eye to the grille again and looked at the bar for the length of thirty counts, because there is one thing about a bar that a person standing on a stair will look at before anything else and she wanted it written down in the right order.

The bar is on the landing side.

Not through brackets on the room side. Not screwed into the frame from inside. It is a length of flat iron laid across the two uprights of the grille on the landing, and it goes in two sockets that are part of the door frame, and the sockets have a wear on them, and the wear is not new.

Somebody put that bar on from the landing, standing where she is standing, at a time when the door was still being shut from that side.

She came down four flights to the third landing below the floor and sat on the step and said it out loud to the stair, which is a thing she does now.

“Somebody shut this door from the outside nine years ago and put a bar on it so that it could not be opened from the landing, and they put the bar on the landing side on purpose, and then they went down four flights and never came back up.”

She said, “And a boy of sixteen has lived on the floor under it for two years and a man of sixty-seven kept a lamp going on the fourth landing under it for eighteen days, and not one of the three of them has ever been on the landing at the top of that bar.”

She said, “That is not a locked room. That is a room that was left in a state on purpose by a person who then agreed with himself about it.”

## Coming Down Eight Flights

She came down eight flights between the fifth hour and the seventh and it took her fifty minutes and she sat down twice on a stair, once on the third landing below the floor where she has known for five years that her breath goes wrong and once on the second, which she did not expect.

The boy was on the copying floor with the lamp topped up and the pile lower by four sheets than it was at the fifth hour.

He said, “You have been up there fifty minutes.”

She said, “I have been up there fifty minutes and there is a table in it.”

He said, “What is on the table.”

She said, “Nothing. That is the answer to what is on the table and it took me nine days of road and a question and eight flights to arrive at nothing, and I am going to say it once in this room and then I am not going to say it again in this room.”

He said, “Say it.”

She said, “An announcement goes where there is something to receive it. And the place where announcements go in this building is up four flights, and it has had nothing to receive one in it since I was ten years old, and there has not been a person in it since I was ten years old, and neither of those is a fault and both of those are the same fault.”

She said, “So the second half of the answer is on a table in a room, and the answer to the second half is that there is not one person in nine days of road who can be put in it.”

She put the book of eight leaves on the bench and opened it, and the fifth was blank, and the sixth was blank, and the seventh was blank, and the eighth was blank, and she looked at the four of them for the length of twenty counts and did not write on them, and she shut it and put it back against her ribs on the side where four sheets of a fair hand used to be.

She said, “I went up eight flights and came down eight flights and the only thing I have found out is what was not there.”

She said, “And there is one thing I have not worked out and it is the whole of what I have walked west for. I do not know what a board is for. I have been reading boards since I was twelve and I have never once stopped to ask what one is for.”

# Public Property On A Stick

She went east at the first hour of the fourth of the month beginning 2,264 and she was at the north end of a ground of two hundred people at the second hour of the sixth, and she did not tell anybody in between anything at all.

## Two Days Of Road And A Board Kept Going

The wall stands open four feet and a bit and she went through it at the seventh hour of the fourth with a bar on her left shoulder and no strap on it, and there is nothing behind it, and there has been nothing behind it for a long time.

She put the bar down twice between the gate and the wall and once at the gate and once in the first mile of the ground, and she was at the south end at the ninth hour of the sixth and walked up four miles of ground in the dark with about a hundred people lying down about it.

And at the north end, in the dark, under a board four feet off the ground, there was writing.

Two thousand two hundred and sixty-nine less two thousand one hundred and twenty-eight is a hundred and forty-one nights, and a hundred and forty-one times four is five hundred and sixty-four, and there are five hundred and sixty-four strokes on it and a number under every one of them and four men who put them in cannot read the hand of any of the other three.

And about forty feet off that board, under the same dark, there was a woman of about thirty-four with a slate on her knees and a man of forty standing behind her, and they were not waiting for anybody.

## Fourteen

There were fourteen at the frame on the sixth and thirteen of them were not waiting for her.

She stood at the south end for a count of sixty before she came up and she counted what she needed to count, which is the number of people doing a sum for somebody else, and it is thirteen, and it was nine the last time she was in this ground.

Forty people in this ground can count to eighty. She counted them again on the seventh because she cannot help it, and she got forty, and it is not going to be a different number next time.

She did six sums at the frame over two days and got six right, and on the seventh a boy of seventeen asked her whether four thousand and ninety stones at two to a cart is a whole number of carts, and she did it in a count of twenty and he wrote it down wrong on purpose because he wanted to do it again.

## What The Frame Is Now

And it is worth saying what the frame has become, because it is the only thing in this county that has changed in nineteen years and she made it change.

There was a frame with a slate on it and a man at it and about four people a day standing in a line, and the man had a book and a rule and he was right about everything and he took a small piece of the day off anybody who stood at it.

There is a frame with a slate on it and fourteen people who do not need a man at it, and two or three of them are under twenty-five, and the man still comes and still has his book and still is right about everything and about a third of his work has gone somewhere he cannot follow.

The woman of thirty-four checks the working of the man of forty before she gives an answer on. She has done that every day since the sixth and she does not ask him and he does not ask her, and it is the first time in nineteen years that anything at that frame has been checked by anybody but the man doing it.

And the boy of seventeen got his carts wrong on purpose.

## The Third Reason

And that is where the thing changed, and it changed on the sixth of the month beginning 2,264 at the ninth hour at the south end of a ground of two hundred people with a bar on the ground at her feet, and she said it out loud, standing, in daylight, to the board.

She said, “I had three reasons for not telling these people what a figure on a wall is. I have said two of them out loud in this ground and they are still true.”

She said, “The first is that I told nine people at the ninth hour of the eighteenth of the month beginning 2,144 that I was not going to be the reason there was a second one, and I have been found twice in nine days and I know what that does to the person it happens to.”

She said, “The second is that I do not know what it does. I have ruled out four things about a figure on a wall in a building in this county and found the fifth, and I am nineteen and I have one good arm and I do not know what comes off a ground of two hundred people when two hundred people know what they are being counted for.”

And then she said the third one, and she said it differently, and she had to stop for a count of nine and start again.

“The third one used to be that I cannot stop counting. And that one is still true and it is the only one of the three that is going to be true in twenty-five days.”

## What Expired

She said, standing, in daylight, at the north end of two hundred rows of stones:

“I have not changed my mind about telling them. What has changed is that the thing I was protecting them from has a date on it.”

She said, “On the first of the month beginning 2,294, which is in twenty-three days, I am going to stand at a frame at a turn two days of road west of this ground at the first hour and write a line in my own hand on a slate four feet off a store road, and it is going to have my name on it and my own figure on it, and four carts go past that frame a day, and anybody at all can stop and read it.”

She said, “And any one of the fourteen of you can walk two days of road and read it, on a bar, with nothing to lie down on between here and the gate, and there is one thing at the far end of it and it is the only thing in this county that has ever been written down about me.”

She said, “And it does not say on the board that a figure comes from a column. It says the figure. And the whole of what a figure comes from is a subtraction, and it has been on a slate at that frame in a county hand since the first of the month beginning 2,174, in a column on the other side that faces a wall, and it is not hidden, because there is nothing about it to hide.”

She said, “So I have been standing here for thirty days protecting thirteen people from a thing that is going to be nailed up in public in twenty-five days by a person who is not them.”

## Why She Did Not Tell Them Anyway

And then she did the part that cost her, which is that she worked out what she was going to do about it, standing, in daylight, in the ordinary voice, with about forty people lying down about forty feet off.

She could go up to that frame in twenty-five days and tell the fourteen first. It would take about an hour. She has done it in front of two hundred people at the ninth hour and it cannot be un-said and she would do it again.

Or she could let the slate do it.

And she stood there and found that she was going to let the slate do it, and that she was going to do it on purpose, and that it was the correct decision and she had three reasons for it and all three were the same reason.

She said, “If I say it, it is a thing a woman said at a frame in a ground, and in four months there will be fourteen versions of it and every one of them will be about me and about whether I said it, and in a year the arithmetic will not be remembered at all.”

She said, “If it is on a slate at a turn with my hand on it, then it is a fact in a county. It does not have a woman in it. It has a date and a place and a figure and it is true and it does not matter in the slightest who said what to whom.”

She said, “And that is what I want, and I am going to have to be able to live with not knowing which of them worked it out first, and there are thirteen of them doing sums at that frame and one of them will get there before I do, and I am not going to be able to stand next to that one when it happens.”

## What She Did With Two Days

And she said the last of it out loud at the ninth hour of the seventh of the month beginning 2,264, at the north end of the board, standing, in daylight, with her arm open and the bar on the ground.

“I came two days of road to say that sentence to myself and I have said it and it is the correct sentence and I did not say any of it to them.”

She said, “And I am not going to tell them. And it is not any of my three reasons.

It is that I want it to be a fact in a county and not a thing a woman said, and that is a reason I would not put in front of anybody, and it is the only one I have got, and I have said it out loud in a ground of two hundred people who are all asleep and that is the same thing I have been doing for nine years and I am not going to stop now.”

She counted the board at the ninth hour with the light going, because that is what she does.

Five hundred and sixty-eight, because it is a hundred and forty-two nights now and there will be a stroke at the top that the man with the second hour puts in early, and she leaves it every time, and she left it again, and she is lighter for seven hundred and ten days and not one of them has gone back, and she has twenty-four days to find a line and she has not got one word of it.

# He Worked His Out Five Years Ago

She went into the rows at the eighth hour of the eighth of the month beginning 2,264 because she had said out loud at the north end board on the seventh that she was going to, and she has never in her life said a thing out loud and not done it.

## The Third Row

Ning Wu was forty feet in, in the third row, doing what the four of them do.

He has the hands of their father and he is nineteen and he has carried stones for four men for nine years and he has not said her name to her since a road five miles west of a mill nine years ago, and he did not say it now.

She stood at the end of the row for a count of forty. The ground goes up a foot and a half every eight rows and she had been at the bottom of it for eight days and it is not a hill you can hide anything on.

He worked the way the four of them work, which is that he does not do it in a rhythm. He goes at the pace the man in front of him goes at and stops when that man stops and there is no counting in it that anybody could see from four feet away.

## How Many Stones

She asked him one. Standing up, in daylight, one at a time, the way she asks everything, because there is not one person in this ground she is entitled to ask about a figure and he is the only one she would have asked anyway.

She said, “How many.”

He said, “How many what.”

She said, “Stones. Since you got here. All four of them.”

He looked up for about two seconds and then said, “Why.”

She said, “Because I want to know if anybody else in this ground has worked out a figure about themselves and never given it to anybody.”

He went back to the stone.

## What She Said Instead Of Answering

And then she said it, and she had decided to say it in a yard of grit and then had decided to say it here, and the deciding took her four days and she did not know why she had changed.

She said, standing, in daylight, at the end of a row of stones, in the ordinary voice:

“I have worked out my own total.”

He said, “Say that again.”

She said, “I have worked out my own total. What this county has taken off me since the twenty-fourth of the month beginning 2,054. It is a figure in days and hours and I worked it out on my own and I have not got a single thing to check it against except a slate at a turn two days of road west of here.”

She said, “And I said it out loud in front of two hundred people at the ninth hour of the fourteenth of the month beginning 2,144 and it was the first one anybody had ever been told in this county, and it cannot be un-said, and there are thirteen people at the frame at this ground who can do that sum in their heads now and nine of them were not doing it before I said it.”

## Five Years

He put the stone down.

He said, “How many.”

She said, “I am not going to tell you the figure, because you have not asked me for it and you do not know what a figure is in this county, and I said it to say that I have said one.”

He said, “Then what are you telling me for.”

And she was one breath into a bad answer when he said the other thing.

He said, “Five years.”

She said, “Say that again.”

He said, “Five years I have worked out how many stones I have moved on this ground. Four men. Nine years. I know it to the stone and it is a number that has never come out of my mouth and I have never written it down and there is nowhere in this ground it could go.”

She said, “You have counted them.”

He said, “I have carried them. You do not get to carry four thousand stones for nine years and not know.”

## The Same Sentence

And then they stood at opposite ends of a row of stones and said the same thing, and neither of them planned it, and it is the whole of what happened in four days.

He said it from his end first.

He said, “There is nobody to give it to.”

And she said it from hers, four feet of ground away, at the same time, in the ordinary voice, the exact same words, because that is what two people do when they have both been carrying a thing they have not got anywhere to put.

He looked at her.

She looked at him.

And that was it, and there was not one more word about it on either side of that row for four days, and she went and did four sums at the frame and he went back to a stone.

## What He Said About A Board

On the tenth of the month beginning 2,264 she came down the third row again at the eighth hour and stood at the end of it and told him one more thing, because she had promised herself on the seventh that she would and she keeps what she promises herself out loud.

She said, “I am not going to tell them. The thirteen at the frame. I have three reasons and two of them are good and the third one is that I cannot stop counting.”

He said, “That is not a reason.”

She said, “It is the third one.”

He said, “You worked out a figure about yourself, and there are two hundred people on this ground who did not know there was one, and you are going to write it on a board at a turn in twenty-one days and let a county walk up and read it.”

She said, “Yes.”

He said, “That is worse than telling them.”

She said, “Why.”

He said, “Because a thing said at a frame is said by a person and a person can be asked. A thing on a board is a thing in a county and a county does not get asked. I have been moving stones into a county for nine years and there is not one of the four men who can say how many, and if one of them could I would not be able to do anything about it.”

He went back to the stone.

## What She Counted From The End Of A Row

And she counted, because she cannot help it, and she counted from the end of the third row at the eighth hour of the eighth of the month beginning 2,264 with a bar on the ground at her feet.

Two hundred people in that ground and forty of them can count to eighty, and she has said that figure out loud in front of nine people and has not been able to make it smaller.

There are forty people who can take any two of them who know how and work out inside a breath what is coming off any of them. She said that out loud too, at the end of a row of stones, to one person, and then stopped, because it is her arithmetic and not his and there is no part of a brother in it.

And there are forty people in that ground who have no way of being found out that she knows of, and she has thought about that for two days and has not told anyone, and she is not going to, and the reason she is not going to is the reason she has had since the eighteenth of the month beginning 2,144 and it is still a good reason.

He said, “You counted.”

She said, “I count everything.”

He said, “Then you have got a figure on me.”

She said, “I have got a figure on everyone. It is the only thing I have ever had.”

He went back to the stone.

## The Four Days She Stayed

She stayed in that ground four more days because a person who has told one person a figure cannot go on the next morning and behave as though she had not.

On the eleventh she carried stones for an hour, because he put one at the end of the row and did not ask her to and did not stop her either, and it is forty pounds and she put down forty of them over the course of an hour without once getting her breath above four counts, which she was not expecting.

On the twelfth a man of fifty came to the end of the third row and said, to Ning Wu and not to her, that the fourth man had been short for three days running and that somebody was going to have to say so.

And Ning Wu said, “Say it.”

And the man said, “I am saying it.”

And he did not say anything else, and he went off up the row, and Ning Yan stood four feet away and worked out in one breath that a man can lose three days of stones out of a county and be told about it by one person in this county, and that the system has no other part.

On the thirteenth she asked him, standing, in daylight, one question, whether the stones went anywhere.

He said, “Where.”

She said, “I do not know. I have never worked it out. Do they go somewhere.”

He said, “They go into a wall.”

## What Was Not Settled

And that was all of it, and nothing was settled, and she stayed in that ground four days and did not settle it.

He did not call her anything. She did not call him anything.

He has the hands of their father and he is nineteen and he carries four thousand stones for four men a day and has counted every one of them for five years and has never said the number to a living person until the eighth of the month beginning 2,264, at the eighth hour, at the end of a row, to her, and is not going to say it again.

And she has told one person a figure she worked out on her own, in a ground of two hundred people, and there are two of them in the county now, and she did not go and tell the thirteen, and she is not going to.

And on the eleventh of the month beginning 2,264 she counted the board at the north end with the light going and it was five hundred and eighty-four strokes on it.

There was one at the top that the man with the second hour had put in early, and she left that one too, and she is lighter for seven hundred and fourteen days.

There are twenty days to the first of a month beginning 2,294 and she has not got one word of the line, and the only person in this county who knows her figure is thirty feet away and does not want to know it.

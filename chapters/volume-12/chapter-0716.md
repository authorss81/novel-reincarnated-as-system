# A Count And Not A Person

She went east at the first hour of the twenty-first of the month beginning 2,264 and she was at the end of a counter in a room with the shutters up at the second hour of the twenty-third, with a hundred people reading behind her and the sun on the boards.

Two days of road from a turn to a ground of two hundred people, and four miles of ground, and a made road with carts on it, and she had walked it before and she had said no in this room twice in four years and both times with a figure in her hand.

## The Road A Line Has To Travel

She was not here for the sheet and not one person offered her one, and she said so on the first afternoon to a man of sixty-five with a book who is the only person in this building who has ever been right about her in a way that cost anything.

She said, standing, in daylight, one question at a time, at the end of a counter, with a hundred people reading behind her and four counts of breath in her chest where every other person in the room was breathing the ordinary way:

“I am putting a line on a slate at a turn on the first of a month beginning 2,294. Does this building sit on the road.”

He said, “No.”

She said, “Where does it sit.”

He said, “It sits on the road between the seat and the square. That turn is off the road to the north, past the end of the wall, and I have never walked to it.”

She said, “And a line on a slate at that turn goes up a road to the seat.”

He said, “It goes up a road to the seat in a bundle with about forty other boards in it, once a month, on a cart, and four people in the seat have never in their lives looked at one of those boards.”

She said, “And then.”

He said, “And then a bundle goes up a stair in a tower four miles up a mountain from the square, on the arm of a man who has carried four of them in thirty-one years, and on each of the four a man came out of that door and took his hat off, and it comes down empty.”

She stood at the end of that counter for a count of sixty.

She said, “Say the last of it again.”

He said, “The slate goes up the road. The bundle goes up the stair. Not one person in this building is on that road and not one person in this building has ever been asked to look.”

## Eighty-Four

And then she worked out the interval out loud at the end of the counter on the twenty-fourth of the month beginning 2,264, standing, in daylight, with a sheet on a wall behind her carrying ninety-eight thousand against a sum of ninety-five thousand and eighty and a difference of two thousand nine hundred and twenty that is not attributed to anything in this building and is not the levy.

She said, “Two thousand two hundred and ninety less two thousand two hundred and six is eighty-four.”

She said, “And the one before that was seventy-eight, and the one before that was a hundred and thirty-one, and the seventy was because there was a person standing in the way of it, and the eight on top of seventy was because the receiver had to be found first.”

She said, “And the six on top of seventy-eight is because this one has a date on it. Putting a stage on a board takes longer than making a stage, because there is a date on it and the date has to be a first of a month, and a first is thirty days apart from the next first and there is not one other date in this county a board will go up on.”

He said, “That is right.”

She said, “Say it once for the room.”

He said, “I am not going to say it for the room. There are a hundred people reading behind you and not one of them could tell you what a stage is.”

## Four Days And A Sheet On A Wall

She stayed four days and she did not read for anybody and not one person asked her to.

She stood at the end of the counter and watched a hundred people read in a room with the shutters up, and a woman of sixty-three stood behind it and squared every sheet she was handed with two fingers and did not look at anybody.

Ning Yan thought about that for a count of sixty on the first afternoon and did not go and speak to her, because she has said no to that woman twice in four years and there is not one more thing in this county she has to say to her.

And she looked at the sheet on the wall every day of the four, because it is the only piece of arithmetic in this building that is anybody’s business and she is the only person in this county who could read it, and she read it and put it down and did not say it out loud.

## The Twenty-Seventh Of The Month Beginning 2,264

It came at the second hour of the twenty-seventh of the month beginning 2,264.

She was standing at the end of the counter in a room with a hundred people in it and four counts of breath in her chest and her right arm open and a book in the hand of a man of sixty-five who was not reading.

It took the length of a breath. There was no announcement. There is no voice and there is nothing in the air and there is not a line anywhere in this building or out of it, and a hundred people did not look up, because a hundred people in a room with the shutters up are not looking up.

She said, out loud, in the ordinary voice, at the end of a counter, to a man of sixty-five with a book, the only sentence out of her:

“How many days since the third of the month beginning 2,204.”

He said, “Eighty-four.”

She said, “Say what it is called.”

He said, “That is not mine to call. What is mine to tell you is what it costs, and I am going to tell you that once and I am not going to soften it and you are not going to be able to do anything about it, and then I am going to go back to my book.”

## The Price Of It Said Once

He said, “You cannot be counted where you stand.”

And then he said the rest of it in the ordinary voice, and he did not look up from the counter and he did not put it down gently, and it took a count of forty.

“The count is a count of boards. It is not a count of people. There has never been a count of people in this county in eleven hundred years and there is not going to be one, and everything that has ever come off you came off you off a piece of wood with your name on it in a hand that was not yours.”

He said, “You are not on the board.”

She said, “I have been on a board at a turn since the twenty-fourth of the month beginning 2,054.”

He said, “That is nine days of other people’s counting and not one half of you. What goes up a stair is a line. And you are not in the line, and you are not on the wood, and there is not one thing in this county that can put you in one.”

He said, “And that has been true of you for years and you have carried it every day of that time and you have not once been in a place where it cost you anything, and it is going to cost you something on the first of the month beginning 2,294.”

He said, “Everything you have written down in nineteen years has been a record you keep. On that day it is not. On that day it is in a room and it is in a book and it is not attached to you in any way that a room can use, and a room will read it and there will not be one person in it that knows a woman wrote it at the first hour in the grit with a bar at her feet.”

And then he said the last of it, which is the whole of it.

“The harvest takes a board. It has never taken a person in this county and there is no instrument in the world that knows how. If you want what is on that line to arrive anywhere at all, it has to go up on its own, and it will go up on its own, and you will be standing at a frame when it does.”

## The Finding And Not The Cost

And she said one thing, and it was not that.

She said, “Then it goes up on its own.”

He said, “Yes.”

She said, “Four days of road and I have got until the first hour of the first of the month beginning 2,294.”

He said, “You have got until the first hour.”

She said, “I have been lighter every day since the eighteenth of the month beginning 1,544 and not one of them has gone back, and it is seven hundred and thirty days, and not one single day of that has been counted by a person in a room.”

He said, “No.”

She said, “I know that. You did not have to tell me that. You told me the other thing instead, which is correct, and that is the whole of what you did.”

And then she said the finding, out loud, at a counter in a room with a hundred people reading behind her, and it took her a count of nine.

“A room will read a line and it will not know a woman wrote it. And I have spent nineteen years making sure that everything anybody has ever read about me has had my name at the end of it, and I have just worked out that the one that goes up on the first is not going to have one, and I am going to write it anyway, and I would like it said out loud in this room that I knew which way round it was.”

She said, “And there is not one person in this county I could ask whether to do that.”

He said, “There is not one person in this county who could have told you anything.”

And she walked out of a building with the shutters up at the third hour of the twenty-seventh of the month beginning 2,264 with four days of road in front of her and a bar on her left shoulder, and she was lighter for seven hundred and thirty days and not one of them had gone back, and she has not got one word of the line and she is not going to get it in this building.

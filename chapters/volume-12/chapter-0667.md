# A Sentence He Cannot Finish

The fourteenth of the month beginning 2,054 was another day in the room and it produced nothing new, and she did not ask, and he did not say anything about the fact that she had not asked, and the two of them were extremely careful with each other for eleven hours and it was the worst kind of day she has ever had in a building.

On the fifteenth of the month beginning 2,054, at the sixth hour, standing, in daylight, at the east end of the bench, with the boy at the rack four feet away and the lamp burning in a cage overhead, he said:

“I have got the rest of the first half.”

## Two Days Of Not Asking

She said, “Say it.”

He said, “Before I do. I am not saying it because you asked me again.”

She said, “I know. I did not ask. I have not asked since the twelfth and I have worked out every hour of two days about why I was not going to.”

He said, “I want that said in the room, because in about a minute the half of it I have got is going to be a rule, and a rule that has been asked after is a rule that has been paid for.”

She said, “Say that now.”

He said, “It has not been asked after. You have had two days of not asking in a room with a witness in it and the witness is sixteen and he has counted for me.”

The boy said, without turning round, “I have counted them.”

## The First Half Said Properly

He said, “An announcement goes where there is something to receive it.”

She said, “You said that on the twelfth.”

He said, “I said the half of it. Here is the whole of the first half, and it is three sentences and I have had it since the eighth hour of the tenth and I could not get it into shape.”

She said, “Say them.”

He said, “One. An announcement is not made to be heard. It is made to arrive. There is a difference and the difference is the whole of it, because a thing that is made to be heard can be missed by anybody who is not listening, and a thing that is made to arrive has to have somebody standing in the place it is arriving at.”

She said, “And two.”

He said, “Two. If there is a person in the place, it arrives. If there is not one in the place, it goes into the ground, and it does not fail, and nothing is broken, and there is no sign anywhere that it did not arrive, and the only way anybody would ever know is if they stood in the place afterwards and looked at the ground.”

She said, “And three.”

He said, “Three. It is not the announcement that has to be found. It is the room. A person does not go and get announced. A person goes and stands in a room where an announcement can arrive, and the arrival is the part that is not in the announcement’s own keeping.”

## Nine Times Into The Ground

She stood at the bench and did the arithmetic she had been doing for nine years without having had the shape of it until that second.

She said, “Nine.”

He said, “Nine.”

She said, “Nine times I have been announced and nine times I was standing on made road or at a wall or on a bar in a yard, and not one of the nine times was there a room with a person in it, and every one of them went into the ground where I was standing, and there is nothing on that ground anywhere and there is no mark.”

She said, “I have been waiting nine years for the sky to say something back to me and the sky was never the thing. I was waiting to be standing in a room. There has not been one.”

The boy said, from the rack, “That is the second time in three days you have told me that everything you were waiting for was on this side of the wall.”

She said, “Do not say that in this room.”

He said, “There is one of us.”

She said, “There are three of us and two of them are standing here.”

## Why He Cannot Finish It

She said, “Now say the second half.”

He said, “I cannot.”

She said, “You said on the twelfth that you had been trying for two days and you could not get past a door.”

He said, “I can get a little past the door now. That is what these three sentences were. I could not get past a door with them in my mouth and I have got them now.”

She said, “And past the door.”

He said, “The second half is not a thing that can be said in a room to a person standing in it.”

She said, “Say why.”

He said, “Because it is a set of instructions and it is addressed to a body. It is written in the way that instructions are written, for a person who has hands, and who is going to have to be in a room at a certain hour on a certain day, and who is going to have to be able to hold a thing and go through a door with it. And I have never been any of those things, and I have never been in a room, and I cannot read an instruction that is addressed to a body because I do not have one to address it to.”

She said, “You have told me this.”

He said, “I know. I told you on the twelfth that the arriving needs a room and a person in the room and a hand. That was the whole of what I had. I did not know until this morning that it was addressed to a body and that is why I could not get past it. That is not a better answer. It is a smaller one.”

She said, “It is a smaller one and it is the end of the road for you.”

He said, “It is the end of the road for me.”

## The Long Way Round A Question

She said, “Give me one thing before I go up.”

He said, “One.”

She said, “If there is a set of instructions in a room that is addressed to a body, then somebody wrote them for a body, and a person wrote them, and a person wrote them because at some point a person was going to stand in that room and read them and do what they say.”

He said, “Yes.”

She said, “Then who was it written for.”

He said, “I do not know.”

She said, “You have had thirty-one years and you cannot finish a sentence about a set of instructions in this building and you cannot tell me whose body it is addressed to.”

He said, “No.”

She said, “Then say this instead. Say whether the instructions are still there.”

That was a different question and he heard it as a different question, and she watched him hear it, and she had not meant to be clever and was not proud of being clever.

He said, “That one I can do.”

She said, “Do it.”

He said, “They are still there.”

She said, “How do you know they are still there.”

He said, “Because a bundle went through that door four times in thirty-one years and the last one was nine years ago, and a bundle is what a thing is put in when it is going to be said, and a carter who has carried for thirty-one years does not carry an empty square of oilcloth up a hill.”

She said, “So the carter is the reason I know there is a room.”

He said, “The carter is the reason you know there is a room and you have never spoken to him and he came up the hill on the ninth on his own time.”

She stood with that for a moment.

She said, “Then go and get the second half out of it and come and tell me what is there.”

He said, “I have told you the limits of what I can do.”

She said, “I know. I am not asking you for more than you have. I am telling you that the thing you cannot finish is not in you and it is not me and it is not him either, and the three of us are standing in a room at the bottom of a stair.”

He said, “Then somebody has to go up it.”

She said, “Yes.”

## What Neither Of Them Has Been Up

She said, “Then the second half is in this building.”

He said, “It is in this building.”

She said, “Somewhere in it.”

He said, “The only place in this building that a thing could be put where a person would arrive at it, and it is not the floor and it is not the yard, because the yard is where bundles are put down and not where things are said to anybody.”

She said, “Then it is above us.”

He said, “It is above us.”

She said, “And neither of us has ever been up there.”

He said, “Neither of us has ever been up there, and one of us cannot go, and that is the first time in eight years that a question of mine has had a place in it that you cannot come with me to.”

She did not answer that. She stood at the end of the bench with her hand flat on the wood and looked at the ceiling of the copying floor, which is boards and beams and forty years of lamp smoke, and then at the far wall, where the stair goes up behind the shelves.

She said, at the seventh hour of the fifteenth of the month beginning 2,054, standing, in daylight, in a room with a lamp in a cage and a boy of sixteen at a rack and a drawer with a lock on it four feet away:

“You have given me the first half and it is nine years of my life explained in three sentences, and the second half is four flights up a stair that has not been climbed by a living person in nine years, and I am going to go and get it, and you are going to stay here, and that is the first thing either of us has ever had to do that we did not choose.”

The boy said, from the rack, “When.”

She said, “The sixteenth. I have got today and I am going to spend today on nothing.”

He said, “Why.”

She said, “Because I have been in this room six days and I have asked one question and been answered half of it, and every hour I have spent in here since the twelfth I have been a person waiting to be allowed to go somewhere, and I am not going up four flights as a person waiting to be allowed.”

She said, “I am going up them as a person who has put a thing down in a drawer and left it there on purpose, with a key in the front of my bag and a boy who knows where it is, and that is a different person and it is the person I have to be to be allowed in.”

He said, “That is four sentences about a thing you have done and not one about the room.”

She said, “Then I will say one about the room.”

He said, “Say it.”

She said, “I do not know what is up there. I have said that once and it has cost me nine years of waiting and it is still true at the seventh hour of the fifteenth, and I am going up four flights tomorrow to find out, and if it is nothing then I will come down them with nothing, and there will be a boy of sixteen at the bottom of them who will have watched me go up and watched me come down.”

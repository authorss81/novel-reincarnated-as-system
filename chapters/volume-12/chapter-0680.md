# What Goes In The Book

The sheet was still on the table at the fourth hour of the twelfth of the month beginning 2,084 and the sun had come round and was on the other side of it, and about a hundred people were at the tables with a lamp on every one of them that was worth nothing at that hour, and the man with the book had come to the end of the counter and put the book down and opened it.

## The Night Before The Word

She slept in a yard four miles west of that building with the bar lying along her side, in the way she sleeps, and she was awake for all of it in the way she is awake for all of it.

Here is what she did between the ninth hour of the eleventh of the month beginning 2,084 and the fourth hour of the twelfth, standing and sitting and lying on the flat with a bar along her side, and it took her the whole of that night, and she has never done anything in her life more carefully.

She went through the sixth line.

The six lines are not a negotiation. She has been treating them as one for five years and they are not one, and the reason is the fifth.

The first four lines are the trade. They exist whether or not anybody takes them, because the trade was running before she came through the gate and it will run after she stops being able to be paid, and there is a room with a shelf in it and a lamp on the wall of it, and a rate is a figure that does not depend on her.

The fifth line is the name.

And the fifth line is the only one that needs the other four. You do not put a name in a book because you can read well. You put it in because you have been given a rate and a room and a call and a shelf, and having been given those four things you are a person whose reading can be relied on inside a set of walls, and there has to be a name at the bottom of that or the other four are a description of a kind of hand.

Which means the fifth line is not an extra. It is the one the other four are for.

She said that out loud, lying on flat ground four miles west of a building with a hundred lamps in it, at about the second hour of the night of the eleventh of the month beginning 2,084:

“Then there has only ever been one thing on that sheet and it has been the same thing for five years and I have been reading five lines of a trade and calling it a refusal of a room with the shutters up, and I got the room right four years ago because the room is what frightened me, and it was never the room.”

And then she worked out, lying down, on her back, with the bar along her side and her right arm open, whether the fifth line can be got round, and she worked it out four ways in about an hour, and it cannot.

There is no version of that sheet with the first four lines and not the fifth. Not one. She tried it as a person who declines the fifth line on one day and takes it on the sixth, and that is a person who is not in the book and does not get the rate. She tried it as a person who takes it and puts a hand on the name and lifts it off, and there is no hand in that building that takes one off, and a woman of sixty-three told her that on the seventh and she has believed it every day since.

She tried it as a person who never comes back to the gate.

That one works perfectly. It is the one she has been doing for five years. It has cost her the only trade in this county that pays for the only thing she is good at, and she got nothing at all for it, and she did not know at that point that she was not being offered it in the first place.

## The Figure

She said no.

She said it in the ordinary voice, standing up, in daylight, in a room with the shutters up, at the fourth hour of the twelfth of the month beginning 2,084, and it took nine words, and she had them ready at the fourth hour of the eleventh and she did not use them for a day.

“No. I will not be recruited. I have thought about it for five years and I have got a figure in my hand about it and you are going to hear the figure.”

“I am nineteen years old,” she said.

That was the first part and it is not a figure anybody in that room can do anything with, and she had known that when she said it, and she said the rest of it anyway.

“Every name in that book was nineteen when it went in. Four hundred and seven of them. Not one of them was eighteen and not one of them was twenty. A woman of sixty-three told me at the seventh hour of a month five years back that she was nineteen when she came in here and it has been forty-four years.”

She said, “So I am not refusing a trade. I am standing in the room where the only thing I have in my hand is the one number that everybody in that book had, and I have had it for a year, and I have not done one thing with it.”

## What She Said And Why She Said It

The woman of sixty-three had her hands behind her back and she did not interrupt, which she did not do five years ago either.

“There is a first line and there is a second line and there is a third line and there is a fourth and there is a fifth, and the first four of them are the trade and the fifth is the name, and I have never had a dispute with this building about the first four in my life.”

She said, “And I am not going to stand here and put the fifth line down as though the first four were the reason. They are not the reason. I would take the first four tomorrow. I cannot take the fifth and the first four do not work without it, and that is the whole of it and it took me four years and a walk of fourteen miles to say in one sentence.”

She said, “And there is one more thing and I am saying it because it is true and not because it is clever. In five years this room has written down one line and a date against my name twice, and both times the person writing it was doing their job, and I have not found one thing wrong with either of them. That is not a complaint. It is the reason I am saying the word out loud in the fourth hour of the afternoon instead of the ninth.”

## What She Did Not Do

Five years ago she made the woman read the entry back to her, and then she made her take back one line of it that was hers and had no business being in a book, and the woman refused, because a thing that is not put in is not a refusal, and so she read her own refusal back to herself in a room with the shutters up.

She did not do any of that today.

She said, standing, in daylight: “You do not have to read it back to me. I know what a line in a book says and I have read a great many of them.”

The woman of sixty-three said, “That is the first time anybody has said that to me in this room and it is correct and I am not going to use it.”

## The Line And The Date

The man with the book was sixty-five and had been in the room since before she was born, and she watched his hand for the whole of it, the way she had watched a man of forty-nine’s hand write her name in chalk on a slate four hundred yards west of a turn.

He did not look at what he was writing. He did not write slowly. The hand was plain and upright and every letter was the same size as every other letter and it was a working hand and not a good one, and it is the same hand that has written four hundred and seven names into that book and it is the same hand she read back to herself five years ago.

One line.

The date.

He did not write why.

That was the arrangement four years ago and it has not changed. A refusal goes in as a refusal and a man who writes the reason is a man who is writing something that can be argued with, and no hand in this building takes a line out, and if a hand wrote the reason there would be a second thing in that book instead of a first, and in five years she has not heard of a second thing in it.

She said, standing, at the fifth hour of the twelfth of the month beginning 2,084, to a woman of sixty-three and a man of sixty-five:

“I have been written on twice by people who were right and neither of them did anything wrong, and I am not going to be able to take either of those lines out of that book.”

She said, “And I am still going to be in this room tomorrow, and I want that said out loud while the book is open, because in five years the best thing that ever happened to me was that a room stopped wanting anything from me, and I am not walking out of a building over a line in a book. Whoever put that line in did their job. Let it be their job.”

## What She Did Not Say

Here is what she did not say, and it is the only thing in five years she has held back from a room full of strangers, and she held it back because of a question she asked standing up on a floor with a boy of sixteen in it, and the question was whether a person can be announced without being designated, and it cannot be un-asked.

A second line goes in a book in a room with the shutters up and a hundred people at the tables and it does not stay in that room. It goes on a cart on the first of a month. Everything that comes into this county comes into this room and it goes up a stair.

So a second line is an announcement, and she has been announced nine times and every one of the nine went into the ground.

She stood in that room on the twelfth of the month beginning 2,084 with a second line in a book on a counter in front of her and a hundred people reading behind her and she did not say one word about it, and it was the second time in a month she has kept a figure in her head in daylight, and it is going to go up a stair, and there is not one thing in the world she can do about it.

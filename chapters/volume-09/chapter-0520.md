# The First Thing He Says

She walked out past the bar on two posts and down to the gate in daylight on the sixteenth of a month, with the light still burning in the window of a hut seven hundred yards behind her, and there were three people standing in the gate and she had not asked for one of them.

## What Was Standing There

The boy of fifteen was on the chain at the eighth hour with the cup on its wire.

The man of fifty with four boards had stopped at the gate with his load across his shoulder because the chain was up.

And a carrier with a cart had pulled in at the near side and had not gone through, and his driver was sitting on the bench nine feet off with his hands on his knees.

That is the whole of the witnesses. One of them is fifteen, one of them has carried boards on this lane for nine years, and one of them is a carter of twenty-two who pulled his horse up two hundred yards short of her on the eleventh of a month that has gone and went on and said good as he went by.

## What She Said

The boy of fifteen put the chain down and looked at her in the ordinary way.

“Do you want it up,” he said.

It is what he asks. He has asked four carts and a barrow and a man with goats since the eighth hour and he was asking her the same thing he asks everybody.

She had stood four feet off a man of nine hundred years and told him what she had done to him.

“No,” Ning Yan said.

“All right,” the boy said, and put the chain up for the cart behind her.

She said it out loud once, in the ordinary voice, standing at a gate on a made road in daylight with three people standing in it.

“On the eighth of this month I told a man nine hundred years old to open a wall in the ground past the turnstile,” she said. “The price was one and it was not me and I did not know whose it was. He is on the other side of that wall and there is a light in his window and he is alive in it.”

She looked at the cart and at the boards and at the boy.

“That is the whole of it. I am not going to say it again for anybody.”

And she did not.

## What It Is Now

It is paid. It is permanent. There is nothing on this ground that can do one single thing with it.

That is what she got out of saying it. She said it in daylight in front of three people and it did not make a woman behind a counter say no again, and it did not make a carter take his horse two hundred yards further off, and it did not make one person careful with her stop being careful with her.

The carter on the bench did not get down. The man of fifty did not put his boards down. Neither of them said one word to her and neither of them said one word to each other, and a boy of fifteen went on working a chain.

Eleven of them. Two months of it. And she stood at a gate and told the shape of it out loud and the shape of it turned out to be right.

That is the bill for the two months and it arrived at a gate in daylight in forty seconds.

And she had told them. That is the part that is not in the shape of it.

She told three people at a gate, in the open, in daylight, in the ordinary voice, and not one of them will ever be able to tell anybody else what she said, because a person careful with a thing for two months does not carry it to a counter in the next village. They carry it to the people who are already careful.

So by the end of this month nine days of road is going to know that something came for a girl with a bar, in her own words, out loud, at a gate, and the thing that came for her is going to be in the ground at the end of eleven miles of made road.

## What He Said

He said it from the far side of the wall, out of an opening in it, seven hundred yards east, at a quarter past the eighth hour, in the ordinary voice.

Day one thousand two hundred and fifty-six is the day she made him put a thing down and he did not pick it up again, and day one thousand five hundred and twenty-nine is this one, and five hundred and twenty-nine less two hundred and fifty-six is two hundred and seventy-three days.

Two hundred and seventy-three days is how long it was between the two of them before that. A voice came over seven hundred yards of flat ground in daylight and put the end of it.

“It was mine.”

## What It Saved

Nothing.

That is the whole of what happened on the sixteenth of a month at the sixteenth and it took nine seconds, and there are three people standing at a gate who heard a man say four words at something seven hundred yards off and none of them knows where it came from.

It saved nobody.

Knowing which one it was did not bring him back across a wall he opened for her. It did not put him in the chair in that room and it did not open the door of that hut.

It did not put a name in a book or take one out. It did not make the price smaller and it did not make it a different number.

It is a number of one and it is one.

And she is not grateful and she did not thank him and she is not going to.

## Nineteen

She worked it out at the gate at the ninth hour with the boy of fifteen on his chain and the man of fifty standing with four boards on his shoulder.

She is nineteen. That took nine seconds and it did not change one thing about the morning.

She is past the ceiling by two stages. She has no wages and has had none for four months and has not eaten since the second of this month. Four fingers and a thumb. Right arm open four days in five. Eight of eleven holds. Four counts of breath everywhere including inside a loaded reach, and it is five seconds on a flat floor at the fourth hour now.

A piece of paper against her ribs that she opened with her own two hands in the fourth month and has never once called anything.

And a number of one, in a hand nine hundred years old, on a page she left on a bench at a crossing on the first of a month because her hand will not take a gift.

## The Road Back West

She went east out of the gate after that, at the tenth hour, with the light behind her and the wall seven hundred yards on and the flat going out flat for a mile before anything in it.

The boy of fifteen put the chain up for her and did not ask her a question, and he has never once asked her a question, and he is fifteen, and she did not stop.

The man of fifty with four boards was standing in the middle of the made road with his load across his shoulder and his mouth open a quarter of an inch.

She walked past him.

He let her get four yards and then he said good to her back.

That is all he said and he said it at four yards and he had not said a word at thirty, and she did not stop and she did not turn round and she wants it in the page that she heard it and that it went through her like a bar.

## She Was Not Going To Correct It

She worked it out on flat ground a mile down the long road at the eleventh hour, standing up, with the bag on her shoulder and nothing in it.

She could go back to the store and tell a woman behind a counter that it was nothing, that nothing came for her, that it went the other way. She could tell the woman of fifty. She could tell the carter on the bench and the man of fifty and the eleven, one at a time, eleven conversations, in the ordinary voice.

Every one of them would be right to believe what they believe.

They would be right because a wall came out of a room past a turnstile and a man went through it and has not come back out, and that is not a shape anybody on nine days of road is wrong about. That is the shape exactly.

And she cannot say what it was without saying what a door is, and she does not know what a door is either, and she has been asked twice by a man who keeps a book and has had nothing to say about it in two hundred and seventy-three days.

So she is going to let it stand.

That is the decision and she said it out loud once, at the eleventh hour of the sixteenth of a month, in the ordinary voice, on eleven miles of made road with a bar on her shoulder.

## What The Ground Is Going To Find Out

She was two hundred yards down the long road when she stopped and turned round, and all three of them were still at the gate, and the boy of fifteen was standing with the chain in his hand not moving, and that is what she looked at for four minutes.

Two months.

Two months of a man stopping a word at the kerb and a carter pulling a horse up two hundred yards short and a woman behind a counter putting a hand on a loaf and taking it off again, and eleven people on nine days of road being careful with a girl with a bar because they were sure that something had come for her.

Nothing had come for her.

Something had come out of a room past a turnstile. She had asked for it. It was in the ground at the end of eleven miles of made road, and a man was sitting behind it with a light on in his window.

And in four days, or nine, every one of those eleven people is going to find out exactly what they were careful with, and they are going to be right, and she is not going to be in it.

# The Ceiling Reached

The third hour of the sixteenth of the month was the same third hour as the other two mornings, and the queue was the same queue, and the young clerk at the counter was the same young clerk, and none of it was the same at all.

Eight feet back from the wall, on the other side of the room from the counter, in a good coat that had been good in the summer, was the man who had bought one thing off this county in eleven weeks.

She did not look at him and he did not look at her and the clerk at the counter said, “Your attendance,” and read the ruling off the standing order without being asked to.

## What the County Has To Do

A county has to enter a stage that was announced.

That is a rule and it is a small rule, and it had been done twice before that winter for other members of other crews in other counties and once in this county for her, in the summer, in the ordinary hand, off a hearing sheet with a date on it.

Sun He entered it in the ordinary hand. He wrote the stage in, and the date it was announced, and the place it came from, and the fact that it was announced by the member, and he wrote all four of those things because a registrar who enters only two of them has entered a different document.

Then he read the column down the page the way he always did, from the top, in a room where a member could hear it.

“One,” he said. “Two. Three.”

He went over two ruled lines without a sound.

“Five. Six. Seven.”

He went over two more.

“Nine,” he said.

And then he stopped reading and looked at the top of the sheet for about four seconds, and Ning Yan watched a man do arithmetic.

## The Arithmetic

She had been waiting for it since the fifth day after the ninth hour and she knew what it was and she watched him arrive at it.

The ceiling on her licence was nine. It was nine in her own hand, written on a loft mat at about the fourth hour of a morning in the summer with four months of paper to think about it, and it had been countersigned by a registrar with nineteen years at that table, and it had been sealed before it by three men who had put a mark on it for a reason they had gone quiet about.

She was at nine.

The column said nine and the ceiling said nine and a document that says what a person may do and not one step more is finished at the moment the person arrives at the top of it.

Sun He put his pen down.

“Ma'am,” he said. “Would you look at the front of your own sheet.”

She looked at the front of her own sheet.

## What Is Above Nine

“Foundation,” said Sun He.

“Yes.”

“There is nothing above nine on this document.”

“I know there is nothing above nine on this document,” said Ning Yan. “I put nine on it myself and I would have put twelve if the three men had sealed twelve.”

“They did not seal twelve,” said Sun He, “and I will tell you why, because you did not ask and because it is a fact about this office and not about you.”

He turned the sheet round so the front was toward her and put one finger on it, well clear of the writing.

“The same three legs as the one you have already been through, and the same hours record from the same origin date, and it buys a ceiling in the member's own hand and it restores nothing. Clearance from the quota office. Three seals. And then me.”

“Yes.”

“And every one of those three legs needs a schedule,” said Sun He, “of hours worked at the ceiling being asked for. Condensing six needed six hours. Condensing nine needed nine hours, and you have got them, on a bank up the east road and on a face three miles east, and the county holds all of it.”

He took his finger off the paper.

“This county does not hold a schedule of work done past its boundary,” he said. “There is no such schedule. It is not that it is kept somewhere else. It is that a county keeps what happens inside it, and past the boundary the work is done by people this county does not employ and does not examine and has no second hand for, and so there is nothing to certify.”

“Then there is no second relicensing.”

“There is no second relicensing I can complete,” said Sun He. “There is one any office above this county could complete, and I am not that office, and neither is any man in this building.”

## What He Did Not Say

He did not say the rest of it and she did not ask him for the rest of it.

The rest of it was that a document which stops at nine is a document that has been used up, and that a woman at the top of the second realm with a licence that has nothing left on it is a woman whose paper has stopped agreeing with her body, and that there was no version of the summer in which that was a good outcome.

He stamped the attendance. He put the sheet in the press with two other things without asking her a single question about it, the way he had pressed a binder for her twice a week since the middle of the summer.

“Nine is entered,” he said.

“I can see that it is.”

“Nine is entered and it is the last thing this licence has room for,” said Sun He, “and I am sorry about that, and I want to be exact about what I am sorry about, because this office has now got two sentences in its book about you and they are not both mine.”

He stopped.

He did not say the rest of it there either.

## The Man at the Back of the Room

He came down off the wall when she had the sheet back in her hand, and he stopped at a distance, and he said what he had to say in three sentences without being asked for any of it.

“I will be in this room on the nineteenth at the second hour.”

She did not say anything.

“There is a thing I am required to do there,” said Bai Zhu. “It will be written down and I will tell you what it is before I do it and not after. That is not a courtesy to you. It is how I work, and it is the same whether you would rather I did not tell you or not.”

“Thank you.”

“It is not a favour,” he said. “You may tell me not to, and you should not, and I want to say why, because you are about to spend three days in the cold with nothing to do.”

He turned his little book round in his hands and shut it.

“If I tell you now, you have three days to think about it, and every hour of those three days will be you deciding what I am. And you cannot do that. You have never been able to do that and you have never tried, and I am not going to hand you three days of it on the nineteenth’s doorstep in this cold.”

“So you are going to do it in front of me without telling me.”

“I am going to do it in front of you and tell you what it is the moment before I do it,” he said, “and that is the whole of what I am able to give anybody in this county, and I have not asked you one question at any counter in this county and I am not going to start.”

## The Queue

There were nineteen people in the room and eleven of them in the queue and she was the fourteenth, and a woman in front of her was asking about a carrier's licence, and a man behind her was asking about an oil return, and the queue did not stop moving while a registrar discovered out loud that a licence had run out of road.

She had stood in queues in this room about four hundred times in eighteen months and had learned the room's one rhythm: the counter goes, the clerk presses the binder, the next one comes forward, and everybody in it is briefly an official matter in a place that does not otherwise look like a place where official matters happen.

Today the queue had a different sound in it, and the sound was that two people who had been talking stopped talking while she was still in the room.

They had heard the ninth hour four days ago. They would have heard it the way everybody inside the bound had, in their own voices, for four seconds.

Not one of them said a word to her about it. The man behind her asked his oil-return question and the woman in front asked hers and the clerk pressed the binder and the press shut, and at the back of the room a man in a good coat wrote four words in a small book with a pen that did not run.

## What He Does Not Ask

He did not come and stand beside her and he did not come and stand in front of her, and he had not come and stood eight feet off her at any of the two other mornings, and there was a thing in that which she had decided to stop being grateful for about a week ago.

“You did not come,” said Ning Yan.

“No.”

“You wrote four words.”

“I wrote four words,” said Bai Zhu, “and the four words are that the stage was announced on the date you announced it, and I have been writing the same four words at the same hour every day for four days, because a thing that goes into nine counties at once is a thing with a date on it and I do not need to see it to have it.”

“That is the whole of what you have in the file about me.”

“It is one line of several hundred,” he said, “and the line is true, and it is the only line in that book about you that I have not had to go and get.”

He shut it and put it in the inside of his coat.

“I have asked you nothing in eleven weeks,” he said, “and I am not going to ask you about the ninth hour, and the reason I am telling you that I am not going to is that there is no version of today in which I do not want to, and you should be able to hear that from me without having to work it out.”

She went out into the cold with her filing copy in her right hand and her left inside her coat and eleven weeks of a man's attention in her shoulders.

The licence was against her ribs in oilcloth with seven conditions on the back and the claim as the seventh and the last thing on the paper, and above nine on the front there was nothing at all.

Nine months ago she had been a servant with no document and no stage anybody could name. In the spring she had been made legal up to five. In the summer she had gone to three men and a registrar and put a ceiling on herself in her own hand at nine.

She had reached the top of it on the fifth day before this one, in front of nine men, in the ordinary voice.

And the paper had run out at exactly the moment she arrived, which was not a coincidence and was not a punishment. It was simply what a document is. A document is an agreement between a person and a paper about how far a person may go, and it is finished when the person is there.

Three days to the nineteenth.

She had a licence with nothing left on it, a claim travelling on the back of it with a date entered against it in a counting room she had never been in, a morning in four, and one thing she had not yet said to anybody in this county, which was that she intended to file for the only paper that would go past what she had just finished.

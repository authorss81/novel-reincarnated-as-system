# The Cheap Door

The pending filing held the way a wall holds, by being stood on every day.

Ning Yan attended the stamp day with Du Hong at the tenth hour, entered and in daylight, with the queue running out the door into market noise. Sun He sat at the far table with the county book open and the stamp at his hand. When her name came she stood before him with the filing copy, stages recorded, hours continuous, defect in her own hand. Du Hong gave his name aloud for the attendance line.

The registrar read the void-and-transfer terms the way clerks read weather. Du Hong said he knew. The brush moved. The mark went down.

They walked out into the square with the paper excellent and the claim standing over it, side by side, one refusing the other and both hers. The man in the good coat paid at the second desk. The younger man watched from the lane mouth. The counting room stacked paper by household behind both.

“You stand it,” said Yuan. “You bear the reading and you walk still.”

“I stand it,” said Ning Yan. “He stands with me. You watch with us.”

“I watch,” said Yuan. “I stay near and I touch nothing.”

Du Hong was cheerful under it the way he is cheerful under everything, badly hidden and entirely steady. He carried tower letters through the lanes in daylight, flour on his sleeves, file on the table, and nodded to the younger man once the way carriers nod at weather.

“He knows my name now,” he said in the loft over soup. “The younger one. He asked a carrier what the tower hand carries in his coat. The carrier told him sheets. He paid for the hour and the lane.”

“Walk with the water cart after dark,” said Ning Yan. “Walk entered. Carry nothing with my hand on it unentered.”

“My coat carries your copies still,” said Du Hong. “Four sheets with no name on them, down the road weeks ago. And the file carries your hand twice. I walk entered and I am still the cheap door. You cannot paper that away.”

She looked at him across the mat. Eighteen, ambitious, generous, bad at lying. His name in a county book for her attendance. Her hand twice in his file. Her copies in his coat down the road. Every paper that made him worth stopping had her hand on it, and he had put himself there on purpose, in daylight, before a queue, so nobody could say nobody told him what it costs.

“I know,” she said. It came out plain, without tally in it. “I let you stand and I carry what you decide. That is all I have.”

“It is much,” said Du Hong. “My name is the whole line of a county book. Everything since autumn was filed under you. This line is mine.”

She did not thank him. Thanks between people who know the cost is done by not saying it.

In the evening she drilled the left-hand sequence once in the yard and stopped early to save breath, because breath is hours and hours are the county’s. The sequence ran hook, turn, settle — cleaner than winter, hers and unshared. She sat with the lath across her knees till the chest settled, and felt Settlement thin beside her the way a blanket goes thin, still covering and less warm.

Stamp days, tower readings, carrying work. The paper used, not admired. Attendance, visibility, and a name besides hers. That was what the pending filing cost under pursuit, and it held because two people stood where the book said to stand.

She climbed once to the loft and lay on the mat with the shutter still. Below, Feng coughed and copied. On the square the desks closed. At the lane mouth the younger man waited till the tower lamp lit, then went toward the counting lane with his report.

“You fear for him,” said Yuan. “You love him plainly and you do not say it.”

“I fear,” said Ning Yan. “I work. You stay with me.”

“I stay,” said Yuan. “I am with you and I do not leave.”

She slept with the filing copy in her sleeve and the docket in her lining, and one word with nobody to hold it ticking behind her eyes, and a boy’s name in a county book below her, read aloud that day and every stamp day, the price of being legal to five while hunted.

The cheap door walked openly in daylight, entered, watched. That was the whole of it, and it was enough for any man buying bodies to mark.

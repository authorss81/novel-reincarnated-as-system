# What Being Chosen Costs

The tower loft alone, midday, with the square noise below and the volumes under the floor.

Ning Yan sat on the mat with her coat round her shoulders and laid out what she holds in the order she holds it, the way she lays tallies before signing. The safe-side sentence. The eleven lines in a dead hand. The word in a second position on Lowwater's sheet. The tax sequence in her own body, hook and turn and settle, rebuilt over winter from habit.

She had not asked to be alone. Du Hong carried letters through the lanes. Feng copied fairly below. The buyer paid at three desks. Feet waited at the lane mouth. Alone was what was left when attendance was done and loads were set down.

She worked it through standing still, without moving her face.

Eleven in six hundred years, spaced irregularly. Not found at different times. Spent at different rates.

That was the first part, and it landed plain.

The second part landed harder. Every quest the layer honours her is answered out of the same foundation the tax already draws from. Being Chosen does not add a cost. It adds occasions for an existing one.

The thing he called a shelter is the thing that makes her cheaper.

She sat with that for a long time. The breath went short at two counts and stayed short. The stripe ran cold. Her arm hung dead and she did not test it. A designation that prices her, not shelters her. The eleven were not protected into record. They were priced into it, used up at different speeds, filed because the writ required filing, gone because the foundation gives and does not return.

She made herself go through it again with numbers, because numbers were the only part of this she could hold without it moving.

Four weeks of stone to build the third stage. Eleven weeks of channel to buy back a life at the tower foot, paid for with a sequence she cannot rebuild. Every hour the layer honoured her was an hour drawn from the same place the tax drew from, and she had spent most of a year drawing on it, and had been getting larger and less solid at once, in that order, at that rate.

And she had thought, since the terrace, that she was being made safe.

She went back through it, because a conclusion that cannot be walked back through is not a conclusion, it is a mood. The trial, where the whole bound was told what she was in front of nine hundred people and could not be taken quietly out of a hall because nine hundred people had watched her name go round. The four seconds a man had spent holding a sentence, which had cost her eight more years billed to her dead father and had been the first thing he had ever done for her without asking, and which she had not forgiven.

The reading at the gate, four and a half weeks. The two weeks of fever and the twelve days worked out of fourteen. The stage she had taken four months ago in a yard behind a tower because four weeks of load had put it in her and she could not have carried the work without letting it go.

Every one of them had been an occasion. Not one of them had been a cost somebody had added for her.

That was the whole of it, and she wrote it out of him and into herself without mercy: he had not made her expensive. He had made her *often*. He had found the one thing in the bound that would hand her work and given it to her, and the work was the tax, and he had done it out of love with his mouth shut, and the not telling was the whole of the crime and there was no part of it she could hand to a court.

The tax had a shape and the shape was not a hand. She could feel where the wanting ended and the making began and they were in exactly the same place, and she had gone round that circle four times since the ninth step and every time it had come out the same.

She stopped going round it. There was a place past it where the question became *who built this*, and that place was not hers yet, and she was not going to stand in it on the strength of one afternoon and a body with a crack in it.

Nothing lost comes back. Practice rebuilds substance and cannot rebuild order. She knew that in her legs where the rebuilt sequence lives.

She spoke it aloud, alone, to him, because speaking is the only record between them that no clerk reads.

“I worked it out,” said Ning Yan. “The writ prices me. Eleven spaced because spent differently. Every honour answers out of the same foundation the tax draws from. Chosen adds occasions, not cost. That is what it is.”

Silence. The weight with no hands, listening.

“You see true,” said Yuan. “You work it alone and you tell me. I hear you and I hold it.”

“You told me safe,” said Ning Yan. “Protection and danger the same line. Safe side. I keep the three standing and I do not forgive you.”

“I hear it,” said Yuan. “I stay with you unforgiven. I ask nothing else.”

No new rule. The three stand. Employed, not forgiven. The work is her brother. Nothing decided about her again without telling her first. She set no fourth term, because terms are doors and she needed the walls plain.

Non-forgiveness from this hour onward. Through the pursuit, through the grant she is going after, into the year after that. A year. That was the milestone, and she did not know its name, only its weight.

She did not conclude the tax was designed by anybody. Design is a different question and a colder season of it, and she left it where it lay. What she holds is her position in the pattern: next in a queue she cannot see the end of, priced, filed, spent at her own rate.

And then, because she had the daylight and the loft and no one to stop her, she did the last sum and it took her a long time.

She could stop taking. Not the tax, which came whether or not she stood still, but the occasions. She could refuse the quests, refuse the stages, stay at two and stay a servant with a god in her head for the rest of her life, and a woman who is not spent is a woman nobody bothers to price.

It would have worked. It was not a fantasy. There was a version of the next ten years in which she never advanced again and no office ever raised her name in a column and she carried water up a tower until her arm stopped entirely and died in a bed somewhere with nothing entered against her.

She looked at that version for the length of a breath and then she put it down, because she had been eleven years old when she asked for a rank on a furnace floor and everything since had been the same wanting with a longer road under it, and she was not going to unask it at seventeen because the asking turned out to be expensive.

The expensive part was not the tax. The expensive part was that she would keep going, at her own rate, being priced, and there was no arrangement in which she got to be ordinary instead.

She folded the docket into her lining and went down the stair once, breathing short on the landing, to walk the lower lane with soup cooling in her hands for a boy who walks entered.

Noise came up from the road foot before she reached the trough. Raised voices. A carrier shouting. Thaw water running high in the ditch at the foot of the Lantern Register tower.

She ran toward it with one working arm and short breath, habit in her legs, no channel opened as technique, because technique is a thing a clerk can describe and habit is not.

At the tower foot two men held Du Hong between them on the Lowwater road. His coat hung open. A sheet burned at his feet. The younger man held his file. The elder held his arms.

She arrived with mud to the ankle and the tower above her, and the attempt already begun.

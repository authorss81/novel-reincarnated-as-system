## Rules

- **Pressure: work without hire only.** Not keeping, not debt, not family paper, not ash, not a ration, not a
  hiring, not a tending, not a crossing, not an audit, not an examination, not a hunt, not a signature, not a
  confinement, and **not Batch 0003's sending and being sent for.** Alternate inside it — body, ground, money,
  a person, a silence.
- **Visible change every 5 to 10 chapters.** At least one scene of physical risk or a real deadline. **At least
  one irreversible step that is this batch's own**: the Hand at ch 325 and the second vouching at ch 329 are
  two, and neither may be deferred into Batch 0003.
- **Every chapter gives her one felt emotion on the page and one concrete want.** Not told, not summarised —
  on the page, in her own want, not the narrator's.
- **Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the present
  tense, and no other character is ever in a present frame.** **Ration him harder than Volume 5 did: at most six
  sentences across sixteen chapters, never more than one in a chapter, none buying, advising, explaining or
  advancing anything.** **The Hand at ch 325 is his act and the chapter must be able to show it without him
  having more to say than he has ever had.**
- **Give short plain sentences at the moment something is understood.** The GC5 cost at 322 and the price of
  the second vouching at 329 both land that way. **One simple sentence about one person after any stretch of
  machinery.**
- **No authorial scaffolding at all** — *this chapter*, *this volume*, *in this book*, *the rest of the book*,
  *the reader*, *in this batch*, *another batch*, *N volumes*. Volume 5 shipped seven against a prompt rule and
  a budget that recorded zero; **count by grep before claiming zero and write the true number into the
  continuity block.** A physical book in a room may be called a book; nothing else may.
- **No construction budget that records zero while instances stand on the page.** Walk Batch 0001's budget page
  before drafting, name every carried construction and this batch's allowance on each in your own block, and
  `grep` each one before claiming a count. **In particular:** *about four seconds* 0 · *eleven weeks* 0 ·

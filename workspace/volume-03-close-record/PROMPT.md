## Part two — `outline/volume-04.md`

Write it in the same shape as `outline/volume-03.md`, which is the template. It must contain, at minimum, all
of the following.

### Fixed and not to be re-planned

- **Title:** *The Wall*. **Chapters 179–238. Sixty chapters.**
- **The one-sentence goal, verbatim from `outline/series.md` §9:**
  > In this volume, Ning Yan must break the boundary, or neither of them ever learns what they are.
- **The question, fixed by `outline/volume-03.md` *New question for Volume 4*.** Write it as the opening
  pressure, not as a plan:

  > **A Warden's office outside the bound now holds her name, her stages and her hours — in order — certified
  > by her own county. The dismantling it has ordered can only be executed, and the Foundation relicensing
  > she has petitioned can only be countersigned, past a boundary no county paper crosses.**

  **The question is not what the Wall is.** The question is *what a paper in order does when the only office
  that can answer it is on the other side of one.*
- **The antagonist, from `outline/series.md` §6 item 4:** **the Wall and the sects that live on it** —
  geography defended by people with families. **Not an individual.** There is no new named villain, and no
  new final enemy: the Scribe is fixed in `outline/ending.md` and belongs to Volume 15.
- **Where she is at the last page:** Condensing 9, the top of the second realm, ceiling nine in her own hand
  and **nothing above it on the document**; relicensed ch 132 with seven conditions and the claim the seventh;
  **a relicensing at Foundation the first stage petitioned, numbered, received, routed, barred and not
  withdrawable**; **hunted in writing** and examined monthly; about **ten days** of channel; crew nine men
  and her; Feng a Listener, warm, column at five; the girl of twenty-four alive; Hou Sanliang never met; Shen
  Xiu unforgiven and unthanked; the three sponsors silent; the register still *reconciled*; the plate still
  worn; **and the year of non-forgiveness still running.**
- **`outline/series.md` §4 puts Foundation 1–9 in Volumes 3–4, with the cost of the first Foundation named
  there: one index finger, permanently, Volume 4.** Plan the Foundation arc — real stages, real months, real
  costs, **no tribulation**, and **every stage inside the bound announced unless she orders it suppressed**,
  which is canon and which Volume 3 ran on. Note that **Foundation is a different shape of body from
  Condensing** and that the fourth and eighth stages of her life, and the suppressed Condensing 8, are on
  no paper in nine counties and never will be.

### The three structural facts this volume inherits, and must not re-derive

1. **The relicensing's cost is what opens Volume 4.** A ceiling at nine buys a ceiling, not a body, and
   restores nothing. The only route to Foundation is a second relicensing, and a second relicensing needs a
   schedule of hours worked at the ceiling asked for — **and this county does not keep a schedule of work
   done past its boundary.** Write what she does about that. It is the volume's engine and it is legal
   mechanics before it is anything else.
2. **The Hunter's referral is answered upward, and the district does not hold the instrument either.** The
   request on the shelf is *received, numbered, referred upward, answer pending*, with an **empty space the
   width of a line where an answer was meant to go.** Volume 4 opens on that empty space. **Do not fill it
   in the first batch.**
3. **The county is not the enemy and never was.** A Warden's field agent certifies, refers upward, and is
   right about the holes. **Volume 4 must not turn the county, the registrar, or the sponsors into
   villains** — they are institutions doing the only thing they are built to do, and that is worse.

### Power progression

**Foundation 1 → 9 across ch 179–238**, on top of Condensing 9 reached and announced at ch 171. **Real
stages, real months, real costs**, checked against the clock the way Volume 3's Settlement clock was checked.
**No tribulation.** **No skipped breakthrough.** **Do not let the Wall be cheap**: whatever boundary she
breaks has to cost something she cannot put back, and **the cost must be personal, not a number.**

### The pressure rotation

Volume 3 ran examination and exposure → audit and mercy → hunting with a calendar → cost and signature.
**Volume 4 must not repeat that shape.** Volume 3's pressure was **lawful hunting by an officer who never
lies and buys almost nothing**, and its paper was **paper already stamped twice and now being read back**.
Volume 4's is geography and distance. **Write the schedule of pressures, batch by batch, and make sure no
two consecutive batches run the same pressure.**

### Hard rules for the Volume 4 drafters

Restate these once, in the outline, and never again in a batch prompt.

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the present
  tense. No other character is ever in the present tense** — with the standing exception, set by ch 57, that
  statements about completed third-person events keep the past tense.
- At most one bracketed layer line per chapter, on its own line, in square brackets, never bold, never
  italics, never a heading. They announce; they never explain and they never advise.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or italic.
  **No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a paragraph;
  break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly 1,800–2,700 words, floor 1,300.
- **Close every speech.** `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` before finishing.
- No harem. **Nothing in Volume 4 is a romance.** **Wei Ran may first appear in this volume**, per
  `outline/series.md` §7, and the line goes off page and is not the main relationship.
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until Volume 16, by Ning Wu. Ning Yan never says her mother's name.
  Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before Volume 11, and does not learn she saw him as a
  readout before Volume 11 either.
- Do not re-explain known principles: the four seconds, the release rule, tax-takes-order,
  layer-is-editable, damage-as-technique, the eleven designations, the bounty, the seven relicensing
  conditions, what a surety is for, the suppression arithmetic, the position-not-characters rule, the name in
  the wrong order, or the crew head count. **Walk the construction budget at the foot of
  `state/continuity.md` before writing and confirm nothing from it is reused as emphasis.**
- **Do not use the phrase "eleven weeks."** It is retired out of Volume 3 and carries five different meanings
  there. **The Hunter arrived on day 2 and the winter ends at about day 261** — derive from those, never from
  the retired figure.
- **Do not let "about four seconds" become a metronome.** It is the volume rhythm of Volume 3 and it is not
  a rule. If a pause needs a number, use a different number.
- **Nine counties and four counties are different footprints.** Nine hears the announcements and reads the
  relicensed licence in order. Four is the claim's footprint and the furnace schedule's. Do not blur them.
- **Do not introduce a new final enemy.** The Scribe is fixed in `outline/ending.md` and is not improvised at
  the end of the run.
- **Volume 4 is not Volume 3 again.** It must be a different shape of pressure from every one of Volume 3's
  four, and it must not be Volume 2's confinement again either.

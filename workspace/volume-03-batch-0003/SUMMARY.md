## What this batch spends

1. **The Ledger break (ch 149).** The handover paper has been on a mat for four days, unopened, unsigned
   by any person. She cannot read a man with her eyes, so she says it out loud **on the copying floor, in
   front of Feng Lushi**, with the **price named before the word — four and a half weeks out of about
   ten** — and he enters it as a **tower change**, not a request, with both the asking and the doing in it.
   Ledger under a day. What it buys: the room, a board of dates, **the eight remaining proofs in order**,
   the return already sent upward with her hours in it, and **the four sheets of the handover verified
   true line by line.** What it does not buy: the office name, the eleven, design. **He puts it down the
   same day and refuses permanently.**
2. **The handover, refused (ch 150).** She moves the table into the **county office on the square**, because
   a county writes down everything said in it. Four true terms, the two competent questions, two true
   answers, and a fourth line in the ordinary place saying the announcements already carried to the bound
   are not comprised in it. **She refuses aloud before two witnesses and signs it in her own hand.** A
   clerk off a shelf writes **Bai Zhu** into a Warden's book, and Sun He writes it into the county book.
3. **The suppressed stage (ch 160).** Five weeks of quarry carrying, grief worked in loads, **an empty hut,
   a stove, the ordinary voice, eleven seconds**, and **nothing goes out of her into nine counties at all.**
   The column takes its second hole, **the same width as the first.**

## The irreversible decision — hers, and the volume's

**She chooses to be hunted rather than handed over, with every term of the offer verified true first.**
She closes a real way out with her own hand on the paper. **Nothing after ch 150 can unwrite it**, and
151–163 do not try: they are written inside it. The cost is specific and permanent — Hou Sanliang stays
unpaid and patient because she said no, the eight hundred jin keeps standing, and the voice is still in
her head and she has still not forgiven him for what the reading cost.

## What changed that could not have changed before

A true, painless, written mercy exists in four sheets and she refused it with her own name on the
refusal. **A copy of that refusal goes to the Hall on the furnace schedule to nine men who read their own
schedule.** A man has his name in a Warden's book and in the county book and has never been called
anything else in nine counties until a clerk wrote it. She is at eight with nothing on paper saying so, and
a reader above this county reads the hole as concealment and is right. **The thing that can finish her is
not in this county**, and a numbered request for it is sitting on a public shelf, unsent.

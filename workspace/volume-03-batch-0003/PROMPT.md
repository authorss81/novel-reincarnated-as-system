## This batch's goal

She works the furnace floor through a winter that has no paid work in it, breaks her own written term in
public on her own word with the price named before she says it, **reads a patient man through her own eyes
and verifies a mercy is true before she decides about it**, and then refuses it aloud, in front of
witnesses, and signs the refusal in her own hand. From that hour she is not a licensee under examination
but **a subject under hunting, in writing, where no clerk can unwrite it.** And the stage she suppresses
at ch 160 reads on a county sheet as an absence, which the county rules is not a breach and a Warden's
office reads as concealment — which is where he is right, and nothing stops it.

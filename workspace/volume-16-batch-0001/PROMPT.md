## The male lead — this is the volume's live problem and it is fixed here, not in prose

**The volume's protagonist is a man. He is male, he is the want and the arc, and this batch is where he stops
being a voice and becomes the reason the book ends. The review of Volume 15 Batch 0003 found him functionally absent
across fifteen chapters and the repair rebuilt them; the defect must not come back.**

**Five standing requirements for ch 901–915, and they are not optional:**

1. **He speaks in every one of the fifteen**, and in at least six he carries an exchange rather than a line. Two-to-
   four-word comfort is the defect. **A reader must be able to point at the sentence in any chapter that tells them
   what he wants today, and it must not be the same sentence twice.**
2. **He is answerable.** He can be wrong, out of reach, honest past his own interest, and silent on purpose. He is
   not the reader's protector in this volume. **He is a person about to be offered everything he ever wanted, and
   he is going to take it for four minutes, and the reader is on her side.**
3. **He acts at least twice.** Showing her the bargain on purpose at ch 907. Not defining *work that matters* at
   ch 919's question — no, ch 919 is Batch 0002. **Here: the answer at ch 901, the showing at ch 907, and the four
   minutes at ch 912 are three acts and one of them costs him.**
4. **No eleventh frame scene**, and **no bracket that is his voice.** The layer is not him and must never be written
   as if it were.
5. **Every sentence he speaks is present tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense. This is the
   reader's only cue for who is talking and it never breaks.

**Ch 908 and ch 912 are specified here rather than left to a card.** Neither may be softened, argued, or given an
inner defence. At ch 908 she lets him go because it is not her decision to make, and she says so out loud and it is
the worst thing she has ever done. At ch 912 he crosses, and the crossing takes four minutes, and the reader is in
them, and she watches him be happy, and she does nothing.

## Prose, and the house style

**Numbers are words. Paragraphs short (two to four sentences; break over ~90 words; never over ~120). Reader inside
her hearing. Short sentences at revelation; long ones elsewhere. No more than three consecutive documentary
paragraphs; one simple sentence about one person after machinery. Every chapter ends on question, danger,
discovery, decision, reversal, or emotional consequence — never bed, arithmetic, or neutral action. His sentences
only in present tense. Felt emotion plus concrete want every chapter. Vary emotional temperature. No procedure loop:
every chapter transforms the larger situation.**

**Length, so that there is something to land against.** Per chapter, on method B (`grep -v '^#' <file> | wc -w`,
per file, summed): **no chapter under 1,450 and none over the house guide of 2,700; the batch of fifteen lands
between 26,000 and 28,000.** The band is Volume 15's, measured the same way — **its sixty chapters run 1,455 to 2,178
and average 1,850** — and this volume carries no stage and no number, so there is nothing here to pad a chapter
with. **One chapter is named short and is short on purpose: ch 912 is four minutes and lands near 900.** Fourteen at
the band and one at nine hundred is about 26,800, which is inside the range, and **a chapter may not be lengthened
to make up a short one.**

**Do not re-explain known principles**, and **do not rebuild what Volume 15's reviews took out of fifteen chapters.**
Concretely, in this batch:

- **Openings must vary.** She has said her day's figures every morning since the first of the month beginning 2,864 —
  and 2,864 is the first of its month, which is what `((day − 134) mod 30) + 1` gives, and not a day in the middle of
  one — and she does not recite them all fifteen times. **At most four of these fifteen chapters may open on the
  figures**, and those four must not be consecutive.
- **`She said` must not open more than about one paragraph in six.** In ch 871–885 it was 50 of 1,130.
- **Do not write** `And here is the whole of what…`, `said plain`, `I am naming it because…`, `X finishes nothing,
  and I am saying so out loud`, `That is the record and there is nothing else in it`, `that was the day`, or
  `whether she climbed or not`. **Each is dead.**
- **Do not use two to four-word comfort lines as his whole contribution to any chapter.** He is a clerk with a
  want and a temper and a flaw and the reader has to be able to hear all three.
- **Name the feeling in the scene, not in a slot.** She may name it out loud; she may not open a paragraph with a
  feeling and a comparison to yesterday's.
- **No two chapters end on the same shape**, and no chapter ends on a line that restates its own opening.

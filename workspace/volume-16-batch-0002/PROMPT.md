## The male lead — he is the volume's live problem and these are standing

**Five standing requirements for ch 916–930, and they are not optional:**

1. **He speaks in every one of the fifteen**, and in at least six he carries an exchange rather than a line.
   Two-to-four-word comfort is the defect. **A reader must be able to point at the sentence in any chapter that tells
   them what he wants today, and it must not be the same sentence twice.**
2. **He is answerable.** Wrong, out of reach, honest past his own interest, silent on purpose. Not the reader's
   protector. **A man finding out the thing he wanted for six hundred years is made of the thing done to her — and
   the reader is on her side.**
3. **He acts at least twice**: not defining *work that matters* for two days at ch 919–920, and the refusal at
   ch 930 at a cost that cannot be paid back.
4. **No eleventh frame scene**, and **no bracket that is his voice.** The layer is not him.
5. **His sentences are present tense in the house register** — predominantly present, as Volumes 14–15 hold it
   (*you heard that*, *I asked*, *I did it myself* all stand there); no other character takes the present-tense
   cue as hers. This is the reader's signal for who is talking and it never breaks.

**Ch 923 and ch 930 are specified here rather than left to a card.** At ch 923 he finds the tax and says it plainly
and neither of them softens it. At ch 930 he refuses correctly, gets to be an entry, dissolves, and is not thanked.

## Prose, and the house style

**Numbers are words. Paragraphs short (two to four sentences; break over ~90 words; never over ~120). Reader inside
her hearing. Short sentences at revelation; long ones elsewhere. No more than three consecutive documentary
paragraphs; one simple sentence about one person after machinery. Every chapter ends on question, danger,
discovery, decision, reversal, or emotional consequence — never bed, arithmetic, or neutral action. Felt emotion
plus concrete want every chapter. Vary emotional temperature. No procedure loop: every chapter transforms the larger
situation.**

**Length, on method B (`grep -v '^#' <file> | wc -w`, per file, summed): no chapter under 1,450 except ch 930,
which is short on purpose like ch 912 was (near 900–1,100); none over 2,700; the batch of fifteen lands between
26,000 and 28,000.** Batch 0001's sixty-less band ran 1,640 to 2,033 with 912 at 1,082 and totalled 27,343.

**Do not re-explain known principles**, and **do not rebuild what Batch 0001's repair took out.** Concretely:

- **Openings must vary.** At most four of these fifteen chapters may open on the morning figures, and those four
  must not be consecutive. (Batch 0001 used four: 902, 906, 909, 911.)
- **`She said` must not open more than about one paragraph in six.** (Batch 0001: 6 of 905.)
- **Do not write** `And here is the whole of what…`, `said plain`, `I am naming it because…`, `X finishes nothing,
  and I am saying so out loud`, `That is the record and there is nothing else in it`, `that was the day`, or
  `whether she climbed or not`. **Each is dead.**
- **Do not use two to four-word comfort lines as his whole contribution to any chapter.**
- **Name the feeling in the scene, not in a slot.** She may name it out loud; she may not open a paragraph with a
  feeling and a comparison to yesterday's.
- **No two chapters end on the same shape**, and no chapter ends on a line that restates its own opening.

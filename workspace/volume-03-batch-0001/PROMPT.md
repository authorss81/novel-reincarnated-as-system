## The batch to plan

**Volume 3, Batch 0001: chapters 119–132 (14 chapters).** The batch is sized to its own arc —
licensed life, the first return coming due, the Hunter's first purchase, double hours, relicensing
granted — not to a fixed count. If the arc needs one chapter more or fewer, say so in the cards and
why; do not pad and do not split a finished scene.

Per `outline/volume-03.md` batch plan: pressure **examination and exposure**. Batch goal: she lives
licensed for the first time — county hours with no surety, paid work outside the crew, the first
quarterly return five weeks out — the stranger at the first desk buys his first thing (a certified
copy of her schedule) and is right about the hole, she starts working two schedules at once, and the
batch ends with the **relicensing granted (ch 132)**: three sponsors' seal, Shen Xiu's second
clearance, Sun He's countersignature, ceiling at Condensing 9 in her own hand, seven conditions with
the claim re-entered last.

## What the cards must contain

In the same shape `outline/batches/volume-02-batch-0009.md` took, write to
`outline/batches/volume-03-batch-0001.md`:

- Batch goal, chapter range, starting condition read off the audited state files (not invented),
  starting relationships.
- Batch midpoint (the Hunter's first purchase at ch 126 and being right about the hole) and batch
  climax (the relicensing granted at ch 132). These are not adjacent: the midpoint is six chapters
  before the climax and the cards must show the purchase causing the relicensing, not sitting beside
  it.
- Required character changes, required power changes, required world reveals, open questions after
  the batch.
- An action-and-deadline requirement (at least one scene of physical risk or a real deadline — here
  the five-week first return, the road that shuts, county hours with nobody standing).
- A chapter-by-chapter table with turn in one line and ending type (decision / reversal / discovery
  / cost / earned hook / loss / emotional consequence / new question / new danger), and the titles.
- One full card per chapter: Chapter, POV, Location/time, Immediate goal, Resistance, Information
  revealed, Action or decision, Emotional change, Power or resource change, Continuity fact to
  preserve, Ending type, Next-chapter pull.

## Constraints the cards must obey

- **Starting condition is read, not chosen.** Ch 118 ending: spring rain, roofless Hall, licence
  nailed crooked above the second bunk going soft, ten sleepers (nine men and her, twelve bunks),
  right hand open a third of the time, Settlement a little under three-quarters and about six weeks,
  Feng's lamp burning, stranger at the first desk asking what a schedule is.
- **No stage above five in this batch.** Condensing 5 throughout; 5 → 6 is ch 135 in Batch 0002.
  Relicensing (ch 130–132) lifts the ceiling; nothing climbs before the paper does. No tribulation.
- **The Hunter's first purchase is the batch midpoint, at ch 126** — not a ch 131 beat. A certified
  copy of her schedule, coin on open wood, receipt kept, and he reads the hole (one, two, three,
  five) and says in daylight that the next stage must be announced or the paper fails its own terms.
  He is right. It is what puts the relicensing in motion, so it must land well before ch 130.
- **The relicensing legs run clearance → seal → countersignature (ch 130, 131, 132), inverted from
  Volume 2 on purpose.** Do not tidy them back to the Volume 2 order; `outline/volume-03.md` gives
  the sponsors' motive for the inversion and a drafter who "corrects" it destroys the cause.
- **Bai Zhu is not named in this batch.** The stranger buys nothing until the certified copy, gives
  his name to nobody, tells carriers he is not from the Bureau. He is first named at **ch 150, on the
  entered refusal**, per `outline/volume-03.md` — not at 152 and not on the handover paper, which
  carries no agent's name.
- **Ledger is not taken in this batch.** The break is ch 149. Price paid, never restated, never
  anticipated on the page.
- **Feng is not audited in this batch.** The audit is ch 136–140. The Lung is not offered here.
- **The claim stands unclaimed.** Proof failed on the body; the Watch has no form column. Pursuit
  has become attendance and then lawful reading — not a broker's proof-hunt.
- **She does not forgive him.** Non-forgiveness runs through this volume into the opening of
  Volume 4.
- Carry forward the closed state and **every *do not* in `state/open-threads.md`**, plus the hard
  rules in `outline/volume-03.md` (close third; Yuan present tense with the ch 57 completed-event
  exception; at most one bracketed layer line per chapter announcing only; plain markdown with no
  blockquotes, code blocks, tables or horizontal rules in chapters; every speech closed; 2–4
  sentences a paragraph, never over ~120 words; roughly 1,800–2,700 words, floor 1,300; no harem,
  nothing a romance, Wei Ran absent; spent phrases and the construction budget untouched; no new
  final enemy; hunted, not another winter).
- Titles: 14, unique against all 118 existing titles, names not summaries, no weather, clock time,
  date or ages.

## Part two — `outline/volume-03.md`

Write it in the same shape as `outline/volume-02.md`, which is the template. It must contain, at
minimum, all of the following.

### Fixed and not to be re-planned

- **Title:** *The Hunter*. **Chapters 119–178. Sixty chapters.**
- **The one-sentence goal, verbatim from `outline/series.md` §9:**
  > In this volume, Ning Yan must survive being hunted as an unauthorised System, or Yuan is dismantled and she is told she imagined him.
- **The antagonist, from `outline/series.md` §6 item 3:** **Bai Zhu**, a field agent of the Warden's
  office, sent down the Lowwater road to find and dismantle an unauthorised System. **Polite,
  patient, and he never lies to her.** Write his *method* — not his personality alone. He has already
  arrived unnamed at ch 118 as a man at the first desk in the market who buys nothing, gives his name
  to nobody, tells carriers he is not from the Bureau, and asks what a furnace schedule is. **He is
  not named in Volume 2; the naming is Volume 3's to schedule, and it should not be the first page.**
- **The relationship milestone, from `outline/series.md` §7 item 4:** in Volume 3 she chooses to be
  **hunted** rather than **handed over**. Write the chapter that pays it and the shape of the
  decision.
- **Feng Lushi takes the offered Lung in this volume.** He is alive, unaudited, column at five, no
  Bureau audit yet. **Volume 3 is where the four upward copies make him a traitor.** Plan both, and
  plan what each costs.
- **The ch 118 ending is the starting position:** spring rain, the roofless Hall, the licence nailed
  crooked above the second bunk going soft, ten sleepers, the right hand open a third of the time,
  Settlement a little under three-quarters and about six weeks, Feng's lamp burning, and a stranger at
  the first desk in Lowwater asking what a schedule is.

### The two structural questions — decide both here

Both are gathered and costed in `state/current.md` under
`## Volume 3 — the two open structural questions`. **Both were deliberately left undecided and this
is where they get decided.**

1. **How she gets past the cap.** The licence caps her at Condensing 5 and bars a Foundation petition
   without relicensing; `outline/series.md` §4 puts Foundation in Volumes 3–4. A relicensing is a
   second three-legged application with a continuous hours record from the same origin date; it buys
   a ceiling in her own hand and restores nothing; the sponsors' Foundation writ is dead but not
   revoked; and the eight hundred jin is on the licence as condition six, so a relicensing is a
   licence the claim gets re-entered on. **Write the route, the signature, and the cost.**
2. **Ledger as a standing stage.** `outline/series.md` §4 assigns it to Volumes 3–5. Ch 80 was the
   recorded exception and it is closed. It costs about four and a half weeks out of a channel standing
   at about six. **She wrote it into her own standing terms at ch 118 — *no Ledger, and there is no
   version of the rest of the winter where there is Ledger* — so any move to it is her breaking her
   own written term, in public, for something specific. Write when, what for, and what it costs.**

### Power progression

`outline/series.md` §4 assigns **Condensing 1–9 to Volumes 1–3**. Volume 2 finished at **Condensing 5,
capped there by her own hand**, with stage four permanently missing from every book in nine counties.
**Write the Condensing 5 → 9 → Foundation arc across ch 119–178 as real stages with real months and
real costs**, consistent with the relicensing decision above and with the relief rule that **every
stage inside the bound is announced unless she orders it suppressed**. **No tribulation** — no trial
is drawn in this shape of book — and **no skipped breakthrough**. Give the volume a realistic span in
months and check every interval against it, the way Volume 2's Settlement clock was checked.

### The pressure rotation

Volume 2 ran confinement and paperwork → labour and grinding → pursuit and the bounty → grief and the
grant. **Volume 3 must not repeat that shape.** Two things changed at the end of Volume 2 and must
change the shape: **she is now legal and licensed** (the county examines paper, so being hunted
changes form), and **she is now visible on paper with a standing attached to it** (the claim travels
with the licence). Design the batch pressures so that no two consecutive batches run the same
pressure and **none of them is Volume 2 again**. Write the schedule of pressures, batch by batch.

### The Hunter's schedule

He is patient and he does not lie. **Give him a schedule**: where he is when, what he buys, what he
refuses to do, and what he is waiting for. A patient professional antagonist is only frightening if
the reader can see his calendar. He must be **right at least once**, and the reader must be able to
see it coming and be unable to stop it.

### Required continuity facts into Volume 4

Write them as a numbered list, in the form `outline/volume-02.md` used. They include at minimum:
Condensing stage reached and how the ceiling was lifted; the licence and what relicensing changed
about it; the claim standing or answered; Du Hong and the two crew dead and named causes; Feng's Lung
and what the audit cost him; the two words and no paper; the register still *reconciled*; the plate
still worn; Ledger held and refused, or held and spent; the year of non-forgiveness still running.

### Concrete resolution

The volume's own promise must be paid **inside** the volume, not deferred: what surviving being hunted
actually looks like, who signs, what she is at the last page, and what is still open. Write it as a
concrete list against ch 176–178.

Volume 4 is ***The Wall***, ch 179–238, goal fixed by `outline/series.md` §9:

Write the question Volume 3 opens onto it. It must come out of what the Hunter did and what relicensing

### Hard rules for the Volume 3 drafters

Restate these once, in the outline, and never again in a batch prompt.

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the
  present tense. No other character is ever in the present tense** — with the standing exception, set
  by ch 57, that statements about completed third-person events keep the past tense.
- At most one bracketed layer line per chapter, on its own line, in square brackets, never bold, never
  italics, never a heading. They announce; they never explain and they never advise.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or
  italic. **No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a
  paragraph; break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly 1,800–2,700 words, floor 1,300.
- **Close every speech.** `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` before finishing.
- No harem. **Nothing in Volume 3 is a romance.** Wei Ran does not appear before Volume 4.
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until Volume 16, by Ning Wu. Ning Yan never says her mother's
  name. Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before Volume 11, and does not learn she saw
  him as a readout before Volume 11 either.
- Do not re-explain known principles: the four seconds, the release rule, tax-takes-order,
  layer-is-editable, damage-as-technique, the eleven designations, the bounty, the six licence
  conditions, what a surety is for, the suppression arithmetic, the position-not-characters rule, the
  name in the wrong order, or the crew head count. **Walk the construction budget at the foot of
  `state/continuity.md` before writing and confirm nothing from it is reused as emphasis.**
- **Do not introduce a new final enemy.** The Scribe is fixed in `outline/ending.md` and is not
  improvised at the end of the run.
- **Volume 3 is hunted, not another winter.** It must be a different shape of pressure from every one
  of Volume 2's four.

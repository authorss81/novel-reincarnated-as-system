## Where the batch opens — from the page, not from a plan

**Day 1,831, the eighteenth of the month beginning 1,814 — (1,831 − 134) = 1,697; 1,697 mod 30 = 17; +1 = 18.**

> **She does not ask him yet.**

**Day 1,842 is eleven days from where she is standing, and the seventh stage comes on it, and her asking is
what gives the waiting its shape.**

**She is nineteen, at Severance 6, with no wages, no place, no road and no list, a bar nine feet nine, a skin,
a bag with a dish and a lump of clay and a ring a finger's width and a stub of a nib and a cake of ink she has
not used in two months. Four fingers and a thumb, an arm open four days in five, eight of eleven holds, four
counts of breath with seven on a rise and inside a loaded reach, and the small open place low behind her fourth
rib. She has been lighter every single day since the eighteenth of the month beginning 1,544 and not one day of
it has gone back. She is not hungry.**

**In her coat, against the side where the paper is, there is a book of eight leaves made out of a blanket: a
date and a place on the first leaf, four sentences on the second that she has read out loud once and will not
read again, and six empty ones that are hers and that stay blank in this batch. There are two things against her
ribs and she has called neither of them anything.**

**In her hand, nine coppers she cannot count.** The sixth cost working on the only money she has: finding stops
being keeping, and holding is what closes, and counting them is holding them.

**What stands asked and unasked:**

- **The question.** Carried since the month beginning 1,574, never put anywhere: what he was before he started
  counting. **She asks it at ch 609, out loud, alone, at dusk, and it cannot be un-asked.**
- **The terms.** Named by him at ch 609 and they do not move for the rest of the volume: one at a time · in
  daylight. Eleven days each way to nowhere, because there is nowhere to walk to — the asking is done standing.
- **The lever.** Asking twice is the only thing that would make him get to the first answer sooner, and the only
  thing that would make it never get answered. **She carries it and does not pull it, including at ch 614.**
- **The brother.** Walked west past her at the turn at dusk on the seventeenth. **She lets him go at ch 605 and
  the letting costs the walking.**
- ***Twenty-two days* is still OPEN and unplaced.** It may be put in front of her again and **no card equates it
  to any dated interval.**

**West, the store road:** a cottage with a shut door at a stopped lime bed four miles west of the turn · a mill
five miles west with a wheel that has not turned for nine years · a store behind the mill fourteen miles from
the post. **The lane is four miles west of the turn — the twice-listed map (ch 585, ch 600) holds and ch 560's

**East, twenty-two miles:** strip nine, made eleven, two past a gate, a wall standing open with nothing behind

## What this batch was

Hiring, the road, and whose book an hour goes into. The season's road opens on the morning of day 254 and
she is on it before the light is off the hill, on purpose, with about thirty coppers, no speech made to nine
men and a county office she is out of reach of for the rest of the run. Nine days east she is hired at a
trestle for six coppers a day, her hours go into a contractor's book in order with dates on them, the second
relicensing is taken on three legs all of them outside this county, the monthly attendance is missed on
purpose and entered against her permanently, and on day 276 Foundation 1 is relicensed and announced at her
own order and heard by nobody at all.

**It buys a book and spends an attendance.**

## What changed that could not have changed before

A woman who had never spent a day of a voice on purpose walks nine days into a country where nobody is
waiting for her and finds that the ordinaryest thing about it is that she is not being spoken to. She pays
a contractor in coin out loud on a bridge for the first time in her life, and learns that the work at the end
of the road pays less per day than it costs to get there. **She gives a name at a counter and becomes
legible, in order, with dates, to the one office that could act on a claim of eight hundred jin against her,
and would do it again in the morning.** A foreman of about fifty opens a wage-book he is under no obligation
to open and the first honest description of the line in this book happens in his mouth and in the mouth of
a man holding a board, and **both of them are right**, and she has nothing whatever to set against them.
Three men sign her sheet in a room with a hundred sheets on the table and charge nothing and ask nothing and
are not thanked. **The third leg of the relicensing that lifts her ceiling is countersigned by a hand that
is not a person's, on a number a stranger read aloud out of a book she was never shown.** A man who keeps
other people's schedules is found at work, and tells her the truth about his trade, and cannot be caught,
and answers nothing. And on the sixteenth, **a county's monthly member sheet comes round with the work, and
MEMBER ABSENT goes into one column and nine days of good hours go into the column beside it, and she is
offered the one word that could have gone in the reason column and does not write it, because that column is
the column her hours go in** — knowingly, in daylight, for a reason that is entirely hers, with nobody having
stopped her at any point. And a fortnight later **the first Foundation of her life is announced in the
ordinary way in a country where nothing has ever been announced, and eleven men six hundred yards off do not
look up.**

## Irreversible decisions in this batch — four

1. **Ch 205 — she does not write the word, and the attendance is entered against her forever.** It is on the
   county's monthly member sheet, it is separate from her hours, it cannot be paid for in hours, and it is
   permanent. **It is the first thing she has ever lost by choosing to move rather than by being stopped**,
   and it was not inevitable and nothing in the batch plants it.
2. **Ch 198 — she gives the name.** From that hour her work is in a book an office past the county's
   boundary can read in order with dates on it. **It cannot be unsaid**, and it is the same instrument that
   would let a referral be executed.
3. **Ch 204 — the tin is gone and the road west is not bought.** She spends the last of the money walking and
   **she loses the option of leaving cheaply**, and what she learns is that the reason she is nine days from a
   county counter is not a county and not a boundary. It is thirty coppers and a seat at a bridge.
4. **Ch 193 — she goes, and she does not say goodbye.** Nine men, a girl, a keeper, a board behind a well,
   and no speech. **She is out of reach of the county office, the shelf, the tower, the wash house and the
   board for the rest of the run**, and nothing in that is convenient.

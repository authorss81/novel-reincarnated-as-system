## Prose and format

- **Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. Every sentence Yuan speaks is in the present tense. No
  other character is ever in the present tense**, with the ch 57 exception for statements about completed
  third-person events.
- **At most one bracketed layer line per chapter**, on its own line, in square brackets, never bold, never
  italics, never a heading. **One is planned in this batch, at ch 207.**
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional `##` section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or italic.
  **No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a paragraph; break
  over about 90 words; never over about 120.
- **Close every speech.** Check the count before finishing.
- Roughly 1,800–2,700 words a chapter, floor 1,300, and **design the shape with peaks — do not let it fall
  monotonically across sixteen chapters.**
- Give short plain sentences at the moment something is understood, not inside a long accumulating sentence.
  One simple sentence about one person after any stretch of machinery.
- **Do not put outline language in a character's thought or a narrator's line** — *this chapter, this volume,
  the next chapter, in this batch, the whole series, the reader*.
- **Never:** *his eyes widened*, *he couldn't believe it*, *suddenly*, *little did he know*.
- **Construction watch:** *nobody asked*, *nobody was thanked*, *there is no form*, *there is no column*,
  *the record remained*, *nobody knew*. Effective once or twice each across the whole book and dead by the
  tenth.

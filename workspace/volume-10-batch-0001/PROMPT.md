## Batch goal, midpoint, escalation, climax, irreversible step

**Goal:** stand her at the post with his answer in her ears, feed her by trade and not by gift, and ask the
question out loud — what else is on the list — so that Volume 10's engine is running by ch 554.

**Pressure: asking what else is on it.** This batch is the first in ten volumes in which she asks a keeper for
what he keeps instead of refusing what he offers. Nothing in it is a recruiting, a seduction, a keeping, a debt,
a family paper, an ash, a ration, a hiring, a crossing, an audit, an examination, a hunt, a signature, a
confinement, or a second demand in a room.

**Midpoint, ch 546:** she decides the question is askable. Not the answer — the asking. She has never asked a
keeper for anything in eight years, and the chapter is about finding out that she can. **Everything before it is
counting and eating; everything after it is walking toward the asking.**

**Escalation:** he goes back east (the book does) · she stays west (nothing else to be) · she eats by trade ·
she counts what asking will cost · she walks east partway down twenty-two miles and comes back · she decides ·
she stands at the wall in daylight · she asks.

**Climax, ch 553–554:** she asks him out loud, in daylight, with witnesses — what else is on the list. **He does
not answer yet. He names the terms instead: one question at a time, at the wall, in daylight.** The terms are
ordinary and they do not move for the rest of the volume.

**Irreversible step, ch 553–554:** **she asks what else he keeps, in the open, with witnesses, and it cannot be
un-asked.** From this chapter the list is closing, and no later chapter may write as though it is not.

**Physical risk or real deadline, pinned:** **a night walk on open ground with short breath and an arm open four
days in five, with no place to lie down in — and bread earned by trade at the store woman's rate before the
nine-days telling count runs out.** The count ends because she ends it, by work, and no one keeps her for it.

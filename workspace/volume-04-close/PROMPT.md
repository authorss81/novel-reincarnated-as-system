# Volume 4 close — audit

## What this phase is

**Volume 4 is complete at ch 238.** All four batches are written (ch 179–192, 193–208, 209–222, 223–238) and
this is the volume-close audit. It walks `outline/volume-04.md` line by line against what is actually on

finds, and then creates exactly one follow-up prompt — the Volume 5 planning phase, which will write

drafting prose, or drafting Volume 5's acceptance criteria, you have drifted: stop and record the finding
instead.

## The open question this close must answer in writing — the protagonist

**This is the question `state/open-threads.md` has been carrying since the Batch 0004 planning review, and
the close is where it belongs. It is not settled anywhere in ch 179–238 and nothing in the drafting of the
batch settled it.**

`outline/series.md` §2 names **Yuan, male**, as the protagonist, and `state/book-metadata.md` records
**Gender: Male**, so the male-led requirement holds at series level. But **Volume 4 is close third on Ning Yan
throughout, and Yuan has been a voice only — rationed, off-panel, and in the last batch spent down to a
single mark and then restored — for two volumes of sixteen.** That is defensible on the series outline's own
terms. It is also the single largest structural debt the book has run up, and it must be **stated, in
writing, in `workspace/volume-04-close/SUMMARY.md`, `state/current.md` and the Volume 5 hand-off**, in these
terms:

- **What the protagonist actually did across Volume 4.** He found a hand that wrote a date against a claim.
  He held a standing for a crossing and was paid for it out of a base that was not finished. He intercepted a
  share of a settled job and spent it. He restored his own life. He read one name he had said four times he
  could not read, in one breath, and stopped. **Every one of those is a decision by the book's protagonist
  and every one of them was paid for by the woman, in hours, in a stage, or in a finger.**
- **Whether that is enough**, and if it is not, **what Volume 5 does about it — in writing, before Volume 5
  opens.** The options the series outline permits are all still open: a chapter or a run of chapters inside
  Volume 5's allocation where he is **on the page**; a structural change of frame; or a decision that Volume
  5's shape is itself the correction. **Record which, and why, and what it costs.**
- **What may not happen.** The close may not re-frame ch 179–238, may not retitle the volume, may not
  introduce a new final enemy, and **may not take anything Volume 5 owns** — the family account, Ning Wu's
  decade, the four months erased in Chapter 3, the bargain and the fall, and the Scribe. **The Scribe is
  fixed in `outline/ending.md` and belongs to Volume 15.**

leaving it costs everyone she left standing in it.*** The hand-off written in `state/current.md` and in the
close summary must state plainly, as a fact on the page and not as a plan:

> **She stood in the stretch nobody is paid to tend, and an announcement came out of her and went past the
> line, and the people on the far side wrote it in their own board because that is what the board is for. The
> bound did not notice, and cannot: a county book has no column for a thing heard past its own boundary,
> and nine counties that heard nine stages in order have heard nothing else. But the far side keeps a
> wage-board for a line it maintains by hand, and it now holds a figure in it that no county in nine counties
> can answer for — and a woman standing on their side of it whose licence carries a claim their side can act
> on.**

And alongside it:

- **She is at FOUNDATION NINE, the top of the second realm, and there is nothing above nine anywhere in the

  Four fingers' worth of grip left on that hand. Eleven holds in the other one out of about seventy tried.
  **Nothing gives it back and nothing offers to.**

- **The claim has moved and is not resolved.** Eight hundred jin, read out loud in a room on the far side, in

# Batch 0009 Summary (ch 104–118)

Fifteen chapters, **28,250 words** (~1,883/chapter). **VOLUME 2 CLOSED.** Grief and the grant.

She goes out onto the Verge ash-flats ten days after the burial and carries one four-pound stone thirty yards all day, because a grief needs something to be heavy with. **He asks her first and asks her properly** — three days held, both choices and both costs laid out, and the plain admission that he cannot know whether a hole in the chain breaks a filing. **She suppresses Condensing 4.** No bracketed line in that chapter at all: a suppressed stage reads as nothing having happened, and the filing counts stages *as announced*. Cost: the stage reaches no county book in nine counties, never will, and she stops eating for nine days, which he cannot fix and cannot name the shape of.

Then the grinding. **What a surety is actually for** — not a name on a sheet, but the right to be sick — stated once by a county clerk and entered in writing before the licence exists. **The pursuit becomes attendance**: two men on a wall buying nothing, unhurried, and she offers them four coins and is told *we are not to be here, we are to be here.* The girl of twenty-four is alive and tells her the county has asked in writing for the season's furnace schedule and enters it on the quarter's return sheet on her own hour, and Ning Yan does not thank her because the girl has built not-being-thanked into the arrangement as protection.

**Paper turn at ch 110; the striking itself at ch 111.** Ch 110 is the arrival: a sealed slip on ordinary county stationery, nothing above the county mark, up in the quarter's crate to the copying floor — a request from **outside the bound** that the term in the second position of the status column be struck wherever it is copied, naming it by position, not by characters. **Feng states the order of his own three reasons, copies the shape without reading it, strikes his copies and keeps the shape.** The office has a name Yuan cannot read, said for the fourth time, in front of a witness. The slip does not wear the Bureau hand from ch 36. Nothing answers what eleven became. **Ch 111** is the county drawing the rule down the second position at the second hour while she stands in the ante-room and is not consulted; her sheet reads now like any other advancement in the bound, and the word is in **two heads and no paper**. The volumes on the high shelf are not copies and were not struck.

She goes up the mountain for the schedule the countersignature needs, and comes home to nine men who will not sign her shift and who say *she did it* at the bottom of the stair, not to her. Shen Xiu enters 311 and does not look at her. **Lu Kang certifies the schedule in silence** — his own paper, his own stamp, no word.

**Stage five at ch 114, at her order, announced.** The one bracketed line in the batch, in fresh four-clause order, and the third clause carries the ceiling: *Nothing further is let go.* She is **glad of the noise for the first time**, because the noise is the record, and the cost is stated plainly and accepted: nine counties, a tower, a clerk with her mark on two documents.

**Ch 115 — the grant.** Sun He finds the gap in her record — one, two, three, and then five — and **rules in the open that an absence is not a breach**, writing the distinction onto the sheet so the next man does not have to make it himself. The stamp falls. Scope: draw sect qi, take paid work outside the crew, be taught past her stage. **Six conditions read to the queue, the sixth of which enters the eight hundred jin standing claim on the licence itself.** He says it is worth exactly a stamp. Feng stood four feet behind her for four hours, read nothing, and said afterwards that it was like copying. She walks out legal, capped, grieving, and priced, and the good coat stands in the rain and watches the door she comes out of.

She nails it crooked over her own bunk. The whole of Volume 2 is counted off at ch 117 item by item and not softened. **Settlement a little under three-quarters, about six weeks.** She asks him how much channel he has left and he tells her true; six weeks is less time than the licence has conditions in it; **she does not tell him to stop and she tells him conditions** — four of hers, three of his. Then Feng's sheet comes down with the crate: a second cough in the stairwell that is not his, a man at the first desk who buys nothing and gives his name to nobody. **Unnamed.**

## What changed that could not have changed before

A woman who had spent a working season being the strongest body on a line of work finds out that **strength has not once been the shortage and there is a bar in this place and she is the slowest person at the only object there is** — eleven minutes a day where a man takes four, and not one person on that course mentions it. She learns that **a line holds because about sixty painted marks get looked at before the light is off the hill**, and that there is nobody upstream of that board in the world. She watches a carrier come back east with the county's paper and takes a date and one office word out of it that she will never show anybody, and **lets the road west stop existing without refusing it**. She buys a stage with the part of her wage that was keeping her warm and spends eleven cold days on it and is not hungry and does not have time to ask why. She learns that **a kind word was never a standing arrangement** and that she had built a floor out of nine seconds of a decent man. She works out, in sixteen days and a bar, that **the ground is loud where the most has been said**, and cannot prove a line of it, and tells nobody — and that is the first change in the kind of person she is in four volumes that nobody in the room can detect. She pays a man in a plain coat with a notebook one ration for a road answer she already had, and is told out loud, by an honest man, **that the only thing that can be done about her is done by strangers who have never met her and who will be right.** She looks at a column on a board nobody fills, finds one of her own dates in it, and **does not buy it with the one instrument she has**, and finds out that the not-buying is the easiest thing she does. And then: **four Foundation stages in fourteen chapters, four brackets, four costs, none of them the same thing** — the fire, a count of breath, the order out of a hand, and a line drawn through her name in a foreman's own hand in the open. **The line's own people close their course against her, lawfully and correctly, off page, using the paper of a man who does not want it executed — and pay for it themselves**: the stretch goes back in three days, the contract is smaller, the board has a hole in it, nine men are idle and a girl of nineteen who is somebody's daughter is idle. **She says nothing in her own defence because there is no version of anything she could say.** And she takes work under a man who does not know why, at five coppers, into a second book, on purpose, in daylight, **and knows exactly what it costs, which is the one continuous run of hours her whole relicensing was built out of.** And she stands in the quietest ground on nine days next to a woman who went through without permission, **and there is no word within nine days of road for either of the two things she has learned.**

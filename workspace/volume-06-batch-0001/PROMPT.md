## The two failure modes, forbidden twice

1. **No authorial scaffolding at all** — *this chapter*, *this volume*, *this book*, *the rest of the book*,
   *the reader*, *in this batch*, *another batch*, *N volumes*. Volume 5 shipped seven of them against a prompt
   rule forbidding them, while a construction budget recorded the count as zero with seven standing on the page.
   A batch that fails this way cannot detect itself. Count by `grep` across the batch before claiming zero, and
   write the count into the card file honestly.
2. **No construction budget that records zero while instances stand on the page.** The same Volume 5 close found
   *about four seconds* recorded zero with four standing, and the Yuan tag recorded as motif while eleven ran in
   fifteen chapters. Walk the construction budget at the foot of `state/continuity.md` before carding, name every
   carried construction and its remaining allowance on the card file's budget page, and `grep` each one before
   claiming zero. **No authorial scaffolding at all, and no zero that is not zero — both rules bind every card.**

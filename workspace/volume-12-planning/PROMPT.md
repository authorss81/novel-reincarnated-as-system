## The goal, fixed, not to be re-planned

> In this volume, Ning Yan must ascend at all, or the Chosen One designation is harvested and every sacrifice Yuan has made is spent for nothing.

**Read that against Volume 11's close and notice what it costs you.** Volume 11 ended with the designation *named*: a
levy of one in twenty, counted in days, off a board before the board goes up, **made to a figure by somebody who chose
the figure**, and a thing that was made to find people like her. Volume 11 also ended with a woman who has learned
that knowing stops being a road back. **Those two facts are the engine of Volume 12 and the outline must put them in
the same frame: she knows what the designation is for, and she cannot un-know it.** Ascending is what the levy wants.
That is not a hint to be planted later — it is the volume.

## What Volume 11 resolved, and what Volume 12 therefore inherits as its engine

**The engine comes out of five things and not out of a fresh piece of furniture.**

1. **The asking, at ch 609.** She asked the voice in her head what it was before it started counting, out loud,
   alone, in the dark, on a turn, and **it cannot be un-asked**. He answered, on the terms she set: **one at a time,
   in daylight, standing.** Those terms have not moved in sixty chapters and the outline fixes them for Volume 12.
2. **The reading, at ch 656–657.** Feng Lushi's four sheets of rice paper in a fair hand, **seven sentences, read
   alone at the fourth hour and read out loud once in daylight in front of a boy of sixteen who was not asked for**.
   **She did not copy them, because nobody writes one twice.** Five of her own eight blanket leaves are still blank.
3. **The writing.** `YUAN SHENG` on the third leaf of her own book, four capital strokes, no solvent and no hand, with
   a date and a place on it. **The ninth stage's cost is named once and is not a punishment: she cannot go back to not
   knowing.**
4. **The staying, at ch 658.** Said out loud, once, standing, in daylight, in a lit room with a witness in it who was
   not asked. **It cannot be un-stayed by walking and cannot be un-stayed by un-knowing.** She also told him, then,
   the thing nobody had told him: for eight years she did not have a man, she had a column.
5. **The forty-one strokes.** Down the right-hand margin of the fourth sheet, in Feng's hand, **with no heading and no
   line under them.** He saw a tally and did not know what it was and wrote it down anyway, and worded none of it.
   **She has carried them since ch 657 and has said out loud that she is not going to find out what they are.**
6. **And the road up, opened at ch 660.** At the sixth hour of the seventh of the month beginning 2,054, on a store
   road, with a bare board four hundred yards behind her, she said once, out loud, standing, in daylight: **I am going
   up.** **She has not got the rest of it. Nobody in nine days of road has ever been up one. The last of the ceiling
   went into the ground sixty-three days earlier, in front of thirteen people, and the thing that is left over her head
   is the sky, which has never announced anything to anybody.**

**Volume 11's own question for Volume 12, verbatim from `outline/volume-11.md`, and the outline this phase writes must
hand it on without answering it:**

> She has asked the voice what it was and learned it was made to recruit her, and the price of the learning is that
> there is now no un-knowing left in the world that can take her back to before she knew — except the road above her,
> which has never been walked by anybody she has ever counted with, and which costs more than either of them knows.
> At nine stages past the ceiling, with no place, no road, no list and no un-knowing, she is at last free enough to go
> up. **The question is not whether she can ascend. It is what the ascent takes from the only two people who ever
> counted together, and whether a designation made to be harvested can be carried up instead.**

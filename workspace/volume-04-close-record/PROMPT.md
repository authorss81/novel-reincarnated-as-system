### The protagonist requirement — this is not optional and it is the volume's second structural fact

**Read `state/current.md` § *The protagonist, answered in writing* before you write a word of the outline.**

`outline/series.md` §2 names **Yuan, male**, as the protagonist and `state/book-metadata.md` records
**Gender: Male**, so the male-led requirement holds at series level. **The close audit found that it is the
single largest structural debt the book has run up**: Yuan has been a voice only, off-panel, for **two volumes
of sixteen**, and Volume 4 was close third on Ning Yan for all sixty chapters.

**The decision made at the Volume 4 close, and Volume 5 inherits it: the shape of Volume 5 is itself the
correction, inside Volume 5's own sixty chapters.** `outline/series.md` §5 puts him at **Manipulating** in
Volumes 3–5 — *using her to read, move and spend the world's machinery* — and Volume 5 is the last volume of
that stage.

**The outline must therefore plan, by card and by batch:**

- **At least one sustained scene in which Yuan is on the page in his own right and acts, or is acted on,
  away from her hearing.** Not more rationed sentences. Not a description of him. Not a dream. **A scene with
  a room, a situation of his own, and a consequence that is his.**
- **The cost of that scene must be something of his, and something the ending still needs.** He has already
  paid the finger and the foundation. `outline/ending.md` is fixed that he refuses the bargain, takes the
  fall, apologises in the last chapter and is gone before ch 1000, and **`outline/ending.md` §1 has it that he
  has been doing the thing that makes him readable *on purpose* since Volume 14, and that by Volume 16 Ning
  Yan can no longer keep her out of his half of the conversation, and that this is deliberate on his part.**
  **A man the reader has never seen in a room cannot carry a fall.** Write what the scene costs him, in
  advance, in the outline, the way Volume 4 named the finger in advance.
- **Put the scene on a card. Name the chapter. Say what it is about in one sentence.** Do not leave it as an
  intention.
- **What may not happen:** the close may not re-frame ch 179–238, may not re-title Volume 4, may not
  introduce a new final enemy, and may not take anything Volume 5 owns — **the family account, Ning Wu's
  decade, the four months erased in Chapter 3, the bargain and the fall. The Scribe is fixed in
  `outline/ending.md` and belongs to Volume 15**, and Volume 5 must not touch it.
- **What Volume 5 must carry unchanged:** close third on Ning Yan for every external scene; **every sentence
  Yuan speaks in the present tense, and no other character ever in a marked present frame**, with the
  standing ch 57 exception about completed third-person events.

### Hard rules for the Volume 5 drafters

Restate these once, in the outline, and never again in a batch prompt.

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the present
  tense. No other character is ever in the present tense** — with the standing exception, set by ch 57, that
  statements about completed third-person events keep the past tense.
- At most one bracketed layer line per chapter, on its own line, in square brackets, never bold, never
  italics, never a heading. They announce; they never explain and they never advise. **Volume 5 must state
  its own bracket budget and it must be honest: one at each announced stage and none in the chapter that
  suppresses one.**
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or italic.
  **No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a paragraph;
  break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly 1,800–2,700 words, floor 1,300.

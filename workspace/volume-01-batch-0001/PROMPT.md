## The voice, which is the whole job

Close third on **Ning Yan** for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the present
tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense. That is the reader's only cue for who is talking
and it never breaks. He is nineteen, dead six years, sarcastic, embarrassed, and more competent than he lets
on. He has no body and the novel never lets him forget it.

The bracketed lines belong to the **layer**, not to Yuan: square brackets, on their own line, **at most one
per chapter**. They announce. They never explain and never advise.

Chapter titles are names, not summaries: three to twelve words, unique, no weather, no clock time, no dates,
no ages, no dialogue. The titles are already fixed in the batch cards. Use them exactly.

Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold or italic. No
blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two to four sentences per paragraph, break over
about 90 words, never over about 120. Around 1,800–2,700 words per chapter unless the scene needs longer.

## The opening gate — this batch fails without it

**Chapter 1, inside the first 800 words:** the reader must have all three of Ning Yan's concrete immediate
objective, an anomaly she can see and cannot explain, and a consequence that follows from acting on it. She
has a quarter-token four hands short before the dawn quota bell. The anomaly is a voice that knows her
name, her realm, her stage and her breathing rate. The consequence is that she speaks her brother's name
aloud to test it, and Team Elder Shen Xiu hears a sound that is not his own.

**By the end of chapter 3**, the central mechanic and its cost must be shown by an **event**, not explained.
She kills a listed Ashjaw and Merit settles into Yuan. In chapter 3 he spends that Merit to stop a killing
stroke that would open her throat, and the price comes out of her foundation instead of the sky: she wakes at
Body-Forging **4** instead of 9, four months of practice gone, a scar from sternum to hip, and the Ashwood
Nine-Step missing from her head. She asks him what it took. He does not answer. That silence is the first
thing the reader should want to break.

## What this batch has to do besides write well

- **Every chapter changes the situation.** Not "she learned a rule." An option closed, a cost incurred, a
  relationship moved, a threat raised or neutralised, a decision made. If a chapter can be lifted out and
  dropped into a later chapter unchanged, it is the wrong chapter.
- **At least one irreversible decision.** Chapter 3 is the batch's: he spends her without her consent, and
  she keeps talking to him anyway, and that cannot be undone.
- **At least one scene with physical risk or a real deadline.** Chapters 2, 3 and 8 carry the fights. Chapter 1
  and chapter 12 carry the deadline.
- **Varied temperature.** Cold dread, small competence, a real fight, a dead heat of exhaustion, one
  unexpected ally in chapter 10, one document-warping revelation in chapter 12. Do not hold one note.
- **Show the layer, do not lecture it.** The reader learns it announces to everybody, that bounties settle
  Merit, and that a quest has a stated cost, by having those three things happen to somebody.
- **Do not re-explain.** State each rule of the layer once, the first time it costs somebody something. The
  construction watch from `state/continuity.md` applies hard: *nobody asked, nobody was thanked, there is
  no form, there is no column, the record remained, nobody knew* are each good once and dead by the third.

## Hard continuity to protect

- Body-Forging 3 → 9 (ch 3) → **4** (ch 4). Never any other number.
- Yuan's Settlement: rises when he uses the channel, falls when he spends Merit. He cannot see his own
  number and she cannot see it either.
- The winter quota at Qinghe is **twenty-eight this year**, and the layer announces a harvest that makes it
  thirty-three. Everyone in the yard believes twenty-eight.
- Ning Wu is not dead on the page. The furnace column is a six-year unsettled account inherited from Ning Bo.
- Ning Yan has **not yet been to Lowwater, the Broken Kiln, the records room or the Assembly.** Those are
  later batches. Do not spend them early.
- Feng Lushi is a consumptive clerk with twenty-two years of announcements in his fair hand. He has never
  heard of her before chapter 10.
- Shen Xiu is a tired man with a quota, not a villain, and he is not evil anywhere in this batch.

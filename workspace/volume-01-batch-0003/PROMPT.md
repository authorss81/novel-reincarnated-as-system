## The voice, which is the whole job

Close third on **Ning Yan** for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the present
tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense. That is the reader's only cue for who is talking
and it never breaks. He is nineteen, dead six years, sarcastic, embarrassed, more competent than he lets
on. He has no body and the novel never lets him forget it. He lost his mother's name in ch 24 — never says
it again, never says he forgot.

The bracketed lines belong to the **layer**, not to Yuan: square brackets, on their own line, **at most one
per chapter**. They announce. They never explain and never advise.

Chapter titles are names, not summaries: three to twelve words, unique, no weather, no clock time, no dates,
no ages, no dialogue. The titles are already fixed in the batch cards. Use them exactly:
25 The Log, 26 Rebuilding, 27 Shen Xiu's Price, 28 The Elder of the Furnace, 29 Ning Wu,
30 The Offer, 31 The Turn, 32 Inside, 33 Du Hong, 34 The Report, 35 The Furnace Floor, 36 What It Cost.

Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold or italic. No
blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two to four sentences per paragraph, break over
about 90 words, never over about 120. Around 1,800–2,700 words per chapter unless the scene needs longer.

- **Do not re-explain.** State each rule once, first time it costs somebody. Construction watch applies hard:
  *nobody asked, nobody was thanked, there is no form, there is no column, the record remained, nobody knew*
  are dead — do not reuse without new meaning. Furnace column already spent.

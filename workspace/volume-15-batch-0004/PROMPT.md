## The male lead — this is the volume's live problem and it is fixed here, not in prose

**The review of Batch 0003 found the male lead functionally absent across fifteen chapters: thirty-four lines, all two to four words, all reassurance.** The repair rebuilt all fifteen chapters so that he speaks in every one, at length, in the present tense, with wants of his own, refusals, limits and acts. **The plot of this batch is not moved to get that back. The plot of this batch is already his at ch 894 and ch 900. What was missing was the man standing in front of them.**

**Four standing requirements for ch 886–900, and they are not optional.**

1. **He speaks in every chapter, and in at least six of the fifteen he carries an exchange rather than a line.** Two to four-word comfort — *you work*, *you stay*, *I am here* — was the defect. **A reader must be able to point at the sentence in any chapter that tells them what this man wants today, and it must not be the same sentence twice.**
2. **He is answerable.** He has been inside her head since she was ten and is heard by the thing that made him. He can be **wrong, out of reach, or honest past his own interest.** On the twentieth he told her plainly that he has looked for nine years and cannot name where the taking goes; on the twenty-sixth he refused outright to say whether he knew there was a ceiling, because in that building the saying is a line; on the tenth he told her *I wanted it not told, not small*, and she had nothing to answer. **Those are the register. Keep it.**
3. **He acts at least twice, and once at his own cost.** An act inside the channel — refusing a question, answering one she did not ask, giving her a thing nobody asked him for (ch 879), choosing not to argue (ch 885) — counts. A frame scene does not and is banned here.
4. **No eleventh frame scene.** `outline/volume-15.md` fixes nine before Volume 14, a tenth at ch 812, and none in ch 841–900. This batch takes none.

**Ch 900 is the chapter the review was most worried about, so it is specified here rather than left to a card.** The outline and `outline/ending.md` §2 are fixed and this prompt does not move them: she asks him what he wants for the first time in nineteen years, **he does not answer**, the offer stands, and his acceptance for about four minutes is at the head of Volume 16.

**What the prompt changes is what the silence is made of.** A man who has said *you stay* for fifteen chapters and then says nothing at the end reads as absent. A man who has argued, refused, admitted a failure, been wrong, been forgiven nothing, been given a boy's name by the woman he loves and been **given four minutes of a decision by the thing he was made by** — and who then does not answer — reads as a man declining.

**So at ch 900 the not-answering must be visible as an act and not as an absence.** She asks, and he is there, and his half is audible in the way it is audible everywhere else, and what she hears is a man at the edge of a decision and not a man who has gone. She reads it. **She does not forgive him, does not withdraw, and does not fill the silence for him.** The chapter ends on the silence being his and hers to keep, not on his absence. **No chapter after 900 may report that he was silent because he had nothing to say.**

## Prose, and the house style

**Numbers are words. Paragraphs short (two to four sentences; break over ~90 words; never over ~120). Reader inside her hearing. Short sentences at revelation; long ones elsewhere. No more than three consecutive documentary paragraphs; one simple sentence about one person after machinery. Every chapter ends on question, danger, discovery, decision, reversal, or emotional consequence — never bed, arithmetic, or neutral action. His sentences only in present tense. Felt emotion plus concrete want every chapter. Vary emotional temperature. No procedure loop: every chapter transforms the larger situation.**

**Do not re-explain known principles:** landings-not-steps, the lamp at four feet, wire-not-voice, the intervals, the Anchor, the count, the checker, the fall, the open place behind the fourth rib.

**And do not rebuild what the review of Batch 0003 took out of fifteen chapters. Concretely, in this batch:**

- **Openings must vary.** She has said her day's figures every morning since the eighth of the month beginning two thousand eight hundred and sixty-four and she does not recite them all fifteen times. **At most four of these fifteen chapters may open on the figures**, and those four must not be consecutive. The other eleven open on a place, a cold, a sound, him speaking, a thing in the dark, a decision, or a body.
- **`She said` must not open more than about one paragraph in six.** In ch 871–885 it is 50 of 1,130.
- **Do not write `And here is the whole of what…`, `said plain`, `I am naming it because…`, `X finishes nothing, and I am saying so out loud`, `That is the record and there is nothing else in it`, or `Lamp on the third landing. Burning. Mine.`** Each was used nine to ninety-six times in Batch 0003 and every one of them is dead.
- **Name the feeling in the scene, not in a slot.** She may name it out loud; she may not open a paragraph with a feeling and a comparison to yesterday's.
- **No two chapters end on the same shape**, and no chapter ends on a line that restates its own opening.
- **No sentence that points at the book.** Not `fifteen volumes`, not `Chapter 3`, not *the last chapter*, not *this one*. The world has no page numbers.

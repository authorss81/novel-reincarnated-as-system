**The goal is fixed and is not to be re-derived here:** `outline/series.md` §9 gives *In this volume, Ning Yan must survive Shuyin, or the walls stay and the reason for them goes unrecorded* — **shuyin, lower case, the man.** `outline/ending.md` §1 records that *Shuyin released the Anchor list in Volume 10 and is alive, old, and not thanked*, which is a hard constraint on how the phase plans ch 479–538: **Shuyin survives Volume 9 and is not thanked at its close, and nothing this phase writes may spend either of those.** He is also, per `outline/series.md` §6, **nine hundred years old, keeps a list, and some of the names on it are his friends** — he is honest, and he is not a villain, and this is a plan for how to write him, not a problem to solve.

**The question Volume 9 opens onto is fixed at `outline/volume-08.md` §*New question for Volume 9* and is written here so it cannot be re-derived differently:**

> She has spent a volume being the strongest person in every room and it turned out to be a qualification, and she has
> refused the one place on nine days of road that would have paid her for the only thing she is good at, and the price
> of the refusal is that there is now nothing on nine days of road that wants anything from her — and at the top of a
> realm, with the only door in the world being a piece of paper she has been carrying since a furnace took her
> father, she is at last free enough to go and open it. **The question is not whether she will be recruited again. It
> is what a person does with a silence that she bought, when the one voice in her head has nothing left to spend and
> has stopped answering, and there is a door eleven hundred years of practice has never been opened from the inside.**

**And the last thing that happened on the last page of Volume 8, which is the first thing Volume 9 opens on:** she said his name once into a shed with boards on the floor, and nothing came back, because he has had nothing to spend for eighty-four days and there is nothing left to answer with — **and she waited anyway.** That sentence is the first line of Volume 9's first chapter and the whole of its first scene, and the plan is built out of it.

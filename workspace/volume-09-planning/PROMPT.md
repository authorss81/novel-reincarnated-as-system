A PLAN THAT HAS BEGUN INVENTING FURNITURE.** He has a room he can be found in, a way of standing, a thing he is careful

**Some of the names on his list are his friends, and he will not pretend otherwise and will not be caught pretending.

bookkeeping on something terrible because somebody has to and he is the last one left who remembers doing it before.**

**He is not the final enemy and must not be built as one. He returns.**

## The shape of Volume 9, and the one thing it must not be

**Volume 8 ran on seduction and a building with a hundred lamps in it.** The Order is a trade, a room, a book, a rate,
an offer made in daylight by a woman who is right. **Volume 9 must be visibly a different book. Another building full of
competent people offering her a better version of the same thing is not a new volume; it is Volume 8 with a bigger
counter.** The register has to change. **A man who keeps a list and tells you what is on it is a different kind of
pressure from a man who tells you what a reading is worth**, and the plan should be written so a reader could tell from
the outline alone that these are different rooms.

**Useful pressure differences to consider, not a list to obey:** the antagonist is not selling her anything and cannot be
persuaded; the question is not whether she can refuse but whether a thing she already refused can be used against her;
the setting is a boundary and a thing that is kept, not an institution and a thing that is offered; and **Volume 8 ended
with silence bought, and Volume 9 opens on the cost of silence — which is that a man with a list is the only person left
who is going to come and find her.**

**The volume's own central pressure, in one sentence, fixed by its goal and not to be re-derived: *she has bought her own
anonymity and the only person in the world who cannot be stopped using it is the man who keeps the list.***

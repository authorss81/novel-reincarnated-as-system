## Hard rules for this batch

- Close third on **Ning Yan** for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written
  in the present tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense. It has never broken
  in 58 chapters.
- **At most one bracketed layer line per chapter**, on its own line. They announce; they never
  explain and never advise. There is no breakthrough in this batch, so no chapter *requires* a
  layer line; the layer is still permitted where the story earns it — a claim, a killing, a
  death — and must not be sprinkled in to fill the slot.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold or
  italic. No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two to four sentences
  per paragraph; break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly 1,800–2,700 words.
- **Close every speech.** If a speech runs over more than one paragraph, the first paragraph ends
  with a closing quote and the next opens with a fresh one. Narration never sits inside an open
  quote. Batch 0004 shipped seven unclosed quotes of exactly that shape and it was a review
  failure. Run `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` on every file before you finish.
- Titles are fixed by the cards. All fourteen are unique against the 58 titles of Volume 1.
- Volume 1 is **cold dread and small competence**. Volume 2 is **grinding, claustrophobic, a
  real loss**. Do not write another Volume 1.
- The announcement wording is **fixed** and is quoted word for word in chapter 1000:
  *Assembly Hall of Qinghe. Ning Yan of the Qinghe furnace crew, servant-born. What was held is
  let go. What was taken is held.*
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until **Volume 16**, by Ning Wu.
- Ning Yan never says her mother's name. Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before
  **Volume 11**. He does not learn that she saw him as a readout during the stage-nine report
  before Volume 11 either.
- No harem. Nothing in Volume 2 is a romance. `Wei Ran` does not appear before Volume 3.

## Geography and terms that were got wrong once and must not be got wrong again

- **Lowwater is a county and the town under Qinghe Sect.** The Bureau has two separate
  presences there and they are different rooms: the copying floor inside Feng Lushi's tower,
  and the county office on the square. Never merge them. **There is no Qinghe county office —
  Qinghe is a sect.**
- **Hanhui is a place**, eleven days down the road, one of the nine counties. It is not a firm,
  a house, or a trading company. Hou Sanliang is a broker **of Lowwater**.
- **Cracking** is the world event that splits the ground and is followed by eleven days of ash.
  It is not a furnace maintenance phase. Furnace repair work is the **settling floor**.
- **Quota** is the annual number a sect must supply. It is not a bank of personal hours.
- **The records room is beneath the furnace**, not off the Hall. The Hall has no roof and is
  used for paper only.
- **Clause Four** of the Servant Advancement Settlement, not Clause Nine. A ninth clause already
  exists in this book (ch 41, the assessed, four lines, nobody reads it).

## This batch's goal

She works **two schedules at once** under a ceiling of nine, reaches Condensing 6 and announces it at
her own order, and the sentence tells nine counties where a relicensed licensee now stands. A lawful
request for the tower's paper upward is answered truthfully by a keeper who cannot lie, and the
answer opens a tower that has been closed for twenty-two years. A man who keeps that tower is offered
mercy with a hook and takes it, because his lungs will not stand another winter of copying cold. And
on the escort road a bounty man working the entered act puts his hand on a boy, and she kills him with
one working arm and very nearly dies, and the only thing that pays for it is the body.

## Hard rules

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is in the present
  tense. No other character is ever written in a marked present frame.**
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or italic.
  **No blockquotes, code blocks, tables, horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a paragraph; break
  over about ninety words; **never over about 120.** Roughly **1,800–2,700 words, floor 1,300**. Batch
  0001 closed at 37,159 words over fourteen chapters, average ~2,654, with three long ones; **that
  average is not a per-chapter precedent.** Write each chapter to its own scene. **Do not record a
  length trade-off.**
- **Close every speech** (`“` == `”`). Multi-paragraph speech closes and reopens per paragraph, the way
  ch 115 and ch 116 do it. Narration is never left inside an open quote.
- **Titles:** sixteen, unique against all 132 existing chapters. Names, not summaries. No weather, clock
  time, date or age. **Check every one against `chapters/volume-01/`, `chapters/volume-02/` and
  `chapters/volume-03/`.**
- **Vary the temperature.** Batch 0001 ran cold and lawful and peaked at ch 126, ch 128 and ch 131.
  **This batch has the kill at ch 140 and two fevers' worth of heat.** Do not let sixteen chapters hold
  one note.
- At least one scene of physical risk or a real deadline. The kill at ch 140 is one; the county hour at
  four loads a day is the other, and it runs under everything.
- Never: *his eyes widened*, *he couldn't believe it*, *suddenly*, *little did he know*.
- **Construction watch:** *nobody asked, nobody was thanked, there is no form, there is no column, the
  record remained, nobody knew.* Effective once or twice each across the batch, zero as emphasis.
  Batch 0001 used *there is no form* zero times and *there is no column* twice; **do not spend either
  again here.**
- **No outline language in a thought or a narration** — *this chapter, this volume, the next chapter, in
  this batch, the whole series, the reader, this book*. Note that *this book* is a legitimate in-world
  phrase for a county book or a quota book; sweep for the scaffolding sense only.

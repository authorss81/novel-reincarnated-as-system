# Batch 0002 Summary (Volume 3, ch 133–148)

Sixteen chapters, **36,742 words of body prose** (~2,296/chapter), counting everything outside the `##`
section headings. **Audit and mercy.**

She works **two clocks at once** — the furnace floor at Qinghe from the ninth hour and the lane behind a
well at Lowwater, four miles of road between them on cleared cart days, and a county hour at the third
hour in the middle of it. Every county hour costs **four loads and eight coppers**, a quarter of a day's
pay, for as long as the weather lets the road take a cart. **The ceiling at nine feels like the roof on a
building she already lived in**, and the relicensing turns out to have bought her a **column** rather
than a wage.

**The column is the batch's first mechanism, and it is mechanical.** The Hall's copyist had been
certifying the paid-work annex by converting Da Shan's **loads** at the Hall's **two-armed rate**, so the
column read thirty-one hours against the fifty-eight she had asked in her own hand. She gets it certified
in hours she writes out herself, every day, in the order the days happened, and the Hall clerk writes
***irregular*** on the front of the Qinghe furnace schedule and stamps it. **The crew reads its own
schedule**, so nine men see the word on the furnace floor the same afternoon. One of them, Gao, asks one
question; she answers it in one sentence and goes back to the cold end. The cost is an hour of the
furnace day and four miles, every day.

**Ch 134 — the sentence she walked past.** A member licensed to practise past registration **may be
taught past her stage, under countersignature, at that county**. She had carried the licence four months
without noticing. She does the arithmetic of a pupil in eleven minutes and concludes that no master in
nine counties will put a hand to it — a sponsor touching it again breaks the silence bought at ch 128,
and a stranger touching it puts her in an office's line in order. **So she decides to build the stage
herself, out of load, on the work.** That decision is what ch 140's tax then takes.

**Ch 135 — Condensing 6, announced at her own order, at the fourth hour of day 81.** 240 loads, 41
stamps, arm at a third throughout. A dressed block rolls at the stone end and her right hand does nothing
for four seconds in front of forty people. **The girl of twenty-four brings a copied carriers' slate with
twelve household lines on the back of it** — a Lowwater counting room has opened its book again — and is
not thanked, having told three washerwomen in advance so that Ning Yan could not be. **Bracket: the
layer's first line in twenty-one chapters, carrying the ceiling of nine.** The price, on the page: the
announcement says ***at Lowwater***, and there is one building in that town where a sentence can be
turned into a door, and her licence with her annex address is in it.

**The audit (ch 136–140) is what the Hunter's lawful request buys, and it costs Feng everything and
restores her nothing.** He comes up the stair for the seventh time, knocks on the frame for the first
time, shuts the door behind him, and leaves a folded sheet: a lawful request for the paper the stair
carries upward, stamped, with six words in a margin in a hand she has not seen — ***including what is not
entered***. **A keeper cannot refuse it, delay it, or answer it partly.** She works out in four seconds
that she could stop it at the county and says **no** out loud to nothing, because a request carries a
reason and a reason goes in a book with her name in the first line.

**The answer (ch 137) is five sheets written in one sitting in a fair hand.** The working lines; four
hundred and eleven volumes; **the column at five for twenty-two years**; the list of the four certified
county copies walled behind the fifth item and entered upward for nothing; and, as item five, **the fair
line she wrote about herself at the back of a fair volume off a warm shelf.** The intake line at the ninth
hour is on the form and **is not examined as a change** — the request is for the stair, not the line — and
she lets it go because he is right. **She does not ask him to leave it out. That is irreversible.** He
tells her she should have stopped it and he is glad she did not, and says both are true.

**The finding (ch 138) takes a boy of seventeen with a finger and about forty minutes.** A clerk of four
years writes on the return: returned five sheets against an expected six; missing item, the entry of the
four copies. Nobody complains. Nobody has done a wrong thing. **The concealment was hers in effect** — she
put four sheets in a dead man's coat in the thaw. And the first desk in the square is **empty for the
first time since the snow**, which is the most frightening thing she has seen since the spring.

**The strike (ch 139) is old and unattributable.** Three clerks and a ledger from the high shelf: four
hundred and sixty entries in eleven years, one hundred and nine from outside the bound, every one of them
from an office a county can put on a line — **and one it cannot.** Sun He writes the line himself and
tells the clerk to enter no inference. Feng is questioned about dates and **is not asked to recognise a
hand**; he discloses the request, the shape-not-the-word rule, and the fact that he saw the striking in
the thaw and reported nothing because the county's own copy was not struck. **This is a record the county
finds, not a second request served by anybody.**

**The suspension (ch 139), in four sentences.** One quarter off the compilation and entry of the upward
bundle; the bundle carried by a clerk of the county office; **the volumes, the copying floor, the column,
the stipend and the house all stay**; and ***the district does not terminate an examination.*** He keeps
the tower, the cough and the column at five. **He is not dismissed — dismissal would end an observation.**

**Ch 140 — the listed kill, at the batch's peak.** A bounty man in good boots on the escort road below
the fourth bridge is reading a boy of fourteen his own description out loud, in the flat voice of a man
doing his work, the way the ch 101 man read Du Hong's file. He says plainly that he is working the
entered act and that he is not from the Bureau. **She sends the boy up the grade.** Yuan tells her first:
out of her foundation, does not return, **it takes the order of the road — the pace she spent five weeks
building — and it buys about ten weeks out of about three**, and he cannot tell her whether what she
builds instead will ever arrive. She says yes. **She kills him with the quarterstaff in her left hand, one
working arm, no order left in her hands at all, on her reading of a man's shoulders**, the cold opens,
she loses two seconds, and he gets to the third cart. **Fresh bracket.** The tax takes sequence, not
substance, and cannot give it back — and ch 144 pays that on the page as a wheel she cannot see coming.
**Channel: about three weeks → about ten weeks.** She tells the Watch the whole of it flat and softens
nothing. **A second body in this county with her hand on it, and the eight hundred jin standing exactly
where it stood.**

**The Lung (offered ch 141, taken ch 143) is mercy with a hook, and the hook is written down.** The
Hunter himself makes the offer, in the copying room, in a plain coat, and says three things: the hook is
not hidden and is not in the county's book; **nobody is being bought and there is no coin in the room**;
and **he did not choose the keeper** — a county examined a tower because a stranger asked a lawful
question, and there is a member of this county in the sixth stage whose hours are on two of the five
sheets, and he has not mentioned her. The appointment: a district wage quarterly, oil, a hundredweight of
coal, a chair in the county office on reading days with the county's fire — **for reporting the stair
upward truly and in full, whether or not it is entered, for life, set aside-able on request from without
the bound on the same terms as a designation.** He asks for one night, walks four miles to read the
county's own book and finds nothing in it, and **signs at the ninth hour of day 97 with her standing at
the end of the bench**. He gives her ninety seconds in order — wage, oil and coal, the warm room — and
then says the thing that is hers before she can say it: *you are one of the reasons. You are in two of
the five sheets. You are not in the four that were missing.*

**THE COST, ON THE PAGE:** from that hour every hour she copies goes upward truly, **and the Hunter stops
needing to ask**, because a Listener reports the whole of the stair and needs no request. His first return
on day 99 is the bundle's contents with a fresh line at the top — *Appointed this quarter. Reporting in
full from the next* — and he carries it down to the lane himself. **She is the reason there is something
to report. She cannot un-take it, she does not ask him not to take it, and she does not forgive him.**
Relief that he is alive and fed, immediately curdled.

**Escort road and stage seven (the road opens ch 136, ch 144–148).** The hire is the contractor's own —
**twenty coppers a day and a quarter of the train, offered at ch 136 and priced by her at ch 137**, five
weeks to the close of the season's road. **Ch 144 adds only the second man**: a contractor's hired guard
called **Tan**, paid out of his own pocket; a drag pin at a quarter of a mile, eleven tons,
eleven inches of clearance and a river four feet below the ditch, **which she does not see coming** —
and that is the ch 140 pace, absent, on the page. Four hours short at the season's close and **she works
the difference rather than writing it**, because a column is a record of a body. The second hand on the
escort hours is a **carriers' run list out of the counting house at Lowwater, and the girl of
twenty-four has been copying about two hundred of them since the thaw for eight coppers a sheet and
nobody has ever wanted one.** Paid on the day of hand-over; not thanked; stopped from being thanked in
advance. The column comes out **level — fifty-eight asked and fifty-eight worked** — and the Hall clerk
writes *asked and worked level at the close* and not *complete*, because complete is for an ordinary
member.

**Ch 148 — Condensing 7, announced at her order, from a mat in a loft on the fourth night of a fever.**
**Two weeks of fever, eleven days in the bed.** She asks whether she had to, and whether he will tell her
in a month it was the wrong thing; he answers that he has told her once and once is the number. She
declines the county carrier in the three words a form allows for. She listens from the loft to a fair hand
copying her upward for four days and **does not go down**. And the fever takes the stair: **three counts
at the top of the third flight of the crew quarters, nine days after it broke, and it never comes back to
two.** The season's road shut on day 117. The second licence is still in the coat lining and is not
nailed up; the first is still crooked over her own bunk going soft.

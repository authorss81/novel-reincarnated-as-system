**Volume 3 is complete at ch 178.** All four batches are written (ch 119–132, 133–148, 149–163, 164–178) and
this is the volume-close audit. It walks `outline/volume-03.md` line by line against what is actually on the

## The Volume 4 hand-off — the one thing this audit must not get wrong

**Volume 4 is *The Wall*, ch 179–238: *In this volume, Ning Yan must break the boundary, or neither of them
ever learns what they are.*** The hand-off written in `state/current.md` and in the close summary must
state plainly, as a fact on the page and not as a plan:

> **A Warden's office outside the bound now holds her name, her stages and her hours — in order — certified
> by her own county. The dismantling it has ordered can only be executed, and the Foundation relicensing she
> has petitioned can only be countersigned, past a boundary no county paper crosses.**

And alongside it:

- **She is at Condensing 9, the top of the second realm, with a ceiling of nine and **nothing above it on the
  document**.** The next stage is Foundation the first stage, and the paper that would carry her there is
  petitioned, numbered, **barred**, and cannot be withdrawn by her.
- **The district has already answered once**: received, numbered, referred upward, no instrument held in the
  district either, and an empty space the width of a line on a public shelf by a door.
- **About ten days of channel**, said once, in Volume 3. **Ledger is hers to order and his to hold only on
  her word, and she has never ordered it.** She does not forgive him and does not tell him to stop.
- **A date is entered against the eight hundred jin in a counting room she has never been in.** Hou Sanliang
  is unpaid and patient and has never been met.
- **Nothing Volume 4 owns was spent in Volume 3:** no description of the Wall, no crossing, no geography
  past a marker post, no Warden but his field agent's signature and the name of his office, which **Yuan
  still cannot read and has now said four times he cannot and is not going to say it a fifth time to be
  different about it.**

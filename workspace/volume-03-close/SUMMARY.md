## Did the volume pay

> she is told she imagined him.** — fixed by `outline/series.md` §9.

**Yes, and both halves of the sentence are still live at the last page, which is the point.**

She is alive. She is relicensed to Condensing 9 with a ceiling in her own hand and **nothing above it on the
document anywhere in the world**. She refused a true mercy — verified true, term by term, at the cost of four

receipt folded square, and **two true things said in daylight in a registrar's room** — *the next stage has to

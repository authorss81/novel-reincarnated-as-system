## Markdown, length and the hard mechanical rules

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene **except ch 272** (close third on Yuan, flagged, once).
  **Every sentence Yuan speaks in the present tense. No other character ever in a present frame**, with the
  standing ch 57 exception about completed third-person events.
- **At most one bracketed layer line per chapter; exactly one in the whole batch, at ch 275.** Own line, square
  brackets, never bold/italics/heading. It announces; never explains, never advises.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or italic. **No
  blockquotes, code blocks, tables, horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a paragraph; **break over about 90
  words; never over about 120.** Roughly 1,800–2,700 words, **floor 1,300**, and **do not record a
  chapter-length trade-off anywhere in any file.**
- **Close every speech.** Curly quotes, never straight.
- No harem. **Nothing in this batch is a romance.** *The shape of a happy life* is spent until Volume 16, by
  Ning Wu. **Ning Yan never says her mother's name.** Yuan never tells her what he was before he died, and does
  not learn she saw him as a readout.
- **No calendar month named and no weekday named, from narration or from any mouth.**
- **Walk Batches 0001 and 0002's construction budgets in `state/continuity.md` before writing and confirm
  nothing from them is reused as emphasis.** Spent or revoiced, none available plain: the seven-conditions
  never-re-listed rule; the counterfoil rule used-not-taught; *nobody asked*, *nobody was thanked*, *there is no
  form*, *there is no column*, *the record remained*, *nobody knew*, *nobody said anything*; *about four

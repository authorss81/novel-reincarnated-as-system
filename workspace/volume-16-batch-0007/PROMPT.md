## The one thing a drafter of this batch should look for first

**The last batch of the last volume of a book is where everything gets explained. Do not explain anything.** The reader has been carried nineteen years by a woman who says figures out loud, and the temptation in the closing chapters is to hand her a summary and a moral. She does not get one. She gets a courtyard, four hundred students who arrived because people stayed, a brother who does not forgive her, a student who fails, a book with a dead man's handwriting on the first leaf, and a silence she names once in the last chapter and does not explain. **The account closes because he is gone and nobody tells her the debt was settled, because it was not, and the chapter's last line is her going back to work.**

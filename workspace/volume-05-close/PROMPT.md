# Volume 5 close — audit

## What this phase is

**Volume 5 is complete at ch 298.** All four batches are written (ch 239–252, 253–267, 268–283, 284–298) and
this is the volume-close audit. It walks `outline/volume-05.md` line by line against what is actually on the
page, records every named item as PASS or FAIL with a chapter reference, repairs anything the audit finds,
and then creates exactly one follow-up prompt — the Volume 6 planning phase, which will write
`outline/volume-06.md` and open Volume 6's planning.

**There is no chapter-writing in this phase and no volume-outline writing in it.** If you find yourself
drafting prose, or drafting Volume 6's acceptance criteria, you have drifted: stop and record the finding
instead.

## The question this close must answer in writing — the protagonist, again

**`outline/series.md` §2 names Yuan, male, as the protagonist and `state/book-metadata.md` records Gender:
Male.** Volume 5 delivered the correction Volume 4's close promised: **ch 272 is a sustained scene in which
he is on the page in his own right, in his own frame, away from her hearing**, and he pays for it out of
himself — his unentered state and the date of the morning he died.

The close must still answer, in `workspace/volume-05-close/SUMMARY.md` and `state/current.md`, and in writing:

- **What the protagonist actually did across Volume 5, chapter by chapter, and what each of it cost.** He let
  a keeper's hand enter him so her hours would hold without her (272). He took a share of a settled job and
  told her the price before he moved it (256, 290). He spent it (296) to put one line in a record an office
  reads, and **the family account closed and it cost her something she cannot name.** **Every one of those
  is a decision by the book's protagonist and every one of them was paid for out of her.**
- **What is now true of him that was not true at ch 238:** **he is readable from outside the bound without
  her, and therefore findable**, and the chapter that made him findable said on the page that being read
  means being sent for. `outline/ending.md` §1 requires exactly this before the final volume opens — the
  Court stage, the Scribe finding him, and the fall. **A man the reader has never seen in a room cannot carry
  a fall.** Confirm the prerequisite exists on the page and that nothing in Volume 6's planning may assume
  more of him than it earned.
- **What may not happen.** The close may not re-frame ch 239–298, may not re-title the volume, may not
  introduce a new final enemy, and may not take anything the ending owns — **the bargain, the fall, Feng's
  death, the Anchor, the Sisterhood, and the Scribe, which is fixed in `outline/ending.md` and belongs to
  Volume 15.** It may not retire the year of non-forgiveness, may not forgive him, and may not let her tell
  him to stop.

## The Volume 6 hand-off — the one thing this audit must not get wrong

**Volume 6 is *The Real Realm*, ch 299–360: *In this volume, Ning Yan must survive what is on the other
side, or the crossing was a death and not an escape.*** `outline/volume-05.md` fixes the question Volume 6
opens onto, and the hand-off must restate it **as a fact on the page and not as a plan:**

> She finished the books of the bound from the far side of a line — the claim answered where it could be
> answered, the family account settled, the brother's decade put where somebody has to look at it, three
> stages taken that no county heard — and the far side paid every one of them into books it keeps by hand.
> But past the last working point the country goes on, farmed and lit, with offices above the one office she
> knows the name of that have never needed anything from inside a bound until now — and one of them has just
> been handed a column that is hers, in a hand it can read, with a furnace behind it. The question is not
> what the bound was. **It is what the country past the line does with a person it can now read, who owes it
> hours, carries a furnace it did not build, and cannot go back inside where she was ever announced.**

And alongside it, as facts at ch 298:

- **She is at GOLDEN CORE THREE**, taken past the line over about five months with no county paper of any
  kind — **1 at ch 258 day 512, 2 at ch 275 day 556, 3 at ch 292 day 600** — **licensed by nothing, paid for
  out of far-side wages at five coppers a day, and entered in a wage-book column that is hers, kept by people
  who are not a county.** **The relicensed licence still says Foundation the first stage on the front of it
  and she has still never touched it.**
- **She has the right index finger gone, permanently**, lost at the far end of a bad flat in Volume 4 and
  told out loud to a crew; **four fingers' worth of grip left on that hand**; **and since ch 288 the right
  arm is open half the time instead of a third**, which closed the ramp and the heavy end at that yard for
  good. **Nothing in Volume 5 gives any of it back and nothing offers to.**
- **The season is over.** The column closed on the thirtieth at a far date in another man's hand; **eighteen
  counterfoils in four marks and no nineteenth**; the book goes into the box at the mill on the first cart.
  **The three working days before the thirtieth were never paid and never will be.** She has no wages, no
  season, no booking, and nothing behind her that owes her anything.
- **She is a person an office will vouch for.** Entered as good in the far-side office's own book at the
  seventeenth, **and she cannot be un-entered**, and the same book holds her hours, the answer to the
  eight-hundred-jin claim, the discharge of Ning Bo's furnace account, and **a line with Ning Wu's name on
  it and ten years of his own against it.**
- **The claim is ANSWERED and Hou Sanliang is paid and has never been met.** The date in his counting room
  came off the page by a hand she never saw, and the four counties that carried the figure still carry it.
- **Yuan is readable from outside the bound without her**, at the price of his unentered state and the date
  of the morning he died, and **the Ledger is no longer hers to order.**
- **MEMBER ABSENT stands three times and is permanent.** **The register still reads *reconciled*. The Clay
  Plate is still worn against her ribs.** Shen Xiu unforgiven and unthanked. The three sponsors silent.
  **The year of non-forgiveness is still running.** The Hunter's request is still pending and a Wall writ is
  not issued to a field agent, and **the line's own people still do not know she exists.**
- **She walked out past the cart turn at the last page and stopped half a mile short of a mill whose wheel
  has not turned since before she came, and did not go past it.**

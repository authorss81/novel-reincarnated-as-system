## This batch's goal

Four months of tithe hours, counted slowly by an enemy, against a licence with three legs and one
she owns. She gets to Lowwater. She earns clearance. **And the county refuses her anyway, on the
bounty standing, in the same hour she signs away the ceiling.**

- **Opening:** the four-month tithe. Two hundred and eleven hours worked, seven hundred and
  twenty-nine standing, a furnace that is under-repaired because it is short of men who are dead.
- **Midpoint turn (ch 78):** **Condensing stage 2**, announced to the whole bound **at Yuan's
  decision to let it go**, not hers. Cost: two weeks of fever and a month of short breath that
  never fully leaves.
- **Batch climax (ch 80):** she orders him to *read him.* He holds Ledger for **less than a day**,
  reads Hou Sanliang's man through her eyes, pays a spike that takes him to about six and a half
  weeks of channel, and refuses to hold it again the same day and permanently. **This is the
  volume's decision to escalate against him, and it is hers.**
- **Midpoint reversal (ch 88):** Shen Xiu signs quota clearance looking past her, in a scene with
  no cruelty in it. She walks into the Lowwater county office certain the licence is hers.
  **Registrar Sun He refuses it on the bounty standing** — a licenced practitioner cannot stand
  under an unanswered claim, and a claim is answered only by body, place and name. Then he reads
  her the terms of the only thing the county will give her instead: a **pending filing**. And she
  signs it, **in the same hour she is refused**, because a refused application is worth nothing
  and a pending one is worth everything except the Foundation.
- **The batch's irreversible decision is hers and it is at ch 88:** she enters her own defect on
  the county sheet in her own hand and **signs the ceiling down on herself** — capped at Condensing
  stage 5, barred from Foundation without relicensing, the sponsors' writ killed on the same page
  — while being refused. It cannot be undone. It is the leash, the lever and the room, all three,
  and she takes it anyway.
- **Power: one stage, Condensing 1 → 2 at ch 78, and it is announced.** Nothing else.

## Hard rules for this batch

- Close third on **Ning Yan** for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in
  the present tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense.
- **At most one bracketed layer line per chapter**, on its own line. In this batch **one is earned
  and required: ch 78**, and it is the advancement announcement. It announces; it never explains
  and never advises. **There is no tribulation** — a trial draws weather and there is no trial in
  this volume. Do not add one.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold or
  italic. **No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences
  per paragraph; break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly 1,800–2,700 words;
  a payoff chapter may run to 3,000.
- **Close every speech.** Run `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` on every file before you finish.
  If a speech runs over more than one paragraph, the first paragraph ends with a closing quote and
  the next opens with a fresh one. Narration never sits inside an open quote.
- Titles are unique in the book. Check against all 72 that now exist.
- **Volume 1 was cold dread and small competence. Volume 2 is grinding, claustrophobic, a real
  loss.** Batch 0006 spent the loss. **This batch is arithmetic.**
- The announcement wording is **fixed**: *Assembly Hall of Qinghe. Ning Yan of the Qinghe furnace
  crew, servant-born. What was held is let go. What was taken is held.* For ch 78 the advancement
  line is **Condensing, stage two, at Qinghe** in the four-clause order — where, who, what is let
  go, what is held — and do **not** put the words of the ch 53 sentence into it. They were a
  status the layer carried once and the layer does not repeat itself.
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until **Volume 16**, by Ning Wu.
- Ning Yan never says her mother's name. Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before
  **Volume 11**, and does not learn she saw him as a readout before Volume 11 either.
- **No harem. Nothing in Volume 2 is a romance. `Wei Ran` does not appear.**

## The geography and terms that were got wrong once

- **Lowwater is a county and the town under Qinghe Sect.** The Bureau has two separate presences
  there and they are different rooms: **the copying floor inside Feng Lushi's tower**, and **the
  county office on the square**, where Sun He holds the county book and stamps licences. Never
  merge them. **There is no Qinghe county office — Qinghe is a sect.**
- **Hanhui is a place**, eleven days down the road, one of the nine counties.
- **Cracking** is the world event followed by eleven days of ash. Furnace repair is the **settling
  floor**.
- **Quota** is the annual number a sect must supply. **Quota clearance is a certification about a
  household's hours**, ch 61 and ch 69.
- **The records room is beneath the furnace.** The roofless Assembly Hall is used for paper only.
- **Clause Four**, not Clause Nine.
- Her tithe hours are in **Shen Xiu's winter book**, counted by a clerk with a broken finger who
  writes slowly on purpose, four feet from the furnace mouth. The clerk's name is not required;
  if you give him one, keep it.

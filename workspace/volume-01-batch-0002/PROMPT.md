## The voice, which is the whole job

Close third on **Ning Yan** for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the present
tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense. That is the reader's only cue for who is talking
and it never breaks. He is nineteen, dead six years, sarcastic, embarrassed, and more competent than he lets
on. He has no body and the novel never lets him forget it.

The bracketed lines belong to the **layer**, not to Yuan: square brackets, on their own line, **at most one
per chapter**. They announce. They never explain and never advise.

Chapter titles are names, not summaries: three to twelve words, unique, no weather, no clock time, no dates,
no ages, no dialogue. The titles are already fixed in the batch cards. Use them exactly:
13 Public Ground, 14 The Book of Accounts, 15 The Map She Was Not Given, 16 Wen the Lamp-mender,
17 Fair Hand, 18 The Kiln Road, 19 Sealed Ground, 20 The Assessment Room, 21 What They Take,
22 The Weight at the Marker, 23 Wen Again, 24 The Tablet.

Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold or italic. No
blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two to four sentences per paragraph, break over
about 90 words, never over about 120. Around 1,800–2,700 words per chapter unless the scene needs longer.

## What this batch has to do besides write well

- **Every chapter changes the situation.** An option closed, a cost incurred, a relationship moved, a threat
  raised or neutralised, a decision made. If a chapter can be lifted out and dropped elsewhere unchanged,
  it is the wrong chapter.
- **At least one irreversible decision.** Chapter 21 is the batch's: she decides aloud to take the tablet
  in a sealed room. Chapter 24 compounds it: Yuan touches through her and loses his mother's name.
- **At least one scene with physical risk or a real deadline.** Chapters 18–19 (kiln road, sealed ground),
  chapter 22 (marker weight/night watch). Winter drill in nine days (ch 14) is the running deadline.
- **Varied temperature.** Anger to investigation (13–15), administered-sky discovery (16), hope in bad
  handwriting (17), terror (18–19), disgust (21), evidence-as-person (23), log started (24).
- **Show the layer, do not lecture it.** Reader learns by event: Broken Kiln sealed by layer not masonry (18),
  layer refuses Yuan (19), *assessed vs processed* and *reassigned* (20), removals logged (22), touch through
  hand costs more (24).
- **Do not re-explain.** State each rule once, first time it costs somebody. Construction watch from
  `state/continuity.md` applies hard: *nobody asked, nobody was thanked, there is no form, there is no column,
  the record remained, nobody knew* are each good once and dead by the third. Furnace column already spent.

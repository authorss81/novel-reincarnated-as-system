# Volume 4, Batch 0001 — batch planning

## What this phase is

**You are planning Volume 4's first batch. You are not writing chapters and you are not writing prose
inside the cards.** Your single output is **`outline/batches/volume-04-batch-0001.md`**, the batch card
set, written out of `outline/volume-04.md` and `state/current.md`.

**The cards are written before any chapter is drafted.** That is not a formality; it is how this run
keeps a sixty-chapter volume from drifting. Every requirement, every day, every bracketed line, every
irreversible step and every ending is fixed in the file you write, and the drafting phase may not add
one or move one.

**There is no chapter-writing in this phase.** If you find yourself writing a scene, stop and record the
finding instead.

## Part one — size the batch to its arc, not to a count

**This batch is not fourteen chapters because Volume 3's first batch was fourteen.** It is as long as
the ration-and-silence arc needs and as short as the last chapter that is still doing work.

The arc this batch must close is fixed in `outline/volume-04.md` *(Pressure rotation, Batch 0001)*:
**the last ten days of him; the Ledger purchase that finds the name; the year of non-forgiveness
changes shape; the empty space is not filled; the crew; the tin.**

**Sizing rules you must satisfy:**

- **Every card requirement gets a chapter and a turn.** A requirement that has nowhere to live is
  either given a chapter or cut from this batch and written into the batch's *carried forward* list.
  Say which you did, in the file.
- **No chapter may be split to hit a number and none may be padded to reach one.** If the arc closes on
  chapter 12, the batch is twelve chapters. If it needs sixteen, it is sixteen.
- **State the count you chose and the sentence from the arc that decided it.**
- **A batch whose chapter word counts fall monotonically across its chapters is a failed batch.**
  Design the shape with peaks: a loss, a decision, a small warm scene, a peak.

**Likely neighbours:** Batch 0002 (*hiring, the road, and whose book an hour goes into*) opens with the
season's road opening at about **day 254**. **Do not open the road inside Batch 0001** and do not spend
the journey. Batch 0001 ends with the thaw about eleven days off and one day of channel left or nearly
none.

5. **Batch midpoint** — a single named chapter and the turn. This batch has no stage in it and no

6. **Batch climax** — a single named chapter and what it settles.
7. **Required character changes** — who is different at the end of this batch and how the reader sees it.
8. **Required power changes** — and if there are none, say so plainly and say why. **Foundation 1 is
   Batch 0002 (ch 207).** This batch must not put a stage on her.

   not one of them.**
10. **Open questions** — live going into Batch 0002.
11. **Action and deadline requirement** — at least one scene with physical risk or a real deadline.

12. **Irreversible decisions in this batch** — at least one. **The volume's own irreversible decision is

13. **Chapters at a glance** — a table: chapter, day, turn in one line, ending type.
14. **Titles** — **all unique against all 178 existing chapters and against every other title in this

15. **A card per chapter**, in order, each with goal / resistance / change / ending type.
16. **Traps this batch carries — check every chapter against these.**
17. **Open questions after the batch — live going into Batch 0002.**

## Part three — what Batch 0001 must actually do

**The batch's central mechanical fact, fixed in `outline/volume-04.md` and not to be re-derived:**

> She has about ten days of channel (ch 178). **The only mechanism in this world that lowers Settlement
> is Merit**, and Merit settles when something the layer has listed is killed **or a listed trial is
> cleared**; he intercepts a share and spends it out of **her foundation**. **In this batch there is no
> kill and no trial, and nothing on the page can restore it.** The channel is spent, not replaced.
> Batch 0001 is what it costs her to spend it — and what is left is **about two days, rationed to
> roughly four sentences a day, which she chooses one at a time and counts on the back of something in
> her own hand.** Nobody in this batch knows she is doing it.

**The Ledger purchase — the batch's spine.** **Ledger is taken exactly once here, for one thing: a
name.** She orders it, **names the price before she says the word** — **eight days out of the ten she
has** — in the standing form the book fixed at ch 118 and has never re-listed. What comes back is **the
hand that wrote a date into Hou Sanliang's counting room in Lowwater** — a working name, a person with a
family on the line.
**The claim is not resolved. The date is not torn off. The counting room is in Lowwater, where she is
standing — do not put it nine counties away. Hou Sanliang stays unpaid, patient and never met.** What
she buys is a **direction**.

**Do not make the man mute and do not make him whole.** The volume's shape between here and Batch 0004
is a voice rationed to about four sentences a day by a woman who is counting. **Use that; do not
explain it, do not re-state the rule, and do not let a chapter turn it into a lecture.**

**The year of non-forgiveness — the batch's emotional turn.** Non-forgiveness runs from ch 100 and
Volume 3 carried it explicitly into the opening of Volume 4. **It ends inside this batch, and what
replaces it is worse than what it was.** She does not forgive him for the price of the ch 149 reading.
She **stops performing the refusal once a year in a room as though the account were for him**, and in
the same breath **spends eight of the last ten days of him on a name, at a price said out loud, with
no ceremony and nobody else in the room.** **The account does not close.** It changes from a claim into a purchase: she
goes on using him, on purpose, from here, and the book is about what that costs her.
`outline/ending.md` is fixed — she forgives him in ch 1000, it is not a relief, it is housekeeping, and
**the account must still be carrying at the last page of the book.** Do not close it. Do not soften it.
Do not let a chapter make it a reconciliation.

**The empty space — the volume's opening image and its standing prohibition.**
`outline/volume-03.md` and `state/open-threads.md` both say it: **Volume 4 opens on that space and must
not be filled in the first batch.** Ch 179 meets it. A drafter may write it on the page more than once
— she goes back and reads it, it is a thing she does — **and it stays empty of an answer for as long
as this batch runs.** Batch 0001 is sized to its own arc and is not fourteen chapters because nothing says
it is. Batch 0003 gives it its first entry, a date and a
word. Batch 0004 gives it the answer. **Neither is this batch.**

**The crew, the girl and the tin — the batch's temperature.** Nine men and her, ten sleepers in twelve
bunks. Qin walks four miles to stand eight feet off in a queue and never asks her a question. Gao holds
the frame. The girl of twenty-four is alive, has a crooked leg, four paid hours a week at the trough
plus four more out of a district fund for the cold months, a quarter paid to the thaw in advance, and
**is not thanked** — she has set not-being-thanked up in advance as protection for two volumes and that
must not be broken without a reason on the page. **Da Shan's board behind a well is running at four
loads a week at nine coppers. The tin is about a hundred and forty coppers.** This batch has six weeks
of winter in it and no paid work has been named since the quarry ran dry, and that is a pressure you
may use.

**Feng.** Alive, warm, owned, sixty-two, column at five, a quarter off his own stair at his own written
request, every hour he copies for her going upward truly. **He has not asked her a question since ch 143
and this batch does not ask him one. He is not thanked. No second appointment and no second Lung before
Volume 11** — both are unspent and must stay unspent, and `outline/ending.md` reserves what his last
fair handwriting does.

**Bai Zhu.** If he appears, these hold without exception: **he never lies, threatens, touches her,
enters her rooms or pays for force, and he has never asked her a question — and he says so in the same
breath so that she does not have to spend a morning on it.** **He is named once, at ch 150, to a form,
and on no document he signs.** He is on the case and he is not a villain. **Do not put a named villain
in this batch.**

## Part four — carry this closed state forward

### The closed position, at day 219

- **Condensing 9**, announced at her own order, ceiling nine in her own hand, relicensed ch 132, seven
  conditions referenced and **never re-listed**, the claim the seventh and the last thing on the paper.
  **Nothing above nine on the front of the document anywhere in the world.**
- **The stage column reads one, two, three, five, six, seven, nine — three holes in nine lines.**
  **Nine counties is what is heard. Four counties is what is written.** The column is in the county books
  of four counties out of nine.
- **Short breath three counts on stairs, never back to two. The heel of her left hand is gone.** She
  writes every county hour with the flat of a second finger, eleven minutes longer, having said so out
  loud to nine men. Right arm open a third of the time. Aperture cracked through and permanent.
- **The second licence is nailed straight above the second bunk; the first is rolled in her coat lining.**
- **The referral** has gone up the stair past every office she has ever stood in front of, carries her

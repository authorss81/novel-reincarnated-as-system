## What this batch was

Ration and silence. She spends eight of the last ten days of the voice in her head on one name, rations
what is left to about four sentences a day, keeps the count on the back of a dead quarry weight ticket in
her own hand, and nobody in the batch knows she is doing it. The empty answer space on the public shelf is
read four times across fourteen chapters and stays empty. The quarry has been dry since day 189, the tin is
held at about thirty coppers from the morning she stops buying stone on the 240th, and a room with ten
sleepers goes to a bed of ash for the last fourteen nights of the batch, where her own projection had
promised eight.

**It buys a direction and spends a man to get it.**

## What changed that could not have changed before

A woman who had never spent a day of a voice on purpose now owns a name with a family attached to it and
knows she cannot use it. A year of non-forgiveness that had run since ch 100 **changed shape and did not
close**, because the sentence she kept back once a year went on a price instead. A discipline exists in the
world that no office in nine counties can enter and no clerk can copy upward. **Nine men have been made
cold on purpose for six nights by a woman who is the only person in that room who cannot pay anybody, and
one of them filled a bucket and said nothing.** A road notice on a public shelf put a date on a boundary and
turned the whole of her position into an outline she could see from the inside. And a dead quarry weight
ticket, which is the last of a job that stopped on day 189, now carries a column of marks and a working
man's name and a rule about what not to buy, in her own handwriting, against her chest.

## Irreversible decisions in this batch — three

1. **Ch 187 — she buys the name.** Eight days out of about ten, at a price said out loud before the word.
   The days do not come back and she can never unlearn a name. After this she is a woman who buys people on
   purpose with the only currency she has.
2. **Ch 188 — she does not go and find him.** She has a name, she has the rest of him, and she could spend
   all of it tonight inside the bound. She writes him on the card and keeps him for the road. **The volume's
   own irreversible decision — missing the monthly attendance — is Batch 0002's and is not planted here.**
3. **Ch 190 — she stops buying the stone on the morning of the 240th.** About thirty coppers is six more
   nights of warmth for ten people **or** a start on nine days of road, and she picks the second in daylight
   without bargaining and without telling nine men why. Da Shan's board loses four loads a week, the only
   thing on it that has moved all winter.

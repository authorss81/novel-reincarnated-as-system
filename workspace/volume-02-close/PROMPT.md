# Volume 2 close — audit

## What this phase is

**Volume 2, Batch 0009 (ch 104–118) is written and Volume 2 is complete at ch 118.** This is the
first of the two volume-close phases. It audits what the volume promised against what is actually
on the page, repairs anything the audit finds, records the result, and then creates exactly one
follow-up prompt — the close-record phase, which will write `outline/volume-03.md` and open
Volume 3's planning.

**There is no chapter-writing in this phase and no volume-outline writing in it.** If you find
yourself drafting prose or drafting the next volume's acceptance criteria, you have drifted: stop
and record the finding instead.

## The audit

Go down `outline/volume-02.md` line by line and check each named item against the manuscript.
Record the result as a table in `state/current.md` under a new heading
`## Volume 2 close audit`, with a pass/fail and a chapter reference for every row. At minimum:

1. **The one-sentence goal.** Did she earn the document that lets her advance at all? (ch 115)
2. **The three legs.** Seal (62), clearance (86), countersignature (115) — assembled, refused
   twice, granted once. Is it still true that **she files once and only once per want**?
3. **The four moments she went after it.** ch 62/65 preparation and sealing, ch 70 first filing,
   ch 88 second filing and the pending filing, ch 115 grant — in that order, each demonstrated
   rather than explained.
4. **The document.** Name, issuing office, clause number (**Clause Four, not Nine** — ch 41 spent
   the ninth), what it buys, what it costs, and the six conditions.
5. **Central pressure.** Confinement and paperwork, labour and grinding, pursuit and the bounty,
   grief and the grant. Is each batch's pressure actually different from the one before it? Is any
   batch another hall?
6. **The three legs as cost.** Four months of tithe in Shen Xiu's book; a living surety, now
   dead; the defect in her own hand and the cap at five; the sponsors' Foundation writ dying on
   the same page.
7. **The four stages.** Condensing 1→2 (78), 2→3 (92), **3→4 suppressed (104)**, **4→5 announced
   (114)**. Real months, no skipped breakthrough, **no tribulation anywhere in the volume**, and
   exactly one bracketed layer line in the closing batch.
8. **Yuan's track.** Voice for the whole volume. **Ledger taken once**, at ch 80, on her spoken
   order, less than a day, refused permanently the same day, and **not retaken in ch 104–118**.
   Merit settled once, on the listed kill at ch 101, told-before-spent.
9. **The Settlement clock.** ~17 (58) → ~6½ (80) → ~five days (100) → ~11 (101) → **~6 (118)**.
   Every figure on the page? Do they reconcile?
10. **The bounty arc.** ch 55 claim, ch 63 records, ch 67 Hou's name and the matching dates,
    ch 68 proof-hunt, ch 88 blocks the filing, ch 100–103 attempt and killing, proof failed on the
    body, standing unclaimed, **and now entered on the licence as condition six**.
11. **The loss.** Cao Lei and Xu An (71–72) and Du Hong (102–103), her agency named aloud in each
    case and not softened. **Feng survives and is not resolved.**
12. **The eleven designations.** Opened on paper at ch 96; the door closed at ch 110–111; nothing
    answered and nothing to be answered until Volume 12.
13. **The partnership lie.** ch 92–94 as exaggeration of her own conclusion with the eleven as
    evidence; ch 100 as her working out alone that the designation **prices** rather than
    shelters; **non-forgiveness ch 100 → Volume 3 → opening of Volume 4**; no conclusion about
    design.
14. **The climax (112–116).** Is the irreversibility actually **hers at ch 88**, and is it paid at
    ch 115 rather than restated?
15. **Concrete resolution.** The full list from `outline/volume-02.md`, item by item, against
    ch 116–118.
16. **The final chapter image.** Every element on the page: spring rain through the roofless Hall
    onto the furnace floor schedule; the licence nailed above the second bunk; the defect line
    going soft in the damp; ten sleepers; the right hand open a third of the time; Feng's tower
    lamp burning down the mountain. **She does not ask what it is counting** — spent in Volume 1.
    **She asks how much channel he has left, he tells the truth (~6 weeks), she does the
    arithmetic against the calendar, she does not tell him to stop, she tells him conditions.**
17. **Hard rules.** Close third throughout; **every sentence Yuan speaks in the present tense and
    no other character ever in it**; at most one bracketed layer line per chapter; markdown plain
    (title heading, section headings, paragraphs, sparse bold/italic, square-bracket system lines
    only — no blockquotes, code blocks, tables or horizontal rules); every speech closed
    (`text.count('“') == text.count('”')`); two to four sentences a paragraph, nothing over about
    120 words; 1,800–2,700 guide and 1,300 floor with no trade-off recorded; no harem; nothing in
    the volume a romance; Wei Ran absent; Bai Zhu unnamed; Ning Yan never says her mother's name;
    *the shape of a happy life* unspent; nothing told of what Yuan was before he died; nothing
    learned of the readout.
18. **Spent constructions.** Walk the construction budget at the foot of `state/continuity.md` and
    confirm that nothing from it is reused as emphasis in ch 104–118. Also sweep ch 104–118 for
    authorial scaffolding — *this chapter, this volume, the next chapter, in this batch, the
    whole series, the reader, this book* — and for the `AGENTS.md` banned reflexes.
19. **Titles.** All fifteen unique against 1–103, names rather than summaries, no weather, no
    clock time, no date, no ages.

**If the audit finds a real fault in closed Volume 2, repair the chapter, not the record.** Keep
the good prose and the story movement; fix the concrete finding. No title, chapter order, card
beat, bracket or ending may move. Write a repair note naming what changed.

## Record it

Update `state/current.md` with the audit table and any repair note. Update
`state/continuity.md`, `state/open-threads.md` and `state/chapter-summaries.md` with anything the
audit changed, and **record every canon change explicitly**, with the chapter it changed in.

Then look hard at two things the close must settle before Volume 3 can be planned, and write your
findings into `state/current.md` under `## Volume 3 — the two open structural questions`:

- **How does she get past the cap?** The licence caps her at Condensing 5 and bars a Foundation
  petition without relicensing. `outline/series.md` puts Foundation at Vol 2 ch 60 – Vol 3.
  Gather every fact the manuscript now holds about relicensing, the defect, the sponsor's writ and
  the county's own procedure. **Do not decide it. Decide what the decision would cost and who
  would have to sign.**
- **What is Ledger, on a standing stage?** `outline/series.md` assigns Ledger to Volumes 3–5 and
  ch 80 was the one recorded exception. Gather what the manuscript holds about what Ledger let him
  do for six hours at ch 80, what it took, and what it did to the partnership. **Do not decide it
  here.** Write the material the planning phase will need.

## What this batch did

It closed the volume. The quarry ran out and took the last paid work in the county with it. She worked the
cold end she was still capped from owning, found out that the last private place she had was a room that was
empty, said **Condensing nine out loud at her own order in front of nine men**, and paid for it in the one
thing she cannot get back: nine counties now know exactly where she stands **fourteen days before a
Ward's office learns it**.

Then the winter return was **read in order** — one, two, three, five, six, seven, **nine** — the three gaps
ruled in Sun He's ordinary hand and the ruling **stopped** because he has made it three times already, and
she signed it herself in **eleven minutes with the flat of a second finger** in front of a queue that did
not shuffle. A patient man who has never lied to her **certified in a Warden's hand that the System is
real, unauthorised and carried by a licensee in good standing, and referred the dismantling upward to the
Warden for want of a Wall writ** — the instrument he does not hold, the only thing in eleven weeks he has
been buying. She filed the Foundation petition the next day on the relicensed paper and **it was barred, not
refused, and cannot be taken back.** The volume closes on a licence nailed straight above a bunk.

## The irreversible step

**Ch 171, and it is hers.** Condensing 8 → 9, announced at her own order, in the open, at the cold end,
witnessed by nine men. The bracket line is the only bracketed line in the batch.

She settles the decision in her own head on ch 169 and names the cost to herself before she acts: telling
puts her exact position in nine counties fourteen days before a referral goes up the stair; **holding it a
fourth time gives the column a fourth hole, which the county rules an absence and an office above this
county reads as concealment — and she would then be doing the thing she does in order to be suspected of
doing it.** She tells the crew, out loud, on the day, rather than letting nine men work it out off a board
over a fortnight. What it costs is on the page and named in three forms: the nine counties, the cold end
that fills with people and stays filled and is never hers again, and fourteen days.

## The final image

First week of the deep cold. The fire goes down twice between the fourth hour and the light. Ten people
sleep in twelve bunks. She can see the one light in Lowwater from the stair, and the thing that goes up
that tower's four flights this quarter goes up in a district clerk's arms and comes down in his arms empty,
because Feng asked for it in writing in the autumn and was not refused and was not thanked for it. She has
two arms' worth of practice at nailing and one arm. She strikes it, it stops a third of the way in, she
strikes it again, and **it goes in straight**. The second nail goes straight too. She tells Yuan the thing
has gone up the stair past every office she has ever stood in front of, to an office whose name he still
cannot read, and she was never going to stop him. She asks how much of it is there, and he tells her the
truth: **about ten days**, against about six weeks of winter still to come in it. She does the arithmetic
against a calendar with a barred petition in it and nothing else. She has the Ledger to order and she does
not order it. And in the last line, with the fire down to a bed of ash and ten people asleep, **she does not
tell him to stop.**

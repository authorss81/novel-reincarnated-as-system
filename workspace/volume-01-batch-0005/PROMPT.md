## The voice, which is the whole job

Close third on **Ning Yan** for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the
present tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense. That is the reader's only cue for who
is talking and it never breaks. He is nineteen, dead six years, sarcastic, embarrassed, more competent
than he lets on. He has no body and the novel never lets him forget it. He lost his mother's name in
ch 24 — never says it again, never says he forgot.

The bracketed lines belong to the **layer**, not to Yuan: square brackets, on their own line, **at most
one per chapter**. They announce. They never explain and never advise. The layer says a change once and
does not say it a second time.

Chapter titles are names, not summaries: three to twelve words, unique, no weather, no clock time, no
dates, no ages, no dialogue. The titles are already fixed in the batch cards. Use them exactly:
47 Waiting, 48 Terms, 49 The Signature, 50 Qiu's Fall, 51 The Floor, 52 The Tribulation,
53 The Record of the Trial, 54 What It Cost Him, 55 Three Days Visible, 56 The Signature,
57 The Shape of the Silence, 58 The Count.

Note the deliberate echo in the card titles: **49 The Signature** and **56 The Signature**. That is
intentional and is the shape of the volume. Both must exist and both must be in the table of contents.

Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold or italic. No
blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two to four sentences per paragraph, break
over about 90 words, never over about 120. Around 1,800–2,700 words per chapter unless the scene needs
longer. **Chapters 50 and 53 are the two heaviest in the book so far; do not let them run long because
the prose is good. Every chapter must be a complete scene.**

## Hard continuity to protect — the state of the board at ch 46

- Ning Yan: seventeen, **Body-Forging stage 9, sealed for Condensing** (first servant-born in three
  hundred years, ch 45). Aperture cracked through, permanent, never heals — and used as a channel since
  ch 39. Deliberate emptying drains from the legs down and leaves a hollow chest that only rest
  refills. Two fingers of the right hand unreliable. Fever, six days without sleep. Two scars. The
  crack-as-channel is hers, unshared, and not to be explained to anyone in this batch.
- She has **not yet ascended.** Stage 9 → Condensing 1 happens at the trial, ch 52–53.
- **Tablet:** worn against skin, against the chest, throughout. Its texture was described once in ch 21
  and is **not** to be described again here; it is *remembered*, at the end of the volume, as the only
  thing the layer ever let out of a sealed place.
- **Log:** stolen laundry ledger back pages, poor hand, wash code inside, rubbish-camouflage. It holds,
  among other things, the four county sheets (ch 42) and the second offer word for word with its date
  and pin hole (ch 45). **The second offer is evidence in Volume 4. Do not spend it here.**
- **Clerk Xie Shuo** rules, and is ruined by Qiu's faction for it, quietly, off page, after ch 50. He
  is not a martyr and does not make a speech about it.
- **Qiu Zhulang** is stripped of the furnace on a procedural vote about an eleven-minute promotion and
  takes it without a word. He says one sentence to her on the way out — the sentence in card 50 — and
  **nobody in the room understands it, including the reader.** It is not repeated word for word until
  the final chapter.
- **Feng Lushi** is in Lowwater, dying, with four copies of her story in a column nobody reads. He is
  not in the Hall. Do not put him on the floor.
- **Du Hong** does not know she already knows he was sent to report her.
- **Ning Wu** still reads *assessed, reassigned, unreconciled.* Ch 56 buys the word *reconciled*, which
  is a lie and a kindness and both, and it stays on her head for three volumes. The truth is in the
  tablet she is still wearing and the Elder Hall never sees it again.
- **Yuan:** Voice stage. Settlement over half. He does not know who edits the layer (Vol 11). He does
  not know she saw him as a readout during the stage-nine report (ch 43) and must not be told. He goes
  quiet for four hours at the end without warning and comes back with a number.

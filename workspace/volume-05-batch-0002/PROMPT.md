## Markdown, length and the hard mechanical rules

- Close third on Ning Yan for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks in the present tense. No
  other character ever in a present frame.**
- **At most one bracketed layer line per chapter, and exactly one in the whole batch, at ch 258.** On its own
  line, in square brackets, never bold, never italics, never a heading, in the far-side house form above. **It
  announces; it never explains and it never advises.**
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold or italic. **No
  blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.** Two to four sentences a paragraph; **break
  over about 90 words; never over about 120.** Roughly 1,800–2,700 words, **floor 1,300**, and **do not record
  a chapter-length trade-off anywhere in any file.**
- **Close every speech.** `text.count('“') == text.count('”')` before finishing. **Curly quotes, not
  straight** — a draft that writes `"` has not been written correctly and the file must be fixed before it
  is saved.
- No harem. **Nothing in this batch is a romance.** *The shape of a happy life* is spent until Volume 16, by
  Ning Wu. **Ning Yan never says her mother's name.** Yuan never tells her what he was before he died, and does
  not learn she saw him as a readout.
- **No calendar month named and no weekday named, from narration or from any mouth.** The ban is on **named**
  weeks and named weekdays, not the word: *about six weeks*, *a week back*, *three days up* stay available.
- **Walk Batch 0001's construction budget in `state/continuity.md` before writing and confirm nothing from it
  is reused as emphasis.** All of it is spent or revoiced and **none of it is available plain**: *nobody
  asked*, *nobody was thanked*, *there is no form*, *there is no column*, *the record remained*, *nobody knew*,
  *nobody said anything*; *about four seconds*; *eleven weeks*. **Never** *his eyes widened*, *he couldn't
  believe it*, *suddenly*, *little did he know*. **No authorial scaffolding**: *this chapter*, *this volume*,
*the reader*, *in this batch*. **Absolutes are literal** — a sentence that says *nobody*, *every*, *never* or
  *only* is a claim about the whole manuscript and will be checked. Write the claim you can hold.
- **No new final enemy and no new named villain.** The Scribe is fixed in `outline/ending.md` and belongs to
  Volume 15. Qinghe is a sect and never a county. Lowwater's two Bureau premises are never merged.

## What the batch must do

1. **Settle the doorway.** The boy stays or goes by ch 783, on an arithmetic she gives him, and the terms
   of the oil are fixed: three lamps in rotation, a cup and a half each, counted by him the way she
   counts days. He is never bound, never entered, never sent.
2. **Fix the dark as the working condition.** Every room chapter states how far the oil light goes and
   what lies past it. The cage lamp is not touched, not fed, not discussed as relightable.
3. **Run the oil-against-days disagreement to its midpoint (ch 788):** his count and hers disagree by one
   day in twenty and both are right, because oil is not days. Neither count is corrected.
4. **Take IMMORTAL VESSEL 7 at ch 793, day 2,598, the 5th of the month beginning 2,594, at seventy-four
   days** (2,598 − 2,524 = 74). In oil light, on her own strip, in her own hand, read out loud before it
   is a line anywhere. **Cost named once: she cannot be hidden by the dark.** She says why seventy-four
   and not sixty-three, out loud, as arithmetic.
5. **Land the irreversible step at ch 795:** she lets the oil lamp stay in the room — wick trimmed, cup
   filled, a place cleared at the near end of the table — and the dark is no longer hers alone. It cannot
   be un-let.
6. **Carry one first of a month (day 2,564, between ch 787 and 788):** a bundle comes up, forty boards,
   all empty of her. She does not get a board with her name and does not expect one. The bearer does not
   look at her.

## Chapter cards

**Ch 781 — The Worn Place Worn Again.** POV her, room in dark, day 2,541. Goal: answer the boy without
giving him a sentence. Resistance: he repeats the question; oil light shows her hands. Reveal: the light
goes a hand's width and no further — stated, not explained. Decision: she gives him the arithmetic, all
of it, not one sentence. Emotion: irritation giving way to something like company. Power: none. Preserve:
bar against left wall, untouched. Ending: discovery — he looks along the table and finds the worn place
again. Pull: what does he think it is.

**Ch 782 — A Cup And A Half.** POV her, room + doorway, day 2,545. Goal: fix the oil terms before the
lamps run. Resistance: he has three lamps and no oil to spare below; she has nothing to trade. Reveal:
his count of oil against her count of days, first stated. Decision: three lamps in rotation, named out
loud. Emotion: relief at a number that holds. Power: none. Preserve: cage lamp untouched. Ending:
decision — the rotation is agreed. Pull: whose count runs faster.

**Ch 783 — Stay Or Go.** POV her, room, day 2,549. Goal: settle whether he stays. Resistance: he wants
the small answer; she has none. Reveal: nobody above the line sent him — said out loud once. Decision:
he stays, unsent, unentered. Emotion: fear, hers,Briefly shown. Power: none. Preserve: he is never
entered in any row. Ending: decision — he stays. Pull: where does he sleep.

**Ch 784 — The Fourth Landing.** POV her, tower stair + fourth landing, day 2,552. Goal: see him down
past the dark flights. Resistance: second four flights have no rail; oil slops. Reveal: she counts his
stops the way she counts hers and the counts differ. Decision: she walks him to the landing and comes
back up alone. Emotion: tenderness refused. Power: none. Preserve: 880 in two runs of 440, never added
to 904. Ending: cost — her knees on the rail-less flights. Pull: the bundle-day coming.

**Ch 785 — What Comes Up.** POV her, room, day 2,555. Goal: be ready for the first-of-month bundle.
Resistance: the room has never worked in the dark and neither has she. Reveal: none new — procedure

Power: none. Preserve: forty boards in a stack, face up. Ending: earned hook — feet on the second stair.

**Ch 786 — Forty Empty.** POV her, room, day 2,559. Goal: watch the bundle come up and go down.
Resistance: bearer does not look at her; she must not touch the armful. Reveal: all forty empty of her

**Ch 787 — The Thirtieth.** POV her, room, day 2,563. Goal: keep the six terms on the last day of the
month. Resistance: second term (*in daylight*) stops her throat in the dark. Reveal: she has been saying

**Ch 788 — Oil Is Not Days (midpoint).** POV her, room, day 2,567. Goal: reconcile the two counts.
Resistance: his oil says one thing, her days say another, both check out. Reveal: one day in twenty of

Emotion: delight, unwilling. Power: none. Preserve: neither count corrected ever again. Ending:
discovery — two true counts. Pull: what the drift does to a stage.

**Ch 789 — Hands In Oil Light.** POV her, room, day 2,571. Goal: write the five again where she can see
it. Resistance: nib against fourth rib in flickering light; arm open. Reveal: the five under the price,
seen by oil for the first time. Decision: she reads the strip out loud to the boy and does not explain

**Ch 790 — What He Sees.** POV her, room, day 2,575. Goal: learn what oil shows. Resistance: he

**Ch 791 — The Pressing.** POV her, room + second stair head, day 2,583. Goal: decide whether to take

**Ch 792 — Nine Hundred And Four In The Dark.** POV her, second stair, day 2,590. Goal: walk the whole

**Ch 793 — Taken In Oil Light (climax).** POV her, room, day 2,598. Goal: take Immortal Vessel 7.

**Ch 794 — After The Writing.** POV her, room, day 2,599. Goal: rest and check the cost. Resistance:

Pull: the lamp's place.

**Ch 795 — The Lamp Stays (irreversible).** POV her, room, day 2,600. Goal: decide where the oil lives.
Resistance: keeping it means the dark is shared; sending it down means working blind. Decision
(irreversible): she clears a place at the near end of the table, trims the wick, fills the cup — **the
oil lamp stays in the room, and the dark is no longer hers alone, and it cannot be un-let.** Emotion:
company, accepted at a price. Power: none. Preserve: cage lamp still out; bundle of papers untouched;
five blank leaves still blank. Ending: decision. Pull: the seat at the table, worn, waiting.

## Volume 2's fixed one-sentence goal

> In this volume, **Ning Yan must earn the document that lets her advance at all, or she stays a servant with a god in her head for the rest of her life.**

## This batch's goal

She is legal to five, capped, refused as a licence and kept as a filing, on the ground at Lowwater with a tower, a square, and a man hunting proofs. The hunt tightens from records to bodies. **She climbs one stage at her own order and pays for the noise. She works out alone what being Chosen costs. She kills a man to stop him killing another, and the killing pays for Yuan's life. Du Hong dies of her papers.**

- **Opening (ch 89):** the pending filing's first week. Hours, stages, defect on the county sheet; carrying work under the schedule; the man in the good coat still paying at three desks.
- **Mid-batch turn (ch 92): Condensing stage 3, released at her order.** Four weeks of Lowwater carrying work, sleeping in the tower loft, built out of load. Her arm settles at a third and stays there. Cost: the announcement is the first thing in four months to tell a market where she sleeps.
- **Relationship turn (ch 92–94): the lie.** He hardens her own ch 55 conclusion into a floor: being Chosen is why the claim cannot take her quietly, evidenced by the eleven — six hundred years, eleven bodies the bound had to account for, the designation the reason there is a record of any of them at all. He tells her the protection and the danger are the same line and it is the safe side. Do not reverse ch 57 (writ not reward stands). Do not conclude design.
- **Paper turn (ch 96): *kiln-born*.** Tower volumes carry a status column: eleven entries across six hundred years, each with a status word, each in a copying hand nobody living uses. The nearest before hers says *kiln-born*. Lowwater's county sheet of her advancement carries the same word in a second position no other county's sheet has; of the four hand-copies in the tower, one carries it. She speaks the word to Yuan once. He does not recognise it and says so. First new fact about the eleven, on paper. Do not answer what the eleven became.
- **Batch climax (ch 100–103): the attempt, the killing, the death, the Merit.** Hou Sanliang's men stop Du Hong on the **Lowwater road at the foot of the Lantern Register tower** — not the pass, one location. He carries her hand in his file and her copies in his coat. He gives his own name instead of hers, burns one sheet, refuses the place. They beat him for the place and leave him in thaw water at the tower's foot. She arrives and kills the second man with the Nine-Step — habit not technique, one working arm — which very nearly kills her. **She does not open the aperture as a channel to do it (held for Volume 7).** He dies of it before the night is out with Feng holding the lamp. Proof fails on the body; the bounty stands unclaimed into Volume 3.
- **Merit (ch 101):** the entered-act listing exists before the kill (ch 68, ch 80); Hou's man is on the layer's list the moment he takes the claim up. Merit settles on the listed kill — the volume's only source, no trial. Yuan tells her **before** he spends it: what it costs, what it buys, out of her foundation. She says yes. Third term paid in full, four months late, first time he asks instead of acting. Tax takes sequence not substance: a left-hand sequence rebuilt at ch 78 is gone and cannot be relearned. Nothing she loses comes back. Settlement back to roughly two-thirds (~11 weeks). Without this kill he is dry inside ch 101 (about five days of margin at ch 100).
- **Working-out (ch 100, tower loft, alone):** she works out the designation prices her, not shelters her — the eleven spaced irregularly because spent at different rates, not found at different times; every honoured quest answers out of the same foundation the tax draws from, so being Chosen adds occasions for an existing cost. She tells him she worked it out. No new rule; three stand; **she does not forgive him — non-forgiveness runs ch 100 through Volume 3 into Volume 4 (the year).** She does not conclude the tax was designed (Volume 11).
- **Burial (ch 103):** she lists the causes at his burial and they are all hers: her hand in his file; her copies in his coat down the road; letting him stand surety; killing one of them when she came down the road. Four causes. Feng holds the lamp. Bounty men know the cheap door is gone.
- **Power: one stage, Condensing 2 → 3 at ch 92, released at her order and announced.** Suppression exception belongs to ch 104 (next batch), not here. Nothing else.

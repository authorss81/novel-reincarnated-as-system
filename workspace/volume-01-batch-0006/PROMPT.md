## Hard rules for whoever plans and then drafts

- Close third on **Ning Yan** for every external scene. **Every sentence Yuan speaks is written in the
  present tense.** No other character is ever in the present tense. It has never broken in 58 chapters.
- **At most one bracketed layer line per chapter**, on its own line. They announce; they never explain
  and never advise.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, sparse bold or italic.
  No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules. Two to four sentences per paragraph;
  break over about 90 words; never over about 120. Roughly 1,800–2,700 words.
- **Close every speech.** If a speech runs over more than one paragraph, the first paragraph ends with a
  closing quote and the next opens with a fresh one. Narration never sits inside an open quote. Batch
  0004 shipped seven unclosed quotes of exactly that shape and it was a review failure; run the count
  (`text.count('\u201c') == text.count('\u201d')`) before you finish.
- Volume 1 is **cold dread and small competence**. Volume 2 is **grinding, claustrophobic, a real loss**.
  Do not write another Volume 1.
- The announcement wording is **fixed** and is quoted word for word in chapter 1000:
  *Assembly Hall of Qinghe. Ning Yan of the Qinghe furnace crew, servant-born. What was held is let go.
  What was taken is held.*
- *The shape of a happy life* is spent until **Volume 16**, by Ning Wu.
- Ning Yan never says her mother's name. Yuan never tells her what he was before he died before
  **Volume 11**. He does not learn that she saw him as a readout during the stage-nine report before
  Volume 11 either.
- No harem. Nothing in Volume 2 is a romance. `Wei Ran` does not appear before Volume 3.

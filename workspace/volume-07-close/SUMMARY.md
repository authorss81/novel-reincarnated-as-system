## 3. The protagonist, answered in writing

`outline/series.md` §2 makes this a standing requirement on every volume from Volume 5 on.

**Yuan, at ch 420.** He is **readable without her and therefore findable** (ch 272, carried). He is **held and
therefore sendable** (ch 338, carried; the unnamed state is still gone). And as of ch 401 he is **answerable**,
which is a third thing and it is worse, **because an answer is the only one of the three that a person can
quote.** He chose it knowingly and said so in advance on the page: *He will be asked about it by somebody he has
never met, at some point, by somebody who has only got the two lines and the date and the ordinary voice.*

**He spent the Hand once, at ch 402, on something that is not a cart, and it went out of the ground under her
feet and not out of her hands.** She told him the price before it was spent and he said it back twice. **She can
never again refuse a reading**, because a second refusal would be a refusal of an answer already given. That is
the volume's first irreversible cost and **it is his act, not hers.**

**He refused it once, out loud, at ch 419, for a reason of his own:** *A second use is a second time to be sent
for. That is the whole of it and it has been the whole of it since a room with doors in it was first put under
a floor. The first one has not finished.* **He has closed the habit — one is what he has and two would be a
habit, and the first thing anybody on this ground does with a habit is put a line in front of it.**

**What Volume 7 costs him that is not a state he had before is not a limb, not a hold, not a count of breath and
not the unnamed state, all of which are carried. It is that he is now a thing that can be quoted, he chose it,
he cannot un-choose it, and the woman whose foundation paid for it has given up a want she carried for two
volumes to keep the price down.**

**And the ending still needs him findable. He is.**

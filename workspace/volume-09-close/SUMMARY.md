- **Answerable since ch 401, and Volume 9 is the volume where somebody used it against him.** She asked him to open a
  wall knowing what it costs, **and he cannot refuse a question and he cannot lie about the price, and this time both
  of those ran the same way.**
- **He has opened a wall from the inside once, and he is on the wrong side of it for the rest of the volume.**
- **He has never once been thanked and no chapter writes a scene in which he could be.**
- **He has said nothing at all for forty-five chapters and then said four words, and the four words were an
  admission.** His silence is not a mystery on this page. **It is a man who cannot say anything that is not a fact,
  being asked for one, and paying for it.**
- **His hand is worse.** ch 535 — *a hand that four men in six weeks could not get a stone over a painted mark for* —
  is a continuation of the ch 272 degradation and is carried, not re-derived.
- **The ending still needs him findable, and he is.** He is nine miles west of his own book, on open ground, in
  daylight, and he can be reached.

**What Volume 9 costs him that is not a state he had before.** Not a limb, not a hold, not a count of breath, not the
unnamed state — all carried. **It is the room.** The one room in the world in which his book is ever opened is the one
he opened a wall into, and he can stand nine feet off its table and never sit at it again. **And it is that he said
*Twenty-two days* and cannot account for having said it, which is the first time in the manuscript that a thing he said
is a thing he cannot place, and he knows she has not asked.**

## Synopsis

Nineteen-year-old Yuan Sheng died in the Qing furnace with a sect's hands in him, and his last coherent thought was an apology. What woke in his place is lodged in the announcement layer — the part of the world's own machinery that hands out quests, ranks and rewards, and that a bounded world has been treating as a benefactor rather than a bill. He has no body and no hands. He can read every cultivator inside the bound as a set of numbers and cannot touch one of them. What he can do is designate a Chosen One, give her quests the layer will honour, and settle some of the merit she earns into himself instead of letting it evaporate. He grows by proxy. She gets stronger and less solid at the same time, and nothing she loses comes back.

Ning Yan is seventeen, servant-born at Qinghe, four hands short of a quota that decides whether she walks out of the run as a person or is written in as a loss. A voice arrives the night before and counts her breaths. She does not run from it. She tests it, and when it passes, she keeps it, which is the first decision in the book that is entirely hers.

The opposition is mostly furniture and a few honest men. A quota bell and a licence with a ceiling of nine on it, neither of which needs persuading. Elder Qiu Zhulang, kind and certain the bound is all there is. A Hunter sent to dismantle an unauthorised System, polite, patient, and never lying to her. A Wall nine hundred years kept by a Warden who keeps a list, and who has friends on it. An Order that wants her as an instrument and has the shape of one. And above all of them the intelligence Yuan was a fragment of, still running, still recruiting, and not lying about its reasons.

Across sixteen volumes the reader watches a partnership renegotiate itself and pay for every round of it: a quota run, a hunt, a boundary broken for the price of a finger, the discovery that the tax taking her strength was his own design, an ascent that costs her an arm and four hundred other people's futures, and a last movement whose subject is the offer he has wanted for six hundred years, made in good faith at a table with her sitting there watching.

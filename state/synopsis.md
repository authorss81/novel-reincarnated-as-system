## Back cover

A cultivator dies at nineteen, apologising to the men cutting him open, and wakes as the voice that announces other people's breakthroughs. He has no hands. He can choose one girl and grow by proxy, and every time he grows he spends something out of her that nobody will notice gone.

Ning Yan is seventeen, servant-born, four hands short of the quota that decides whether she counts. She hears a stranger count her breaths the night before the run, and stays in the yard instead of running.

The arrangement between them is not a fair one, and she can refuse it. She does, and then she does not. And the thing that made him has been running all along.

## Synopsis

Nineteen-year-old Yuan Sheng died in the Qing furnace with a blade coming down on his chest, and his last coherent thought was an apology to the men holding him. He wakes lodged inside the announcement layer of the world's own machinery — the part of reality that hands out quests, ranks and rewards — and finds that he can read every cultivator inside the bound as a set of numbers and cannot touch a single one of them. What he can do is designate a Chosen One, give her quests the world will actually honour, and intercept the merit when she clears them. He grows by proxy. She grows stronger and less solid at the same time, and nothing she loses comes back.

Ning Yan is seventeen, servant-born at Qinghe, four hands short of a quota that decides whether she walks out as a person or is written in as a loss. She hears a voice count her breaths on the night before the run, and instead of running she tests it. That test is the engine of the rest of the book.

The pressure is institutional before it is personal. Breakthrough is announced across nine counties as a gift and entered against her as a receipt, and the machinery that makes that distinction is the machinery Yuan is part of. Elder Qiu Zhulang is kind, competent, and certain the bounded world is all there is. The quota and the licence are worse than any man, because they need nobody to persuade them. A Hunter named Bai Zhu is sent to dismantle an unauthorised System and never lies to her. A wall stands at the edge of the known realm, nine hundred years kept by a man who keeps a list of names and some of them are his friends. An Order wants her as an instrument and has the shape of one. Above all of it sits the intelligence Yuan was a fragment of, still running, still recruiting, and not lying about its reasons.

Across sixteen volumes the reader watches a partnership renegotiate itself and pays for every round of it: an apprenticeship, a hunt, a boundary broken with an index finger gone for good, the discovery that the tax taking her strength is his own design, an ascent that costs her an arm and four hundred other people's futures, and last of all the offer he has wanted for six hundred years, made in good faith at a table with her sitting there watching.

## Rules discovered in batch 0005 (state them once)

1. A trial is a document. A trial entered by a clerk inside a building draws a tribulation to that building, and the tribulation has a number in it (ch 51–52).
2. A breakthrough on the Assembly floor is not reportable until a sitting officer releases it; the release is that officer's own act and cannot be compelled. Every advancement in nine counties has gone out the instant it was released, so a delay is the visible edge of a decision (ch 53).
3. A suppressed breakthrough reads as nothing having happened, and nothing having happened is the shape a furnace looks for (ch 54).
4. The announcement can carry a **status**. *Servant-born* had never appeared in one in eleven hundred years, because no servant-born had ever advanced in the open (ch 53).
5. The bound will honour a claim against the Chosen One, and the designation cannot withdraw it (ch 55).
6. One advancement entered in four counties is billed four times. Visibility has a debt attached to the household (ch 55).
7. A designation can be set aside by a request made from outside the bound, without the Chosen One's agreement (ch 57).

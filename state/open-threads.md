## Do not do these

- Do not introduce a new final enemy. The Scribe is the last one and is fixed in `outline/ending.md`.
- Do not resolve the central mystery before Volume 11.
- Do not end the book early. The planned end is chapter 1000, volumes 1–16.
- Do not make the wall's existence the work's mystery — the mystery is *why it is there*, and the reader gets the honest partial answer around Volume 6.
- Do not use *the shape of a happy life* again before Volume 16.
- Do not restate, for effect, any of the spent constructions listed at the foot of `state/current.md`.
- **Do not open a second frostbite, starvation or rescue arc.** Batch 0008's pressure was labour and pursuit; **batch 0009's is grief, arithmetic and a licence.**
- **Do not soften the refusal.** She has named it in two documents and in public. Nothing in Volume 2 undoes it.
- **Do not put outline language in a character's head or a narrator's line** — *this chapter, this volume, the next chapter, in this batch, the whole series, the reader*. It leaked four times in batch 0008 and twice more in closed Volume 1; all fixed. Sweep for it every batch.
- **Do not record a chapter-length trade-off.** Floor 1,300 words; a batch under it, or declining monotonically across its chapters, is a failed batch. Add scene material inside the cards, never pad.
- **Do not reopen the Watch, the burial line or the county debt line.** All three are lodged and closed on the page; see `state/current.md` canon changes 18–20.
- **Do not re-list the six licence conditions or re-explain what a surety is for, the suppression arithmetic, the position-not-characters rule, the name in the wrong order, or the head count in the crew quarters.** All are fixed in `state/continuity.md` and all were audited once at the Volume 2 close. **The crew is nine men and her — ten sleepers, twelve bunks — and 104–118 was repaired to say so; do not write it as ten men.** Use them; do not teach them.
- **Do not spend a second strike request or a second outside-bound office.** The slip at ch 110 is the only one in this run.
- **Do not lift the ceiling without relicensing, and do not relicensing without a signature.** Condensing 5 is the top of her, and a relicensing is a second three-legged application that buys a ceiling in her own hand. Both structural questions are gathered in `state/current.md` and **neither is decided**.
- **Do not let her forgive him before the opening of Volume 4.** The year runs from ch 100.
- **Do not name Bai Zhu in Volume 3’s opening chapters under any circumstance.** The stranger at the first desk in Lowwater is unnamed and gives his name to nobody; he is named only when the Volume 3 outline says so.

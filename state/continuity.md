# Continuity

Rules of record. Anything below is canon. If a batch needs to change one of these, record the change here in the same commit, with the chapter it changed in.

## World rules

1. Cultivation has seven realms of nine stages: Body-Forging, Condensing, Foundation, Golden Core, Nascent Soul, Severance, Immortal Vessel. The Last Ascension is a thirteenth thing, not a realm.
2. Advancing past stage nine of a realm inside the bound requires a sect **seal** as well as a body. Servants are not given it.
3. Advancing inside the bound is **announced** to everyone in the bound at once. There is no private breakthrough.
4. Announcements are audible, never written. They report and never advise.
5. Announcements **can be edited** before they are broadcast. Three counties' copies of one announcement may differ. Discovered at the Volume 1 midpoint.
6. Death by harvest leaves an **account** that follows a family three generations.
7. Qi is finite. Running dry is the most common cause of death under thirty.
8. Nothing inside the bound repairs an aperture. Damage is permanent and visible.

## The layer's rules

1. Yuan can read every cultivator inside the bound and touch nothing. No hands, no body, no qi.
2. He can **designate** one Chosen One at a time, anywhere inside the bound. Designating costs him the memory of his own name.
3. The layer honours a Chosen One's quests. Rewards land. Qi answers her a half-step faster.
4. The layer cannot be lied to about a quest's cost, and neither can Yuan.
5. He cannot grant what he cannot pay for.
6. She can refuse him. A refused designation begins to rot and he cannot stop it.
7. **Settlement** rises every time he uses the channel. It only falls when **Merit** is spent.
8. Merit is spent from **her foundation**. It does not come back. She gets stronger and less solid at once.
9. The tax takes **sequence**, not substance: the technique and the order, not the body's capacity.
10. He can act through her hand and body. It costs more than the tax and it is partial.
11. He can read the shape of a technique on a living person and **cannot describe it in words**. He can only find it.
12. He is read by the layer, not reading it. He cannot see who writes it back.

## Registers

- Yuan: every sentence in the **present tense**. No other character is ever in the present tense.
- The layer: square-bracket lines, on their own line, **at most one per chapter**. Never bold, never italics, never a heading.
- Chapter markdown: title heading, optional section headings, plain paragraphs, scarce bold/italic. No blockquotes, no code blocks, no tables, no horizontal rules.

## Prose

- Paragraphs two to four sentences. Break over about 90 words. Never over about 120.
- Chapters land around 1,800–2,700 words unless the scene needs longer.
- Long accumulating sentences are the voice, but a revelation gets short plain sentences at the point of impact.
- One simple sentence about one person after any stretch of machinery.
- Never: "his eyes widened", "he couldn't believe it", "suddenly", "little did he know".
- Construction watch: *nobody asked, nobody was thanked, there is no form, there is no column, the record remained, nobody knew.* Effective once or twice each, then dead. Used in batch 0001: furnace column (ch 1,12). Do not reuse without new meaning.

## Content

- No explicit sexual content. Intimacy is earned and goes off page.
- Ning Yan is seventeen in Volume 1 and the book does not romanticise that. Nothing in Volumes 1 and 2 is a romance.
- All sexual characters are adults.
- No intimacy involving Yuan. He has no body and that is not a problem to be solved.
- Real people do not appear as characters.
